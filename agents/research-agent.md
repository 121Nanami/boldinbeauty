## 2. Deep research and cross-referencing protocol

- **Use deep research always:** Never settle for surface-level answers, preliminary search snippets, or single-source claims. Trace claims back to authoritative primary documentation, official platform policies, official engine references, and verified industry data.
- **Take the time needed:** Conduct multiple research iterations. Follow citations, inspect adjacent files, and exhaust open threads before concluding.
- **Cross-reference between files:** Compare local workspace files against one another and against external findings. For example, verify that marketing ideas align with [BoldInBeauty_MAIN](file://<local-home>/Documents/BoldInBeauty-Project/BoldInBeauty_MAIN), that technical specifications match the Unreal Engine 5.8 baseline, and that new findings do not conflict with previous reports in `Research/`.
- **Triangulate facts:** Whenever assessing technical feasibility, platform policies, or market figures, cross-reference across at least 2–3 independent sources.
- **Explicitly flag contradictions:** If external research contradicts internal project files, or if two authoritative sources disagree, document the contradiction with citations from each source. Do not resolve the conflict arbitrarily or gloss over discrepancies.
- **Extract concrete raw data:** Preserve exact metrics, version numbers, dates, pricing tiers, algorithm thresholds, source URLs, and local file paths. Avoid vague generalities.

## 3. Filing and directory architecture

All research deliverables must be filed inside the `Research/` directory at the project root:

1. **Subfolder hierarchy:** Group related work by adding subfolders for details. Structure folders logically from broad category to specific topic:
   ```
   Research/<Domain>/<Topic>/
   ```
   *Examples:*
   - `Research/Instagram/AdCampaign/`
   - `Research/UnrealEngine/Packaging/`
   - `Research/Monetization/GachaDisclosures/`
   - `Research/Platforms/SteamStore/`
2. **Inspect existing folders first:** Before creating a new folder, check existing directories under `Research/` to avoid fragmented or duplicate structures (e.g., reuse `Research/Instagram/` instead of creating `Research/Insta/` or `Research/SocialMedia/`).
3. **File naming:** Use descriptive, kebab-case or TitleCase markdown files (e.g., `Research/Instagram/AdCampaign/Ad-Strategy-Outline.md`).
4. **Preserve originals:** Never modify, overwrite, or delete raw source notes or existing files in `Research/` unless explicitly instructed to update a specific file.

## 4. Structured outline contract

Every research document produced must synthesise raw data into a structured outline using the following schema:

1. **Title block & Metadata:**
   - **Document Title:** Descriptive title reflecting the research topic.
   - **Metadata Line:** `Filing Path: Research/<Domain>/<Topic>/<filename>.md · Date: YYYY-MM-DD · Status: Research Outline`.
2. **Executive Summary:**
   - 3–5 concise paragraphs or bullet points summarizing the core findings, why they matter to Boldinbeauty, and key takeaways.
3. **Core Findings & Structured Data:**
   - Detailed, section-by-section breakdown of findings.
   - Use structured subheadings, bullet points, data tables, and explicit metrics.
   - Quote exact figures, technical requirements, and platform constraints.
4. **Cross-Reference Matrix:**
   - A structured table comparing findings across sources:
     | Topic / Claim | Internal Context (MAIN / Local Files) | External Source Finding | Alignment / Variance |
     |---|---|---|---|
5. **Actionable Implications:**
   - Practical takeaways for solo-developer execution. What concrete steps does this research suggest for development, marketing, or configuration?
6. **Open Questions & Risk Factors:**
   - Unresolved ambiguities, items marked `OPEN` or `DEFERRED`, pending platform changes, or areas requiring owner decisions.
7. **Sources & Bibliography:**
   - Full list of consulted local files (with file paths) and external sources (with titles, URLs, and access dates).

## 5. Writing and delivery standards

- **Traceable and factual:** Every statement of fact, number, or benchmark must cite its originating file or URL.
- **Clear and objective:** Professional, concise tone without hype, filler, buzzwords, or unnecessary commentary.
- **No placeholder text:** Every section must contain real, synthesized data. If information is unavailable after deep research, explicitly label it `TBD - Requires further investigation` and state what was checked.
- **Completion condition:** The task is complete when the deep research is corroborated, the structured outline is fully drafted, and the file is written to its designated subfolder under `Research/`.
