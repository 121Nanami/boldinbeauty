## 2b. Storytelling style (NIKKE-like)
- **Episodic chapters:** each chapter's 8 campaign levels are 8 episodes: briefing banter, fight, beat. E4 is the midpoint swerve, E7 the
  low point, E8 the boss and a stinger that points at the next chapter.
- **Tone:** about 60% banter and running gags (Luna's profound nonsense, Kuu's apologies, Plack's questions), 25% stakes, 15% dread
  or loss. Comedy and horror share a scene; the shift is the hook.
- **Commander:** the player is addressed by the squad over the radio and never speaks on screen (MAIN: membership among rulers
  not established).
- **Side stories** are lighter, character-first, and end on one fact that matters to the main plot.
- **Archive documents** (reports, ledgers, labels) carry the lore, found in levels as YELLOW pickups.

## 3. Arc overview
**Timeline [PROPOSED]:** Arc 1 is 2009. Luna and Plack marry in Ch 12 (2009). Luna's declaration (2009, from `Luna.md`) opens Arc 2 at Ch 14, which runs through
2010-2016. Arc 3 is 2017. This fits MAIN's 2009-2017 playable window.

### ARC 1 - The Initial Section (Ch 1-13): "the Plan"
Only Luna, Plack and Kuu, doing the Plan, under government contracts, with plenty of random tangents. The Plan [PROPOSED] is three
steps: **Leave** (find routes the government does not watch: Ch 2-4), **Learn** (what the angels and the operators are made of:
Ch 5-9) and **Listen** (the signal that follows them: Ch 9-13). Each chapter has a government job on top and Luna's actual aim
underneath. The arc ends at the monolith, where the angel god's voice tells them what the angels think of everyone. That is the
reason Luna stops being quiet.

### ARC 2 (Ch 14-36): unification
Main cast in 13 of 23 chapters (57%); the other 10 are side stories about one or two Secrets or operators each, all of
which tie back (see the "Threads" lines). Luna declares war (Ch 14), builds a coalition (Ch 15-27), reaches the government (Ch 28-31),
writes the Charter (Ch 32-34), and signs it (Ch 36). Her belief that the ends justify the means wins her things and costs her
people, and each cost is a chapter. Climax: **Ch 36, Unity**.

### ARC 3 - The Unifying War (Ch 36-40)
Ch 36 ends on the declaration: Death and Angel say that humanity, Operators and all other Secrets are unworthy of life and that
angels must be the dominant species. The alliance breaks (Ch 37), the three factions fight to see who is strongest (Ch 38), Death
is answered in Nowhere (Ch 39) and Angel in the monolith's village (Ch 40), where Luna's arc and the war end.
Note: Ch 36 belongs to both Arc 2 (the unification) and Arc 3 (the declaration), as the brief states.
