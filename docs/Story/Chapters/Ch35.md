# Ch 35 - The Ledger

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** The Ledger
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Side story: Death
- **Playable character(s):** Death (story-forced guest)
- **Map:** new `L_Ch35_TheLedger` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Death's last night before the signing. The chapter is a walk through the Long Gallery, Death's archive of the dead, as it is rearranged by the hour. Death is not sentimental and does not need to be. Each room is a year, 2002 to 2009, and the rooms are populated by the counted: people who have died and have not been forgotten. Death sorts them in silence. Near the end a new column appears in the ledger and the heading is not Death's handwriting. It is the red glyphs of the monolith. Death reads it. The heading says unworthy. The counted rise to defend the gallery from the ledger's change and the boss, the Unsigned, is the first name on the page that was never written down. Death walks through. In the morning Death will sign the Charter, and the pen will hover. The player does not see what Death decides. Death does not either, until the hall.

## Key reveal
The monolith's glyphs have written a new heading in Death's ledger: unworthy. Death has been spoken to as well.
