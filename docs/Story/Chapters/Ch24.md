# Ch 24 - The Joke

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** The Joke
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch24_TheJoke` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The Jester left a card in a school in Chapter 2 and has been waiting for the trio to find a use for it. The Big Top under Neo Tokyo is the Jester's carnival: a house of mirrors, a shooting gallery, a tilting floor. The Jester offers the Secrets' vote on one condition: Luna must win three games of the Jester's choosing, honestly. The first two she wins by being bored enough to see the pattern. The third is rigged against her and she knows it. She cheats. The Jester, delighted, announces that it was the only honest move on offer. Kuu is made the butt of the third game and bears it with a smile that will be remembered. Plack does not speak to Luna for the rest of the night. The Ringmaster Machine tries to eject them and loses. The Jester's vote is Luna's, and so is the cost: something between her and Kuu has been used up.

## Key reveal
Jester left the Chapter 2 card; Jester respects cheating that is honest about itself. Luna's methods cost her Kuu's trust.
