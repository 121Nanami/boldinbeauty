# Ch 38 - Who Is Strongest

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Who Is Strongest
- **Arc:** 3 - The Unifying War
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch38_WhoIsStrongest` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The three factions have decided to settle it by force, and each believes it will win. The Operators hold the Docks front, the Secrets have taken the Moon Kingdom, the government the Hanging City's chain. Luna has one argument: that none of them is the strongest, and that she can prove it. She takes the trio to each front in turn and fights the champion there without killing it: the Secrets' Adapted, the government's Chain Marshal, the Operators' own Warlord. Each champion falls in a different way and each wakes up to a surface that is full of angels. Kuu holds the Docks. Plack negotiates a ceasefire with the Hanging City. Luna takes the Moon Kingdom's island, where the Moon flickers cube-yellow for the second time. In the fog a lighthouse beam sweeps the sea and finds nothing. The truce is signed on the lighthouse stair. It lasts two days. Death and Angel's hosts begin to move.

## Key reveal
No faction is strongest; the factions' truce is the first move of Luna's endgame.
