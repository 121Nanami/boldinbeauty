## Summary
On the night of the declaration someone cut the feed, and Media is the one who did. Chapter 15 is her night. Media runs a pirate broadcast station from an abandoned subway studio and has been listening to the arc's official channels for years. She is the only person who has the dead data centre's transmission from Chapter 9: she rebroadcast the garbled Morse herself as a warning and nobody listened. When Luna's speech began she saw something in the feed's metadata that nobody else was looking for, a kill order embedded in the signal that would have triggered on the word 'oppression'. She cut the line to save Luna and then had to hold the studio against sponsor-owned clean-up squads and jammer drones until sunrise. Her studio, her tapes, her gear: all of it is at stake. Luna arrives at dawn with a coffee she did not pay for and says, in her usual way, that she owes Media nothing and will therefore pay it all back. This is the start of a friendship.

## Key reveal
