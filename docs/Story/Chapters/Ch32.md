# Ch 32 - The Charter

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** The Charter
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch32_TheCharter` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The Charter needs three signatures: the Operators, the Secrets and the government. Judge's precedent from Chapter 17 says that the document must be witnessed in the Charter Vault, a sealed legal archive under the old city hall. The trio go to the vault to draft it. The vault tests each clause. Each trial is a room that stands for one of the three. The Operators' room is a barracks, the Secrets' room is a stage, the government's room is a courtroom. Luna writes the Charter's first draft in her usual nonsense and Plack rewrites it into something people can sign. Kuu reads it out. The Charter Guardian, the vault's keeper, tests that the text is binding. When it is, a seventh line is left blank on the signature page: the seat that was never filled. The Guardian says that it is the only line that matters. Luna asks Judge to find the one who belongs there.

## Key reveal
The Charter needs seven Secret signatures, including the unlisted seat; Judge is sent to find it.
