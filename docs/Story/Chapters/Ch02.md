## Summary
The government hands the trio a routine salvage job: go up to the surface through one of sixteen hatches and bring back a dead operator's field recorder from the ruins above the arc. For Luna it is the first step of her Plan, which is to learn which parts of the Outer World still stand and which routes stay quiet. The school, the police and fire station, the hospital and the broken stadium are worked in turn. Plack checks every classroom for survivors who are not there. Kuu spends a whole level hunting a lost-and-found box because of a rumour about a lunchbox. Luna sells half the scrap to a roaming peddler before they have left the hatch pad. At the stadium a mass of fused angels rises out of the stands. Afterwards Luna plays back the recorder and notices what it shows: the angels did not wander. They stood in rows, all facing west, toward the sea. The next route is chosen. In the school's staff room, under a fridge magnet, a single playing card sits face down. Nobody mentions it.

## Key reveal
Surface angels hold a formation facing west, toward the sea. (Hint only: one face-down playing card.)

## Locations
Hatch pad (16 surface hatches), School, Police and Fire station, Hospital, Gym / broken stadium (boss).

## Gameplay beats
  - Level 1: hatch pad, learn the surface (first section is a safe walk, then the first angels).
  - Levels 2-7 follow the Outer World building types: school, police + fire station, hospital, stadium approach. One mini-boss each.
  - Traversal set piece: walk or swim across the flooded crater via the toppled tower.
  - Puzzle: the 16 hatches only open in numeric order; the player works out the order from the school's room numbers.
  - Random tangent: Kuu's lost-and-found quest (optional pickups, YELLOW), Luna's peddler sale (BLUE).
  - CUTSCENE (PURPLE): playback of the recorder, the angels in rows facing west.
  - Boss: The Crowd, fused angels in the stadium stands (climb the stands to reach the weak points).
  - Abilities unlocked: none proposed (combat kits are owner-owned).

## New characters / creatures / props
Scrap Peddler (one-off NPC), The Crowd (boss), field recorder (prop), face-down playing card (prop).

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: sixteen hatches, one stuck. Luna's first act on the surface is selling the hatch's hinge.
2. School: Plack reads every classroom noticeboard aloud while Kuu hunts the lost-and-found box.
3. The peddler returns with Luna's own hinge for sale at double the price; a short, truly petty argument.
4. Midpoint swerve: the field recorder's last entry plays: the dead operator said the angels 'were counting us'.
5. Police and fire station: a locked evidence room, a cat, a fire truck that still starts.
6. Hospital: Plack stops speaking; there are no bodies and every bed has a name card in the same handwriting.
7. Low point: the stadium's stands are moving; the Crowd is assembling out of the angels that stood facing west.
8. Boss: the Crowd. Stinger: the recorder replay shows the rows of angels, all facing the sea. Luna says 'west it is' and pockets a playing card she did not mention.
