## Summary
A desert nobody should be able to survive, with violent winds and a constant downpour, and in the middle of it a data centre that is still powered. The trio's government orders say only 'recover what you can'. Luna reads that as permission to take whatever is useful. They climb the cable trunks, shelter in cooling towers and wade through flooded hollows. In the server hall a small screen shows the only line of text that is not a log. Plack types the Morse into the recorder from Chapter 2 and gets a damaged version of a sentence; Luna says she can read it. She cannot, and says so only to Kuu later. The data centre's last defender, a thing built from fallen server racks the team calls the Uptime, takes the hall down with it. As they leave the vents breathe out a hum that does not stop for several hours. Kuu says his head hurts. Nobody connects it to what happens next.

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: a desert, a downpour, a wind that speaks three words and leaves.
2. Dunes: Kuu loses a boot and Plack finds a sock in a different place.
3. Cable trunks: climbing along the black lines of a thing buried in the sand.
4. Midpoint swerve: the data centre is lit, warm and tidy. It has been waiting.
5. Cooling towers; steam; the vents breathe a low note Kuu says he can feel in his teeth.
6. Server hall: the small screen, the damaged Morse; Luna says she can read it. She cannot.
7. Low point: the Uptime assembles from server racks and the hall's lights say, over and over, 'Operator failed.'
8. Boss: the Uptime. Stinger: the hum does not stop when they leave. It follows them across the desert.
