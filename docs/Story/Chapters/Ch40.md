- **Map:** new `L_Ch40_TheyWerePurity` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The black monolith stands in the rain at the middle of the village where it began. The trio walk in at dawn, past the torii, up the shrine stair, and the angel god speaks, not to Luna but through Angel, who is waiting at its foot. Angel is not hostile. Angel is certain. The fight is the last one and the longest, in the rice paddies and on the river bridges, in rain that does not stop. Luna uses her black and white hole as she has never used it, and the monolith's glyphs go out one by one. Angel falls last, quietly, asking only whether any of it was purity. Luna says no. The Charter is intact. The war ends. Luna gives up her command seat, takes her place before Judge's bench for the charge of war crimes in the spring of 2017 and accepts the verdict, whatever it is. Plack is beside her. Kuu keeps the door. It is raining in Neo Tokyo.

## Key reveal
The angel god's voice, Angel's origin in the monolith, and Luna's arc: the one who treated people as pawns accepts judgment and gives up command. (Epilogue, 2017.)
