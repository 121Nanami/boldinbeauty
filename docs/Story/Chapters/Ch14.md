# Ch 14 - Declaration

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Declaration
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch14_Declaration` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The trio come back from the countryside with a rainstorm on their shoulders and a decision in Luna's head: the Plan cannot be quiet any more. Luna wants the whole arc to hear it. They break into the government broadcast spire above Neo Tokyo and take the main transmitter. Plack argues against the speech all the way up the stairs and then sits next to Luna as she gives it. Kuu holds the door. On air, Luna declares war on the government in her usual register of nonsense that sounds profound, and the feed is cut halfway through the word 'oppression' by an unseen hand. The commanders answer within the hour: Luna is a war criminal, the trio are fugitives and operators found helping them are to be arrested. The Primetime Squad, the sponsors' favourite fighters, blocks the way down the spire. Kuu is hurt, not badly. The trio escape into the rain. The year is 2009. For the first time since Chapter 1 the arc's streets are loud with people arguing about them.

## Key reveal
Luna declares war on the government (2009); she is branded a war criminal.

Neo Tokyo downtown, government broadcast spire (lobby, studios, transmitter, roof), escape streets.

  - Random tangent: Luna insists on a second take.

Primetime Squad (boss), spire security, the commanders' answer (voice, no models).

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: dawn over the spire; Luna rehearses the speech to a mirror and rejects three drafts.
2. Lobby: the trio walk in on a school tour and are given visitor stickers.
3. Studio floors: a quiz show set; Kuu wins a toaster while avoiding a guard.
4. Midpoint swerve: the transmitter is guarded by the Primetime Squad, who recognise them from the Paradise wedding.
5. Climb: the antenna mast; Plack fixes the wiring while Luna reads her speech out loud.
6. Luna goes live. Plack reads over her shoulder and changes one word. The feed is cut in the middle of 'oppression'.
7. Low point: Kuu is shot while holding the door; he says it is a flesh wound and passes out.
8. Boss: Primetime Squad. Stinger: the commanders' answer comes in on every screen: 'Luna is a war criminal.' She says, 'Good title.'
