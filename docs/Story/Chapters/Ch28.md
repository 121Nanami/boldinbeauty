# Ch 28 - The Commanders' Table

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** The Commanders' Table
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch28_CommandersTable` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Luna asks to meet the government and, to everyone's surprise, is given a time. The commanders sit in a council chamber at the top of the government tower, seven of them, under a ceiling of sponsor logos. The trio come in under truce, which means the elevator ride is the most dangerous part. The commanders have always known she would come. They offer terms: Luna's war crime charge dropped in return for the Secrets' loyalty to the government. Luna counters with the Charter that she has not yet written. The Table splits into hardliners and reformers over the answer, in public, in front of her. Plack reads the wing logo on the ceiling and recognises it from Paradise. Kuu reads the room and the guards and does not like what he sees. The truce ends at the chamber door. The Sovereign Guard fight the trio out of the tower. Luna has the votes she did not know she needed: the reformers' names.

## Key reveal
Seven commanders, split into hardliners and reformers; the Patron's wing logo is over the Table.

Government tower (lobby, elevators, council chamber, roof).

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: the elevator of the government tower; the music is a sponsored jingle.
2. Lobby: truce rules read aloud by a guard who is plainly afraid.
3. Floors: offices; a thousand framed logos; Kuu is offered a sponsorship by an intern.
4. Midpoint swerve: seven commanders under a ceiling of wings.
5. The Table: hardliners and reformers split in front of her, on purpose.
6. Plack names the logo on the ceiling: the Paradise wing.
7. Low point: the truce ends at the chamber door.
8. Boss: the Sovereign Guard. Stinger: Luna leaves with the reformers' names and a coffee she does not drink.
