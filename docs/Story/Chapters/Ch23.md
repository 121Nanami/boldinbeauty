- **Map:** new `L_Ch23_RedSky` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Weather runs storms and Stargazer maps the sky, from an observatory at the arc's crown, where the hatches to the surface come together. The chapter follows them through the worst storm either has seen: an angel front that does not behave like weather. Weather reads it by feel and Stargazer by pattern. The two do not agree and the chapter's drama is how they settle it. Their instruments fight them. A swarm of storm angels takes the observatory floor by floor. The finding, when the front breaks, is a map: all of the angels' movements converge on one spot far to the west, where there is a black monolith in a countryside village, and there is a second point where nothing converges at all, a hole in the pattern. Stargazer draws both. Weather names the first 'the Centre' and refuses to name the second. Luna files the map. The boss, the Stormfront, goes down in a single clap of thunder.

## Key reveal
The angels converge on a point in the countryside (the monolith) and avoid another point (Nowhere).
