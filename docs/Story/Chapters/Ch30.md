## Summary
The lights are on again at A.R.C TECH. The commanders have ordered the Line restarted and the trio go back to the factory they left running in Chapter 5 to shut it. This time there are people: engineers, guards, a new Foreman. The factory is making something else. The cubes are the same yellow but larger. Plack follows the cage labels from the convention hall to a cradle hall at the end of the line. It holds a single empty cradle and a plate that reads ANGEL V1, RELEASED TO CUSTODY. The custody log, when Kuu finds the terminal, lists the patron by name and by seat: not an operator, not a commander, but a Secret. The one they had thought did not exist. It has been in the arc the whole time, signing the sponsors' cheques. Luna sabotages the Line anyway. The Foreman Mk II goes down. The Patron's name is on every document in the building.

## Key reveal
ANGEL V1 was released into the arc's custody and is a Secret, the Patron, whom all the sponsor money comes from. Angel is the seventh Secret.
