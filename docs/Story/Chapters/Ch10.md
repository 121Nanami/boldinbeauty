## Summary
Nothing here is real, and the trio know that and it does not help. They wake on a pastel island that looks like a child's drawing, joined to three more by floating stairs, with a chair as tall as a house and a teacup you can climb. Then something hyper-real walks past: a face in too much detail, a shape that should not stand. Each island belongs to one of them. Luna's is a perpetual waiting room where nothing is ever on. Kuu's is a street he cannot leave. Plack's is a wedding cake that bleeds. A Morse message is carved into each island's floor and, read in order, tells a small story: the angels bring mercy for all, not those stupid pure ones you call angels but TRUE ANGELS THE ONE, the rest are filth compared to its existence. The Mimic wears each of their faces in turn and falls only when the three agree it is not one of them. The hum stops. They do not talk about it.

## Key reveal
There is a single TRUE ANGEL, and ordinary angels are 'filth' compared to it. (Verbatim Morse lines.)

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: a pastel island, a chair as tall as a house; nothing here is real, which does not help.
2. Luna's island: a waiting room where the television is off, and she waits anyway.
3. Kuu's island: a street he cannot leave; every shop sells one thing: apologies.
4. Midpoint swerve: the Morse in the floor reads 'THE ANGELS BRING MERCY FOR ALL'. Under it, a smaller line: 'not those'.
5. Plack's island: a wedding cake that bleeds and a guest list written in teeth.
6. Impossible stairs; hyper-real horrors walking past; Luna decides to ignore them out of rudeness.
7. Low point: the Mimic wears Luna's face and says what she is afraid to: 'I do not know why I am doing this.'
8. Boss: the Mimic. Stinger: the fourth Morse line, 'THE REST ARE ALL FILTH COMPARED TO ITS EXISTENCE', is carved by someone who had time.
