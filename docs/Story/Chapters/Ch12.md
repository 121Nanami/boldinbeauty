## Summary
The trio arrive in a neo-tropical city that has not been touched by anything: bright, clean, heavy rain on warm streets, canals, rooftop gardens, waterfalls. The government calls it a showcase and the sponsors have paid for every umbrella. The trio are guests of the Ambassador, a celebrity operator whose job is to be seen being nice. Luna eats five meals and then reads the menu: every dish, building and banner carries the same stylised wing logo. Plack, who has carried a ring through four chapters of horror, decides this is the last quiet day they will get and asks, in a rooftop garden, before the cameras arrive. Luna says yes before the question is finished. They marry in 2009 under a waterfall, with Kuu as best-person losing the rings twice. The sponsors stream it to the whole arc and name the moment. Kuu finds the service tunnels and the rationing office under the showcase, and Luna collects receipts instead of souvenirs. The Ambassador crashes the reception to bless the union, on camera, and the lagoon fight is broadcast to everyone. The trio leave by the back road at dusk, soaked, married and carrying a bag of fruit.

## Key reveal
The government's wealth and sponsorship are paid for by one patron whose logo is a stylised wing. (The Patron is not named.) Luna and Plack marry (2009).

2. Tour: Luna eats five meals; Plack reads the menu and finds the same wing logo on every plate.
3. Midpoint swerve: Plack proposes. The city streams it live to the whole arc; the sponsors name the moment.
