# Ch 18 - Backend Deals

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Backend Deals
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch18_BackendDeals` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The war is not going well on paper. Payments are late, a recruit is feeding someone information and defections are quietly rising. The trio go into the black market under Neo Tokyo, the Exchange, to find the leak. Kuu spots the spy first: a recruit who keeps a second ledger. Luna's instinct is to eliminate the leak. Plack's is to ask what the leak wanted. It turns out the spy was being paid to keep a sick sibling alive and the government's offer was a medical contract. Luna writes a cheque and calls it 'a business deal'. It is the first time she acts on a question Plack has put to her. The spy handler, the Broker, is not so easily bought and the Exchange's floor turns into a fight. When the money has stopped moving Luna stays at a stall alone and asks the stall-holder how much a favour is worth. The answer is that it is the same as a person. She does not like that answer.

## Key reveal
Luna's recruits are paid, bought and sold: the war runs on backend deals, and treating people as pawns costs her.

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: Kuu's ledger shows nine coins missing; he is sure it is ten.
2. The Exchange: stall-holders who all smile the same smile.
3. A ledger vault; a spy-hunt that Luna treats like a game.
4. Midpoint swerve: the spy is a recruit with a sick sibling and a medical contract.
5. Luna pays him. 'A business deal.' Plack looks at her for a long time.
6. The Broker's men arrive; the floor becomes a fight.
7. Low point: Luna realises she does not know how much a favour is worth.
8. Boss: the Broker. Stinger: a stall-holder tells her a favour is worth exactly one person. She does not like that.
