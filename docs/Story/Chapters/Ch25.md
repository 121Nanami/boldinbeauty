## Summary
The government's sponsored Cadet Cup is a live-fire exercise in which young operators fight for brand deals. Adapt is range master. The chapter follows Adapt through one run of the Cup. Every course Adapt builds changes to suit the one running it, and the point of the exercise is that the cadets are being trained to be sponsors' products. Adapt's own rule is that nobody should be harmed on a course they cannot learn. A sponsor's representative changes the live ammunition mid-run. The course adapts as designed and the cadets are caught in it. Adapt tears up its own rules and fights the sponsor's guard personally. The chapter's boss is the Sponsor's Warden. In the aftermath Adapt tells Luna that it will join her unification on one condition: the course must adapt to her. She agrees without asking what that means. The cadets are sent home with their prize money and a very careful lecture about reading contracts. Adapt keeps the course's blueprint and, for the first time, does not rebuild it.

## Key reveal
Adapt has been training cadets as products for the sponsors, and will join Luna if the course adapts to her.
