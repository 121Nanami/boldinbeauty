- **Map:** new `L_Ch39_NowhereInhabited` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The map in Stargazer's drawer has a hole in it. Luna takes the trio to the hole. It is the place the trio walked through once and nothing happened: grey, silent, a mashup of every place they have ever been. This time it is not empty. The counted are here, all of them, standing in rows, and they do not look at the trio. It is Death's seat. Nowhere is the place where nothing is counted because everything is. Death has been waiting since Chapter 11. The trio walk through the maps of their whole story in reverse: Neo Tokyo's crossing, the toppled tower, the lighthouse, the data centre, the teacup. Each is grey, each is slightly wrong and each holds one thing the trio left behind. Death meets them at the exit and asks, again, whether a count is a life. Luna answers at last. The fight is long. When Death yields the counted lie down.

## Key reveal
Nowhere is Death's domain; the trio were counted in Chapter 11. Luna answers Death's question.
