# Ch 27 - Ordinary Hours

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Arc:** 2 - Unification

- **Playable character(s):** Ishii, Shiraishi and Arita (story-forced guests)
- **Map:** new `L_Ch27_OrdinaryHours` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Three three-star operators go to work. The chapter is their shift in the Barracks and the Sponsor Mall: unpaid overtime, branded armour that does not fit, a manager who calls them 'talent'. Nobody is a hero. Ishii counts the hours, Shiraishi keeps the ledger of grievances, Arita fixes the armour with tape. When a sponsor's vault takes a month's wages, the three decide to take it back, in a heist that goes wrong in small ways. The chapter is full of tangents and one big idea: that the operators have never been asked. By the end they have a table in the Barracks and a sign on it that reads COUNCIL, in marker. The Compliance Officer, a mech with a clipboard, is the boss. Luna's recruiters find the table later. The unification needs a body that can sign for operators. This is it. Nobody in the Barracks knows yet that the table is the most important piece of furniture in the arc, and the three of them go back to work the next morning, on time, because somebody has to.

## Key reveal
The Operators have their own body, grown from below: the Council.
