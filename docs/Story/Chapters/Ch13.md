## Episodes
1. Cold open: a countryside village in steady rain; a torii, a shrine, a stone stair, a stillness.
2. The village is lived-in: deserter operators who live off the land, polite and not at all happy to see anyone in a uniform.
3. Kuu fixes a bridge. Plack helps with the paddies. Luna lets herself be taught to plant rice, badly.
4. Midpoint swerve: the villagers kneel in the evening, facing the middle of the valley, without a word.
5. The monolith: fifty metres tall, black, with red glyphs nobody can read.
6. When the trio step close the voice of the angel god speaks: 'They wree purity, Impure sin, Thats all.'
7. Low point: the Shepherd, the village's guardian operator, will not let them leave unjudged.
8. Boss: the Shepherd. Stinger: Luna looks at the monolith and says, 'Is that all?'. The glyphs brighten. The voice does not answer.
