## Summary
From outside it is the biggest structure anyone has seen, a black shell taller than the arc's ceiling should allow. Inside it is a perfectly ordinary ten-floor office with a lobby, reception, cubicles and a coffee machine that still works. The trio are sent to retrieve a single filing box. Plack finds the filing system and does what Plack does: reads everything. Kuu vaults desks for sport. Luna is bored until she finds the five doors that look exactly like walls. Behind them are sealed rooms of classified angel research, each stamped with a grade above the highest operator rating on file. The folder names are the only things Plack copies: ANGEL V1 and OPERATOR V!. There is also a dead man's calendar with one entry circled, a date she cannot place. The Auditor, an angel in a suit, defends the ninth floor like a reception desk. When it falls the elevators all go down at once and the lobby clock chimes a time that is not the time.

## Key reveal
A classified project with two halves exists: an ANGEL V1 file and an OPERATOR V! file. (The rooms' grade stamp hints at something above operators; it is not named.)

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: a black shell taller than the ceiling; inside, a lobby with a plant and a bell.
2. Reception: Luna rings the bell nineteen times; Plack signs them in as visitors.
3. Cubicles: Kuu vaults desks; the coffee machine is warm.
4. Midpoint swerve: five doors that look exactly like walls. Behind them, folders. Two names repeat: ANGEL V1, OPERATOR V!.
5. Floor 6: a meeting room set for a meeting that already happened. The minutes are one line long.
6. Elevator shafts: Luna presses every button; the building notes this on a form.
7. Low point: the Auditor asks them to fill in a form, and if they refuse it fills it for them.
8. Boss: the Auditor. Stinger: the lobby clock chimes a time that is not the time and the plant has moved.
