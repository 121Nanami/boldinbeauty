- **Map:** new `L_Ch37_TheAllianceBreaks` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The hall empties in seconds. Death and Angel walk out untouched, because no one in the room is sure who is allowed to stop them. By nightfall the Charter is in pieces. The Operators' Council wants the Secrets disarmed, the Secrets want the government dissolved and the commanders want everyone back at their posts. Each faction takes a district of the Ark and fortifies it. Luna spends the chapter running between them with the Charter's last copy under her arm, trying to keep one door open. The trio fight on three sides at once. The first factional clash, between a Secrets' garrison and the government's Interceptors, burns the Operators' dome. Plack is hurt shielding a civilian and Kuu carries them out. The Hardliner Colossus, a government weapon that has never been used, walks. Luna, perpetually bored, is for once not. Outside, on the surface, the sky is filling with angels in rows facing the arc.

## Key reveal
The alliance fractures along its old lines; the surface angels are massing.
