# Ch 34 - The Night Before

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Arc:** 2 - Unification

- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch34_TheNightBefore` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The signing is at dawn. The Operators' dome in the arc is full of people: operators, Secrets, commanders, cooks. The trio walk through it and are stopped by everyone. It is the quietest chapter and the longest: parties, arguments, apologies, sabotage. Luna and Kuu finally talk about the Jester's games and fail to settle it. Plack and Luna sit on a rooftop and do not talk. The Narrator's last move arrives at midnight: a forged Charter with the clauses rewritten in the Narrator's favour, being printed in a thousand copies. The trio fight through the printing hall. The Narrator's Final Edition, a press the size of a house, is the boss. Luna's offer to the Narrator is the cheap one: write whatever you like, under my name. The Narrator, after a long silence, accepts. Judge's quiet nod from the gallery tells Luna it is the first thing she has done this year that she cannot take back.

## Key reveal
Narrator signs; the war's account will be written under Luna's name. The trio mend what they can.
