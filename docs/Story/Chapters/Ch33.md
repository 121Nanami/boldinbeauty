## Summary
Judge goes down alone to the sanctum below T.O.M.B.S, where the unlisted Secret lives. She expects a prisoner. She finds a room that is entirely white, a man who is entirely angel and a courtesy so complete it is chilling. He is quiet, composed and unfailingly polite. They talk for a day. Judge, who disdains humans, finds that Angel disdains them better. In the corridors outside, the honour guard, the Hush, tests her. She wins, which Angel notes. Angel agrees to sign the Charter in the way a judge agrees to a verdict: in advance and without surprise. Judge walks out thinking she has made a bridge. In the lift she realises that she was the one being weighed. The Charter is signed in her handwriting, not Angel's, and Angel's mark is a small black circle of the monolith's glyphs. Back in the sunlight she tells Luna the Secret's seat is filled, and that she would like it recorded that she said so under her own name. Luna records it. Neither of them says what they think the circle means.

## Key reveal
Angel is 100% angel, and is polite, patient and contemptuous. The monolith's glyphs are Angel's mark.
