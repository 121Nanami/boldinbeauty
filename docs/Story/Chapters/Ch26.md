# Ch 26 - Death's Door

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Death's Door
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch26_DeathsDoor` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The Mortuary Level is where the arc keeps the dead since 2002, a tiered cemetery under the old city with a hall of records at the bottom. Death is the last of the Secrets on Luna's list and the one that frightens her. The route is long and quiet. Plack reads the names on the walls. Kuu stops talking. Luna talks too much. Death receives them at the bottom, in the hall of records, in front of a ledger that is open to a page headed with their own names. Next to each is a single word: visited. Luna tries to ask about it and is answered with the arc's true casualty count since 2002, which is much higher than the government says. Death will attend the unification, and asks only that Luna tell them one thing before it is done: whether a count is a life. She cannot answer. The Pallbearers, Death's guards, escort them out.

## Key reveal
Death keeps the true count of the dead and has recorded the trio as 'visited' somewhere none of them remembers. Death asks whether a count is a life.
