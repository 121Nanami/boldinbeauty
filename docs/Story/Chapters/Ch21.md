- **Arc:** 2 - Unification

- **Map:** new `L_Ch21_FixedDose` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Luna's recruits keep burning out. Alchemist runs a greenhouse laboratory on the arc's farm terraces and believes the problem can be fixed with chemistry. Blood does the heavy lifting. The chapter is the two of them in the middle of an experiment that goes wrong: they have been trying to raise an operator's angel share above 1% using a cube Luna sold them from Chapter 5. The first test subject recovers. The second mutates into something that the whole greenhouse has to fight. Alchemist wants to keep going. Blood wants to stop. The chapter's drama is between the two of them as the glass breaks. The finding is that the 1% is not a choice made by the factory; it is a ceiling. Above it, operators do not become stronger. They become something else. The mutated test subject, Overdose, is the chapter boss. Afterwards Alchemist writes the number 1 on a label and drops the project, and Luna files the cube under 'not worth it'.

## Key reveal
The 1% angel share is a hard ceiling: above it, operators become something else.
