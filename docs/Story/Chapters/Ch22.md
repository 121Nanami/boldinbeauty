# Ch 22 - Narrator's Page

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Narrator's Page
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch22_NarratorsPage` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The Narrator is Luna's enemy, and has been writing about her for months. The trio go into the Library of Unwritten Pages, a vast archive under the old city, because the official account of the war is being written there. Luna wants to meet the author. The Library is a maze of reading rooms whose walls re-arrange when something is read. Plack finds the drafts of Luna's war-criminal charge. Kuu finds the loudspeaker voice from Chapter 5 is the Narrator's, recorded at the factory a long time ago. The Narrator receives them in the central reading room, politely, in the third person. They offer to write Luna a better ending in return for her silence. Luna refuses. The Editor, the Narrator's proxy, attacks. The trio fight their way to the archive's door and the Narrator is gone, leaving a page on the desk with Luna's name and no ending. It is not yet clear who is the author of whom.

## Key reveal
Narrator wrote Luna's charge and the Chapter 5 loudspeaker voice; the arc's official account is fiction by design.
