# Ch 29 - Sponsor Night

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Sponsor Night
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification

- **Playable character(s):** Myrcia and Fujita (story-forced guests)
- **Map:** new `L_Ch29_SponsorNight` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Two three-star operators are hired as 'talent' for the sponsors' gala in Paradise's convention hall. They are told they will pose and smile. They are not told that the gala is built around an incident. Myrcia is the first to notice the stage rigging is not for the show: captive angels have been caged under the stage, and when the lights go up they will be released into the crowd so that the sponsor's favourite fighters can 'rescue' it on live television. Fujita starts quietly breaking locks. The two of them spend the night in the convention hall's back corridors getting the captive angels out in a controlled way, one cage at a time, while the gala goes on above. The Showrunner, the sponsor's producer, is the boss. When the lights go up the audience applauds a staged rescue that never happens. The sponsor's ratings drop. A cage's label, on the way out, has a number on it and the name of a factory.

## Key reveal
The government stages angel incidents for ratings, using captive angels from the Line.
