## Summary
Deep in the rock the trio find a second arc. It has everything the first one has: gates, markets, houses, a town square, a lake. It has no living thing. Flora covers every roof and a vast dim Moon hangs inside the cavern, casting a darkness that makes the world feel under water. The trio are there because a government survey drone went in three times and never came back. Beds are made. Plates are laid. Tombs along the cemetery wall are open and empty. Plack looks for a name on every door. Kuu argues with Luna about whether to take anything at all, and loses. Luna argues with the Moon, which does not answer. On the island under it the Gardener, a thing grown out of the flora itself, defends nothing in particular. When it falls the Moon flickers, and for one second the light is the same yellow as the cubes in Chapter 5. Then it is dim again. Nobody says anything on the way back.

## Key reveal
A second arc exists and was built to be lived in, but it holds no one and no bodies. (Hint only: the empty tombs.)

8. Boss: the Gardener. Stinger: the Moon flickers the exact yellow of the cubes, for one second.
