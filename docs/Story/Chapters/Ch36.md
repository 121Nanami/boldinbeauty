- **Map:** new `L_Ch36_Unity` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Dawn. The Great Hall of the arc is full. Luna walks to the signature table with the Charter in her hands and the whole project behind her: the Operators' Council, five of the Secrets with Judge as witness, the three reformer commanders, and the hardliners who have come to sign under protest. Plack stands at her side. Kuu keeps the door. A hardliner coup fires as the first pen touches paper: Commander Prime and a war mech try to take the hall. The trio hold the room while the signatures go on, one at a time, while the fight ends. It ends. The last line is signed. For one minute everyone in the arc is on one side. Then Death and Angel put down their pens, stand, and declare that humanity, the Operators and every other Secret are unworthy of life, and that the angels must be the dominant species. The hall does not move. Luna says, in nonsense that sounds profound, that she did not hear that. Nobody believes her. The war begins.

## Key reveal
Luna unifies the Operators, the Secrets and the government; Death and Angel then declare the three unworthy of life. (Climax of Arc 2; opening of Arc 3.)
