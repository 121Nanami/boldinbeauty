# Ch 31 - Fracture

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Fracture
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch31_Fracture` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The commanders' split becomes a crack. Three of the seven defect to Luna's side and ask to be taken to a place the hardliners cannot reach: Terminus Nine, an abandoned rail terminal at the bottom of the arc. The trio escort them in an armoured train through three stations. They are not allies. They fear the Line restart, and they want protection from the Patron. Luna takes them anyway, and bargains with them for the Charter. Plack objects openly: these are the people who wrote the contracts. Luna says the ends justify the means and Plack leaves the train at the second station and walks the tunnel for a night. Kuu stays, which costs him something. In the third station the hardliners' armoured train catches up. Luna's alliance holds by a single shared compartment. When Plack comes back it is without a word and with a sandwich. The reformers watch the two of them settle the argument without speaking and decide, privately, that this is a woman they can do business with. Luna does not like that either. The train pulls out of the third station before the dust settles.

## Key reveal
Reformer commanders defect out of fear, not principle. Luna's ends-justify-means belief costs Plack's trust for one night.
