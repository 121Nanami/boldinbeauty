- **Map:** new `L_Ch20_TheHour` (Phase 2)

## Summary
The first of the Secrets Luna has to ask is the Timekeeper, and the Timekeeper has stopped the clocks in half the arc. The Clock Cathedral stands in the old district, where time is a place with doors. Luna comes with an offer and a plan to lose an argument gracefully. Plack takes the lead because Plack is the only one the Timekeeper does not find boring. The Cathedral is a puzzle of rooms with the same minute stuck on them. Kuu notices the pattern first: the stopped places are where the government has no records. The Timekeeper admits it in a sentence. Time you are not counted in cannot be taxed, and that is why the Hanging City's clocks stopped. The Timekeeper says that one place on the arc's map is not one of theirs: a place where the clocks stopped all by themselves. They will not say which. They agree to attend the meeting and give Luna an hour that does not count against her. The Cathedral's guardian, a clock of fused hands, is the price.

## Key reveal
The Timekeeper stopped the Hanging City's clocks on purpose; there is one stopped place that is not theirs.

8. Boss: the Minute Hand. Stinger: 'I never stopped that one,' the Timekeeper says. 'Someone else did.'
