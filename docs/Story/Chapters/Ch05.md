## Summary
A factory runs at full tilt with no one inside it. Conveyors, robot arms and presses make small cubes that glow a violent yellow. The trio came on a government order to 'verify output' and soon find that nothing needs verifying: the machines verify themselves and a calm, friendly voice on the loudspeakers narrates the line's status as if it were reading a bedtime story. Plack follows the pallets to the loading dock and finds each crate stamped with a single figure: 1%. Kuu, who has never liked loud places, sorts out the press hall by timing runs under ten stampers. Luna asks the voice a joke question and it answers, politely, in the third person. Near the cube core the automatic security wakes and the factory's Foreman comes to meet them. When it is down, Luna pockets one cube 'for leverage' and says nothing about it to the other two. The lights do not go out when they leave. The line keeps running.

## Key reveal
The cubes are doses of something and the stamp reads 1%, the same figure as an operator's angel share. (Not explained.) (Hint only: the story-telling loudspeaker voice.)

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)

3. Conveyors: cubes the colour of a sunrise; Plack picks one up and puts it back very carefully.
4. Midpoint swerve: the pallet stamp reads 1%. Plack says the number aloud and Kuu goes quiet.

7. Low point: the Foreman is a person-shaped automaton with a clipboard and it knows all three of their names.
8. Boss: the Foreman. Stinger: Luna's pocket is warm. She does not tell Plack or Kuu about the cube.
