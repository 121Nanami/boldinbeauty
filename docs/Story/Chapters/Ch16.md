# Ch 16 - Recruitment Drive

**Status:** PROPOSED, awaiting owner approval (Phase 1). NEW chapter.

- **Title:** Recruitment Drive
- **Arc:** 2 - Unification
- **Focus:** Main cast (Luna, Plack, Kuu)
- **Playable character(s):** Luna, Plack, Kuu (story-forced); the rest of the team is the player's choice
- **Map:** new `L_Ch16_RecruitmentDrive` (Phase 2)

## Summary
Luna needs an army and has no idea what an army needs. She sets up a recruiting table in the Underworld below the town square and posts one rule: she pays on time. People come: unregistered operators from the Hanging City, off-duty fighters, three-star grunts tired of branded armour, a few gang members who sign up for the pay. Plack runs the sign-in. Kuu keeps the peace. Luna treats the lot of them as chess pieces and her briefings are full of nonsense that sounds like strategy. The operators' contracts surface in the paperwork: sponsorship clauses that own their likeness and a share of their wages. A recruit sells the table's location to the government and an ambush takes the Underworld. In the aftermath Luna's first response is to blame the recruit's character. Plack's first response is to ask what the recruit was being paid. The chapter ends with Luna writing the question down, which is not the same as asking it.

## Key reveal
Operators' contracts own their likeness and wages (the cause of why operators are cheap to the government). Luna's pawn mindset has a first cost.

## Locations
The Underworld (town-square underside), recruiting table, market stalls, tunnels.

  - Random tangent: a recruit who only wants to talk about soup.
  - Abilities unlocked: none proposed.

Repo (boss), the recruit who defects, recruit NPCs (BLUE).

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: a hand-painted sign in the Underworld: 'LUNA PAYS ON TIME.'
2. The sign-in table: Plack's clipboard, Kuu's patience, Luna's briefing about 'chess pieces'.
3. Hanging City operators arrive and ask where the lift is.
4. Midpoint swerve: the contract: sponsor owns your likeness, voice, and a third of your wages.
5. Recruit missions: four operators, four terrible reasons, one of them about soup.
6. A recruit smiles too much. Kuu watches him. Luna does not.
7. Low point: the Underworld is ambushed; the recruit sold the table.
8. Boss: Repo. Stinger: Plack asks what the recruit was being paid. Luna writes the question down.
