## Summary
A flooded transport tunnel, rain pouring through the roof grates, the water rising at every junction. This is the road the angels use when they come out of wherever they come from. The government wants it cut and has given the trio a demolition kit and a deadline. Luna refuses to hurry and takes a long look at what is moving through. Angels stream through the tunnel in one direction only, orderly in lanes, each carrying a small glowing shard. None of them returns. None attacks until the trio get in the way. Plack counts them until counting stops being useful. Kuu swims the pump room alone, because someone has to. At the far end, where the lanes bend toward the assumed headquarters, the Toll Collector stands in the path and asks, in a voice made of light, for something the trio do not have. They fight their way past it and Luna decides not to blow the tunnel after all. She tells the other two it is because the explosives are damp.

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: rain pours through the grates; Luna looks at the demolition kit and puts it back in the bag.
2. The tunnel: angels in lanes, all moving the same way; Plack counts until the numbers are not useful.
3. A flood junction; Kuu volunteers to check the depth and goes under.
4. Midpoint swerve: every angel is carrying a shard. They are not attacking; they are delivering.
5. Pump room: a dive, a flooded control desk, a rubber duck Kuu insists on keeping.
6. Luna refuses to blow the tunnel: 'the explosives are damp.' Plack decides not to ask.
7. Low point: the lanes bend at the gate and the Toll Collector asks them for a ticket none of them have.
8. Boss: the Toll Collector. Stinger: Luna watches the last angel go by, shard glowing, toward the arc.
