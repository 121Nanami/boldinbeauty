## Summary
The Deck is a casino on the arc's upper terrace where everyone has a price. Gambler runs the book on the war: odds on Luna, on the government, on the Secrets, on whether the trio last the year. The chapter is Gambler's night when a mysterious backer tries to fix the book: someone has been buying every bet against Luna, with money that does not come from any known fund. The Deck's floors, vaults and private rooms are hunted by the backer's enforcers. Gambler honours her rules and keeps her own count of the house. At the end of the night she recognises the playing card Luna carries from Chapter 2: it is not from her tables and not from any house in the arc. She says it is a calling card, and that there is only one person in the arc who leaves cards behind. Luna buys the information with an IOU. Gambler declines to say what the odds on her are.

## Key reveal
The playing card from Chapter 2 is a calling card and belongs to Jester, not to any house.
