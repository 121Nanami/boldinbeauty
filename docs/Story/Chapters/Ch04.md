## Summary
The western route ends in a city that should not exist: a disc of streets hanging over an endless void from a single chain whose top cannot be seen. It is on no government map. Its people are unregistered operators who have lived off the record for years, travelling between four hanging districts by lifts and plank bridges. They hire the trio to clear angels that have started to drift down the chain. To Luna this is the first group she can recruit without the government finding out, and she counts heads like inventory. Plack stays to repair a broken lift and ends up running the lift rota. Kuu is sick with vertigo and everyone has a joke about it. Every clock in every apartment shows the same minute. None are broken; they are simply stopped. Luna asks nobody why. At the chain anchor the biggest angel they have met so far waits for them, and when it falls the city goes quiet for the first time in years. The residents ask the trio to stay. Luna says she will be back, and writes the lift schedule down.

## Key reveal
Operators exist outside government records; an off-grid city is possible. (Hint only: every clock stopped on the same minute.)

## Locations
Rim and city disc, four hanging districts, swinging plank bridges, lifts, the chain (climb), chain anchor (boss).

## Gameplay beats
  - Level 1: arrival by the chain; learn lifts and bridges.
  - Levels 2-7: one hanging district per level pair; plank-bridge jump sequences, lift timing, climbing the chain links to bypass angels.
  - No ground anywhere: falling is death (kill Z); checkpoints (GREEN) on every district.
  - Random tangent: the lift rota mini-task for Plack, Kuu's vertigo bark lines, a resident dispute about whose turn it is to feed a cat.
  - Boss: The Anchorite, a district-sized angel at the chain anchor.
  - Abilities unlocked: none proposed.

## New characters / creatures / props
The Anchorite (boss), unregistered residents (BLUE NPCs), the stopped clocks (prop).

## Episodes (NIKKE-style: briefing banter, fight, beat; E4 midpoint swerve, E7 low point, E8 boss + stinger)
1. Cold open: a city hangs from a chain into the fog; Kuu announces he is staying at the entrance.
2. Lifts and plank bridges; the residents ask for ID and are delighted when nobody has any.
3. Plack fixes the north lift; the residents elect Plack 'rota captain' by acclamation.
4. Midpoint swerve: every clock in the city shows 4:12. 'It has always been 4:12,' says a child, cheerfully.
5. A district of angels drifting down the chain; Kuu climbs links while narrating his own death.
6. Luna recruits her first unregistered operators and is told, kindly, that she is 'doing it wrong'.
7. Low point: the chain groans; the Anchorite is not defending the anchor, it is holding the city up.
8. Boss: the Anchorite. Stinger: the residents let the trio keep the lift schedule, and one clock moves to 4:13 for the first time in years.
