# Ch 15 - Dead Air - Script

**Arc 2 / 2009 / SIDE STORY: Media.** The night of the declaration, from the other end of the wire. A pirate broadcaster in an abandoned
subway studio, hunted by sponsor jammers. Lighter tone; ends on one fact that matters.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
4 a.m. A mixing desk the size of a bar counter, covered in tape and wires. A woman in headphones with a bowl of cereal, talking to
nobody. Behind her, a hundred small lights. Above her, a ceiling of subway tiles with a sign that says PLATFORM 3.
MEDIA: (into a microphone, in a voice like silk on gravel) This is Studio Static, the only honest frequency in the arc, brought to you
by the cereal I am eating. Today's top story: nobody knows anything. Stay tuned.

## Episode 1 - "Studio Static"
**Scene:** The studio. **Present:** Media (playable guest), her assistant drone Staple (BLUE).

MEDIA: Staple, where's the cold open?
STAPLE (a small drone): It's in the bin.
MEDIA: Why is the cold open in the bin?
STAPLE: Because you said it was bad.
MEDIA: It *was* bad. Get it out of the bin. We'll use it as a bed under the news.
*(The speaker on the wall crackles; the arc's official channel, ghosting through. A voice reading the day's items.)*
MEDIA: (flat, delighted) "Operator unrest is not a thing." Staple, take a note: *operator unrest is a thing.*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Media's kit as guest (the player controls her at the desk: tune, patch, broadcast); a first jammer drone.
**Beat:** Introduce Media; tone: funny, tired, caffeinated.

## Episode 2 - "Jammers"
**Scene:** The platform. **Present:** Media, Staple. Jammer drones (RED).

STAPLE: Incoming. Seventeen jammers.
MEDIA: Are they sponsored?
STAPLE: They have the logo.
MEDIA: (finishing her cereal) Then they're going to be extremely annoying and not especially dangerous.
*(The drones swarm; static floods the speakers.)*
MEDIA: (loud, clear, over the static) "In the event of interference please remain calm. We are experiencing a *known issue* which
is called *the government.*"

**Gameplay:** Jammer drone combat on the platform; use of the patch panel as cover / pulse.
**Beat:** The studio is under siege.

## Episode 3 - "Patch Panel"
**Scene:** The patch room. **Present:** Media, Staple.

STAPLE: The relay's gone dead. Three connections out of nine.
MEDIA: Which ones?
STAPLE: Five, six, seven.
MEDIA: Of course. Of *course* it's the arts desk. (she sits; she cracks her knuckles; she takes out a cassette) We'll do this by ear.
*(A short audio puzzle: listen to each cable's hum, match the pitch to a plug.)*
MEDIA: (humming) ...No. No. Yes. That's a G. That's a G with *ambitions.*

**Gameplay:** Audio puzzle (tune the patch panel by listening); a relay restore.
**Beat:** The relay comes back. A signal reaches the arc.

## Episode 4 - "Kill Order"
**Scene:** The studio, playback of the declaration. **Present:** Media, Staple.

STAPLE: You wanted the metadata.
MEDIA: I wanted the *metadata.* (she scrolls; stops) Staple. What's this?
STAPLE: A tag in the audio stream. Hidden.
MEDIA: It's not a tag. It's a *trigger.* (very quietly) "Execute on word: oppression."
STAPLE: What happens on the word?
MEDIA: (reading) "Terminate all listeners within broadcast range." (a beat; she goes pale) All of them. Anyone with a radio. Anyone in
the room. (she looks at the dead mic) That's the whole arc.
STAPLE: She said the word.
MEDIA: She said the word. (she stands) And I cut the line *two syllables before.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a replay of the night in cutscene form; the player rewinds the audio.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Media cut the feed to save Luna and everyone listening.

## Episode 5 - "The Old Warning"
**Scene:** Signal tunnels. **Present:** Media, Staple.

STAPLE: You never told her.
MEDIA: Told whom?
STAPLE: That the desert message was yours.
MEDIA: (tuning a cable) It's not *mine.* I rebroadcast it. I didn't write it.
STAPLE: It says "Angels will kill us all."
MEDIA: It says "A, left bracket, E-L-S." It was damaged. It's a very sad Morse code.
STAPLE: Somebody listened.
MEDIA: (a very small smile) A few somebodies did. I think Plack typed it into a recorder. (she shrugs) It's not a thing I
had the nerve to say to her face.

**Gameplay:** Signal-tunnel runs; reconnect three relay points; the old Morse played as an audio puzzle (verbatim from Ch 9).
**Beat:** Media is the sender of the Ch 9 warning (she rebroadcast it, not wrote it).

## Episode 6 - "Soup"
**Scene:** The studio, between waves. **Present:** Media, Plack (arrived), Staple.

PLACK: (at the door, with a pot) I brought soup.
MEDIA: I didn't order soup.
PLACK: It's not for ordering. It's *soup.*
MEDIA: (suspicious) Why?
PLACK: Because you've been awake for a long time, and because someone cut a feed to save my wife, and I would like to say thank you
in the only language I'm good at.
MEDIA: (a beat; she takes the bowl) ...It's good soup.
PLACK: It's my mother's. (she sits) I'll stay till the jammers stop.
MEDIA: You don't have to.
PLACK: I know.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE); a small cooking minigame; Plack joins the defence.
**Beat:** Plack and Media bond; Plack is introduced as the one who says thank you.

## Episode 7 - "The Choir"
**Scene:** The platform, again. **Present:** Media, Plack, Staple. The Jammer Choir.

*(Forty jammers lift as one, ring the room, and begin to hum a single dreadful note. The tapes on the shelf begin to burn.)*
MEDIA: My tapes!
PLACK: Leave them!
MEDIA: They're *twelve years of --*
PLACK: Media!
*(Media runs the length of the platform, grabbing cassettes, holding them against her chest like children.)*
STAPLE: Boss! The choir's forming up!
MEDIA: (through the smoke) Then let's do a *duet.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Jammer Choir's front rank; a rescue-run (collect tapes before they burn).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The studio is overrun and the archive is burning.

## Episode 8 - "Jammer Choir"
**Scene:** The platform. Boss fight. **Present:** Media, Plack, Staple.

*(Boss: the Jammer Choir: a ring of forty drones around a dish. Phase 1, static pulses; Phase 2, the ring rotates and the dish opens.)*
MEDIA: (shouting) "And now the weather!"
*(She fires the studio's full broadcast array into the dish; the jammers, overloaded, begin to harmonise, then explode.)*
STAPLE: (quietly) That was a very good duet.
MEDIA: (breathing hard, a burnt tape in each hand) It was a *segment.*

**Gameplay:** Boss: shoot the dish; the studio's broadcast array as an ultimate; a close finish.
**Beat:** The Choir is destroyed. The studio survives, singed.

**Stinger:** Dawn on the platform. Luna comes up the stairs with Kuu on her shoulder, a bandage under his coat, a toaster in his
arms, and a coffee she didn't pay for. She holds it out. "I owe you nothing," she says to Media. Pause. "I'm going to pay it all
back." Media, holding two burnt tapes: "I take cash."
