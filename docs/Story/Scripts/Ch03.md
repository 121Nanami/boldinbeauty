# Ch 03 - Docks - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / LOCKED map (events authored).** A semi-ruined quay by the sea, heavy fog, waves crashing; the only structure is the
lighthouse. Boss on the lighthouse island.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 25 s)
White. Not mist, not cloud: fog, a wall of it, and inside it three small voices and one very long sound of water.
KUU: (muffled) Sorry. Sorry. Sorry, bollard.

## Episode 1 - "Fog"
**Scene:** The quay. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: I walked into a post.
PLACK: It's a bollard.
KUU: I apologised to it.
PLACK: I heard.
LUNA: (somewhere ahead) I have found a boat.
PLACK: Where?
LUNA: ...I have found the *outline* of a boat.
KUU: Is it floating?
LUNA: It is on the bottom. It is a very thorough outline.
PLACK: There is no boat, Luna.
LUNA: I know. I wanted there to be one for a moment. It was nice.

**Gameplay:** Fog navigation tutorial: follow rope lines; hearing-led waypoints. First swell hazards.
**Beat:** No way across by boat. The only structure in the fog is a light.

## Episode 2 - "The Broken Piers"
**Scene:** Three piers with missing planks. **Present:** all three. Angels: ranged.

PLACK: The planks are gone from here to here.
KUU: I can jump that.
PLACK: You cannot jump that.
KUU: I can. I'm afraid of the *height,* not the *distance.*
LUNA: That is a very precise fear.
KUU: I've had time to refine it.
*(Waves break across the pier. A plank-run section with gaps.)*

**Gameplay:** Platforming along pier gaps; a collapsed section slopes into the water (swim optional); ranged angels fire from the rocks.
**Beat:** They reach the stepping rocks.

## Episode 3 - "Stepping Rocks"
**Scene:** The sea; a chain of rocks. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: I would like to swim it.
PLACK: Why?
LUNA: Because it is in the rules that I may.
KUU: There are no rules --
LUNA: There are always rules. I like to find them and be on the edge of them. (she dives)
PLACK: (watching her go, to Kuu) She hasn't swum since --
KUU: I know.
PLACK: Should we --
KUU: She'll be bored by the third rock. Wait.
*(Luna surfaces, shouting: "It's cold!" and carries on.)*

**Gameplay:** Swim and stepping-stone segment; mixed approaches (jump or swim). Light combat on the rocks.
**Beat:** They cross; Luna is soaked and in good spirits.

## Episode 4 - "The Lamp Is Lit"
**Scene:** Lighthouse island shore. **Present:** all three. Surface angels everywhere, standing.

KUU: (whispering) They're not moving.
PLACK: They're looking at the light.
*(Every angel on the shore stands in rows, facing the lighthouse. The beam sweeps over them; none of them flinches.)*
LUNA: It is *on.*
KUU: It's a lighthouse. It's supposed to be on.
PLACK: There is no sea traffic. There has been no sea traffic for seven years. Why does it still run?
LUNA: (slowly) Because it is not a lighthouse.

**Gameplay:** Sneak across the shore (angels do not react to the squad unless attacked). Optional: pull one aside for a scan.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The beam holds the angels in place.

## Episode 5 - "Eighty-Four Steps"
**Scene:** The outer spiral stair. **Present:** all three. Light combat.

LUNA: (counting) Twelve. Thirteen. Fourteen. Fifteen --
KUU: Why are you counting?
LUNA: So I know when to stop.
PLACK: There are eighty-four.
LUNA: Sixty-one. Sixty-two.
PLACK: I counted from below.
LUNA: Sixty-three. (flatly) There are four hundred, Plack. You will want to remember that for the report.
KUU: ...There are eighty-four.
LUNA: I will tell the Commander four hundred.

**Gameplay:** Spiral stair climb under wind and spray; angels dropping from the gallery.
**Beat:** A shared joke before the unpleasant thing at the top.

## Episode 6 - "The Lamp Room"
**Scene:** Lamp room. **Present:** all three.

*(A huge lens of brass and glass. A serial plate. A sponsor logo, a small wing, screwed to the casing. A technician's cot in a corner.)*
PLACK: This is not a navigation light.
KUU: It's a -- it has a serial number. It has a *warranty.*
PLACK: It is a unit. It's government equipment. It is *holding* them.
LUNA: Hold on. If this goes off --
PLACK: They go to the arc. All of them.
LUNA: And if this goes on --
PLACK: They stay where they are.
LUNA: (pause) And if someone could set it to *flicker.*
PLACK: ...Luna.
LUNA: I am only thinking of the technical possibilities.

**Gameplay:** Examine the lamp (non-combat PURPLE); a puzzle: unlock the casing without extinguishing the beam.
**Beat:** The beam is a leash. The government can lengthen or shorten it.

## Episode 7 - "The Keeper"
**Scene:** Lamp room, under attack. **Present:** all three. The Keeper (BLUE then RED).

KEEPER: (from the stairs; gaunt, in oilskins, years of salt on him) Don't touch that.
KUU: We aren't --
KEEPER: Don't touch it. I have kept this light seven years. If it goes out they come in. If it flickers they come in worse.
LUNA: Who told you that?
KEEPER: The manual.
LUNA: Who wrote the manual?
KEEPER: ...Someone with a wing on their letterhead.
PLACK: You've been alone here seven years.
KEEPER: I had a dog. (beat) The dog is not here.
*(The beam shudders. The Keeper's face changes. The lens swings round, hunting the squad.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Keeper's Lens (a turret phase); avoid the beam.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The beam has a mind (a safety interlock) and it sees them as "unauthorised".

## Episode 8 - "The Keeper's Lens"
**Scene:** Lamp room gallery, outside. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: Break the lens, not the lamp.
KUU: How do you *tell* the difference?
PLACK: The lens is the one trying to kill us.
LUNA: A boss with a *warranty.* I love it.
*(Boss phases: 1, rotating beam sweep; 2, the lens detaches and chases; the spare lens falls free.)*
KEEPER: (after, quietly) You've broken the spare.
LUNA: (picking up the second lens from the ruin) I have not. This is a *different* lens. (she turns it over) I will keep it.
PLACK: Why?
LUNA: In case we need a light that doesn't belong to anyone.

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge the beam; shoot the lens; collect the spare.
**Beat:** The beam stays on. Luna keeps the spare lens.

**Stinger:** On the quay, as the fog lifts for the first time in the chapter, the three of them look at the lighthouse. Kuu says, "We
could turn them off." Luna: "Or on." A beat. Kuu does not like the second one.
