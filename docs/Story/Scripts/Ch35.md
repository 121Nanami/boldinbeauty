# Ch 35 - The Ledger - Script

**Arc 2 / 2016 / SIDE STORY: Death.** Death's last night before the signing. A walk through the Long Gallery as it rearranges by the hour.
Death is not sentimental and does not need to be. A new column appears in the ledger.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A long gallery of narrow rooms, each a year. A desk at the far end under a single lamp. A figure in a plain dark coat, sorting small
pieces of paper by hand into long drawers. No hurry. The lamp does not flicker.
DEATH: (to the room) Eleven. Twelve.
*(A pause.)*
DEATH: Thirteen.

## Episode 1 - "2002"
**Scene:** Year room 2002. **Present:** Death (playable guest). The counted (enemy/NPC).

*(The room is full of figures in everyday clothes, standing in rows, quiet, slightly faded, as if washed too often.)*
DEATH: (walking among them, touching none) Abe. Aizawa. (a nod) Aoki.
*(They turn their heads as Death passes. They do not speak. They have not spoken for a very long time.)*
DEATH: (quietly) I have you.
*(One of the counted lifts a hand in a very small wave.)*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Death's kit (a tally mechanic: each defeated enemy is added to a count); walk and listen.
**Beat:** The counted are not hostile.

## Episode 2 - "Annotations"
**Scene:** Year rooms 2003-2005. **Present:** Death.

*(A room for each year. In each, the same figures, a little older, a little more faded. Death writes a number on a card, drops it into a
drawer, and walks on.)*
DEATH: Nine hundred and twelve.
*(A figure in the next row says, in a thin voice:)*
COUNTED: ...Are we... done?
DEATH: Not yet.
COUNTED: Will we be?
DEATH: (after a pause) I do not know.
COUNTED: (disappointed, accepting) That's all right.

**Gameplay:** Walk-through combat (the counted turn hostile only when the player raises a weapon).
**Beat:** Death's work is care, without comfort.

## Episode 3 - "A New Column"
**Scene:** The ledger desk. **Present:** Death.

*(Death sits. The ledger is open. Death turns a page. In the margin, beside the usual columns, a new column has been ruled, in red.)*
DEATH: (reading) That is not my hand.
*(The heading is written in glyphs. As Death looks at them they rearrange themselves into plain script.)*
DEATH: "Unworthy."
*(A silence. The lamp does not flicker. It does not need to.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The monolith's glyphs have written in Death's ledger.

## Episode 4 - "The Unwritten Name"
**Scene:** Year room 2008. **Present:** Death.

*(A room with a single figure, facing the wall. It has no face. It has the outline of one.)*
DEATH: (pausing) Who are you?
*(The figure does not turn.)*
DEATH: You are not in the book.
FIGURE: (in a voice like ink) ...I was never written.
DEATH: (a very long moment) That is not possible.
FIGURE: It is a very *thorough* ledger.
*(The figure turns. For a moment Death's face, which has not changed in fourteen years, does.)*

**Gameplay:** Combat: the Unsigned appears as a recurring shape; the first encounter (an introduction).
**Beat:** A name that was never written down.

## Episode 5 - "Refusing The Column"
**Scene:** The ledger desk. **Present:** Death.

*(Death takes a pen, draws a line through the red column, and writes beneath: *No.*)*
*(The red column re-forms. Death crosses it out again. It re-forms.)*
DEATH: (quietly) I do not accept the heading.
*(The glyph-lines on the page curl around the pen. The pen warms. Death sets it down.)*
DEATH: I am being *spoken to.*
*(A beat. The lamp, for the first time, dims.)*

**Gameplay:** A short rhythm segment: cross out the column faster than it re-forms.
**Beat:** Death resists, and the column writes itself.

## Episode 6 - "The Counted Rise"
**Scene:** Year rooms (all). **Present:** Death, the counted.

*(The counted, in all rooms, have turned. They stand, in rows, facing the ledger desk. Not hostile. Not friendly. Attentive.)*
COUNTED (a thousand voices, soft): ...Is it us?
DEATH: What?
COUNTED: ...The column.
DEATH: (after a long moment) It is not you.
COUNTED: ...Then who?
DEATH: (looking at the page; at the heading; at the glyphs) ...I am not certain.
*(The counted do not move. The lamp goes out. In the dark, the red column glows.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); a darkness segment with only the column's glow.
**Beat:** The counted are afraid, too.

## Episode 7 - "The Unsigned"
**Scene:** The Long Gallery. **Present:** Death.

*(The Unsigned, a nameless blur of ink with pen-nib hands, stands at the end of the gallery. As Death approaches it begins to *write.* The
walls, as it writes, become names, and the names become a crowd.)*
UNSIGNED: (soft, patient) ...*write me.* ...*write me down.*
DEATH: (very level) I cannot. You are not a number.
UNSIGNED: ...*then what am I?*
DEATH: I do not know.
UNSIGNED: (almost a sigh) ...*that is why I am here.*
*(The walls of the gallery fold, one by one, into pages.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the counted (a swarm); a pen-nib hand.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The Unsigned is a question Death cannot answer.

## Episode 8 - "The Unsigned"
**Scene:** The ledger desk. Boss fight. **Present:** Death.

*(Boss: the Unsigned. Phase 1, ink strokes and pen-nib spears; Phase 2, the arena becomes a blank page and everything written on it is a hazard.)*
DEATH: (parrying, patient) I do not fight what I cannot count.
UNSIGNED: *...then count me.*
DEATH: (a long, very still pause) ...One.
*(The Unsigned halts, as if struck. For a moment it is only a very small, very tired shape.)*
UNSIGNED: (softly) ...*one.*
DEATH: (writing, at last) One.
*(The ink gathers itself, drops, and is a name.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: counting mechanic (reduce the Unsigned's arms to one by tallying); ink hazards.
**Beat:** Death counts the Unsigned; it is quiet.

**Stinger:** At the desk Death writes a name in the ledger for the first time in fourteen years. In the morning Death will sign the Charter
and the pen will hover. The player does not see what Death decides. Death does not either, until the hall. The red column, at the
margin of the page, has not gone.
