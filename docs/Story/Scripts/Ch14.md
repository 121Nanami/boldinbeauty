# Ch 14 - Declaration - Script

**Arc 2 / 2009 / NEW.** Main cast. The government broadcast spire above Neo Tokyo. Luna declares war; the feed is cut; she is
branded a war criminal.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 40 s)
Dawn, finally, in Neo Tokyo: a grey sky going pink behind a thousand antennae. A rooftop. Luna, in front of a cracked mirror leaned on
a water tank, rehearsing with a toothbrush for a microphone.
LUNA: (to the mirror) "People of the arc." (frowns) "Persons of the arc." (frowns) "Whoever is listening." (pause) That's the one.
PLACK: (behind her, with a coffee) You've done that nine times.
LUNA: The tenth is the one that counts.

## Episode 1 - "Visitor Stickers"
**Scene:** Spire lobby. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. A receptionist (BLUE).

RECEPTIONIST: Welcome to the Neo Tokyo Broadcast Spire. Are you here for the tour?
KUU: (instantly) Yes.
LUNA: We're *very* interested in the tour.
RECEPTIONIST: Lovely. Please take a visitor sticker. (she hands out three) The tour leaves in five minutes with a group of
schoolchildren.
PLACK: (looking at the sticker, which says MY NAME IS: LUNA) ...They wrote it in advance.
LUNA: Oh. They were expecting me. (a beat, then lightly) How *rude* of them to be right.
KUU: We could leave --
LUNA: Kuu, I have a *sticker.*

**Gameplay:** Lobby infiltration (stealth, non-combat first half); joining a school tour as cover.
**Beat:** The squad is inside, with stickers.

## Episode 2 - "The Toaster"
**Scene:** Studio floors, a quiz show set. **Present:** all three. Quiz-show host (BLUE).

HOST: And our next contestant is -- a visitor! What's your name, dear?
KUU: (very quickly) Kuu. I'm -- I'm not a contestant, I'm just *passing --*
HOST: Round one! Question one! What is the capital of the arc?
KUU: ...Neo Tokyo?
HOST: That is *correct!*
*(The audience claps. A spotlight. A toaster is lowered on a rope.)*
KUU: (to the squad, hissing) I *won* something.
LUNA: Don't hold it.
KUU: It's a toaster.
PLACK: It's evidence that you were here.
KUU: ...I'll hide it.
LUNA: Where?
KUU: (looking down) ...In my coat?

**Gameplay:** Quiz-show set combat (security guards in the wings); a timed puzzle: reach the next door before the toaster lowers.
**Beat:** A comic breather; Kuu carries a toaster for the rest of the chapter.

## Episode 3 - "Rotations"
**Scene:** Control floors, the data lobby. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: (at a console, reading) These are the day's scripts. "Item one: Operators remain productive. Item two: Operator
unrest is not a thing. Item three: the Paradise showcase has been a joy."
KUU: Is it ... the same script as every day?
PLACK: Only the date changes.
LUNA: (leaning on the console, bored) So the government has *one speech* and they use it every day.
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: I should like to give them a second.

**Gameplay:** Control-floor infiltration; hacking mini-interactions; the broadcast schedule as an in-world clock.
**Beat:** The mechanics of the lie are laid bare.

## Episode 4 - "The Primetime Squad"
**Scene:** The antenna mast, base. **Present:** all three. The Squad (RED).

*(Four sponsor-colour heroes drop onto the platform, in a pose, with a camera drone for each.)*
PRIMETIME LEAD: (tilting his chin to the lens) Luna. Plack. Kuu. We've been *expecting* you.
KUU: (hoarse) They recognised us from the wedding.
PLACK: Eight million people watched the wedding.
PRIMETIME LEAD: Please don't make this difficult. The arc is *watching.*
LUNA: (mildly) Is it.
*(She lifts the toaster out of Kuu's coat and holds it in front of the nearest drone.)*
LUNA: This is a *toaster.* My friend won it fairly. Please convey to the arc that it is a very nice toaster.
*(The Squad, for a second, doesn't know what to do.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Primetime Understudy; the squad has to take them down before the Lead's cue.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The government knew they would come. It is staging a trap on air.

## Episode 5 - "The Mast"
**Scene:** Antenna mast, climb. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: (climbing, wiring in her teeth) Pass me the cutters.
KUU: Which ones?
PLACK: The ones that *cut.*
KUU: They're all cutters.
PLACK: The red handles.
LUNA: (above them, reading from a slip of paper) "People of the arc." (frowns) "Persons of the arc." (frowns) "Whoever is listening."
PLACK: You've written it again.
LUNA: I've written it *better.*
PLACK: What did you change?
LUNA: ...I added a word.
PLACK: Which?
LUNA: You'll hear it.

**Gameplay:** Vertical climb of the antenna mast; wind; Primetime snipers as hazards.
**Beat:** The squad reaches the transmitter.

## Episode 6 - "Going Live"
**Scene:** Transmitter room. **Present:** all three.

*(A small room, a single microphone, a red light. Luna sits. Plack reads over her shoulder. Kuu holds the door.)*
PLACK: (reading) You wrote "oppression."
LUNA: It's the word.
PLACK: It's a *long* word for a radio.
LUNA: Then they'll have to wait for it.
KUU: (at the door, to the Commander, urgent) We have about two minutes!
LUNA: (to the lens, mild, a tiny bit too slow) People of the arc. Persons of the arc. Whoever is listening. This is Luna. I'd like to
say a thing and then I'd like to leave. (a pause) I am declaring war on the government.
*(Beat. Plack, softly, taps the word "oppression" on the page. Luna looks at it, and changes one letter.)*
LUNA: ...On the oppression of operators, and on the government that profits from it. (she smiles at the camera) Thank you. That's
all. (a beat) I mean *oppr --*
*(The feed cuts. A hiss. A red light goes out.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the declaration. A single scene the player controls only by pressing to continue.
**Beat:** The declaration is made, and it is cut mid-word. *(Who cut it? See Ch 15.)*

## Episode 7 - "Flesh Wound"
**Scene:** Roof of the spire. **Present:** all three. The Primetime Squad.

*(Kuu has been shot holding the door. He leans on the frame, pale, a grin on his face.)*
KUU: It's a flesh wound.
PLACK: Don't be brave.
KUU: It's a *small* wound. (he slides a few inches down the wall) It's mostly flesh.
LUNA: (quietly, very close) Kuu.
KUU: I'm *fine.* I'm -- (he looks at his hand) ...Could somebody apologise to the toaster for me? It got caught in the --
LUNA: Kuu.
KUU: -- crossfire.
*(He passes out. The Squad closes. Luna's face has no expression at all.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Squad's lead (a sponsor hero with cape-gliding patterns); a defensive stand protecting Kuu.
**Beat:** **Low point.** Kuu is hurt and the squad is cornered on the roof.

## Episode 8 - "Primetime"
**Scene:** The spire roof. Boss fight. **Present:** Luna, Plack; Kuu down.

*(Boss: the Primetime Squad: four heroes with a shared health bar and one camera drone each. Defeat the drones to break their buffs.)*
PRIMETIME LEAD: (posing) The arc is watching!
LUNA: (coldly, to the drone) Then it can watch *this.*
*(Luna's black hole drags the Squad into a heap; Plack fires; the drones go out one by one.)*
PLACK: (as the last one falls) The feed is cut.
LUNA: (looks at the antenna, the dead drones, the dawn) Good. (a beat) It was cut *once* tonight already, and it was not by me.

**Gameplay:** Boss: break camera drones to remove shields; a roof dash while carrying Kuu (escort).
**Beat:** The Squad falls; the squad escapes.

**Stinger:** Down in the street, in the rain, a thousand screens switch on at once. A grave voice reads: "A person named Luna has been
declared a war criminal." Luna, supporting Kuu, looks up at the screens. "Good title," she says. "Hell of a title."
