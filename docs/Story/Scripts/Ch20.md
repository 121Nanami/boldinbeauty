# Ch 20 - The Hour - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / NEW.** Main cast. The Clock Cathedral in the old district; the Timekeeper, the first Secret Luna asks. Time as a place
with doors.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
An old district, rooftops like stacked playing cards, every clock on every tower at the same hour. A cathedral, its nave full of gears the size of
carousels, turning in a silence that is more like a held breath. A figure in a collar of watch faces, writing a number in a ledger
with a very fine pen.
TIMEKEEPER: (without looking up) You are early, Luna.
LUNA: (from the door) I'm *exactly* on time.
TIMEKEEPER: Then you are early by precisely nothing, which is the rudest way of being it.

## Episode 1 - "Early By Precisely Nothing"
**Scene:** Cathedral nave. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Timekeeper (BLUE).

TIMEKEEPER: You have come to ask.
LUNA: I've come to make an offer.
TIMEKEEPER: The two are the same, from here.
PLACK: We would like to meet you properly.
TIMEKEEPER: (finally looking up; a face of unusual patience) You are Plack. You ask the question under the question. I have been
looking forward to this. The others will wait.
LUNA: (aside) I'm the one with the plan.
KUU: (aside, helpful) She's the one with the *speech.*
TIMEKEEPER: I know. (a small smile) I have listened to it forty-one times.

**Gameplay:** Cathedral tutorial: time-pocket rooms (environmental stops when the player stands still).
**Beat:** The Timekeeper takes a liking to Plack. Luna lets her lead.

## Episode 2 - "Gear Halls"
**Scene:** Gear halls. **Present:** all three.

*(Rooms of turning gears; rooms of falling sand; a long corridor where each step the squad takes is one second slower than the last.)*
KUU: My legs feel heavy.
PLACK: It's not your legs. It's the *floor.*
LUNA: (walking, unbothered) It's a courtesy. The room's checking whether you're in a hurry.
KUU: Am I?
LUNA: You are *always* in a hurry. You apologise before things happen.
KUU: (a beat) ...That's fair.

**Gameplay:** Gear-hall puzzle rooms: freeze-and-move; paused pilgrims as enemies (RED).
**Beat:** The cathedral tests patience.

## Episode 3 - "A Hall That Is Stopped"
**Scene:** A side hall. **Present:** all three.

*(A single hall, doors locked, dust on the ground, three hundred wall clocks stopped at different times, none of them at 4:12.)*
KUU: These ones are broken.
PLACK: These ones have *stopped.* There's a difference.
KUU: What's the difference?
PLACK: Broken is something that happens to a clock. Stopped is something someone *did.*
LUNA: (tapping a face) Who did this one?
*(The Timekeeper, at the end of the hall, is quite still.)*
TIMEKEEPER: (very evenly) Not me.
LUNA: (turning) Sorry?
TIMEKEEPER: I did not stop those. (a pause) Please don't go in.

**Gameplay:** Short combat; observing the hall; a gate puzzle you cannot solve (locked door).
**Beat:** The Timekeeper's stillness gives a hint: another stopper exists.

## Episode 4 - "Untaxed Time"
**Scene:** The Timekeeper's study. **Present:** all three.

*(A desk, a candle, a hundred pocket watches. The Timekeeper sits.)*
PLACK: The Hanging City. Four-twelve.
TIMEKEEPER: Yes.
PLACK: You stopped the clocks there.
TIMEKEEPER: I did.
KUU: Why?
TIMEKEEPER: (gently) Because time you are not counted in cannot be taxed. (a small lift of an eyebrow) The government taxes hours,
Kuu. It taxes *minutes.* A city that is not *in* the hour cannot be billed for it.
PLACK: (slowly) So the city is hidden.
TIMEKEEPER: The city is *unpaid.* It's the only free place in the arc.
LUNA: (a beat; to Plack, low) She's a *genius.*
PLACK: She's a *Secret.*
LUNA: Same thing.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a long conversation scene; codex update.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The Timekeeper stopped the Hanging City's clocks on purpose.

## Episode 5 - "An Hour That Does Not Count"
**Scene:** The nave. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: I want your vote.
TIMEKEEPER: For?
LUNA: The unification.
TIMEKEEPER: Which does not yet exist.
LUNA: I'd like it to.
TIMEKEEPER: (considering) I'll tell you what I'll give you. One hour. It does not count. It is not on any ledger, it is not in any
record, no one can tax it, and no one can see you in it.
LUNA: What can I do in an hour nobody sees?
TIMEKEEPER: (almost amused) Whatever you would not do in an hour *anyone* sees.
PLACK: (low) That's a very dangerous gift.
TIMEKEEPER: It is. (to Luna) You'll want to spend it well.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the Timekeeper's gift; a prompt to bank the hour.
**Beat:** Luna is given a free hour, off the record.

## Episode 6 - "Forty-One Times"
**Scene:** The belfry. **Present:** all three.

*(A long climb in the dark; the sound of a huge pendulum, slow. Plack, ahead of them, stops.)*
PLACK: Why forty-one?
TIMEKEEPER: (arriving beside her) Why do you ask?
PLACK: You said you had listened to Luna's speech forty-one times. She has only given it once.
TIMEKEEPER: (a small, delighted breath) Ah. There she is.
PLACK: Answer.
TIMEKEEPER: It is the number of times it has been said *to me.* By whom, I will tell you at the end.
PLACK: (flatly) At the end of what?
TIMEKEEPER: (gently) At the end.

**Gameplay:** Belfry climb; pendulum timing; the cathedral reconfigures between jumps.
**Beat:** The Timekeeper knows more than the present.

## Episode 7 - "The Minute Hand"
**Scene:** The nave. **Present:** all three. The Minute Hand.

*(From the nave's ceiling a vast clock-hand, a guardian of fused hands, a fist of minutes, descends and begins to sweep the hall.)*
TIMEKEEPER: (rising, calm) The guardian is not mine. It was set before I came. I would ask you not to dawdle.
KUU: (dodging) We aren't *dawdling!*
PLACK: Keep moving or be paused!
LUNA: (quietly) That's... a very *specific* threat.
*(Anything that stops is frozen in a bright halo for three seconds.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: pause-sweep patterns; the player must keep moving.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The cathedral turns on them.

## Episode 8 - "The Minute Hand"
**Scene:** The nave. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Minute Hand. Phase 1, sweeps; Phase 2, the gears of the hall reverse and the hand splits into twelve.)*
PLACK: It's pointing at us.
LUNA: It's pointing at *twelve.*
KUU: Which is?
LUNA: The hour. (she hurls the black hole at the clock's mast) Let's give it a *better* one.
*(The hand halts. For a long moment the entire cathedral is silent.)*
TIMEKEEPER: (very softly) Well done.
*(The Minute Hand, quite still, lowers itself to the floor like a tired animal.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge the sweep; hit the mast; reverse the gears.
**Beat:** The hand stops. The Timekeeper will attend.

**Stinger:** On the cathedral steps the Timekeeper says, very quietly, "I never stopped that one." Luna: "Which?" The Timekeeper: "The
hall with the three hundred clocks. Someone else did." Plack: "Who?" The Timekeeper looks at the sky. "A place where nothing happens,
Plack. I should not like to be there at four-twelve."
