# Ch 02 - The Ruins - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** Surface above the arc: a ruined school, police and fire station, hospital, stadium. Dust light, fog.
Sixteen hatches. First chapter where the squad leaves the city.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A round steel hatch, number 7, bolted shut from below. A wrench. A silence. Luna's voice, thin and cheerful, from underneath:
"It is stuck." Kuu's, further down: "Is it stuck or are you pulling the wrong way?" "It is stuck *in the right direction.*"

## Episode 1 - "Hatch Seven"
**Scene:** Hatch pad, sixteen hatches in four rows. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Voice: Dispatch.

DISPATCH (radio): Job 5120. Retrieve a field recorder from the school quarter. Dead operator, last known position, classroom
nine. Pay: lunch.
LUNA: It is a different lunch.
PLACK: It is the same lunch.
LUNA: It is the same lunch, but with a *view*. (she kicks the hatch; it opens) Commander, the sky.
KUU: It's very big.
PLACK: It's very empty. Look at the rooftops. Nothing is moving.
LUNA: Good. (she steps onto the pad) I'd like to sell the hinge before we go.
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: It is a good hinge.

**Gameplay:** Open the hatch (tutorial: interact), first surface walk, dust-light vistas, the hatch ring as a radar of 16.
**Beat:** The squad leaves the arc. Luna unscrews the hinge for later.

## Episode 2 - "Classroom Nine"
**Scene:** School interior, corridors, noticeboards. **Present:** all three. Angels: basic and ranged.

PLACK: (reading a noticeboard aloud) "Sports Day is postponed. Lost property can be claimed at the front desk. The boy who took
the red kite: we know it was you." (she stops) This is dated the fifth of June, 2002.
KUU: That's the week --
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: (flat) Plack, you are reading the noticeboards.
PLACK: Someone should.
LUNA: Fine. Read the lost property list. If there is a lunch box on it Kuu will want it.
KUU: I would not want a *stranger's* lunch box.
PLACK: Item 14. A green lunch box with a drawing of a bear.
KUU: ...I would want that one.

**Gameplay:** Corridor combat, classroom puzzle (match room numbers to hatches 1-16), optional pickup: the lunch box (YELLOW).
**Beat:** Plack's habit of reading everything starts paying off; the hatch order is solved.

## Episode 3 - "The Peddler's Price"
**Scene:** School yard. **Present:** all three + the Scrap Peddler (BLUE).

PEDDLER: Hinge! Fine hinge! Sold to the lady in the long coat not twenty minutes ago and now on offer to you at only --
LUNA: That is *my* hinge.
PEDDLER: It is a hinge of high quality.
LUNA: I sold it to you for three ration chits.
PEDDLER: And I am selling it back to you for six, which is a very small profit.
PLACK: That is a hundred percent.
PEDDLER: It is a very *small* hundred percent.
KUU: (quietly) I'd like to apologise to the hinge. It's been through a lot.
LUNA: (to the peddler) I will give you four and a story.
PEDDLER: ...What story?
LUNA: The hatch behind me is one of sixteen, and the other fifteen are for people who have not been born yet. (beat) That is the
story.
PEDDLER: (after a pause) Four.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE) trading scene with a short haggling minigame; shop.
**Beat:** Luna buys her own hinge back and the peddler gives them the stadium's location, free.

## Episode 4 - "They Were Counting Us"
**Scene:** Police and fire station, evidence room. **Present:** all three. The dead operator's field recorder is here.

KUU: (finding the recorder in a drawer) This is it. Oh -- it's still got a charge.
PLACK: Don't --
*(Static. A voice, thin, young, recorded years ago.)*
RECORDER (voice): ...day nine. We've stopped going out at night. The ones on the roofs don't attack, they just... they stand there.
They stand there and they face the same way. I counted a hundred and four. I think they were counting *us.*
KUU: (very quietly) ...Luna.
LUNA: I heard it.
PLACK: They were counting us.
LUNA: (flat) Everyone was counting us. That is what a census is for.
PLACK: A census doesn't have wings.
*(Luna says nothing. For a second the bored expression is not there.)*

**Gameplay:** Evidence room puzzle (locked drawers, fire truck keys); a hostage-style hold-out in the garage; the fire truck starts.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The angels on the surface are counting, not hunting.

## Episode 5 - "Beds With Names"
**Scene:** Hospital, ward three. **Present:** all three. Angels: assassins appear.

*(Rows of beds, each with a name card in the same handwriting. No bodies. A cat on a gurney.)*
PLACK: All the cards are in the same hand.
KUU: One person wrote all of them?
PLACK: One person wrote all of them *after.*
LUNA: Why does it matter who wrote the cards?
PLACK: Because someone went back. After the end of the world. They went around this hospital with a marker and wrote everyone's
name on the beds and then they left.
KUU: ...The cat is wearing a name card.
PLACK: (very softly) What does it say?
KUU: "Cat."
*(A beat. Luna laughs, once, and it is not a nice laugh and it is not a cruel one.)*
LUNA: Somebody *tried*, Plack.

**Gameplay:** Ward-by-ward combat (assassin ambushes in the dark), a hidden pickup (the matron's key).
**Beat:** Plack goes quiet; Luna breaks a rule by touching her shoulder for a second.

## Episode 6 - "The Fire Truck"
**Scene:** Back to the station. A working fire truck. **Present:** all three.

KUU: It starts.
PLACK: It does not start.
KUU: It *starts.* Listen.
LUNA: Kuu, can you drive?
KUU: I can... operate a *steering wheel.*
LUNA: Good. We are going to the stadium and I am not walking, I have been walking for nine chapters. (to the Commander) I am
speaking figuratively.
KUU: There are no chapters --
LUNA: There are always chapters. (she climbs in) Plack, in the back. Hold on to the ladder.
PLACK: I am going to regret this.
LUNA: That is what the ladder is for.

**Gameplay:** Vehicle section (fire truck escort through a collapsed road; a light-shooter on the ladder).
**Beat:** They reach the stadium in a stolen fire truck, which Kuu later apologises for.

## Episode 7 - "The Stands Are Moving"
**Scene:** The broken stadium. **Present:** all three. Heavy angels, a rising crowd.

PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: I see it.
PLACK: The stands. They're not stands.
*(The terraces of the stadium bowl shift. What looked like rubble is shoulders, wings, a thousand folded faces. The Crowd rises.)*
KUU: It's -- they're all *facing the same way.*
LUNA: Of course they are. (she takes her glove off, which she never does) West.
PLACK: What?
LUNA: They are all facing west.
KUU: Is that -- is that important?
LUNA: I have no idea. (she smiles) But I would like to find out.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Crowd's left flank (a Brute with a banner). The environment shifts as the bowl wakes.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The stadium is a creature.

## Episode 8 - "The Crowd"
**Scene:** Stadium bowl. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Crowd. Phase 1, shoulder arms; Phase 2, the stands rise. Climbable terraces.)*
PLACK: (boss intro) There's a weak point in the middle of the field.
LUNA: Which is where we are standing.
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: Then we are in a good place.
KUU: I hate this. I want you to know I hate this.
LUNA: That is how I know it is going well.

**Gameplay:** Boss: stand-climbing, field centre weak point, wing-pull mechanic.
**Beat:** The Crowd falls. The squad survives.

**Stinger:** On the roof of the school (the last location), Luna plays the recorder. In the back of the recording, below the
voice, a faint clock ticks. The angels' rows are visible in the recorder's last photograph, all facing the sea. Luna looks at the
horizon. In her coat pocket is a playing card she picked up in the staff room under a fridge magnet. She does not mention it.
