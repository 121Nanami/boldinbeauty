# Ch 07 - T.O.M.B.S, The Old Man's Battings Site - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A structure larger than any man-made structure; inside, an ordinary ten-floor office that holds many
angel secrets. Lobby, cubicles, atrium, elevator shafts, five hidden rooms, floor nine.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A black wall rising for ever, a door at its foot the size of a postbox. Above it, in modest letters: T.O.M.B.S. Inside the postbox,
a lobby with a potted plant and a brass bell.
RECEPTIONIST: Good morning. Do you have an appointment?

## Episode 1 - "Do You Have An Appointment"
**Scene:** Lobby. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Receptionist (BLUE).

LUNA: We don't.
RECEPTIONIST: That's all right. Many people don't. Please sign in.
PLACK: (signing) "Visitors." Ten floors?
RECEPTIONIST: Ten, if you count the one under the roof. We don't count it.
KUU: Why not?
RECEPTIONIST: It's the roof.
LUNA: (reaching for the bell) May I?
RECEPTIONIST: Of course.
*(She rings it nineteen times.)*
RECEPTIONIST: ...That's enough.
LUNA: It was not enough.

**Gameplay:** Reception tutorial (interact; sign in); the office is quiet, with desk-by-desk exploration.
**Beat:** They enter an office that smells of coffee, and no one has seen them come.

## Episode 2 - "Cubicle Rules"
**Scene:** Cubicle floor 2. **Present:** all three. Office-worker angels (RED).

KUU: I can vault the desks.
PLACK: Don't --
KUU: I'm *vaulting.*
*(He vaults three desks, apologising to each, and lands in the break room.)*
KUU: The coffee machine is warm.
PLACK: Who drank the coffee?
KUU: ...Nobody. Look -- there's the cup, there's the stirrer, it's still *steaming* --
LUNA: Kuu, don't drink it.
KUU: I wasn't going to drink it.
LUNA: You have *a look.*
KUU: ...I have a look.

**Gameplay:** Cubicle combat (desks as cover/vaulting); a break-room health pickup (YELLOW: coffee).
**Beat:** The office is warm, inhabited, and empty.

## Episode 3 - "Minutes"
**Scene:** Floor 6, meeting room. **Present:** all three.

*(A long table, nineteen chairs, a projector still on, a whiteboard with one line in blue marker.)*
PLACK: (reading the minutes on the table) "Item one: attendance. Item two: results. Item three: ..." (she turns the page) That's
it. That's the whole meeting.
KUU: A meeting with no *decisions?*
PLACK: A meeting that was *finished* before it began.
LUNA: Let me see. (reads the whiteboard) "Line is down. See above."
KUU: Above what?
LUNA: ...Above, Kuu. Just above.

**Gameplay:** Meeting-room exploration; combat in the corridor; a clue pickup (the whiteboard photo).
**Beat:** "Line is down" echoes with something the squad has not seen yet.

## Episode 4 - "Five Doors That Are Walls"
**Scene:** Atrium floor 4. **Present:** all three. *(HINT: seventh seat)*

PLACK: This wall's wrong.
LUNA: It's a wall.
PLACK: It's a wall with a draught.
*(She pushes. The wall is a door. Behind it, a small bright room with a single folder on a steel table. There are five such doors in the atrium.)*
KUU: There's a stamp on the file.
PLACK: "Grade -- " (she stops) There's no letter. Just a mark. A circle.
KUU: A grade above operators?
PLACK: A grade *above.*
LUNA: (picks the folder up; it says ANGEL V1) ...Ah.
PLACK: And that one is --
LUNA: "OPERATOR V!" (a long pause) Someone didn't know how to spell the exclamation point.
*(It is the first time she has not made a joke she meant.)*

**Gameplay:** Find and open five hidden doors (one per folder); a puzzle: match doors to folders by the building directory.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** A project exists with two halves. One of them is marked with a grade above operators.

## Episode 5 - "Press Every Button"
**Scene:** Elevator shafts. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: (in the lift, pressing everything) Floor nine. Floor eight. Floor seven. Floor one.
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: It's a very fine panel.
PLACK: You can't press them all at once.
LUNA: I have *just* done it.
VOICE (lift speaker): This building notes that you have pressed all of the buttons.
LUNA: Good.
VOICE: It will be recorded on a form.
KUU: (quietly) There is a form for *that?*
LUNA: There is a form for everything. That is how you know the place is serious.

**Gameplay:** Vertical combat in elevator shafts; climb cables; a falling-lift hazard.
**Beat:** The building keeps a record of their behaviour.

## Episode 6 - "Plack Reads Everything"
**Scene:** Archive on floor 8. **Present:** all three.

*(Plack is cross-legged on the floor with a file on every knee.)*
PLACK: This one's a memo from the Commanders' Council to the Site Director: "Sponsor interest in V1 continues." This one: "V! shows no
improvement; continue pressure." This one is a *budget.*
KUU: What's it for?
PLACK: Coffee. (a beat) A very great deal of coffee.
LUNA: That's the real secret.
PLACK: They've spent more on coffee than on the *V* programme.
LUNA: Then it is well funded and badly run. (she pauses) That's how I know it's real.
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: I know. I know. Read the next one.

**Gameplay:** Archive wing combat (filing sentries); document pickups (YELLOW) that slot into the codex.
**Beat:** The project has a sponsor and a budget.

## Episode 7 - "Fill In The Form"
**Scene:** Floor nine, the Auditor's desk. **Present:** all three.

*(An angel in a business suit sits at a desk, its halo ironed flat to look like a lanyard. A stack of forms. A stamp.)*
AUDITOR: You have pressed every button.
LUNA: I have.
AUDITOR: And entered a restricted atrium.
LUNA: I have.
AUDITOR: And handled files above your grade.
LUNA: I have, and I've enjoyed it.
AUDITOR: Then there is a form.
KUU: (low) It's going to make us fill it in.
AUDITOR: If you refuse, I shall fill it in for you.
PLACK: In what name?
AUDITOR: (the stamp rises) In *yours.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Auditor's clerks (stamp-and-paper waves).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The building will claim them in its records.

## Episode 8 - "The Auditor"
**Scene:** Floor nine. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Auditor. Phase 1, forms and stamps; Phase 2, the floor becomes a form; the squad must stand on the right box.)*
AUDITOR: (stamping) Section one, name. Section two, purpose. Section three --
LUNA: Section three is I'm going to *hit you with a hole.*
AUDITOR: That is not a valid response.
LUNA: It is the only response I have.
*(When the Auditor falls the elevators all go down at once and the lobby clock chimes a time that is not the time.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: stamp-box mechanic; paper-wall phase; the office floor reshapes.
**Beat:** The Auditor is down; the building's records spill.

**Stinger:** In the lobby the plant has moved, and the receptionist is gone. The bell on the desk is still ringing, very quietly,
nineteen times. Plack pockets one of the files. Luna does not stop her.
