# Ch 17 - Court in Session (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Side story: **Judge** (Manton, 68, "She/her", secret grade; per `Judge.md`). Voice from `Judge.md`: very formal language, old country dialect. She disdains humans and operators and still rules by the law as she carries it. The trio appear only in the epilogue.

Chapter promise: the voice of law, tested by a rigged courthouse, rules that commanders are not above it.

---

## Episode 1 - Gavel

*Dawn. The Hall of Justice, a stone courthouse with a copper roof gone green. The floors smell of wax. In an empty courtroom, a very tall elderly woman in a 1980s power dress, white hair cropped short, steel gavel in one robotic hand, reads aloud to rows of empty benches.*

**[Cold open. Calm, regal music. The camera lingers on Judge's metal fingers and the gavel's wires.]**

JUDGE: *(reading, in a voice like old oak)* "Article the first: that no person shall be held in custody beyond the lawful span of a day without charge, nor charge be brought without the names of those who bring it." *(a pause)* One hundred and four times have I read that clause aloud, and one hundred and four times have the benches been empty.

*She raps the gavel once. A single crack, perfectly dry.*

JUDGE: Then let the record show the court is in session.

*The doors at the back open. A clerk, young, nervous, in a government grey suit, edges in.*

CLERK: *(whispering)* Your Honour, there's a case. A summons from above.

JUDGE: *(not looking up)* From whom above?

CLERK: *(swallowing)* The commanders, ma'am.

JUDGE: *(finally lifting her eyes, ancient, calm)* Then I'll hear it.

**[Tutorial: Judge's combat style is deliberate, heavy, rule-bound: "Objection" parries, "Sustained" counters, "Order" area bursts.]**

## Episode 2 - First Case

*Courtroom One. A packed gallery of grey suits. At the bar, a young operator in a torn jacket, wrists cuffed. A prosecutor in a wing-logo lapel pin leafs through a thick file.*

PROSECUTOR: The accused, Operator Sasaki Ren, is charged with "organising without a sponsor". A violation of Section Nine, Subsection Four.

JUDGE: *(not unkindly)* Section Nine of what?

PROSECUTOR: *(slightly flustered)* The Commanders' Code, Your Honour.

JUDGE: *(slowly)* I do not recall that code.

PROSECUTOR: It was ratified in 2008.

JUDGE: By whom?

*A pause.*

PROSECUTOR: By the commanders.

JUDGE: *(drily)* A fine tradition. The accused will rise.

*REN stands, shaking, but upright.*

JUDGE: Do you understand the charge?

REN: *(quietly)* I helped a few friends find work.

JUDGE: Did you take a fee?

REN: No, ma'am.

JUDGE: Did you take a sponsor's money?

REN: No.

JUDGE: Then I fail to see what law you have broken. *(she raps the gavel)* Case dismissed.

*The gallery erupts. The PROSECUTOR, sputtering, half-rises.*

PROSECUTOR: Your Honour, the commanders will...

JUDGE: *(without raising her voice)* The court has spoken.

## Episode 3 - The Bailiffs

*A corridor beside the courtroom. Bailiff Automata stand guard, grey, polished, each with a gavel-arm. Judge sweeps past, dignified. The leading bailiff steps into her path.*

BAILIFF: *(a flat, synthetic voice)* Your Honour. The verdict will be revised.

JUDGE: *(not stopping)* By whom?

BAILIFF: By the authority of the commanders.

JUDGE: *(stopping)* There is no such authority in this court.

BAILIFF: There is, now.

*The gavel-arm rises. Judge sighs, very faintly, the sigh of a woman who has been asked the same foolish question too many times.*

JUDGE: I did so hope we would not need this.

*Her metal hand tightens on the gavel.*

**[Combat: Judge fights Bailiff Automata in the corridor. "Objection" parries are vital; each deflected blow stuns the attacker. The player learns the rhythm of formal procedure as combat.]**

*When the corridor is clear, a vending machine in a corner coughs, and a witness, a thin little man in a suit, crawls out from behind it.*

WITNESS: *(a dazed whisper)* I'm a witness.

JUDGE: *(not looking at him)* Of what?

WITNESS: *(thinking)* ...I don't remember. I was waiting for the machine.

JUDGE: *(dryly)* The machine is a fine witness. It saw everything.

## Episode 4 - House Style (Midpoint)

*Judge's chambers. A desk of dark wood, shelves of bound statutes, a window onto the copper roof. A kettle on a hob. She sits, takes the charge sheet from the day's case, and for the first time reads it closely.*

*A pause. She turns it over, holds it to the light.*

JUDGE: *(softly, to herself)* I know this hand.

*She walks to a shelf, takes down a thick volume, opens it. A second charge sheet, from a case a year ago. A third, from three years before. She lays them side by side.*

JUDGE: Same margins. Same typeface. Same turn of phrase. *(her finger traces a line)* "Hereby noted for the record." Who says "hereby noted" any longer?

*She checks the bottom of each sheet. No signatures. No names. Only a faint watermark, a stylised quill.*

JUDGE: *(very slowly)* One author. For all of them.

*She sits back, hands folded on the gavel. Outside, rain begins to fall on the copper roof.*

JUDGE: *(to the empty room)* Someone has been writing the law in the margins of my court.

**[Gameplay beat: the quill watermark is a lore item. It reappears in Chapter 22 (Narrator).]**

## Episode 5 - Cell Block

*The holding cells under the courthouse. Rows of barred doors, a dozen detainees, some in operator uniforms, some in civilian clothes. A warden in a black uniform, impatient, jingling keys.*

WARDEN: *(to Judge)* Your Honour, with respect, these are scheduled for transfer.

JUDGE: *(pleasantly)* To where?

WARDEN: *(a hint of hesitation)* A facility.

JUDGE: Which facility?

WARDEN: *(after a pause)* One that is not under the court's jurisdiction.

JUDGE: *(softly)* Then they are not scheduled for transfer. They are scheduled for disappearance.

*A silence. The WARDEN's jingling stops.*

JUDGE: *(rising to her full six foot five)* I shall review each of these cases, in person, today. Unlock the cells.

WARDEN: *(a nervous laugh)* Your Honour, I can't...

*The gavel comes down on the iron bars of the nearest cell. A single, ringing note.*

JUDGE: *(without raising her voice)* You may, and you shall.

**[Combat: cell-block fights; guards attack in narrow corridors, Judge's "Order" bursts are effective in confined space.]**

*Afterwards, a detainee, an old man, grey-haired, in a faded operator's coat, takes her hand and presses it to his forehead.*

DETAINEE: *(whispering)* Thank you.

JUDGE: *(stiffly, not unkindly)* Do not thank me. It is merely the law.

*She withdraws her hand, and for a long moment, looks at it.*

## Episode 6 - Precedent

*A private archive behind the courtroom. A single lamp. A great iron-bound book, its pages brown with age. Judge sets it on the lectern and turns carefully to a page near the front.*

*A faded entry, in a younger hand: "The first law after the sky fell. 2002."*

JUDGE: *(reading, in a low voice)* "That all persons, whether born or made, whether of flesh or wire, shall be held equal before the law. This is the first law. All others derive from it."

*She stops. Her metal hand rests on the page.*

JUDGE: *(quietly)* I wrote that on a table in a cellar by lamplight. There were nine of us. Eight died the winter after. *(a pause)* I was left to carry it.

*She turns the page. A second entry, in the same hand: "That no power, however great, shall stand above this law."*

JUDGE: *(almost to herself)* And that is the clause they wish me to forget.

*She closes the book, gently, and for a moment, the lamplight catches the deep lines of her face and the faint shine of the wires in her wrists.*

JUDGE: *(softly)* I am old. I am metal. And I am, I find, quite tired. But the book does not tire.

---

## Episode 7 - The Order

*Courtroom One, evening. The gallery is full. Every bench holds a grey suit. A commander's seat, newly installed on the dais beside the bench, a velvet cushion, a wing logo. A tall man in a general's sash takes it, nodding to JUDGE.*

COMMANDER: *(pleasantly)* Your Honour. A courtesy visit.

JUDGE: *(not looking up)* There is no courtesy visit in a court.

COMMANDER: *(unfazed)* I am here to present an order.

*He hands the CLERK an envelope. The CLERK passes it, trembling, to the bench. JUDGE slits it with the corner of her gavel and reads.*

JUDGE: *(without expression)* "By order of the Council of Commanders, the presiding judge is hereby removed from the bench, effective immediately, for acts of obstruction."

*A murmur ripples across the gallery.*

COMMANDER: *(softly)* The bailiffs will escort you.

*Three Bailiff Automata step forward. A fourth, taller than the rest, bearing a gavel-hammer the size of a man, enters through the main doors, the BAILIFF PRIME.*

JUDGE: *(rising, very slowly, to her full height)* Sir. You are in contempt of this court.

COMMANDER: *(amused)* I beg your pardon?

JUDGE: *(raising her voice for the first time in the chapter)* Contempt. The court will not be interrupted, and it will not be removed. *(she lifts the gavel)* You are in the presence of the law.

*A silence, absolute. The gavel falls. Once.*

## Episode 8 - The Bailiff Prime (Boss)

**[Boss: The Bailiff Prime. Arena: the courtroom, with bench, gallery, and witness stand as hazards. Phase 1: Prime summons lesser bailiffs; use "Objection" parries to turn their gavels on him. Phase 2: he smashes the bench, the court floor cracks; Judge uses "Order" to seal fractures. Phase 3: he flings his own gavel-hammer; the final blow is a parry-counter chain.]**

*The fight is as orderly as a trial: each blow answered, each strike objected. JUDGE moves with a slow, immense economy, parrying a swing that would break stone with a single flick of her wrist.*

JUDGE: *(over the clangour)* "Objection!"

*The BAILIFF PRIME's hammer rebounds into his own chest.*

JUDGE: "Sustained."

*He staggers. She presses forward.*

JUDGE: *(quietly, with terrible calm)* "Order."

*Her gavel descends, and the sound is not the sound of wood on wood, but the sound of a verdict. The BAILIFF PRIME collapses, and the courtroom floor shivers like the surface of a pond.*

*Silence. Dust drifts down through the lamplight.*

*JUDGE turns to the COMMANDER, who has not moved from his seat.*

JUDGE: *(evenly)* The court will now rule on the matter before it.

COMMANDER: *(unable to stop himself)* There is no matter...

JUDGE: There is *always* a matter. *(she faces the gallery)* This court rules that commanders, being persons, are subject to the same law as any other. That no body of persons, however named, however sponsored, stands above it. And that any order to the contrary is void.

*She raps the gavel.*

JUDGE: So ordered.

### Stinger

*A bare apartment, night. LUNA, at a table, a lamp, a newspaper. She is reading Judge's ruling, headline: "COURT DEFIES COMMANDERS." She reads it once. Then again.*

PLACK: *(from the doorway, with tea)* You've read that twice.

LUNA: *(not looking up)* Three times.

PLACK: *(setting the cup down)* It's very good.

LUNA: *(softly)* It's the first time anyone's said it out loud. In a court. In front of them.

PLACK: *(looking at the paper)* She doesn't know you're reading it.

LUNA: *(a faint smile)* And she wouldn't care if she did.

*She folds the paper with great care, and slides it into her notebook.*
