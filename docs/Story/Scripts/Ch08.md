# Ch 08 - Intersection - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A flooded transport tunnel, rain through the roof grates, brimming with angels, the main path they take
from their assumed headquarters. The flood rises through the chapter.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 25 s)
A tunnel mouth like a throat. Rain through iron grates in a roof so far up it makes its own weather. On the floor, an inch of black
water, a lane of white light, and forty angels walking in single file, all the same way, all the same pace.
KUU: (whisper) Is it a procession?
PLACK: It's a *commute.*

## Episode 1 - "Damp Explosives"
**Scene:** Tunnel entrance, dry. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Technician (BLUE).

TECHNICIAN: (soaked, with a rubber duck on his helmet) You the demolition team?
LUNA: We are *a* team. We've been given a demolition kit.
TECHNICIAN: Good. Blow the intersection at the cross tunnel. That's the order. That stops the traffic.
KUU: That stops the *angels?*
TECHNICIAN: That stops the *route.* Angels will find another way. That's the idea. Make them take the long way.
PLACK: Why the long way?
TECHNICIAN: (blank) I just work here.
LUNA: (weighing the satchel) Are these dry?
TECHNICIAN: ...Mostly.
LUNA: Mostly is the opposite of a bomb.

**Gameplay:** Tutorial for water depth (ankle-deep, wading speed); a first lane of angels that do not react.
**Beat:** The mission is to break the route. Luna is unconvinced.

## Episode 2 - "Lanes"
**Scene:** Main tunnel, junction one. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: Count them.
KUU: I'm counting. Forty-one, forty-two, forty --
PLACK: How many leave?
KUU: ...None. I haven't seen any come *back.*
PLACK: There's one road out and no road back.
LUNA: Are they hurt?
PLACK: They aren't *attacking.* Look. They walk around the water-pumps, they step over the cables, they hold the side-rails.
KUU: They're being *careful.*
PLACK: They're carrying something.

**Gameplay:** Observation (non-combat first half); then a skirmish when a lane is blocked.
**Beat:** Angels in this tunnel are on a delivery, not an attack.

## Episode 3 - "Waist Deep"
**Scene:** Flooded junction. **Present:** all three. Light combat; swimming in 4 m of water near the pumps.

KUU: I'll check the depth.
PLACK: Kuu --
KUU: It's fine. It's only -- *(he goes under)*
LUNA: ...Kuu?
*(A pause. Bubbles. Then Kuu surfaces, spitting.)*
KUU: It's fine! It's *deep.*
PLACK: How deep?
KUU: Very.
LUNA: That's the opposite of fine.
KUU: It's fine *relative to the alternative.*

**Gameplay:** Water mechanics (swimming, ledges, overhead pipes to climb); pump-room approach.
**Beat:** The flood rises with each junction.

## Episode 4 - "Shards"
**Scene:** Cross tunnel, the intersection. **Present:** all three.

*(The lanes meet, merge, turn. Each angel carries a small glowing shard held to its chest like a candle.)*
KUU: They're carrying... light?
PLACK: They're carrying *pieces.* Look at the colour.
LUNA: (very quietly) Yellow.
PLACK: The cubes. In the factory. They're carrying the same colour.
KUU: Wait. The angels bring it *to* the factory?
PLACK: Not bring. (she watches one pass; the angel walks through a crowd of her own kind without a glance) They *deliver.*
LUNA: To a place that makes people.
*(The squad watches a thousand angels pass, silent, for the first time not as enemies.)*

**Gameplay:** Observation set piece (non-combat, PURPLE); a tense silent crossing between lanes.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The angels are the supply. The factory is the sink.

## Episode 5 - "The Duck"
**Scene:** Pump room, sunken. **Present:** all three.

KUU: There's a duck.
LUNA: There is a *flooded pump room* and you've found a duck.
KUU: It's a *rubber* duck.
PLACK: Don't take it.
KUU: I'm not going to *take* it.
*(He takes it.)*
PLACK: Kuu.
KUU: He looked sad.
LUNA: He is a duck. He is made of rubber and he has no feelings and he has the expression of a creature who has seen things.
KUU: I'm keeping him.
LUNA: Fine. Name him.
KUU: ...Corporal.
LUNA: Of course.

**Gameplay:** Underwater pump-room dive (optional; air meter); a valve puzzle drains one junction.
**Beat:** A moment of light before the toll gate.

## Episode 6 - "Damp"
**Scene:** The demolition site. **Present:** all three.

*(The charge is set. The detonator is in Luna's hand.)*
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: Yes.
PLACK: You're not going to press it.
LUNA: The explosives are damp.
PLACK: They're not damp.
LUNA: (tests the wires with a licked finger) ...They are *spiritually* damp.
KUU: She's decided.
PLACK: (to Luna, evenly) Why?
LUNA: (a long pause, then lightly) Because if we break the road, they take the long way. And I would like to know where the long
way goes.
*(Plack looks at her. It's the nearest thing to approval Luna gets.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the squad defuses its own bomb; a short ambush on the way out.
**Beat:** Luna chooses to leave the tunnel standing.

## Episode 7 - "The Ticket"
**Scene:** The headquarters-side gate. **Present:** all three.

*(A booth at the end of the tunnel. In the booth, an angel in a conductor's cap, a ticket punch where its right hand should be.)*
TOLL COLLECTOR: Tickets.
KUU: (to Plack) Do we have tickets?
PLACK: No.
TOLL COLLECTOR: Tickets, please.
LUNA: (pleasantly) We were hoping to buy them at the window.
TOLL COLLECTOR: The window is for those who are *expected.*
LUNA: (a tight smile) Then we're not.
TOLL COLLECTOR: Then you are *unpaid.* (the punch rises) And unpaid passengers are asked to alight.
*(The whole lane of angels stops, and turns its head.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Toll Collector's lieutenants (a queue of ticket-punch angels).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The first time the angels in the tunnel notice them.

## Episode 8 - "The Toll Collector"
**Scene:** The gate. Boss fight. **Present:** all three. Flood at four metres.

*(Boss: the Toll Collector. Phase 1, ticket punches; Phase 2, the gate closes and the water rises; the squad fights from pipes.)*
KUU: It wants a ticket!
LUNA: Give it one.
KUU: What ticket?
LUNA: (throws the rubber duck) Corporal, *go.*
KUU: LUNA!
*(The duck hits the punch; the boss, confused, tries to stamp it.)*
PLACK: (grinning for the first time in the chapter) That's -- that's a valid ticket?
LUNA: Anything that floats is a ticket if you're confident.

**Gameplay:** Boss: ticket-stamp patterns, rising water, pipe-climb.
**Beat:** The boss falls. The lane breaks.

**Stinger:** The squad watches the last angel go by, the shard in its hands, toward the arc. Luna doesn't blow the tunnel. Kuu
fishes the duck out of a drain. "He's been through a lot," he says. Luna says, "So have we." Nobody says what the shards are for.
