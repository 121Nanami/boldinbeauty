# Ch 16 - Recruitment Drive (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Core arc beat: Luna treats recruits as pawns; the first cost arrives.

Chapter promise: a hand-painted sign says LUNA PAYS ON TIME, and it turns out that is not the whole job.

---

## Episode 1 - The Sign

*The Underworld, the district below the Ark's town square: tiered shanties, lamp-strings, a market that never closes. Under a bridge arch, a folding table, a hand-painted sign. Plack is on a stool with a clipboard. Kuu, arm in a sling, holds a thermos.*

**[Cold open. Daylight is a rumour here; everything is lit by lanterns.]**

SIGN: LUNA PAYS ON TIME.
*(smaller, underneath)* No prior experience required. No sponsors. No questions about your past.

KUU: *(reading)* I think the "no questions" is a little ambitious.

PLACK: *(clipboard)* It was Luna's idea.

KUU: Luna didn't paint it, though. You did.

PLACK: Somebody had to do the lettering.

*LUNA arrives with three paper cups of tea balanced on a tray, a dozen pastries in a bag, and an air of unearned confidence.*

LUNA: *(setting everything down with a flourish)* Operations headquarters.

PLACK: It's a table.

LUNA: It's a table with *ambition*.

**[Tutorial: Recruit management. A new screen shows a list of recruits, their needs, and "trust". Luna's early briefing text is cheerfully dismissive. The UI is deliberately cold.]**

## Episode 2 - Chess Pieces

*The first hour. A line of operators forms: a few from the Hanging City, three off-duty fighters, a gaggle of 3-stars in too-small armour, a gang member with a cautious smile.*

LUNA: *(addressing the line from a crate)* Friends. Colleagues. Potential pawns.

*A murmur. PLACK, at the table, closes her eyes.*

LUNA: *(realising)* ...Did I say that out loud?

KUU: *(quietly)* You did.

LUNA: I meant "pieces". Valued, strategically placed pieces.

PLACK: *(very low)* Luna.

LUNA: *(to the crowd, a little too fast)* The point is, I'll put you where you're most useful, and I'll pay you on time.

*A woman at the front folds her arms.*

OPERATOR: And what if I don't want to be put?

LUNA: *(genuinely puzzled)* Then... you'll be a very expensive spectator.

*PLACK, softly, passes a note down the table to KUU. It reads: "She's going to have to learn this the hard way."*

**[Dialogue mini-game: the player chooses how Luna pitches; each choice raises "Pay" but lowers "Trust". The mathematically best option is the worst one, narratively.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Hanging City Operators

*A familiar voice from the back of the line.*

LIFT CLERK: *(arriving, clipboard under her arm, six residents behind her)* Is this the recruiting table?

LUNA: *(delighted)* You came!

LIFT CLERK: *(a thin smile)* The lift's broken again.

LUNA: *(a pause)* Again?

LIFT CLERK: *(patiently)* Somebody has to be there to fix it. We elected a captain, remember?

PLACK: *(straightening, a little pink)* I'll... send instructions.

LIFT CLERK: *(dry)* We'd rather have you.

*PLACK glances at LUNA.*

LUNA: *(quickly)* No. Go. Fix the lift. Come back. It's... I mean it. You're not a chess piece.

*A moment. PLACK blinks.*

LIFT CLERK: *(approving)* That's the first right thing she's said all day.

**[Gameplay beat: the Hanging City residents join as "supporters" and appear as NPCs at the table in later chapters.]**

## Episode 4 - The Contract (Midpoint)

*A tent behind the table. A clerk in an off-colour uniform has set up a small desk with a stack of forms and a stamp.*

CLERK: *(nasal, practised)* Contract review. For the squad's protection.

PLACK: *(not looking up)* Whose protection?

CLERK: *(smoothly)* Yours.

*She picks up a sample form. A heading in bold: SPONSORSHIP ASSIGNMENT. A clause, in six-point type, which she reads aloud:*

PLACK: *(slowly)* "The Operator hereby assigns to the Sponsor all rights to likeness, voice, and image in perpetuity. The Operator further agrees that one third of wages shall be retained as 'brand maintenance'."

*A silence. KUU leans over her shoulder.*

KUU: *(whispering)* What's "perpetuity"?

PLACK: *(flat)* Forever.

KUU: Oh.

*LUNA, arriving, picks the contract up and reads it standing. For a very long time, she says nothing.*

LUNA: *(in an unusually quiet voice)* Every operator signs this.

CLERK: *(cheerfully)* It's the standard.

LUNA: *(looking at the page)* They own us.

CLERK: *(not unkindly)* They *sponsor* you.

*LUNA lowers the page.*

LUNA: *(without any joke at all)* That's the thing I'm fighting.

**[Gameplay beat: the contract appears in the player's archive. A "Contracts" tab in the Ark's menu later unlocks a mini-chain of side quests.]**

## Episode 5 - Four Operators

*A string of small scenes across the Underworld. The squad splits to recruit four people. Each is a short vignette, each with its own mini-fight.*

**[Level: four short missions; the player picks their order.]**

**1. The Gambler's Apprentice.** *A cardroom, smoke. A young operator, shuffling.* "I'll join if you win a hand." LUNA wins by accident. "That doesn't count." "It's the only kind that does."

**2. The Medic.** *A clinic in a cargo container. A tired operator with a broken lamp.* "I'll come, but I won't fight." LUNA: "Then you're the most dangerous person here."

**3. The Soup Operator.** *A stall. A cheerful operator in an apron.*

SOUP OPERATOR: *(ladling)* I only want to talk about soup.

LUNA: *(politely)* All right.

SOUP OPERATOR: *(brightening)* It's a broth. It's a lifestyle.

KUU: *(whispering to PLACK)* Is he joining?

PLACK: *(whispering back)* We have no idea.

*Twenty minutes later, he is joining.*

**4. The Smiling Man.** *A rooftop. A lean operator in a good coat, looking out over the city, smiling very slightly.*

SMILING MAN: *(without turning)* I'll join. I like your cause.

*KUU watches him for a long moment, uneasy. LUNA claps him on the shoulder.*

LUNA: *(warmly)* We'll be a family.

*The smile does not change.*

---

## Episode 6 - Table Talk

*Night, the table. Lamps strung overhead. Recruits sit on crates and bedrolls, eating. A low murmur. LUNA, on her stool, eats a pastry and watches, for once, silently.*

*PLACK comes to sit beside her.*

PLACK: You're quiet.

LUNA: I'm making notes.

PLACK: *(looking at the crowd)* On what?

LUNA: *(quietly)* On how many of them have a second job.

*PLACK follows her gaze. Half the recruits have a small, obvious bruise, a limp, a missing tooth. The "pay on time" sign is the only thing they look at.*

PLACK: They're desperate.

LUNA: *(a pause)* I noticed.

PLACK: And you're paying them.

LUNA: *(not looking at her)* It's the only thing I've got.

*A silence. KUU, nearby, is handing a bowl of soup to the Soup Operator, who is delighted.*

PLACK: *(softly)* That's not nothing.

LUNA: *(still looking at the crowd)* It's not enough.

**[Gameplay: a quiet scene. No UI. The player may talk to six recruits; each gives a one-line need.]**

---

## Episode 7 - Ambush

*Dawn. The table is in disarray: a toppled cup, a scatter of papers. In the distance, a siren. The recruits are on their feet, looking around.*

KUU: *(low)* Where's the Smiling Man?

*Nobody answers. A flare goes up over the market, blood red. Armoured shapes drop from the rooftops: a dozen government troopers, black armour, sponsor-wings on their shoulders.*

TROOPER: *(on a loudhailer)* ALL OPERATORS IN THIS AREA ARE BEING DETAINED FOR ASSISTING A WAR CRIMINAL.

*A panic. Recruits scatter, some fight, some freeze.*

LUNA: *(to PLACK)* Who told them?

PLACK: *(her voice hard)* The only person who knew where we'd be at dawn.

*In the middle of the chaos, across the square, the SMILING MAN stands beside a government officer, hand on a clipboard. He catches LUNA's eye. He does not look away. He does not stop smiling.*

**[Low point. Defensive hold across three waves while recruits are evacuated. Casualties are tracked: the player's performance determines how many recruits survive.]**

---

## Episode 8 - Repo (Boss)

**[Boss: Repo, a government debt-collection operator, huge, with a chain of contract pages that wrap around enemies. Phase 1: chains tether recruits; free them. Phase 2: he calls in collectors; hold the choke. Phase 3: he tears off his coat, a cage-like exo-frame; strike the contract-spool on his back.]**

REPO: *(in a booming, bored voice)* Operators of this area owe the sponsor eleven million, four hundred thousand credits. *(he lifts a clipboard)* Payment in kind is acceptable.

LUNA: *(ducking a chain)* What kind?

REPO: *(patiently)* Your bodies.

*The fight is vicious. KUU, one arm in a sling, is a tank. PLACK is everywhere, ledger-throws tearing pages from the chains.*

*At the end, REPO lies in a heap of shredded contracts. LUNA stands over him.*

REPO: *(wheezing)* It's... a standard clause.

LUNA: *(quietly)* Not today.

*She lifts the remaining contract, tears it in half, and tosses the halves into the air. The wind catches them. Scraps drift down over the Underworld like confetti.*

### Stinger

*The wreck of the table. Rain. The recruits, fewer now, bruised, huddle under the bridge. LUNA stands apart, looking at the empty stool where the SMILING MAN sat.*

LUNA: *(coldly)* He betrayed us. I'll find him. I'll make him an example.

*PLACK, behind her, speaks very quietly.*

PLACK: What was he being paid?

*LUNA turns.*

LUNA: *(sharply)* Does it matter?

PLACK: It might.

*A long silence. LUNA, jaw tight, takes a small notebook from her pocket, opens it, and, with a pencil, slowly writes down PLACK's question. She closes the book.*

*That is not the same as asking.*

**[Chapter 16 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 17. Seeds: the contract's "likeness and one third of wages"; the Smiling Man's sale; Plack's question; Luna's notebook.]**
