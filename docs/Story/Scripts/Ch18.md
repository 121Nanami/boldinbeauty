# Ch 18 - Backend Deals - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / NEW.** Main cast. The Exchange, a black-market floor. Payments are late, recruits are leaking, and Luna learns
that favours have a price.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Kuu with a ledger, in a bunk hall at midnight, running a finger down a column. He stops. Starts again. Stops. His lips move. He
closes the book gently, as if it might wake.
KUU: (to nobody) Nine. It should be ten.

## Episode 1 - "Nine Coins"
**Scene:** Bunk hall. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: There's a coin missing.
LUNA: There is *always* a coin missing. It's the nature of coins.
KUU: Not a *coin.* Nine coins, every night, for six nights.
PLACK: It's a pattern.
KUU: It's a *leak.*
LUNA: (a pause) Who has access to the strongbox?
KUU: You, me, Plack, and the quartermaster.
LUNA: Not the new recruits?
KUU: Not unless... (he trails off)
LUNA: Not unless *what?*
KUU: Not unless someone gave them the key.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat investigation: audit the strongbox log; talk to five recruits (BLUE).
**Beat:** The leak is internal.

## Episode 2 - "The Exchange"
**Scene:** The Exchange entrance. **Present:** all three.

*(A cavern the size of a stadium, a thousand stalls under a canopy of wire and cloth, a hundred languages, a hundred smells, one
rule written on a board: NO FIGHTING. Under it, in fresh chalk: *(Pay first.)*)*
LUNA: I love it.
PLACK: Don't touch anything.
LUNA: I'm only *looking.*
STALL-HOLDER: (a woman with a grin like a cut, scales in her hand) Lady! Lady with the long coat! You look like a person who needs a
favour.
LUNA: I look like a person who has *been* owed a favour.
STALL-HOLDER: (delighted) Oh, she's *good.*

**Gameplay:** Market hub: talk to stall-holders; side missions; the first stall fight (cutpurse).
**Beat:** The Exchange as a living market with rules.

## Episode 3 - "The Ledger Vault"
**Scene:** The vault under the Exchange. **Present:** all three.

*(A door the size of a wardrobe. A dial. A guard asleep in a chair with a sandwich on his chest.)*
KUU: We could ask him.
PLACK: We could *sneak.*
LUNA: We could *buy the sandwich.*
PLACK: What?
LUNA: (stepping to the guard) Excuse me. How much for the sandwich?
GUARD: (opening one eye) It's not for sale.
LUNA: Fifty for the sandwich and the combination.
GUARD: (considering, with dignity) Sixty.
LUNA: Done.
KUU: (very quietly) She's *bribing a guard with a sandwich.*
PLACK: It's the most honest thing she's done all week.

**Gameplay:** Vault infiltration; combination puzzle; guards (RED) as optional fights.
**Beat:** The vault opens; the recruit's ledger is inside.

## Episode 4 - "The Second Ledger"
**Scene:** Vault reading room. **Present:** all three. The spy (BLUE).

*(A young man in a recruit's jacket, with a second ledger open on his knee, caught like a deer.)*
SPY: It's not what it looks like.
LUNA: It looks like you keep two ledgers.
SPY: I do. (he swallows) But one of them is --
PLACK: The sponsor's.
SPY: ...Yes.
KUU: (gently) Why?
SPY: My sister is sick. They said if I gave them the strongbox log, they'd get her a place in the medical wing. It's a *real* place. It's a --
(he stops) It's a real *medical contract.*
LUNA: How sick?
SPY: Very.
*(Luna says nothing for a moment. Plack watches her, and does not interrupt.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a conversation with a branching tone (no mechanical consequence): Luna's response is scripted.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The leak is human.

## Episode 5 - "A Business Deal"
**Scene:** The Exchange, a quiet stall. **Present:** all three + the spy.

*(Luna writes a cheque and hands it across. The spy stares at it.)*
SPY: What's this?
LUNA: A business deal.
SPY: With who?
LUNA: With *you.* You do what I ask, and the sponsor does not get the next strongbox log. That's an exchange.
SPY: That's not a deal. That's a *kindness.*
LUNA: It is a deal with the *shape* of a kindness. (she pushes the cheque an inch) Take it. It's an investment.
SPY: In what?
LUNA: (a flicker of something) In your sister. She's *my* operator now.
*(Plack looks at her for a long time.)*

**Gameplay:** Shop and dialogue; the cheque as a YELLOW item that later reappears.
**Beat:** Luna pays; it's the first time she acts on a question Plack put to her.

## Episode 6 - "A Favour Is Worth"
**Scene:** The Exchange, a different stall. **Present:** Luna alone.

STALL-HOLDER: (counting something) You want to know what a favour's worth?
LUNA: I want to know what it costs.
STALL-HOLDER: (looking at her for a moment) A favour's worth exactly one person.
LUNA: One *what?*
STALL-HOLDER: One person. That's what it costs and that's what it's worth. You can't pay it in coin. You pay it in a *person.*
LUNA: (lightly) That's a very poor exchange rate.
STALL-HOLDER: It's the only one there is. (she goes back to her scales) Come back when you've paid.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE). A reflection scene.
**Beat:** Luna begins to see her recruits as people owed, not pieces.

## Episode 7 - "The Broker's Floor"
**Scene:** The Exchange's main floor, night. **Present:** all three.

*(The Broker, a long coat, a gentle voice, a hundred pockets, steps off a gantry. Behind him, the floor's guards.)*
BROKER: You've bought *my* sandwich and *my* spy.
LUNA: I've bought *a* sandwich and *a* spy. They were on offer.
BROKER: (smiling) I hold paper on half the people in this cavern. I hold paper on *you.* I know what you paid the guard.
PLACK: What do you want?
BROKER: What *everyone* wants. A *very* little. A name, a place, a date.
KUU: (low) He's stalling.
PLACK: He's waiting for the guards to get into position.
LUNA: (quietly) Then we're about to have a very bad evening.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Broker's heavies; a stall-and-crate arena.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The Broker has the floor.

## Episode 8 - "The Broker"
**Scene:** The Exchange floor. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Broker. Phase 1, contract slips thrown like cards; Phase 2, the stalls become a maze of pockets; the Broker's abacus arm.)*
BROKER: (flicking a slip) That's twenty. That's thirty. That's *fifty* off your credibility.
LUNA: (taking a slip out of the air) It's a very good *forgery.*
BROKER: (gently) It's a *bill.*
LUNA: (flat) I don't pay *your* bills. I pay *people.*
*(She puts a hand out; the black hole drags the whole abacus arm, and every slip, into a knot. Plack stamps the knot flat.)*
BROKER: (as he falls) ...It was only a favour.
LUNA: (quietly) I know. That's what's so dreadful about it.

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge bills; break the abacus; the market's guard reinforcements.
**Beat:** The Broker falls. The Exchange resettles.

**Stinger:** At the foot of a stall, Luna, alone, looks at her own hand: she's holding a half-torn bill. She folds it and puts it in her
pocket next to the cube. "One person," she says, to nobody. Behind her, in the dark, the stall-holder says: "Come back when you've paid."
