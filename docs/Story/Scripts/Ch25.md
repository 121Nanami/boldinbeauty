# Ch 25 - Adapt - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / SIDE STORY: Adapt.** The government's sponsored Cadet Cup on an academy range. A range master whose courses adapt to
whoever runs them. Warm, merciless; a sponsor changes the ammunition.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A whistle. A sun visor. A woman in a tracksuit in the middle of a huge indoor range, standing very still, watching the first cadet
run the first course. The course is a corridor, a few crates, a ladder. It looks easy.
ADAPT: (quietly) Again.
CADET: But I *finished* it --
ADAPT: Again.

## Episode 1 - "Again"
**Scene:** Training hall. **Present:** Adapt (playable guest), cadets (BLUE).

ADAPT: Four seconds. You did it in four. That's *good.* That's how I know you can do it in three.
CADET: What's the point of three?
ADAPT: (smiling) The point of *four* was that you'd learned the course. The point of *three* is that you'll have to *stop* learning the
course. (she presses a button; the crates rearrange) Again.
*(The course has changed. The cadet looks at it, and, for the first time, grins.)*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Adapt's kit (a copycat counter-move); a course that rearranges as you finish it.
**Beat:** Adapt's philosophy.

## Episode 2 - "The Cup"
**Scene:** The stands. **Present:** Adapt, a Sponsor's Aide (BLUE).

AIDE: The Cadet Cup begins at noon. Sponsors have asked for a spectacle.
ADAPT: It's a *training exercise.*
AIDE: It's *both.*
ADAPT: (a long exhale) Who's judging?
AIDE: The audience.
ADAPT: *Audiences* don't know what they're looking at.
AIDE: They know what they *like.* (a small smile) The Cup is a showcase. Please make it exciting.
*(Adapt looks at the cadets on the floor, thin, young, in brightly stickered armour.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE); a tonal low.
**Beat:** The Cup is not a school; it is an advertisement.

## Episode 3 - "The Targets Learn"
**Scene:** The live-fire course. **Present:** Adapt, cadets.

*(The course's targets are no longer standing still. They're ducking, side-stepping, firing back.)*
CADET: They're *learning!*
ADAPT: That's the *idea.* (she runs the course herself, drawing the targets' attention) Watch which way they move when I do.
CADET: They're copying you.
ADAPT: They're *me,* a half-second late. Now watch what happens when I *don't* do the thing I did.
*(She doesn't. The targets hesitate; one steps into the line of the others.)*
ADAPT: That's what *adapting* is. It's not copying. It's being the one who *changes the question.*

**Gameplay:** Live-fire course: adaptive targets (RED); a puzzle: break the pattern.
**Beat:** The mechanic is explained as a moral.

## Episode 4 - "Live Rounds"
**Scene:** The control tower. **Present:** Adapt, the Aide.

*(A sponsor's representative in a pale suit leans over the console, and slides a lever. Adapt sees the panel change colour.)*
ADAPT: What did you do?
AIDE: (lightly) Increased the realism.
ADAPT: The rounds are *live.*
AIDE: They'll be exciting.
ADAPT: They'll be *deaths!*
AIDE: (a shrug) The courses adapt. That's *your* design. If the cadets are good, they'll adapt. If they aren't...
ADAPT: (very quietly) You've put *my* course in a loop with live ammunition.
AIDE: Yes.
*(The floor's targets, all at once, raise their weapons.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the control tower scene; a short chase.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The sponsor changes the ammunition mid-run.

## Episode 5 - "As Designed"
**Scene:** The range floor. **Present:** Adapt, cadets.

*(The course is doing exactly what it was designed to do. It watches each cadet and gets harder. The cadets are bleeding.)*
CADET: (hit; falling) It's *learning* me!
ADAPT: (running, hauling the cadet) I know. I *know.*
CADET: Make it stop!
ADAPT: I can't make it stop. (grim) I made it so it *doesn't.*
*(She stops, looks at her hands, at the whistle at her neck.)*
ADAPT: ...Right. Then I'll make it learn *me.*

**Gameplay:** Rescue run: extract five cadets from three zones.
**Beat:** Adapt realises her own design is the weapon.

## Episode 6 - "Tearing Up The Rules"
**Scene:** The control tower. **Present:** Adapt.

*(She takes the course's rulebook off the wall: a laminated book of seven hundred pages, bound in steel. She tears it in two.)*
ADAPT: Rule one. *Every* cadet gets to finish.
SPONSOR'S AIDE: (stepping back) That is not --
ADAPT: Rule two. The course adapts to *me.*
SPONSOR'S AIDE: You can't --
ADAPT: Rule three. (she cracks her knuckles) There is no rule three. I'm *improvising.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a defiant scene; the course resets to "Adapt only".
**Beat:** The range becomes hers.

## Episode 7 - "The Warden Sweeps"
**Scene:** The range floor. **Present:** Adapt.

*(The Sponsor's Warden, a hulking range officer in adaptive plates, steps through the arch. Plates shift to match each cadet's weapon.)*
WARDEN: Adapt. You have been relieved.
ADAPT: By whom?
WARDEN: By the sponsor.
ADAPT: Then I'd like to hear them *say* it.
WARDEN: (a thin, sorry sound) They have asked me to say it for them.
*(Plates shift. Every cadet on the floor's weapon is suddenly turned on itself.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Warden's escorts; the cadets' weapons malfunction; a rescue.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The Warden turns the cadets' tools on them.

## Episode 8 - "The Sponsor's Warden"
**Scene:** The range floor. Boss fight. **Present:** Adapt.

*(Boss: the Sponsor's Warden. Its plates adapt to the player's loadout; the only way to win is to change what you're doing.)*
WARDEN: Your pattern is predictable.
ADAPT: (grinning) *Everybody's* is.
*(She breaks her own rhythm, stops, laughs. The Warden's plates, mid-adapt, jam; it stumbles into a trap she built in rule one.)*
WARDEN: (as it falls) ...Not... possible...
ADAPT: It's called *coaching.* (to the cadets) Again!

**Gameplay:** Boss: break your own pattern every ten seconds; trap-trigger; cadet help.
**Beat:** The Warden falls. The Cup is cancelled.

**Stinger:** The cadets are sent home with their prize money and a careful lecture about reading contracts. Adapt keeps the course's
blueprint and, for the first time, does not rebuild it. Luna's recruiters find her on the steps. Adapt: "I'll join your unification on
one condition. The course adapts to *you.*" Luna: "Done." Adapt, kindly: "You don't know what that means." "No," says Luna. "That's why
I agreed."
