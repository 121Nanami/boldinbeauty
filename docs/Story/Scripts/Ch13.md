# Ch 13 - A Secretive Soul - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / LOCKED map (events authored).** A small Japanese-looking countryside town in consistent low to medium rain. In the
middle, a pure black monolith with red hieroglyphics in an undecipherable language. The voice of the angel god speaks (verbatim).

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Rain on paddy water, a hundred small circles. A torii, red, wet. Behind it a village of grey-tiled roofs, and over it, in the
middle of the valley, a thing that is too straight to be a hill. Three travellers in a cart-track, one holding a bag of fruit.

## Episode 1 - "Rain"
**Scene:** The cart-track and the torii. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: It's so *quiet.*
PLACK: It's raining.
KUU: It's quiet *under* the rain.
LUNA: That is a countryside. It is where the noise goes to stop.
PLACK: Are you all right?
LUNA: I am married and I am damp. I am the happiest I have been in a decade.
KUU: (looking at her) ...You're lying.
LUNA: (smiling) I'm *bluffing.* There is a distinction.

**Gameplay:** Walking intro; rain, paddy fields (shallow water), village approach.
**Beat:** A quiet chapter after a loud one.

## Episode 2 - "The Quiet Ones"
**Scene:** The village street. **Present:** all three. Villagers (BLUE).

*(Wooden houses, tile roofs, drying rice on racks. People in plain coats stop at the sight of the three uniforms. One woman steps in front
of a child.)*
VILLAGER: You're government.
LUNA: We're *employed.*
VILLAGER: (a long look at her coat) Operators.
PLACK: We don't mean any harm.
VILLAGER: (not unkind) No. Nobody means any harm. (she steps back) You may pass. Please don't linger.
KUU: Why not?
VILLAGER: Because it gets *evening.*

**Gameplay:** Village hub: talk to six villagers (BLUE) for rumours; no combat.
**Beat:** The villagers are deserters, hiding. They are polite and watchful.

## Episode 3 - "Planting"
**Scene:** The paddies. **Present:** all three.

*(Luna in rubber boots, knee-deep in water, a bundle of seedlings in her fist, being taught by a very small, very patient old man.)*
OLD MAN: Not like that. Like *this.*
LUNA: That is how I did it.
OLD MAN: That is how you strangled it.
KUU: (planting beside her, neatly) It's all in the wrist.
LUNA: Don't be good at this.
KUU: I'm not *good* at it. I'm just not --
LUNA: You are good at it. You're being good at it *loudly.*
PLACK: (standing on the bank, laughing) I'm going to remember this.
LUNA: You do that. I'll be here, killing rice.

**Gameplay:** Short non-combat minigame (plant rows); pickups; a mood scene.
**Beat:** A pastoral lull. It is the happiest the squad has been in this arc.

## Episode 4 - "They Kneel"
**Scene:** The village at dusk. **Present:** all three.

*(As the light drops, the villagers leave their houses one by one, in plain coats, without speaking. They walk to the valley's edge and
kneel, in rows, facing the middle of the valley.)*
KUU: (whisper) What are they doing?
PLACK: They're kneeling.
KUU: To *what?*
LUNA: (looking up the valley, her face suddenly very still) To that.
*(In the dusk, a black pillar. Fifty metres tall. It has been there the whole time. It is the same colour as the sky behind it.)*
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: I know.
KUU: It's -- it's got writing on it.
*(Red marks, glowing. They are not letters. They move when you look at them.)*

**Gameplay:** Cutscene (PURPLE): the villagers' kneel; approach to the monolith begins.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The village has a god.

## Episode 5 - "The Monolith"
**Scene:** Monolith plaza. **Present:** all three.

*(Fifty metres of black, pure, with red hieroglyphics running its height. A trigger circle fifty metres wide, rain striking it and
vanishing.)*
PLACK: (reading the nearest glyph, then gently moving her hand away) I can't read it. It's not a language. It's... *instructions*
in something I don't have.
KUU: I'd like to go back.
LUNA: (stepping into the circle) One more step.
KUU: Luna, please --
LUNA: One. (she takes it)
*(The glyphs brighten. The rain, for a moment, stops.)*

**Gameplay:** Approach the monolith; a stealth walk past the kneelers; the trigger.
**Beat:** The voice is about to speak.

## Episode 6 - "Thats All"
**Scene:** The monolith. **Present:** all three.

VOICE: *(very quiet, very near, not loud at all, as if mouthed from inside the skull)* **They wree purity, Impure sin, Thats all.**
*(Silence. The rain returns, all at once.)*
KUU: (very small) What did it --
PLACK: *(she doesn't answer; she is looking at Luna)*
LUNA: (to the monolith, flat, almost kind) Is that all?
*(The glyphs flare. The voice does not repeat itself.)*

**Gameplay:** The line plays exactly once; the squad's reactions are non-interactive.
**Beat:** The first spoken line of the angel god.

## Episode 7 - "The Shepherd"
**Scene:** The village's outskirts. **Present:** all three. The Shepherd.

*(A tall guardian in a robe with a red glyph sash, a staff, a plain face. It has been standing at the edge of the kneelers the whole time.)*
SHEPHERD: You've heard it.
LUNA: I have.
SHEPHERD: Then you cannot leave unjudged.
KUU: Judged by *who?*
SHEPHERD: By what you carry.
PLACK: (low) The cube.
KUU: What cube?
PLACK: (looks at Luna, who has gone completely still) ...Luna.
LUNA: (quietly) I was going to tell you.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Shepherd's acolytes (kneelers that rise, Brute-type villagers).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The cube in Luna's pocket is exposed to her own squad.

## Episode 8 - "The Shepherd"
**Scene:** The monolith plaza, rain. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Shepherd. Phase 1, staff sweeps; Phase 2, the glyph sash lifts and the Shepherd's weight shifts; the rice paddies as a
shallow arena.)*
SHEPHERD: What you carry is not yours.
LUNA: It's *mine.* I took it.
SHEPHERD: (swinging) That is the *definition* of not yours.
KUU: We fight now and argue later!
PLACK: (parrying) We are *going to argue later.*
LUNA: (landing a hit; to Plack, quiet) Later.
*(The Shepherd falls. The cube, in Luna's pocket, goes quiet for the first time in months.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: staff patterns; rain-slick paddy footing; the cube pulses when the Shepherd is near a weak point.
**Beat:** The Shepherd is defeated. *(The boss is an OPEN item in the owner's documents; this is a proposal.)*

**Stinger:** They climb the road out of the valley in the dark with the monolith behind them. Nobody has said anything for a mile.
Then Plack says, evenly, "How long?" Luna: "Since the factory." Plack: "Four chapters." Luna: "It's an expression." A long beat.
Kuu, in the dark: "We should call it something." Luna: "It's called the Plan." Plack, after a very long time: "Then tell me the Plan."
