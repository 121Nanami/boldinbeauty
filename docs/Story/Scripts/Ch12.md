# Ch 12 - Paradise - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A beautiful neo-tropical city, not ruined, bright vibrant colours, constant good vibes, quite heavy
rainfall. **The wedding of Luna and Plack (2009).**

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A brass band in the rain. Umbrellas of every colour. A banner over a street of coral-pink buildings: WELCOME, HONOURED GUESTS. The
camera tilts down. Three soaked people are stepping out of an unmarked service door, one carrying a sock.

## Episode 1 - "Honoured Guests"
**Scene:** Harbour plaza. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. The Ambassador (BLUE).

AMBASSADOR: (a smile that arrives before he does) Welcome, welcome! You must be our guests of honour!
LUNA: We're soaked.
AMBASSADOR: And radiant! (he gestures; a camera drone lowers) Smile for the arc.
KUU: Sorry -- what is this place?
AMBASSADOR: Paradise! The finest city in the arc.
PLACK: The finest city in the *arc?* It's not in the arc. I've been in the arc. This isn't the arc.
AMBASSADOR: (the smile does not move at all) It is the part of the arc we show.

**Gameplay:** Plaza tutorial (non-combat tour, PURPLE); camera drones follow the squad.
**Beat:** The city is a showcase, and the squad is being filmed.

## Episode 2 - "Five Meals"
**Scene:** The Canal Market. **Present:** all three.

*(Luna is seated at a market stall with five empty bowls in front of her.)*
KUU: You've had five.
LUNA: I've had five meals. I'm having a sixth for the sake of completeness.
PLACK: (reading the menu) Luna.
LUNA: Mm.
PLACK: Look at the menu.
LUNA: It's a menu.
PLACK: Look at the *bottom* of the menu.
*(At the foot of every page, a small stylised wing logo. On the bowl, the same. On the umbrella, the same. On the awning, the same.)*
KUU: It's *everywhere.*
PLACK: It's on the food. It's on the *rain.*
LUNA: (puts down her spoon) ...It's a very *good* dumpling.
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: I'm saying the truth. It is a good dumpling. And I'm saying the other thing. Both.

**Gameplay:** Canal combat; food pickups (YELLOW); the logo as a recurring prop (tracked, a codex entry).
**Beat:** The sponsor is everywhere. The squad notes the logo.

## Episode 3 - "The Proposal"
**Scene:** A rooftop garden, afternoon, under a waterfall. **Present:** Luna, Plack (Kuu offscreen).

*(The sky is heavy. Rain drums the garden. Plack stops on a bench with a ring on a chain between her fingers.)*
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: Mm.
PLACK: I have carried this for four chapters of horror.
LUNA: (without looking) Chapters?
PLACK: It's an expression.
LUNA: It's an odd one.
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: (stops) ...Yes.
PLACK: Will you marry me?
*(A long beat. Rain. A very small bird, undeterred, on the rail.)*
LUNA: You haven't finished the question.
PLACK: I've finished the --
LUNA: Yes. (quickly) Yes. I was saying yes before you said the part with *marry.* I was saying it at *Luna.*
PLACK: (laughing, then not) ...Oh.
LUNA: You're crying.
PLACK: It's the rain.
LUNA: It's *very* heavy rain.
*(From a hedge, the click of a camera drone. They don't look up.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE, ~90 seconds). The choice-free proposal scene. *(Plack is never "caught by cameras" in the shot.)*
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The wedding becomes content.

## Episode 4 - "Kuu Loses The Rings Twice"
**Scene:** The rooftop wedding. **Present:** all three. The wedding, 2009.

*(A ceremony under a waterfall that doesn't get anyone wet. Guests: residents, cameras, a very serious priest-shaped automaton.)*
KUU: (best-person, holding a box) I would like to say that I've had the rings the entire time.
LUNA: Kuu.
KUU: I've had them. They're in the --
*(He opens the box; it is empty.)*
KUU: ...I'd like to apologise to the rings.
PLACK: (to the Commander, seriously) Please help him.
*(A short search sequence: the rings are on the priest, in a hedge, in a pastry. He finds them. He drops them again into the pond. He
retrieves them, wet.)*
KUU: (soaked, offering the box) ...They're clean.
LUNA: (taking one, to Plack) I do.
PLACK: I haven't asked.
LUNA: I know. I'm getting ahead.
PRIEST-AUTOMATON: By the power invested in --
LUNA: Keep it short.
PRIEST-AUTOMATON: -- by the sponsor --
LUNA: (raising a hand) *No.*
*(A pause. The priest-automaton re-reads its card, and says nothing.)*
PRIEST-AUTOMATON: ...By the power invested in the two of you.
*(They kiss. The rain does not stop. Applause, sparse and then enormous.)*

**Gameplay:** Short minigame: find the rings (YELLOW). A non-combat cutscene.
**Beat:** Luna and Plack are married. The year is 2009.

## Episode 5 - "Service Tunnels"
**Scene:** Beneath the city. **Present:** Kuu (and later all three).

*(Kuu, in a tuxedo with a sock in the pocket, wanders under the stage for the champagne. A service tunnel, a long grey corridor.)*
KUU: Hello?
*(A queue of people in plain overalls, three hundred long, shuffling toward a window marked RATIONS.)*
COOK: (a woman in a stained apron, to him) Are you lost?
KUU: I'm -- yes. I think I'm the wrong kind of guest.
COOK: You're the *right* kind. (she lowers her voice) The ones upstairs eat what we cook. We eat what's left.
KUU: Is there a lot left?
COOK: There's what the window says there is.
KUU: ...Can I --
COOK: No. (kindly) Go back up and be a guest. That's what the city needs you to be.

**Gameplay:** Stealth through the tunnels; a short NPC scene; unlock a second route.
**Beat:** Paradise is built on a rationing office.

## Episode 6 - "Receipts"
**Scene:** The Convention Hall's archive. **Present:** all three. Luna in her wedding dress.

PLACK: What are you doing?
LUNA: Collecting.
PLACK: Souvenirs?
LUNA: (holding up a fan of paper) Receipts.
KUU: *Why?*
LUNA: Because I have just been married on camera by a sponsor, and I would like to know what that cost *me.*
PLACK: (slowly) ...What does it cost?
LUNA: (reading) "Event licence. Likeness licence. Ring licence." (she turns the page) "Bride's surname: pending." (a flat beat) They're
going to try to put a name on me.
PLACK: They can't.
LUNA: They can try. (she tucks the receipts into her bodice) I like receipts. They have *dates.*

**Gameplay:** Archive combat (drone sentries); document pickups; a stealth-timed gate.
**Beat:** Luna begins to collect evidence rather than souvenirs.

## Episode 7 - "I Bless This Union"
**Scene:** The wedding reception on the lagoon beach. **Present:** all three. The Ambassador.

*(Fairy lights on the sand. A band. Cameras everywhere. The Ambassador steps up on a stage and takes a microphone.)*
AMBASSADOR: Friends! Honoured guests! In the spirit of unity, I'd like to bless this union.
LUNA: (very evenly) You were not invited to bless it.
AMBASSADOR: A sponsor is *always* invited.
PLACK: (low, to Luna) The cameras are rolling.
AMBASSADOR: (to the drones, to the arc) And so, with the generosity of our partners --
LUNA: (quietly) Plack. The drones are on.
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: Good. (she takes a very slow breath) Commander, you have the floor.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Ambassador's elite guards (Brand Elites); live-broadcast fight with drone hazards.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The wedding is hijacked.

## Episode 8 - "The Ambassador"
**Scene:** Lagoon, beach. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Ambassador. Phase 1, camera-drone volleys; Phase 2, the cape unfolds into a wing-logo sail; the lagoon becomes the arena.)*
AMBASSADOR: (as he fights, beaming) Isn't it wonderful? Eight million people are watching!
KUU: (dodging) They're watching a *fight.*
PLACK: They're watching what the sponsor wants them to see.
LUNA: Then let's show them the receipt.
*(Luna, mid-fight, holds a receipt up to the nearest drone: "Event licence. Likeness licence." The Ambassador's smile drops.)*
AMBASSADOR: Stop -- you can't --
LUNA: (as he falls) I can. It's on the *record.*

**Gameplay:** Boss: shoot the drones, redirect the cameras, lagoon swim.
**Beat:** The Ambassador falls on live feed, and the sponsor's logo is what everyone remembers.

**Stinger:** At dusk, on the back road, wet and married, with a bag of fruit between them. Kuu hands Luna a cheque the sponsor sent
over for their trouble. She tears it in half, and then in half again. "I'm done being nice," she says, mildly. Plack: "You were never
being nice." Luna: "I was practising."
