# Ch 32 - The Charter - Script

**Arc 2 / 2014 / NEW.** Main cast. The Charter Vault under the old city hall: three trial rooms, one for each faction. Drafting the
document that is meant to unify them. A seventh line stays blank.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A round steel door the size of a house, under a city hall that has been a ruin for seven years. A wheel in the door's centre. Three
people on their knees on the floor in front of it, each holding a pen.
PLACK: The first clause is the hardest.
LUNA: The first clause is *always* the hardest.
KUU: (looking at the blank page between them) What's the first clause?
LUNA: ...That's the hardest.

## Episode 1 - "The Archive Halls"
**Scene:** Vault entrance. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Judge (BLUE: she is witness).

JUDGE: (at the door, in her power dress, gavel at her side) You will draft it here.
LUNA: Judge.
JUDGE: Luna. (a nod to Plack, a nod to Kuu) A Charter is not an agreement. It is a *constraint.* Do not mistake the one for the other.
PLACK: What's the difference?
JUDGE: An agreement binds those who sign it. A constraint binds those who *intend not to.*
KUU: (faintly) ...That's very good.
JUDGE: It is *old.* (she pushes the wheel; the door opens) Enter.

**Gameplay:** Vault entrance: puzzle gate; archive-hall combat (vault drones).
**Beat:** The Charter is a constraint.

## Episode 2 - "The Operators' Room"
**Scene:** Trial room one (a barracks). **Present:** all three.

*(A long barracks, bunks, a table with a COUNCIL sign. A voice asks questions of the room.)*
ROOM: What is an operator?
KUU: (promptly) Someone who gets *paid.*
ROOM: Incorrect.
PLACK: Someone who is *owned.*
ROOM: Partly.
LUNA: Someone who's been *forgotten.*
ROOM: (a pause) Closer.
*(A fourth voice, from the table: Arita, in the Council sign's shadow, dressed in taped armour.)*
ARITA: (calling from the table) Someone who wants the *kettle fixed!*
ROOM: Correct.

**Gameplay:** Trial room: answer by fighting the right wave (cosmetic choice; each yields a different clause).
**Beat:** The Operators' clause is drafted by the Council.

## Episode 3 - "The Secrets' Room"
**Scene:** Trial room two (a stage). **Present:** all three.

*(A theatre, a rehearsal under way. A narrator reads from a script; a clown juggles three clocks; a woman in headphones is eating cereal in the
orchestra pit.)*
JESTER: (from a trapeze) Welcome to the *rehearsal!* We are *so* pleased you could come to the part where you don't know your lines!
MEDIA: (from the pit, over the top of a bowl) It's a *very* good rehearsal. I have notes.
TIMEKEEPER: (from the wings) We are four minutes behind.
NARRATOR: (reading) They were *five.*
LUNA: (flat) I have not even *begun.*
JESTER: That is *the best time!*

**Gameplay:** Trial room: a theatre combat (set-piece enemies on a stage).
**Beat:** The Secrets' clause is drafted in a rehearsal.

## Episode 4 - "The Government's Room"
**Scene:** Trial room three (a court). **Present:** all three.

*(A pale court, a bench, a gallery of empty seats. A judge's chair. The squad stands alone on the floor.)*
COURT: *(an impersonal, dry voice)* The matter before the court: the government of the arc.
PLACK: (stepping forward) We'd like to submit a clause.
COURT: The court will *rule.* It does not *receive.*
LUNA: (brightly) Then rule!
COURT: Your clause is that the government be subject to the law.
LUNA: Yes.
COURT: The ruling is *no.*
LUNA: (a flicker; very softly) ...Thank you.
COURT: Pardon?
LUNA: It's the *only* honest thing anyone's said in this building.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); a courtroom puzzle: the court rules against Luna and she thanks it; the clause is redrafted.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The government's clause fails honestly. It is redrafted.

## Episode 5 - "On The Floor"
**Scene:** The signature chamber. **Present:** all three.

*(The three are cross-legged on the floor, three sheets of paper, a single lamp. They are tired.)*
LUNA: ("Clause four.") The Operators shall not be owned.
PLACK: Say it as a right.
LUNA: Operators shall not be *property.* Operators shall...
KUU: (writing) "Not be *used for ratings.*"
LUNA: That's a very specific right.
KUU: It's a *very specific wrong.*
PLACK: Add "nor for sponsorship."
LUNA: I like *nor.*
PLACK: It's a good word.
LUNA: It's a good *drafting* word.
*(A long, quiet scene: a document being born.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a drafting scene (the Charter's clauses appear on-screen).
**Beat:** The Charter is drafted by the three.

## Episode 6 - "Seven"
**Scene:** The signature chamber. **Present:** all three. The Guardian (BLUE).

*(A tall, quiet figure of carved stone, a seal in its hands, steps from the wall.)*
GUARDIAN: The Charter requires signatures.
PLACK: Three. For each faction.
GUARDIAN: The *Secrets* number seven.
LUNA: There are six.
GUARDIAN: There are *seven.* Seven seats have been kept. Six are known. (a pause) The seventh sits at the *back.*
LUNA: (a long breath) I've seen the plate.
GUARDIAN: Then you know the name.
LUNA: I know the *seat.*
GUARDIAN: That is enough, for now.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** The Charter needs seven Secret signatures.

## Episode 7 - "Tested"
**Scene:** The signature chamber. **Present:** all three.

GUARDIAN: The text must be tested.
KUU: Tested how?
GUARDIAN: By *force.*
*(The stone figure raises the seal. The floor tilts. The three sheets rise off the floor and begin to float, each with its own clause.)*
PLACK: It's going to attack the *paper.*
LUNA: (a bright, wicked grin) Of course it is. It's a legal system.
*(The Guardian swings.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Archive Wardens (clause-guarding stone sentries); a paper-defence puzzle.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The Charter must be defended in battle.

## Episode 8 - "The Charter Guardian"
**Scene:** Signature chamber. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Charter Guardian. Phase 1, seal slams; Phase 2, the floating clauses must be kept intact: the squad defends three paper targets.)*
PLACK: Clause one is under the left arm!
LUNA: I've got clause four!
KUU: I've got... *I've got the toaster!*
LUNA: Kuu, it's not a *clause!*
KUU: (hitting the Guardian's knee with it) It's an *exhibit!*
*(The Guardian's seal drops; the stone cracks; the clauses settle back to the floor, whole.)*
GUARDIAN: (quietly) The Charter holds.

**Gameplay:** Boss: defend three paper targets while damaging the Guardian; seal slams.
**Beat:** The Guardian yields. The Charter is binding.

**Stinger:** On the final sheet, in the empty seventh line, only a very faint outline. "It's the only line that matters," says the
Guardian. Luna looks at Judge, at the door. Judge looks back. "Find the one who belongs there," Luna says. Judge: "I do not take
instructions." A beat. "I *take cases.* I will take this one."
