# Ch 30 - The Line Restarts - Script

**Arc 2 / 2012 / NEW.** Main cast. A.R.C TECH again, now with people. The cradle hall. The Patron has a name.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
The same intake door, the same sign, the same rain; but lights in every window and, at the gate, a guard-hut with a person in it
reading a magazine. A voice from the loudspeakers, as warm as before:
VOICE: Welcome back, visitors. We missed you.

## Episode 1 - "Welcome Back"
**Scene:** Intake hall. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: It remembers us.
PLACK: It's got a *better* script.
VOICE: Once upon a time, three visitors came *back* to the factory that made the light. The factory had not slept. It was glad of the
company.
LUNA: (tightly) It's very polite.
PLACK: It's very *accurate.*
VOICE: Luna. Five stars. She had *grown.* (a pause) Plack. Five stars. *Still* asks the right questions. Kuu. Four stars.
KUU: (hurt, small) ...I went *up!*
VOICE: You apologise less.

**Gameplay:** Intake hall; the Voice returns; the factory is staffed (non-hostile engineers BLUE).
**Beat:** The factory's restart is under way.

## Episode 2 - "Engineers"
**Scene:** The line halls. **Present:** all three. Engineers (BLUE).

ENGINEER: (to Luna, tired, polite) Are you from the board?
LUNA: ...Sort of.
ENGINEER: (a long exhale) Thank god. Can you tell them about the overtime? We've had eleven days without a break.
PLACK: You work here?
ENGINEER: (as if it were obvious) *Everyone* works here. (he lowers his voice) You're not going to shut it down, are you?
LUNA: (a pause) Why?
ENGINEER: (almost whispering) Because it *makes* things. And we're not sure what it makes *next.*

**Gameplay:** Line-hall exploration; combat with line drones (RED); a conversation hub (BLUE).
**Beat:** The workers are as trapped as the product.

## Episode 3 - "Fist-Sized"
**Scene:** The cube floor. **Present:** all three.

*(The cubes are larger than before, the size of a fist, a pomegranate: yellow as a sun, wet-looking, and slightly bruised along one face.)*
PLACK: These are different.
KUU: They're... *bigger.*
PLACK: They're *denser.* (she holds up a stamp) "5%."
KUU: Five?
PLACK: Not one. Five.
LUNA: (lightly, too lightly) So the ceiling has been raised.
PLACK: (flat) By whom?
LUNA: (quietly) Someone who doesn't believe in ceilings.

**Gameplay:** Line combat; a conveyor puzzle; a stamped pallet (YELLOW).
**Beat:** The new product is five times stronger.

## Episode 4 - "The Cradle Hall"
**Scene:** The cradle hall. **Present:** all three.

*(A long, high white room. Row after row of cradles, each with a plate and an empty frame. One cradle, at the end, is different:
steel and white and polished, with a small brass plate. It is empty.)*
KUU: (reading) "Angel. V-One."
PLACK: (reading below it) "Released to custody."
LUNA: (very quietly) ...Custody of whom?
*(A terminal at the cradle's foot. A green screen. Plack touches a key.)*
TERMINAL: **CUSTODY LOG. ENTRY 1: RELEASED 2005-03-14 TO: [the Patron].**
PLACK: The Patron.
KUU: The sponsor's...
PLACK: *The* sponsor.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the terminal scene; a log puzzle (find the three keys).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Angel V1 is released into custody of the Patron.

## Episode 5 - "The Patron"
**Scene:** The control room. **Present:** all three.

*(Kuu, at a second terminal, reading, with his lips moving.)*
KUU: The Patron is not a company.
PLACK: What?
KUU: It's a *seat.* (he swallows) Look. "Grade: above operator. Standing: Secret. Number: seven." (he turns) *Seven.*
LUNA: There are six.
KUU: There are *seven.* The seventh isn't on the list.
LUNA: (a slow, careful breath) Of course. The *sixth* is the one they tell you about.
PLACK: The signature on every sponsor document --
KUU: The wing. (a small voice) It's the *same* wing.
LUNA: (the bored expression not even a memory) Whose?

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); a codex update; a short escape (alarm).
**Beat:** The Patron is a seventh Secret, and the wing is theirs.

## Episode 6 - "Foreman Two"
**Scene:** The line halls, alarm. **Present:** all three.

VOICE: (warmly) An *alarm* has gone off. The factory will respond.
KUU: (hoarse) What does that mean?
VOICE: It means the Foreman would like a word.
*(From a service lift, a person-shaped automaton. This one has more arms. It carries its clipboard like a shield.)*
FOREMAN MK II: Visitors. You have read the log.
PLACK: (low) How does it know?
FOREMAN MK II: The log is read by the Foreman.
LUNA: (slow) The log is *read* by the Foreman.
FOREMAN MK II: It is on the *manifest.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Foreman Mk II's guards (line drones, brutes).
**Beat:** The factory knows they know.

## Episode 7 - "Breaking The Line"
**Scene:** The line's control spine. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: (looking at the great, humming column) I'm going to break it.
PLACK: It'll make another.
LUNA: Then it will have to find another *Luna.*
KUU: (quietly) Is that a *good* argument?
LUNA: It's the only one I have.
*(She puts her hand on the control spine; the black hole forms; the lights, down the whole length of the factory, begin to shudder.)*
FOREMAN MK II: (at her back) You will be *rated.*
LUNA: (not turning) I'm very *much* looking forward to it.

**Gameplay:** Set piece: break the line (three nodes, one per operator, timed).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The act is irreversible; the factory fights back.

## Episode 8 - "Foreman Mk II"
**Scene:** The cradle hall. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: Foreman Mk II. Phase 1, multi-arm sweeps and clipboard barrages; Phase 2, the cradles open and a dozen drones pour out.)*
FOREMAN MK II: (as it fights) Your *feedback* is *valued.*
KUU: (hitting a drone) Is it?
FOREMAN MK II: It is *recorded.*
PLACK: (parrying) By whom?
FOREMAN MK II: (a hesitation; the first the machine has shown) ...By the *Patron.*
LUNA: Then let them *read* this.
*(The Foreman falls. The line's last column goes black.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge multi-arm sweeps; break the cradles; drone waves.
**Beat:** The Line is wrecked. The Patron has a name.

**Stinger:** On the road out, in the rain, a single yellow light still glows in the high window of the factory. "It'll make another,"
Plack says. "Then we'll have to find that one too," says Luna. Kuu, behind them, quiet: "There's a *person* in the cradle plate. Did
anyone read the name?" Plack: "There's no name." Luna: "There's a seat." A long beat. "Let's find out who sits in it."
