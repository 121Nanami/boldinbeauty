# Ch 16 - Recruitment Drive - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / NEW.** Main cast. The Underworld below the town square. Luna builds an army and treats it like chess.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A hand-painted sign nailed to a pillar in a cavernous undercity: LUNA PAYS ON TIME. Below it, in smaller paint: *(Terms and
conditions apply. Ask Plack.)* A table. A clipboard. A kettle. A queue that has not started yet.

## Episode 1 - "The Table"
**Scene:** The Underworld market, recruiting table. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

LUNA: (surveying the empty queue) It's early.
PLACK: It's nine.
LUNA: It's a very *early* nine.
KUU: (a fresh bandage under his shirt) Should we put out a sign?
LUNA: I've *put out* a sign.
KUU: A bigger one?
LUNA: ...Kuu, the first rule of an army is that it comes when it's *ready.*
PLACK: (sitting, with a clipboard) The first rule of an army is that you have to feed it. (she writes "BREAD" at the top) Bread. Then
bunks. Then pay. Then *names.*
LUNA: That's four rules.
PLACK: I have a list.

**Gameplay:** Recruiting-table tutorial (a management mini-interface: sign in, assign, brief).
**Beat:** The first recruits arrive.

## Episode 2 - "Chess Pieces"
**Scene:** The briefing hall. **Present:** all three + eight recruits.

LUNA: (at a map, with a pointer) The government has seven commanders, a thousand guards and a thing they call a *plan.* We have this
room. This is the difference between us and them: they are *organised.* We are *inspired.*
RECRUIT (hand up): What's my role?
LUNA: You are a knight.
RECRUIT: What does a knight do?
LUNA: ...Moves in an L.
RECRUIT: (carefully) Is there a *person* who needs saving?
LUNA: Yes. The arc. In the shape of an L.
PLACK: (to Kuu, in a murmur) She's using chess pieces.
KUU: She's good at it.
PLACK: She is *terrible* at it, and they're enjoying it.

**Gameplay:** Briefing mini-scene (a map; assign a role to each recruit). No combat.
**Beat:** Luna treats her recruits as pieces. They love her anyway.

## Episode 3 - "From the Hanging City"
**Scene:** The Underworld gate. **Present:** all three + Hanging City recruits.

RESIDENT: (stepping off a lift, with a duffel) Is this where we sign up?
LUNA: You came.
RESIDENT: We came. (she looks around) Where's the lift?
LUNA: The lift is...
KUU: There's no lift.
RESIDENT: Where do we go when we want to go home?
LUNA: (a beat) ...Home is a *state of mind.*
RESIDENT: (very patient) That's the thing you said last time. It's still not an answer.
PLACK: (quickly) There are bunks on level two, bread at six, and a timetable on the wall.
RESIDENT: (to Plack, relieved) *Thank* you.

**Gameplay:** NPC dialogue; escort the new recruits through a gang-controlled tunnel (short combat).
**Beat:** The first big wave of recruits arrives; Plack is the glue.

## Episode 4 - "The Contract"
**Scene:** The paperwork annex. **Present:** all three. Contract clerk (BLUE).

CLERK: (sliding a document across) Standard operator contract. Sign here, here, and here.
PLACK: Read it to me.
CLERK: It's very boring.
PLACK: Read it.
CLERK: (reading) "The Operator agrees that their likeness, voice and name are the property of the Sponsor, and that one-third of all
wages ... " (he stops) That's the *sponsor's* contract, not yours.
PLACK: Which is the one all your recruits are *still* under.
CLERK: ...It's standard.
LUNA: (leaning on the desk) Say that again.
CLERK: Standard.
LUNA: "One-third of all wages."
CLERK: It's -- yes. (quietly) They own your face.
LUNA: (after a very long moment) They own your *face.*
PLACK: (to Kuu, low) That's what she's been missing.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat document puzzle (find the clause); a minigame: redraft a contract.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The sponsors own the operators' likenesses, and a third of their wages. Luna understands her pool.

## Episode 5 - "Four Terrible Reasons"
**Scene:** The Underworld, a series of vignettes. **Present:** all three, in turns.

*(Four recruits, four reasons.)*
RECRUIT 1: I'm here for the soup.
RECRUIT 2: I'm here because my mother said.
RECRUIT 3: I'm here because I'm *owed.*
RECRUIT 4: I'm here because the government shot my dog.
LUNA: (to Recruit 4) I'm sorry.
RECRUIT 4: It was a *very* good dog.
LUNA: What was its name?
RECRUIT 4: Captain.
LUNA: (a flicker; she turns to Kuu, who is holding a rubber duck in his coat) ...Corporal has a *superior.*
KUU: (very quietly) It's a *sign.*

**Gameplay:** Four short missions (escort, retrieve, deliver, defend); each pays a unique recruit reward.
**Beat:** The recruits are people with terrible, real reasons.

## Episode 6 - "The Smile"
**Scene:** The bunk hall. **Present:** all three. A recruit with a too-wide smile.

*(A new recruit, clean, pressed, smiling. Kuu is staring at him.)*
KUU: (low, to Plack) He hasn't blinked.
PLACK: Maybe he's nervous.
KUU: He's been smiling for eleven minutes.
LUNA: (cheerfully) What's your name?
RECRUIT: (a beat) Hiro.
LUNA: Lovely name. Where are you from?
RECRUIT: The... Underworld.
LUNA: Whereabouts?
RECRUIT: (smiling) ...Near here.
*(Kuu opens his mouth. Luna stops him with a hand on his sleeve. She has noticed. She chooses to let it ride.)*

**Gameplay:** Observation (a short interaction where the player can flag the smiler or let it ride).
**Beat:** A defector is among them; Luna lets him in.

## Episode 7 - "Ambush"
**Scene:** The Underworld, night. **Present:** all three.

*(Sirens. The kettle at the recruiting table goes over. Sponsor thugs, collectors, in sponsor jackets, flood the market from three tunnels at once.)*
KUU: They *knew.*
PLACK: Who knew?
KUU: Hiro.
LUNA: (a pause; she looks at the empty seat where the smiler sat) ...Yes.
PLACK: You *knew.*
LUNA: I thought he was a knight.
PLACK: Luna!
LUNA: (sharp) I know! I *know.*
*(A brute in sponsor colours lifts the table over his head.)*

**Gameplay:** Defence of the Underworld market; escort recruits to the bunkers; mini-boss: a Collector Brute.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The recruit sold the table.

## Episode 8 - "Repo"
**Scene:** The market, a collapsed arcade. **Present:** all three. Repo.

*(Boss: Repo. A hulking collector with a chain of contracts around his arm; each contract that touches a recruit binds them.)*
REPO: Contracts! *Contracts!* (a clamp whips out) I own half your face and a third of your *wages.*
KUU: (dodging) That's *illegal.*
PLACK: It's *standard.*
LUNA: (quietly) Plack, how do we beat a contract?
PLACK: (parrying) We *read* it. Find the clause that doesn't hold.
LUNA: Which one?
PLACK: (shouting) The one that says the Sponsor owns the *person!*
*(Repo's chain breaks, link by link, as each recruit steps out of his reach.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: contract-chain mechanic (break chains by reading clauses: a timed prompt); clamp attack.
**Beat:** Repo falls. The recruits are free of him, if not of the sponsor.

**Stinger:** In the wreck of the table Plack finds a clipboard with a list of every recruit and, beside the defector's name, a small
tick. "What was he being paid?" she asks. Luna, looking at the list: "I don't know." She picks up a pen. Under the question, she
writes it down. Plack: "That's not the same as asking." Luna: "It's the *first half.*"
