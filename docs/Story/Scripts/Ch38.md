# Ch 38 - Who Is Strongest - Script

**Arc 3 / 2017.** Main cast. Three fronts: the Docks, the Moon Kingdom, the Hanging City. Three champions. Luna's one argument: that
none of them is the strongest.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Three flags on three fronts under one rain. A lighthouse beam sweeps a fog-bound sea and finds nothing. A pale Moon hangs over a
second arc. A chain, one link cut, sways under a city of laundry.
LUNA (V.O.): There are three armies and each believes it will win. They're right, in the sense that *one* will. I'd like to try the
other thing.

## Episode 1 - "The Docks Front"
**Scene:** The quay, the lighthouse island. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. A truce clerk (BLUE).

CLERK: The Operators' Council holds the Docks. The terms of the truce are that you do not kill anyone.
LUNA: That's *my* term.
CLERK: I know. It was on the form.
KUU: (stepping off a barge; fog on his shoulders) I've been here before. (he looks at the lighthouse) I'm very sorry. (to the
lighthouse) I'm *very* sorry.
PLACK: It's a lighthouse.
KUU: It was *rude* to it.
LUNA: It's on. (she watches the beam sweep across an empty sea) It's holding nothing.
PLACK: There's nothing for it to hold. They've all gone to the arc.

**Gameplay:** Docks combat (fog, waves); a return to the Ch 3 geography; beam hazards.
**Beat:** The lighthouse beam holds nothing; the angels have moved.

## Episode 2 - "The Warlord"
**Scene:** The quay's barricade. **Present:** all three. The Warlord (champion).

WARLORD: (a broad woman in a sea-worn cloak, a harpoon over her shoulder) Luna. (a nod) I've been told not to kill you.
LUNA: Splendid.
WARLORD: I find I'm not sure I agree.
LUNA: We can discuss it.
WARLORD: We *are.* (she plants the harpoon; the quay shudders) This is what I've got. A dock. A flag. A thousand people I'd die for.
(a beat) What have *you* got?
LUNA: (a long, honest pause) ...A Charter. (she holds up a torn page) In *pieces.*
WARLORD: (almost kindly) That's *brave.*
LUNA: It's very *stupid.*
WARLORD: Those are the same thing, in a general.

**Gameplay:** A duel with a champion (mini-boss: Warlord's lieutenant).
**Beat:** The Warlord is not a villain.

## Episode 3 - "The Moon's Second Flicker"
**Scene:** The Moon Kingdom. **Present:** all three. The Adapted (champion).

*(The second arc, now fortified: barricades of vine and glass, banners of the Secrets in the tombs. The Moon hangs overhead. As the squad enters,
it flickers, the yellow of the cubes, once, twice.)*
KUU: (looking up) It did it *again.*
PLACK: (very softly) It's... *warm.*
LUNA: (looking at the island under the Moon) Something's behind it.
*(From the island, a figure in adaptive plates steps forward. The plates shift to match each of them in turn.)*
THE ADAPTED: (a flat, clipped voice, Adapt's cadence made hard) Again.
LUNA: (to Plack) That's *her* word.
PLACK: That's a *corruption* of it.

**Gameplay:** Moon Kingdom combat; the champion's plates adapt to the squad.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The Moon flickers again, and the Secrets have a champion who adapts.

## Episode 4 - "The Chain"
**Scene:** The Hanging City. **Present:** all three. The Chain Marshal (champion).

*(The chain, one link cut. The city tilts, a degree, two. The residents have strung ropes from district to district; the clocks all say
4:13.)*
RESIDENT: (leaning from a window) The government's been cutting the links.
KUU: Why?
RESIDENT: To make us *leave.*
PLACK: They don't need the city.
RESIDENT: They need it *gone.*
*(On the chain's anchor, in a marshal's uniform, a figure in a chain-cape unhooks a whip.)*
CHAIN MARSHAL: (carrying across the void) One link. Then another. By morning the city is a *sea.*

**Gameplay:** Chain climbing; swing combat; the tilted city (the floor slopes).
**Beat:** The government is cutting the chain.

## Episode 5 - "The Argument"
**Scene:** A bridge between the Docks and the Moon. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

LUNA: (finally, to the three of them in a quiet moment) I have one argument.
PLACK: You've had it for a month.
LUNA: It's *short.*
KUU: Say it.
LUNA: None of them is the strongest.
PLACK: (slowly) Because...
LUNA: Because they're each *stronger* than the other two *separately,* and *weaker* than the three *together.* It's an old observation.
It's called an *army.*
KUU: ...That's *it?*
LUNA: It's *always* been it. That's why it's a good argument.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** Luna states her thesis.

## Episode 6 - "Without Killing"
**Scene:** All three fronts, montage. **Present:** all three.

*(Three set pieces, quickly cut. Luna's black hole takes the Warlord's harpoon and sets it quietly in the sand. Plack disarms the Adapted
by feeding its plates its own pattern. Kuu, on the chain, takes the Marshal's whip in both hands and, trembling, does not let go.)*
WARLORD: (on the ground, laughing, winded) You *didn't.*
THE ADAPTED: (stunned) ...Again?
CHAIN MARSHAL: (the whip in Kuu's fist) Who *are* you people?
KUU: (very small) We're... we're so sorry.
*(Each champion falls without being killed. They are not pleased. They are not dead.)*

**Gameplay:** Three short champion fights, one per operator (a split-squad sequence).
**Beat:** Luna wins by refusing to kill.

## Episode 7 - "Surface"
**Scene:** The Docks, then the arc. **Present:** all three.

*(The three champions, bound, sitting on a quay. Out beyond the fog, the horizon is not a horizon. It is a line of white, the width of
the sea, getting wider.)*
WARLORD: (staring) What's that?
LUNA: (very quiet) The surface.
CHAIN MARSHAL: ...It's got *wings.*
THE ADAPTED: (a small, wondering, human voice) ...There are so many.
KUU: (hoarse) Luna.
LUNA: Yes.
KUU: They're not facing the arc any more.
LUNA: (looking at the wedge of white) ...They're facing *us.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the first wave of the angel host on the quay.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The truce is a minute old when the angel host arrives.

## Episode 8 - "The Warlord of the Docks"
**Scene:** The lighthouse stair. Boss fight. **Present:** all three + the champions.

*(Boss: the Warlord, now an ally in an unfinished fight: Phase 1, harpoon sweeps (the Warlord is aggressive, only half-convinced);
Phase 2, the angel host hits the quay and the champions fight alongside the squad.)*
WARLORD: (as the first angel lands) *Well.* I'll kill *that.*
LUNA: Please.
WARLORD: (swinging) And *then* we'll talk about the rest.
*(The truce is signed on the lighthouse stair, in pencil, on the back of a torn Charter page. It lasts two days.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: a brutal fight against the Warlord, then a joint defence.
**Beat:** The truce is signed.

**Stinger:** From the lighthouse gallery, Luna watches the beam sweep the surface, the first time in fifteen years it has found
anything. A line of white, moving, at walking pace, toward the arc. "Two days," she says. Plack: "Is that how long?" Luna: "It's how long
they *gave* us." Behind them, in the lamp room, a lens has been replaced. It is not the one she kept.
