# Ch 21 - Fixed Dose - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / SIDE STORY: Alchemist and Blood.** A greenhouse laboratory on the farm terraces. Chemistry meets the 1%. Giddy
science and a very bad afternoon.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A glass dome on a terrace, sunlight diffused to a honey-gold. Racks of plants, bubbling flasks, a tangle of tubes. A woman in goggles
standing on a stool, a pipette in each hand, humming. A very large person hauling crates behind her, not humming.
ALCHEMIST: (to a flask, tenderly) You're going to be *wonderful.*
BLOOD: (setting down a crate) Heavy.
ALCHEMIST: It's *glass.*
BLOOD: Heavy glass.

## Episode 1 - "You Are Going To Be Wonderful"
**Scene:** Greenhouse. **Present:** Alchemist (playable guest), Blood (guest).

ALCHEMIST: (peering at a vial of yellow dust) This is the one. This is the cube Luna sold us. A thirtieth of a gram is enough.
BLOOD: Enough for what?
ALCHEMIST: To *see.* (she tilts the vial; the dust glows) Look at that. Look at that *colour.*
BLOOD: ...Bright.
ALCHEMIST: It's the colour of *potential.*
BLOOD: It's the colour of a warning.
ALCHEMIST: Those are the same colour, Blood.

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Alchemist's kit (mix a compound); Blood's carry mechanic.
**Beat:** Introduce the lab, the sample, and the dynamic.

## Episode 2 - "Plant Creatures"
**Scene:** Terrace field. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

*(The greenhouse's overgrowth, drunk on the lab's runoff, has begun to walk. Vines on human legs.)*
ALCHEMIST: Oh, *they're* up.
BLOOD: Up.
ALCHEMIST: I told you not to pour the runoff on the beds.
BLOOD: Heavy. Had to.
ALCHEMIST: You could have poured it on a *different* bed.
BLOOD: (a very slight shrug) ...Same bed.
*(They are surrounded.)*

**Gameplay:** Terrace combat: plant creatures (RED); use runoff as a trap.
**Beat:** A comic opening skirmish.

## Episode 3 - "Test One"
**Scene:** The laboratory. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood, a volunteer (BLUE).

*(A volunteer in a hospital gown sits on a stool. A dose in a tiny syringe. A clock on the wall.)*
VOLUNTEER: Will it hurt?
ALCHEMIST: Only in the interesting ways.
BLOOD: (low) Alchemist.
ALCHEMIST: It's *fine.* It's one-and-a-quarter percent. A tiny, tiny, tiny increase. (she injects) There. How do you feel?
VOLUNTEER: ...Fine. (a blink) I feel *fine.* I want some tea.
ALCHEMIST: (beaming) *Tea!* Blood, a tea!
BLOOD: ...Tea.
*(He puts a kettle on, slowly, as if suspicious of it.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a lab scene; a minigame: titrate the dose.
**Beat:** The first test goes well.

## Episode 4 - "Test Two"
**Scene:** The laboratory, later. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

*(A second volunteer, another stool. The dose is a little higher.)*
ALCHEMIST: (writing) One-point-six. Rising. Slightly rising.
VOLUNTEER 2: I feel warm.
ALCHEMIST: Marvellous!
VOLUNTEER 2: I feel... very warm.
BLOOD: (very quietly) Alchemist.
ALCHEMIST: (not looking up) It's a *response.*
VOLUNTEER 2: I do not want tea.
*(The kettle, in the corner, begins to scream.)*
BLOOD: Stop.
ALCHEMIST: It's a *very* interesting --
BLOOD: *Stop.*

**Gameplay:** Short combat: the second volunteer starts to change; contain him.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Past the ceiling, operators don't improve; they become something else.

## Episode 5 - "Heavy"
**Scene:** The laboratory, containment. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

BLOOD: Sit down.
ALCHEMIST: I'm *thinking.*
BLOOD: Sit.
*(He lifts her, unceremoniously, and sets her on a crate. He is still holding the volunteer's hand through the glass.)*
BLOOD: (low) He was somebody.
ALCHEMIST: (very small) I know.
BLOOD: He wanted tea.
ALCHEMIST: ...I know.
*(The giddiness leaves her face like a lamp being turned down. She takes off her goggles.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE); a tonal low; a short evacuation sequence.
**Beat:** Alchemist stops being playful.

## Episode 6 - "The Ceiling Is A Number"
**Scene:** Reactor annex. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

*(A long room with a single plaque on the wall, behind dust. Alchemist wipes it with her sleeve.)*
ALCHEMIST: (reading) "Maximum safe angel content: 1 percent. Above this threshold the subject ceases to be an operator. Do not
exceed." (a beat) That's not a limit. That's a *wall.*
BLOOD: Who put it up?
ALCHEMIST: Whoever made us.
BLOOD: (slowly) ...So the one percent is not a *choice.*
ALCHEMIST: It's a ceiling. It's the *highest* an operator can be. (she looks at her hands) And it's the lowest a *thing* can be.
*(Blood puts his large hand on her shoulder. Neither says anything.)*

**Gameplay:** Exploration (reactor annex); plaque pickup (YELLOW).
**Beat:** The 1% is a hard limit by design.

## Episode 7 - "Overdose"
**Scene:** Reactor annex, containment hall. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

*(The glass wall of the containment chamber cracks, then goes. What steps out was once a person. It is bright, it is crying, it has
a thousand small yellow veins.)*
ALCHEMIST: (very quietly) That's... that's the second one.
BLOOD: Stay behind me.
ALCHEMIST: He asked for tea.
BLOOD: I know.
OVERDOSE: (a voice in several voices) ...tea...
*(Blood steps forward and takes the first blow on his forearm, planting his feet.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Overdose's first form (a flailing mass in a corridor); a defensive stand.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The mistake is walking.

## Episode 8 - "Overdose"
**Scene:** Reactor annex. Boss fight. **Present:** Alchemist, Blood.

*(Boss: Overdose. Phase 1, flail and crystal spikes; Phase 2, the veins glow and discharge; the room has a pressure vent.)*
ALCHEMIST: (mixing, hands steady now) I can reverse the compound. It's a neutraliser. Two minutes.
BLOOD: Two minutes.
ALCHEMIST: Keep him *busy.*
BLOOD: Heavy.
*(He does. For two minutes he is a wall.)*
ALCHEMIST: (throwing the vial) Now!
*(The compound hits. Overdose, for a moment, is a young man. He looks at his hands. He sits down.)*
OVERDOSE: (quietly) Is there... any tea?
BLOOD: (putting the kettle on the floor beside him) ...Tea.

**Gameplay:** Boss: Blood as a tank (the player defends Alchemist), neutraliser timer.
**Beat:** Overdose is subdued, not killed.

**Stinger:** In the evening Alchemist writes *1* on a label, sticks it on the plaque, and puts the project in a drawer. "We're not
doing that again," she says. Blood: "No." Alchemist: "I'm going to tell Luna it's not worth it." Blood, almost kind: "She knows." On the
terrace, the volunteer drinks his tea.
