# Ch 04 - The Hanging City - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A city hanging above an endless void on a single chain whose top cannot be seen. Four hanging districts,
plank bridges, lifts. Falling is death.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A cloud bank parts. Under it: a chain. A single chain, each link the size of a house, going up into white and down into black. At
the middle of it, hanging, a city: a thousand rooftops on cables, laundry strung between towers, lift cars crawling like beads.
KUU: I am going to stay here at the entrance.
LUNA: There is no entrance. We are hanging off the side of a chain.
KUU: Then I will stay on the chain.

## Episode 1 - "Please Hold The Rail"
**Scene:** Chain approach and rim. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. A rim guard (BLUE).

GUARD: Papers.
LUNA: We don't have papers.
GUARD: (delighted) Oh good! None of us do. Welcome. Do you want the tour or the *real* tour?
PLACK: What's the real tour?
GUARD: You don't look down and you don't ask the clock what time it is.
KUU: Why don't we ask the clock?
GUARD: (smiling) It's a very good clock.
LUNA: I like him.

**Gameplay:** Lift tutorial (lift cars as moving platforms), plank-bridge crossing, kill-Z fall warning.
**Beat:** Arrival. The first bridge is crossed without a fall; Kuu refuses to talk for a minute.

## Episode 2 - "Rota Captain"
**Scene:** North district. A broken lift. **Present:** all three. A lift clerk (BLUE).

CLERK: The north lift's been down since the spring. It's a very good lift. It just doesn't go.
PLACK: What is wrong with it?
CLERK: It needs a person who understands the rota.
PLACK: What rota?
CLERK: The one on the wall. Nobody's touched it in four years because nobody agrees on the order.
PLACK: (reading) It's alphabetical. It's alphabetical by *first name,* with a column for grievances.
CLERK: That's the one.
PLACK: (removing her jacket) Give me a pen.
LUNA: (to Kuu) She has found a purpose. Do not make eye contact.

**Gameplay:** Lift repair puzzle (reorder four gears by the rota); defend the workshop from drifting angels.
**Beat:** Plack fixes the lift and is elected rota captain by acclamation.

## Episode 3 - "Doing It Wrong"
**Scene:** East district, a gathering hall. **Present:** all three. Unregistered operators.

*(Luna is briefing a dozen residents on the war she has not declared yet. She is using a map and a pointer.)*
LUNA: And so we occupy the high ground, and the ground below it, and the ground below *that,* which I'm told does not exist, but
we occupy it conceptually.
RESIDENT: I don't have a gun.
LUNA: Excellent. Neither does the ground.
RESIDENT: (to Plack, quietly) She's doing it wrong, isn't she?
PLACK: Yes.
RESIDENT: Should we tell her?
PLACK: She knows. She enjoys it.
LUNA: (without turning) I can hear you.

**Gameplay:** Recruitment tutorial (a cutscene; talk to four residents for a short loyalty minigame, not a mechanic that is final).
**Beat:** Luna's first recruits. They are told, kindly, that they are not being used correctly.

## Episode 4 - "4:12"
**Scene:** South district, apartments. **Present:** all three. Child (BLUE).

CHILD: Are you the ones who're going to fix the chain?
KUU: What's wrong with the chain?
CHILD: Nothing. It's just very heavy.
PLACK: What time is it?
CHILD: (cheerfully) Four-twelve.
PLACK: Is that the time at home?
CHILD: It's the time everywhere. It's always four-twelve.
*(Every clock on the wall shows 4:12. Not broken: the second hands tick, all of them, in the same place.)*
CHILD: Mum says it's a very good time to be.
KUU: (very softly) Luna.
LUNA: I am looking at it. (to the child) Has anyone ever tried to change it?
CHILD: (blank, then bright) You're the first to *ask.*

**Gameplay:** Environmental: examine ten clocks (YELLOW); a drifting-angel patrol; a lift sequence.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Time has been stopped here on purpose. *(HINT: Timekeeper)*

## Episode 5 - "Narrating Your Own Death"
**Scene:** Chain links, the climb. **Present:** all three. Angels drifting down the chain.

KUU: (climbing) And then, as the chain swung left, our hero lost his grip on the fifth link --
PLACK: Kuu.
KUU: -- and plunged, with a small and unremarkable apology, into the dark --
LUNA: Kuu.
KUU: I'm *coping.*
PLACK: Cope quieter.
*(A swarm of chain drifters passes below, harmlessly, like jellyfish.)*
KUU: ...Oh. They're beautiful.
LUNA: They are enemies.
KUU: They are *beautiful* enemies.

**Gameplay:** Vertical combat: climb chain links; swing across; drifters as hazards; mini-boss: a Heavy drifter.
**Beat:** The squad climbs to the anchor approach.

## Episode 6 - "You Are Doing It Wrong"
**Scene:** The hanging market. **Present:** all three.

*(The recruits Luna won have asked her a question. They are sitting in a circle on the floor.)*
RESIDENT: What do we get?
LUNA: (blank) ...Get?
RESIDENT: If we help. What do we get.
LUNA: A (she searches) ... a better situation.
RESIDENT: That's not an answer.
LUNA: It is an answer with a *shape.*
PLACK: (gently) They want to know if you'll come back.
LUNA: (a long pause) I... will return.
RESIDENT: When?
LUNA: When it is time.
RESIDENT: (patiently, as to a child) That's also not an answer, but thank you for trying.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE). A series of side tasks (deliver a package, find a cat) that earn tokens.
**Beat:** Luna is asked, for the first time, to commit to something. She doesn't, quite.

## Episode 7 - "It Is Holding Us Up"
**Scene:** The chain anchor. **Present:** all three.

*(The Anchorite hangs at the anchor, an angel the size of a district, gripping the chain in both hands. It is not attacking. It is holding.)*
PLACK: It's not guarding the anchor.
KUU: Then what is it doing?
PLACK: Look at the chain. The slack. (she points) It's holding the city *up.*
LUNA: Then we kill it and the city falls.
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: I am *noting* it.
RESIDENT (comms): Something's wrong with the chain. The north lift is swaying -- the tower is --
KUU: The Anchorite is dying on its own. It's getting tired.
PLACK: Then we hold it up.
LUNA: (looks at the monstrous thing, at the city, at her two) ...How?

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Anchorite's hands (the player holds the chain slack by hitting weights; a timing puzzle).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The boss is not the enemy; the chain is.

## Episode 8 - "The Anchorite"
**Scene:** Chain anchor. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Anchorite. It will fall if it must; the squad tries to spare the chain. Phase 1, drop weights; Phase 2, hold the city while fighting.)*
LUNA: Weights on the left, Kuu!
KUU: I'm *afraid of the height,* not the *weight!*
PLACK: Don't let go of the chain!
LUNA: Don't let go of the *Anchorite*!
*(When the boss falls, the chain tightens, humming, and the city stops swaying. The Anchorite does not die; it sits, folding its wings.)*
RESIDENT (comms): The clocks. Luna, the clocks -- one of the clocks has moved.
KUU: Which one?
RESIDENT: ...Four-thirteen.

**Gameplay:** Boss: weight-drop mechanic, chain-hold; a clean fight with a non-lethal finish option.
**Beat:** The city is secured. One clock ticks.

**Stinger:** In the rota hall, Plack has pinned a new rota to the wall: *Captain: Plack. Deputy: Kuu (heights excused). Chain
watch: Luna (bored).* Luna reads it, signs it, and under "Bored" writes *Not*. Outside, one clock shows 4:13.
