# Ch 09 - Remnant Remains - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** An empty desert with violent winds and constant downpour. In the middle, a massive data centre hosting a
small screen. The verbatim text is kept exactly as the owner wrote it.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Rain on sand. A wind that has a shape. Three figures leaning into it, step by step, on a dune that goes on for ever. In the
distance, a pale block with four towers and a red light on top, blinking at the same rate as a heart.

## Episode 1 - "Wind Speaks Three Words"
**Scene:** Dune field. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: (shouting over the wind) Did it say something?
PLACK: It's the wind!
KUU: It said "stay."
PLACK: It said nothing!
LUNA: (flat, quiet, not shouting but heard) It said "stay" and "not" and "here."
KUU: "Stay not here?"
LUNA: That is what the wind says to people it likes. (she walks on) I've decided I will take it as a compliment.

**Gameplay:** Wind mechanics (push), rain pools in the hollows (swim), the data centre as a beacon.
**Beat:** The desert is hostile and sentient.

## Episode 2 - "Boot"
**Scene:** Dunes, a camp ruin. **Present:** all three.

KUU: I've lost a boot.
PLACK: How?
KUU: I put it down for a second.
PLACK: Where?
KUU: *There.* (he points to a patch of sand that is exactly like all of the others) It was right there.
*(Plack finds a sock, three metres away, in a different direction from where the boot would have been.)*
PLACK: This is a sock.
KUU: That's mine.
PLACK: It's a sock, Kuu. Not a boot.
KUU: It's *my* sock.
LUNA: (wading) The desert is trying to tell you something.
KUU: What?
LUNA: ...Wear socks.

**Gameplay:** Combat with sand enemies; hunt for items (YELLOW: the boot, found at the end).
**Beat:** A light moment before the hard one.

## Episode 3 - "Black Lines"
**Scene:** Cable trunks. **Present:** all three.

*(Eight black cables, each as thick as a tree, emerge from the sand and run toward the data centre. Plack presses a palm to one.)*
PLACK: It's warm.
KUU: Like the cube?
PLACK: Like something running.
LUNA: Follow them.
KUU: Why do we follow them?
LUNA: Because they're pointing.
PLACK: They don't point. They *connect.*
LUNA: Same thing. (she starts climbing along one) Everything that connects is pointing at the thing it connects to.

**Gameplay:** Climb along cable trunks; jump between fallen server racks; combat on a swaying cable.
**Beat:** The dune sheds its disguise; the centre is a network hub.

## Episode 4 - "It Has Been Waiting"
**Scene:** Data centre entrance. **Present:** all three.

*(The doors open. The corridors are warm, lit, tidy. A coffee mug is on a desk, steaming, though no one is there.)*
KUU: It's... clean.
PLACK: Someone is maintaining it.
LUNA: Or something.
PLACK: There's no dust. There's no *sand.* We came in from a desert and there's no sand on the floor.
KUU: Maybe it's blown out.
PLACK: Nothing blows out. This is a *sealed building.*
VOICE (a quiet hum, from every wall at once): ...
*(They look at each other. It has been waiting.)*

**Gameplay:** Indoor exploration, silent halls; a safe room with a save (GREEN).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The data centre is active and expects them.

## Episode 5 - "Teeth"
**Scene:** Cooling towers. **Present:** all three.

KUU: There's a sound.
PLACK: It's the vents.
KUU: I can feel it in my teeth.
LUNA: That is what a note does. It finds the nearest thing that is also a note.
KUU: I don't want to be a note.
LUNA: Too late. (she sets her jaw) I'm already a bar.
*(The vents breathe out a low, patient tone that lasts a long time and does not quite end.)*

**Gameplay:** Climb cooling towers; steam vents as hazards; mini-boss: a Cooling Warden.
**Beat:** The note gets under their skin.

## Episode 6 - "I Can Read It"
**Scene:** Server hall, the small screen. **Present:** all three.

*(In the middle of the hall, on a plain metal desk, a small old screen. Green text.)*
SCREEN: **Angel//V1 Success Line down Operator//V! Faillllllllllllllllll ...---... ////// .- -.--. . .-.. ... / .-- .. .-.. .-.. / -.- .. .-.. .-.. / ..- ... / .- .-.. .-..**
KUU: What does it say?
PLACK: The top part says "Angel V1 success. Line down. Operator V! fail."
LUNA: The bottom part is Morse.
PLACK: I can type it into the recorder from the school.
*(She does. The recorder produces a damaged string.)*
PLACK: It's corrupted.
LUNA: I can read it.
PLACK: You can't read it.
LUNA: (looks at the Morse, then away) I can read it, Plack.
KUU: What does it say?
LUNA: ...It says we should go.
*(She is lying. Plack knows. Neither says so.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the screen, verbatim text; a short interaction to record it.
**Beat:** The message is a threat, or a plea, or a warning. Luna conceals her uncertainty.

## Episode 7 - "Operator Failed"
**Scene:** Server hall, main floor. **Present:** all three.

*(The hall's lights flicker. Racks begin to move, joining. A thing assembles from fallen servers, wires for hair, indicator lights for eyes.)*
HALL LIGHTS: *(a sign, then another, then all of them)* OPERATOR FAILED. OPERATOR FAILED. OPERATOR FAILED.
KUU: Is that about *us?*
PLACK: It's about the project.
KUU: Oh. (a beat) Does that mean we're the project?
PLACK: (quietly) Fifty percent human --
LUNA: Stop that.
PLACK: -- forty-nine percent --
LUNA: I said *stop.*
*(The Uptime rises.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: a rack crawler; the environment reassembles around them.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The building sees them as the failure.

## Episode 8 - "The Uptime"
**Scene:** Server hall. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Uptime. Phase 1, rack limbs and cable lashes; Phase 2, the screen at its chest becomes a weak point; flood from the cooling towers.)*
UPTIME: (speaker voice) THE SYSTEM IS AVAILABLE. THE SYSTEM IS AVAILABLE.
LUNA: It has a *service level agreement.*
KUU: It's -- why is it *proud?*
PLACK: It's been up for seven years. It's proud of the uptime.
LUNA: Then we'll be the outage.
*(The Uptime collapses; every light in the building goes out, then one comes back on, at the back, quietly: the small screen.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: cut the cables to disable rack limbs; hit the chest screen; survive the rain-flood.
**Beat:** The Uptime falls. The hum does not stop.

**Stinger:** Outside, the desert is exactly the same. The wind says nothing. Kuu says his head hurts. The hum follows them across the
dunes for an hour, then two, then does not end.
