# Ch 10 - Hallucinations - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** The whole chapter is a hallucination: a nonsensical pastel dreamscape with a drawing look, juxtaposed by
hyper-realistic terrifying things appearing at random, with Morse code messages telling a small story. Verbatim lines kept.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A crayon sun. A hill the colour of strawberry milk. A chair as tall as a house. Three people waking, one by one, on three different
islands, each thinking they are the only one.

## Episode 1 - "Not Real"
**Scene:** Luna's island. **Present:** Luna.

LUNA: (sitting up, flat) Ah. We have been here before. Not here. *Near* here.
*(A pastel sky, flat as paper. Somewhere, an enormous clock ticks the wrong way.)*
LUNA: I know this is not real, and that does not help in the least. (she stands) Commander, if you can hear me, I'm fine.
*(A figure walks past in the distance: a woman in a raincoat, hyper-real, every pore, every raindrop, turning to look at her.)*
LUNA: ...I'm *fine.*
*(The figure smiles with too many teeth, and is gone.)*

**Gameplay:** Solo section: Luna alone; ability use (Blackhole) as the only defence; apparitions spawn at 24 points.
**Beat:** The squad is separated. The first apparition is a shock.

## Episode 2 - "The Waiting Room"
**Scene:** Luna's island (a waiting room under a pastel sky). **Present:** Luna.

*(Rows of orange chairs. A television that is off. A wall clock that has no hands. A number board that reads NOW SERVING: ---.)*
LUNA: (sitting) Is anyone coming?
*(No one. The television hums.)*
LUNA: I can wait. (a pause) I have *always* been able to wait. That's the problem. That's what they all say about me. "She can
wait for ever."
*(A paper ticket falls from a slot. It says: 1.)*
LUNA: (reading) One. Of one.
*(The room turns, gently, like a carousel, and sets her down on the stair to the next island.)*

**Gameplay:** Short non-combat (PURPLE): a puzzle: find the ticket slot that works.
**Beat:** Luna passes; the island leads to the stairs.

## Episode 3 - "The Street With One Shop"
**Scene:** Kuu's island. **Present:** Kuu.

*(A street. Pastel houses, a lamp, a shop. Every shop is the same shop.)*
SHOPKEEPER: (all of them, in unison) Welcome! What are you sorry for today?
KUU: I'm -- I'm not --
SHOPKEEPER: (all of them) It's all right. We have apologies in every size.
KUU: I don't want an apology.
SHOPKEEPER: (all of them) Then what are you doing in the street?
KUU: (very small) I'm trying to get out of it.
*(Apparitions cluster at the street's end: Luna's face, Plack's face, his own.)*

**Gameplay:** Solo section: Kuu; a street of identical shops, a loop puzzle (find the shop that's different).
**Beat:** Kuu finds the way out by apologising to nobody.

## Episode 4 - "Mercy"
**Scene:** The first Morse carving. **Present:** all three (they regroup).

*(On the floor of the central island, in raised dots and dashes under a pastel crust, a message.)*
PLACK: It's Morse.
LUNA: (reading it faster than she should) "THE ANGELS BRING MERCY FOR ALL."
KUU: That's nice.
LUNA: It *isn't.* Underneath.
*(A smaller line below it, scratched in a different hand: "Not those stupid pure ones you call angels.")*
PLACK: "Not those"?
LUNA: (very evenly) "Not those stupid pure ones you call angels."
KUU: ...What are the not-those?
LUNA: I don't know. (she steps back) I do not like being talked about by a floor.

**Gameplay:** Find the first Morse message; a puzzle: assemble its dots from drifting stair steps.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The hallucination is a message, and the sender is not the angels we know.

## Episode 5 - "The Wedding Cake"
**Scene:** Plack's island. **Present:** Plack.

*(A banquet table half a mile long. A cake, six tiers, the icing wet and red. The guests are chairs, each with a name card.)*
PLACK: (reading) "Luna." "Kuu." (the next one) "Plack." (the one after) "Plack." (the one after) "Plack."
*(She turns. Every chair says her name.)*
PLACK: ...Oh.
*(The cake's top tier has a pair of small figures. Their faces are teeth.)*
PLACK: (very quietly) I haven't asked her yet.
*(She picks up the knife, because it is there. The cake is made of something that breathes.)*

**Gameplay:** Solo section: Plack; a puzzle: the correct chair; a horror apparition chase.
**Beat:** Plack's private plan is exposed. It's tender and ghastly in the same breath.

## Episode 6 - "Impossible Stairs"
**Scene:** Floating stairs between islands. **Present:** all three.

KUU: These stairs go up.
PLACK: And down.
LUNA: And *sideways.*
KUU: Don't look at the third one.
PLACK: Why?
KUU: It's looking back.
*(Apparitions walk the stairs in both directions at once. Giant objects float past: a teacup big as a house, a clock that melts.)*
LUNA: This is very rude of it.
PLACK: Of what?
LUNA: Of the world. It is being unreasonable and *proud.*

**Gameplay:** Traverse moving stairs (movers); fight apparitions; the teacup as a platform.
**Beat:** The islands connect, imperfectly.

## Episode 7 - "The Mimic"
**Scene:** The last island. **Present:** all three. The Mimic.

*(Luna's own face walks across the grass, in her coat, with her hands in her pockets. It smiles a slightly off smile.)*
MIMIC (in Luna's voice): I don't know why I'm doing this.
LUNA: (very still) That's mine.
MIMIC: I don't know why I'm doing any of it. I only know that if I stop, someone will notice.
LUNA: That is *not --*
MIMIC: (to Plack, wearing Plack's face now) I want to know who pays.
PLACK: (hoarse) That's mine.
MIMIC: (as Kuu) I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. I'm so sorry.
KUU: ...That's *mine.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Mimic's reflections (three shadow copies, one per operator).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The Mimic says out loud what each of them is afraid of.

## Episode 8 - "The Four Lines"
**Scene:** The last island. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Mimic. It takes each of their faces in turn; to defeat it, all three must agree it is not one of them.)*
LUNA: It's not me.
PLACK: It's not me.
KUU: (a beat; the loudest he's been) It's not *me!*
*(The Mimic falls. Where it stood, carved into the grass, the third and fourth Morse lines.)*
LUNA: "But TRUE ANGELS THE ONE."
PLACK: "The rest are all filth compared to its existence."
KUU: ...There's only one?
LUNA: (looking up at the crayon sky) One of what, I wonder.
*(The hum, finally, stops.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: three-agree mechanic (each player operator must hit a different mirror); the sea rises and falls.
**Beat:** The hallucination ends. The message is complete.

**Stinger:** The sky tears like paper. Behind it, grey: a plain, silent grey with a very faint hiss.
