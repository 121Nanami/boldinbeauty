# Ch 14 - Declaration (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, opens Arc 2 (2010, the year after the wedding). Main cast.

Chapter promise: Luna finally says it out loud, and someone cuts the feed.

---

## Episode 1 - Draft Three

*Dawn, the roof of a safehouse above Neo Tokyo. Rain thins to mist. LUNA stands before a cracked mirror propped on a chimney, a crumpled page in each hand.*

**[Cold open. Intimate, quiet. First scene of Arc 2: no combat.]**

LUNA: *(to the mirror)* "People of the Ark. I come to you tonight with a heavy heart and a light schedule."

*She stops. Crumples the page.*

LUNA: Too jokey.

*Unfolds the next.*

LUNA: "Friends, citizens, operators. The time has come for a new era of..." *(she grimaces)* Era. Who says era?

PLACK: *(from the stairwell, a mug in each hand)* Commanders.

LUNA: *(without turning)* Exactly. Delete.

*PLACK sets a mug on the chimney ledge, leans on the wall.*

PLACK: You've been at this since four.

LUNA: *(still facing the mirror)* I'd like it to be good.

PLACK: It doesn't need to be good. It needs to be true.

LUNA: *(a long pause)* That's harder.

*KUU's head appears at the stairwell, tousled, a blanket over his shoulders.*

KUU: *(yawning)* Is it done?

LUNA: It's draft three.

KUU: Of how many?

LUNA: *(a beat)* Draft three is the one where I stop pretending I have a draft four.

**[Tutorial: a short rooftop walk-and-talk. The player may examine four props: the mirror, the three drafts, the wedding ring on a chain, Kuu's duck. Each gives a short lore line.]**

## Episode 2 - Visitor Stickers

*The base of the Broadcast Spire. A glass lobby ringed by turnstiles. A school tour is in progress: thirty children in matching yellow caps, a guide with a flag.*

GUIDE: *(brightly)* And on the left, boys and girls, the iconic Information Fountain!

*The trio fall in at the back of the line, hoods up.*

KUU: *(whispering)* Are we allowed to just... join them?

LUNA: *(whispering)* Nobody questions a line.

*A receptionist at the desk hands out stickers: a smiling antenna, VISITOR 4417, VISITOR 4418, VISITOR 4419.*

RECEPTIONIST: *(not looking up)* Enjoy your tour.

*PLACK sticks hers to her lapel with the grim efficiency of a soldier accepting a medal.*

PLACK: I hate how well this works.

LUNA: *(delighted, peeling hers off to put on KUU's forehead)* It suits you.

KUU: *(touching it)* I feel very official.

**[Stealth tutorial: avoid guards by staying inside the tour group. Break formation and guards inspect.]**

*A child at the back tugs PLACK's sleeve.*

CHILD: Are you with the school?

PLACK: *(solemnly)* Yes.

CHILD: *(squinting)* Which one?

PLACK: ...The one by the river.

CHILD: *(delighted)* That's my cousin's school!

*PLACK closes her eyes briefly.*

---

## Episode 3 - Quiz Show

*A studio floor, fourth level. A set in primary colours. A flashing sign: WHO WANTS TO BE A COMMANDER? A host in a sequined jacket, a studio audience of cardboard cut-outs.*

HOST: *(beaming)* And our next contestant... is *you*!

*A spotlight lands on KUU, who has been standing, badly camouflaged, beside the prize podium.*

KUU: *(frozen)* I... am?

*The cut-out audience claps, a recorded loop.*

HOST: Question one for a chance at this brand new toaster! What is the capital of the Ark?

KUU: *(very quietly)* It... doesn't have one.

HOST: *(a pause, then laughing)* A wit! Question two!

*Behind the set, LUNA and PLACK are picking the lock of a security door. A guard strolls down the corridor, humming.*

PLACK: *(whispering)* Is he getting the toaster?

LUNA: *(peeking)* He's answering the questions.

**[Mini-game: Kuu answers three trivia questions to stall the guards; the player chooses answers; wrong answers are funnier. Reward: Chrome Toaster.]**

*KUU finishes with a perfect score. The HOST hands over the toaster with a flourish. KUU holds it like a baby.*

KUU: *(whispering)* I won a toaster.

PLACK: *(from the door, under her breath)* Come *on*.

---

## Episode 4 - Primetime (Midpoint)

*The transmitter level. A long, white corridor lined with trophies. At its end, a pair of double doors. In front of them, four figures in studio-bright armour, capes bearing the wing logo. The PRIMETIME SQUAD.*

CAPTAIN: *(stepping forward, a handsome, tired face)* Operators. *(a faint, strange look)* I know you.

LUNA: *(innocently)* Do you?

CAPTAIN: I did the security detail at your wedding.

*A silence. The SQUAD relaxes, very slightly. A younger member, at the back, is staring at PLACK with something like awe.*

JUNIOR: *(whispering)* That's the bride.

CAPTAIN: *(ignoring him)* I'd like to say this is a courtesy. We've been told you're a threat to the broadcast. I don't see it. *(a pause)* But I have orders.

LUNA: *(gently)* So do I.

CAPTAIN: Whose?

LUNA: *(looking at him)* Mine.

*A long moment. The CAPTAIN looks away first.*

CAPTAIN: *(quietly)* Then we'll try to make it quick.

**[Gameplay: the fight begins as a three-section gauntlet; Primetime Squad members are mini-bosses with bright, showy attacks and glossy taunts.]**

---

## Episode 5 - The Mast

*A maintenance hatch leads up the outer wall of the spire. Wind howls. Rungs, rain, a twenty-storey drop. Above, the antenna mast, glittering with warning lights.*

**[Traversal level: vertical climb with wind gusts. Enemies: Antenna Sentries that patrol ledges. Plack rewires panels as the squad climbs.]**

PLACK: *(on a ledge, hands in a panel)* Bypass rack three. Pass me the red wire.

KUU: *(handing it up, one hand on a rung)* Is it dangerous?

PLACK: *(not looking)* Everything is.

LUNA: *(climbing past, reading from the page in her teeth)* "My fellow operators..."

PLACK: *(irritated)* Luna.

LUNA: *(muffled)* "...We have been told that the rain is natural."

PLACK: *(snapping a connector)* Luna, you can't read a speech while climbing.

LUNA: *(muffled)* I can, though.

*A gust shakes the mast. LUNA's page tears from her mouth and flutters away into the dark.*

LUNA: *(a pause)* ...Oh.

PLACK: *(very calmly)* Do you remember it?

LUNA: *(after a moment, a slow smile)* No. *(she reaches for the next rung)* I'll make it up.

## Episode 6 - Live

*The transmitter room. A cramped control booth, banks of switches. A single microphone on a stand, red light blinking. A countdown clock: 00:30.*

PLACK: *(at the console)* We're patched. Every channel. Every screen in the Ark.

KUU: *(guarding the door, toaster under one arm)* How many people?

PLACK: *(looking at the dial)* All of them.

*LUNA stands at the microphone. Her hands, for the first time all chapter, are not moving.*

LUNA: *(very quietly)* Plack.

PLACK: Yes.

LUNA: What if I say it wrong.

PLACK: *(coming to her side)* Then you say it wrong and you say it anyway.

*She takes LUNA's hand, kisses it once, and steps back.*

PLACK: *(softly)* Go.

*The red light goes green.*

LUNA: *(into the microphone, a long breath)* Hello. My name's Luna. I'm nobody important. I'm a courier's wife and a coward's best friend, and I have a message.

*PLACK, at the console, closes her eyes.*

LUNA: I'm going to say something and I'd like you to listen to the end. *(a beat)* The rain isn't natural. The angels aren't wandering. And every one of us has been counted since before we were born.

*On the monitors, Ark-wide, thousands of screens flicker. A crowd in a plaza stops.*

LUNA: I'm declaring war on the government that did it. Not for a seat. Not for a medal. Because someone has to be *rude* to the people who own the weather. And in the face of that rudeness, I'd like to ask each of you: *(she takes a breath)* what is it that you were told to be grateful for, and by whom?

*The feed cuts in the middle of "grateful". Not static: a clean, deliberate silence.*

---

## Episode 7 - Wounded

*The corridor, an alarm sounding. The PRIMETIME SQUAD pouring through the doors. KUU holds the line at the end of the passage, toaster for a shield.*

**[Low point. Defensive hold: the player must survive three waves while Plack and Luna evacuate.]**

KUU: *(shouting)* GO!

PLACK: *(dragging LUNA toward the emergency stair)* Kuu, come on!

KUU: *(parrying a baton with the toaster)* I'll catch up! Sorry! *(a second blow)* Sorry!

*A shot rings out. KUU stops. He looks down at his shoulder, where a small dark spot is spreading. He looks up with an expression of faint surprise.*

KUU: *(politely)* Oh. I think I've been shot.

*He sits down on the floor, still holding the toaster.*

LUNA: *(turning)* KUU!

KUU: *(waving a hand)* It's just a flesh wound. Sorry. It's really not... *(he sways)* ...that bad.

*He passes out. LUNA, white-faced, goes back for him. PLACK draws her own weapon, and for the first time in the chapter, does not look calm.*

PLACK: *(low, cold)* Nobody touches him.

---

## Episode 8 - Primetime Squad (Boss)

**[Boss: Primetime Squad. Phase 1: Captain, shielded, with sweeping spotlight attacks. Phase 2: the Junior joins, with fast dashes, protect the downed Kuu while fighting. Phase 3: the whole squad forms a formation; break it with a combined black/white hole.]**

*The fight is brutal, personal, short. LUNA fights with one hand, the other grips KUU's coat. PLACK holds the stairs, ledger-throws pinning attackers to the wall.*

*At the end, the CAPTAIN lies on the floor, his cape torn, looking up at the ceiling. The JUNIOR kneels next to him.*

CAPTAIN: *(coughing, almost laughing)* You've... done it now.

LUNA: *(standing over him, breathing hard)* Has the feed gone?

CAPTAIN: *(a long exhale)* It's on every screen in the arc. They'll cut it. They'll say you were a hoax. *(he looks at her)* It'll still have been seen.

*A silence.*

LUNA: *(very quietly)* Take care of your people.

CAPTAIN: *(a pause)* You too.

### Stinger

*The emergency stair, rain, dawn. The three descend, KUU leaning on PLACK, the toaster still in his good hand. On a building opposite, a giant screen shows a smiling anchor and a banner:*

*"BREAKING: THE GOVERNMENT CONDEMNS TERRORIST 'LUNA'. WAR CRIMINAL AT LARGE. ALL OPERATORS ASSISTING HER WILL BE ARRESTED."*

LUNA: *(looking up at it)* "Terrorist."

PLACK: *(dryly)* It's not a bad title.

LUNA: *(a faint, slow smile)* Not bad at all.

**[Chapter 14 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 15. Seeds: the declaration (2010); the cut feed (Media, Ch 15); Luna branded a war criminal; Kuu's wound; the Primetime Captain's sympathy.]**
