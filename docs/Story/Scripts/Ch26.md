# Ch 26 - Death's Door - Script

**Arc 2 / 2011 / NEW.** Main cast. The Mortuary Level: tiers of the dead since 2002, a hall of records at the bottom. Death is the last
Secret on Luna's list and the one that frightens her.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A stair that goes down for longer than it should. Walls of names. Lanterns the colour of old milk, one per hundred names. The sound of
three people walking, and one of them, loudly, talking to cover it.
LUNA: -- and the point of an *alliance* is that nobody needs to enjoy each other's company, which is why I'm so good at it --
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: -- because it's *structural* --
PLACK: Luna. It's a graveyard.
LUNA: (quietly) I'm *aware.*

## Episode 1 - "Names"
**Scene:** Upper tier. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

*(Plack stops at the first wall. She reads aloud, name by name. It is not a performance. She does it because someone should.)*
PLACK: Abe. Aizawa. Aoki. Arai. (she moves a hand along the stone) Aoyama.
KUU: Plack, there are... thousands.
PLACK: Then I'll be a while.
LUNA: (after a beat; not unkindly) We have all night.
PLACK: Thank you.
*(Kuu reads the next row, softly. Luna takes the one after. Nobody talks. The lanterns burn.)*

**Gameplay:** Quiet exploration; a few ghost-lanterns (non-combat); light enemies at the stair's end.
**Beat:** The squad treats the dead with care.

## Episode 2 - "Crypt Lanterns"
**Scene:** Crypt tier. **Present:** all three.

*(A hall of locked doors. Lanterns on stands; each must be lit in the order of the plaque.)*
KUU: (reading a plaque) "Light the one who was lost, then the one who was found, then the one who is waiting."
PLACK: Lost, found, waiting.
LUNA: Which is which?
PLACK: (looking along the lanterns) The first is cold. The second is warm. The third is... (she touches it) not there.
KUU: (very small) Waiting is *not there?*
PLACK: It's a lantern with no flame. It's *for* someone.
*(A grave walker, a shrouded figure with a lantern, steps out from behind a pillar.)*

**Gameplay:** Lantern-relight puzzle; grave walkers (RED).
**Beat:** The tier has a rhythm of loss.

## Episode 3 - "Talk Too Much"
**Scene:** The mortuary stair. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: -- and when I think about it, the whole *arrangement* of the dead is rather like a filing system, which is the closest thing to
a religion I've ever respected, and --
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: -- it's *perfectly* alphabetical --
KUU: (gently) You're talking a lot.
LUNA: (a flicker) I'm *aware.*
KUU: I'm not saying stop.
LUNA: Then what are you saying?
KUU: I'm saying I noticed. That's all.
LUNA: (after a moment; very quiet) ...Thank you.

**Gameplay:** Quiet scene (PURPLE); a short fight in the stairwell.
**Beat:** Luna's fear is visible; Kuu is a friend.

## Episode 4 - "Visited"
**Scene:** The hall of records. **Present:** all three. Death (BLUE).

*(A lectern, a ledger open to a page headed with three names in neat black ink. Beside each, a single word.)*
KUU: (reading) "Luna. Visited." (a swallow) "Plack. Visited." "Kuu. Visited."
PLACK: Visited where?
*(From the dark, a quiet, level voice. No hurry in it. No threat. No warmth. A voice that has counted a very great deal.)*
DEATH: Nowhere.
*(Luna goes completely still.)*
LUNA: ...Chapter eleven.
DEATH: Three. One minute and nine seconds for the first. One minute and nine seconds for the second. One minute and nine seconds for the
third. (a pause) You passed the frame in order.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); a reveal scene; the ledger as an interactive object.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Death counted them at the exit of Nowhere.

## Episode 5 - "Casualties"
**Scene:** Records hall. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: (finding her voice) We were looking for you.
DEATH: I know.
LUNA: I wanted to make you an offer.
DEATH: I know. You'll want to know a thing first.
PLACK: (low) What?
DEATH: The number. (the ledger turns itself to the last page; a line, one number, longer than a telephone number) Since the second of
June, 2002. Everyone. Not the number the government gave.
KUU: (a whisper) That's... eleven digits.
DEATH: Eleven. It was nine in the arc's account.
PLACK: (flat) They've been giving us nine.
DEATH: They have been giving you *less.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** The true casualty count is much larger than the government says.

## Episode 6 - "A Joke"
**Scene:** Records hall. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: (a long breath; the bored expression back, like armour) Right. I'd like to begin with a joke.
DEATH: Please.
LUNA: A Death walks into a --
*(She stops. Death does not laugh. Death does not frown. The hall does not answer.)*
LUNA: (quiet) ...That's the joke. It's not very good.
DEATH: It is not *meant* to be.
LUNA: (puzzled) What?
DEATH: It was something you said to be less afraid. That is the *best* kind.
*(Luna laughs, once, helplessly, then puts her hand over her mouth.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a tonal scene.
**Beat:** Death does not mock her fear; Luna's armour gives.

## Episode 7 - "A Count Is A Life"
**Scene:** Records hall. **Present:** all three.

DEATH: I will attend your unification.
LUNA: (blinking) ...You will?
DEATH: I have one condition.
LUNA: (carefully) Which is?
DEATH: Before it is done, I would like you to tell me one thing.
PLACK: What?
DEATH: Whether a count is a life.
*(A silence. The lanterns, all of them, lean slightly.)*
LUNA: ...I don't know.
DEATH: Think.
LUNA: I *am* thinking.
DEATH: (patient) You have time. Not much. But *time.* I will wait.
*(Behind them, the doors begin to open. A procession of pallbearers, carrying a coffin on their shoulders.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Pallbearers' vanguard (a procession arena).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The question cannot be answered; the guard comes to see them out.

## Episode 8 - "The Pallbearers"
**Scene:** The hall. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Pallbearers: six hooded bearers under a coffin. Phase 1, they march and slam; Phase 2, the coffin opens; whoever's name is called is pulled in.)*
KUU: They're calling names!
PLACK: *(listening)* "Aoyama." "Hayashi." (she stops) ...They're calling *ours.*
LUNA: (flat) Then answer.
KUU: What?
LUNA: *Present.* (she throws the black hole at the coffin lid) Say it!
*(Three voices, in unison: "Present." The lid slams. The Pallbearers set the coffin down and bow.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: respond to name-calls with a timed interact; the coffin as a weak point.
**Beat:** The Pallbearers stand down; the squad leaves.

**Stinger:** On the stair up, Kuu says, "He didn't want an answer." Plack: "They." Kuu: "They." Luna, climbing, one hand on the wall:
"They want it to be *true.*" She stops. "I don't know what I think. That's the problem." At the top, the door shuts quietly behind them.
