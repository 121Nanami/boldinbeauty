# Ch 36 - Unity - Script

**Arc 2 climax / Arc 3 opening / 2016.** Main cast. The Great Hall of the arc. The Charter is signed. Then Death and Angel put their pens down.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 60 s)
Dawn in the Great Hall: a vast marble room under a ceiling of painted stars, the galleries full, the floor a sea of uniforms and
overalls and motley and cardigans. A dais. A table. A document. A single pen on a stand. Silence. Then, one by one, the signatories
walk in.
*(We see: Ishii, Shiraishi and Arita in taped armour. Media with a bowl. Judge, immaculate. The reformers, sweating. The Jester, in
a clean tunic, juggling nothing. The Timekeeper, checking a watch that has stopped. Adapt in a visor. Narrator, composed. A small figure
in a plain dark coat. A tall man in white with a ring of light behind his head.)*
KUU: (to Plack, very low) They *came.*
PLACK: They came.

## Episode 1 - "The Hall Fills"
**Scene:** Great Hall. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu, and the signatories (BLUE).

LUNA: (at the foot of the dais, a speech in her fist) I wrote an opening address.
PLACK: Good.
LUNA: It's four pages.
PLACK: (gently) Luna.
LUNA: It's *very* good. It has a part about the *horizon.*
PLACK: Cut the horizon.
LUNA: (a tragic breath) ...Fine.
*(She rips off a page; then another; then three more.)*
LUNA: It's one sentence now.
KUU: What is it?
LUNA: "I would like to thank *everyone* for being here when they would rather not be." (a beat) It's the only one that's true.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat hub: speak to the signatories (BLUE) for a last line each; a final tutorial.
**Beat:** The room fills.

## Episode 2 - "The Address"
**Scene:** The dais. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

*(Luna, at a lectern. A hall of five thousand faces. She breathes.)*
LUNA: Persons of the arc. Whoever is listening. This is Luna. (a long, slightly crooked pause) I would like to thank everyone for being
here when they would rather not be.
*(A ripple of laughter, nervous, then real.)*
LUNA: That's all. (she steps back; to Plack, a whisper) That's *all.*
PLACK: (smiling, gripping her hand) It's perfect.
LUNA: It's one *sentence.*
PLACK: It's the right one.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** The address, kept to a sentence.

## Episode 3 - "The First Pen"
**Scene:** The signature table. **Present:** all three.

*(Judge lifts the pen. She signs. The sound is a single scratch, loud as a gavel. Around the hall, cameras, fewer than before, hold.)*
JUDGE: (blotting) It is *done,* as to me.
*(The first pen touches paper, and with it, from a gallery, a single sharp sound: a latch.)*
PLACK: (looking up) What was that?
KUU: (hoarse) The gallery.
*(Along the east gallery, a row of soldiers in sponsor grey, a grey commander at their head: Commander Prime.)*
COMMANDER PRIME: (not loud; a voice that carries) The Council does not recognise this assembly.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat → combat: the coup fires; a short cutscene transitions to the hold.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The hardliners strike as the first signature dries.

## Episode 4 - "Hold the Hall"
**Scene:** The Great Hall. **Present:** all three.

*(Soldiers pour in from three doors. The signatories scatter to the walls; the table must be held; the signatures must be completed.)*
LUNA: Ishii! Table!
ISHII: (counting) Table. Forty seconds.
LUNA: Shiraishi! Item!
SHIRAISHI: (shield-bashing) Item four hundred and one!
LUNA: Arita!
ARITA: (taping a door shut) *Persuasive!*
KUU: (shoving a guard off the dais with a toaster) Sorry!
PLACK: (low) The pen's still on the table.
LUNA: Then someone's going to *sign.*

**Gameplay:** Hold-the-hall waves; signature checkpoints (GREEN): each signatory approaches the table in turn; protect them.
**Beat:** Waves hold the room while each signature goes down.

## Episode 5 - "The Mech"
**Scene:** The hall's great doors. **Present:** all three.

*(The doors, forty feet high, bulge inward. Something in the street beyond is pushing. They give.)*
KUU: (very small) That's a... that's a *very big* --
PLACK: War mech.
LUNA: (a flat, delighted breath) Of *course* it is.
COMMANDER PRIME: (from within it, a voice made of steel) The Council *dissolves* this assembly.
LUNA: (quietly, to Plack) Can we hold?
PLACK: We can *delay.*
LUNA: (a small, bright nod) That's all I want.

**Gameplay:** Set-piece: the war mech enters; the arena shrinks; the squad defends the table.
**Beat:** The mech arrives.

## Episode 6 - "Plack Takes A Hit"
**Scene:** The dais. **Present:** all three.

*(A mortar shell, a glint, a blast. Plack is thrown against the table. She stands, bleeding at the hairline, and does not stop walking.)*
LUNA: (a cry) Plack!
PLACK: (hoarse, steady) I'm *fine.* I'm walking.
LUNA: You're *bleeding.*
PLACK: (reaching the table; picking up the pen) It's a flesh wound.
KUU: (a stricken half-laugh) That's *my* line.
PLACK: (signing, then handing the pen to the next hand) It's a good line.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE) short; a scripted wound; the squad's health lowers for the next fight.
**Beat:** **Low point.** Plack takes a hit meant for Luna and keeps walking.

## Episode 7 - "The Last Signature"
**Scene:** The signature table. **Present:** all three. Boss: Commander Prime (mech).

*(Boss: Commander Prime in the war mech. Phase 1, cannons and sweeps; Phase 2, the mech sheds armour and charges the table.)*
LUNA: Kuu! The *left* leg!
KUU: The *left?*
LUNA: Your *left,* Kuu!
KUU: (hitting the joint, a heroic wail) *I'm sorry!*
*(The mech staggers. Plack, bleeding, hits the cockpit seam with a wire-cutter; Luna's black hole takes the cannon arm.)*
COMMANDER PRIME: (as the mech falls) *The Charter... is ... un --*
LUNA: (flat) It's *signed.*
*(The last signature is a mark: a small black circle ringed with glyphs. A tall man in white, with a pen that does not touch the paper.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: shed armour phases; table defence; last signature.
**Beat:** The Charter is signed.

## Episode 8 - "Unity"
**Scene:** The Great Hall. **Present:** all.

*(One minute. A hush. A hall where everyone, for the first time in the history of the arc, is on one side. A small cough. A baby laughs.
Someone starts to clap. Then the whole hall.)*
LUNA: (to Plack, her face wet) We did the thing.
PLACK: (laughing, blood on her cheek) We did the thing.
KUU: (to the toaster) We did the thing.
*(At the dais, at the end of the long table, two figures put down their pens. A small figure in a plain dark coat. A tall man in white.)*
DEATH: (quiet, carrying to every corner of the room, no louder than a sentence) Humanity is unworthy of life.
*(The applause dies like a candle in a draught.)*
ANGEL: (gentle, serene, a statement of weather) As are the Operators. As are the other Secrets. The angels shall be the dominant species.
*(Silence. The great hall holds its breath.)*
LUNA: (a long moment; her voice, very small, very bright) ...I'm afraid I didn't hear that.
*(Nobody believes her. The war begins.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the declaration scene.
**Beat:** The war begins.
