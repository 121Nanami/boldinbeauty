# Ch 18 - Backend Deals (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Core arc beat: Luna acts on one of Plack's questions for the first time.

Chapter promise: a spy, a sick sibling, and the exchange rate of a favour.

---

## Episode 1 - Nine Coins

*The squad's new base: a disused tram depot in the Underworld. Bunks, a stove, a map of the arc pinned to a wall with a hundred coloured threads. KUU sits at a table with a ledger and a very small cup of tea.*

**[Cold open. Quiet. Kuu's arm is out of the sling, stiff but healing.]**

KUU: *(counting aloud)* ...eight, nine. Nine coins missing.

PLACK: *(from the stove)* From what?

KUU: From the float. We should have forty. I count thirty-one.

PLACK: Recount.

KUU: I did. *(a pause)* Three times. *(a beat)* It's nine. I'm sure. Or ten.

PLACK: Nine or ten?

KUU: *(rubbing his eyes)* I counted ten once and nine twice. One of them's wrong.

*LUNA enters, shaking off a wet coat, a paper bag of fried bread under her arm.*

LUNA: Who's missing nine?

KUU: Coins.

LUNA: *(crunching a piece of bread)* Someone's skimming.

PLACK: *(very carefully)* That's one possibility.

LUNA: What's the other?

PLACK: That we're being paid late.

*A silence. LUNA stops chewing.*

LUNA: I pay on time.

PLACK: I know you do.

**[Tutorial: ledger mechanic. The player balances an accounts screen; mismatches highlight leaks.]**

## Episode 2 - The Exchange

*The Exchange, a vast underground bazaar under a vaulted roof of riveted iron. Stalls in tiers, hawkers, ledger-boys on rolling ladders. A slow river of people, every face wearing the same polite smile.*

KUU: *(whispering)* They're all smiling.

PLACK: *(low)* It's a trade face.

LUNA: *(thoughtfully)* It's the nicest anyone has ever been to us.

PLACK: Which means somebody's paying.

*A stall-holder, a stout woman in a leather apron, waves them over, scales gleaming in front of her.*

STALL-HOLDER: Fresh intel, hot rumours, favours at a discount.

LUNA: *(sweetly)* How much for a favour?

STALL-HOLDER: Depends who's owing.

LUNA: Me.

STALL-HOLDER: *(a long look)* For you? Cheap. A favour from you is worth very little.

*LUNA blinks.*

STALL-HOLDER: *(not unkindly)* You pay on time. You're known for it. It makes your word good, but it makes your credit weak. *(a shrug)* Nobody owes you.

*LUNA, for the first time in a long while, is lost for a reply.*

**[Hub level: the Exchange has six stalls; each trades information for favours or items. The player learns the exchange-rate system.]**

## Episode 3 - Ledger Vault

*A vault at the Exchange's heart, behind a gate of bronze. A watcher in a black coat stands at the door. LUNA, in a borrowed clerk's robe, approaches with a basket of fried bread and a smile.*

LUNA: *(to the watcher)* Delivery. For the head bookkeeper.

WATCHER: *(unmoved)* Nobody told me.

LUNA: *(sweetly)* That's why it's a *delivery*.

*A pause. The WATCHER, with a slow blink, lifts the gate.*

*Inside, shelves of ledgers rise to the ceiling in tiers, each bound in a different colour. A clerk at a rolling desk glances up, bored.*

CLERK: *(not stopping)* Third aisle. Don't touch the red ones.

**[Stealth level: the player must navigate the vault, avoiding clerks and automated ledger-drones, to find a specific book: "Payments, outbound, 2010".]**

*In the third aisle, PLACK is waiting, a ledger in her hands, a pencil behind her ear.*

PLACK: *(whispering)* It's here. *(she turns a page)* Look.

*A column of figures, each line a payment made to LUNA's recruits. Beside each, a second payment, smaller, regular, to the same names, from a different account.*

PLACK: Somebody's paying our people. Twice.

LUNA: *(very quietly)* Who?

PLACK: *(following the line to the header)* A "medical contract".

## Episode 4 - The Spy (Midpoint)

*A rooftop above the Exchange. A young operator, the Spy Recruit, sits on a crate, a ledger in his lap, eating bread. Behind him, the roof's edge, and a long drop. He sees LUNA, PLACK, KUU in a half-circle around him. He does not run.*

SPY RECRUIT: *(quietly)* I wondered how long it would take.

LUNA: *(her voice level)* A while. You were good.

SPY RECRUIT: *(a thin smile)* I had practice.

KUU: *(very gently)* Why did you do it?

*A silence. The SPY RECRUIT closes the ledger.*

SPY RECRUIT: I have a sister. She's eight. She has a sickness in her blood. There's a medicine. It costs more than you pay me in a year.

*LUNA doesn't move.*

SPY RECRUIT: The government offered me a contract. A small one. A few reports a week. In return, a physician. A bed. *(a breath)* I'd have done it for free, to be honest. But they paid.

*PLACK glances at LUNA. LUNA's hands, in her coat pockets, are tight.*

LUNA: *(after a very long pause)* How much?

SPY RECRUIT: *(startled)* What?

LUNA: The medicine. How much.

*The SPY RECRUIT names a figure. It is large.*

*LUNA reaches into her coat, takes out a small, battered chequebook, a relic she has been carrying since the wedding, and writes a cheque. She tears it off, and holds it out.*

LUNA: *(flat)* A business deal.

SPY RECRUIT: *(staring)* ...What?

LUNA: You keep your job. You keep it with me. You report to me. In return, your sister gets her medicine. *(a pause)* It's a business deal. Nothing more.

*PLACK turns her head away, and when she looks back, she is looking at LUNA with a faint, unmistakable softness.*

**[Gameplay beat: Luna's first unprompted act of mercy. A small cutscene. The cheque becomes an inventory item that the player sees reappear in later chapters.]**

## Episode 5 - The Broker's Men

*The Exchange floor, an hour later. A sudden quiet. Stall-holders pull down shutters. A group of enforcers in grey overcoats walks down the central aisle, scattering the crowd, a tall figure in the middle, in a good coat, a monocle, an abacus on a chain.*

KUU: *(whispering)* Who's that?

PLACK: *(low)* The Broker.

*The BROKER strolls up to the squad, polite as a priest.*

BROKER: Operator Luna. I've been hoping we'd meet.

LUNA: *(stiffly)* You have the advantage.

BROKER: *(smiling)* I have most of them. *(he glances at the SPY RECRUIT, half-hidden behind KUU)* And what's this? One of mine?

LUNA: *(stepping forward)* He's under my protection.

BROKER: *(a delicate eyebrow)* Is he? *(he sighs)* Then I'll have to ask for his contract back.

*A beat. His enforcers spread, fanning across the aisle.*

BROKER: *(gently)* It's a pity. I rather liked you.

**[Combat: Exchange floor fight. Stalls collapse, coins scatter, ledger-boys flee on ladders. Chaos mechanics: stalls can be knocked into enemies.]**

## Episode 6 - The Price of a Favour

*The aftermath. The Exchange floor is a ruin of splintered stalls and scattered coins. Stall-holders emerge, picking through debris. LUNA, breathing hard, wipes a smear of blood from her lip.*

*PLACK, beside her, has a gash along her brow. KUU is carrying the SPY RECRUIT's ledger like a baby.*

STALL-HOLDER: *(emerging from under a collapsed awning)* That was the best entertainment we've had since the Fire.

LUNA: *(a faint laugh)* I'm glad.

STALL-HOLDER: *(offering a flask)* It'll cost you.

LUNA: *(taking it)* Of course.

STALL-HOLDER: *(smiling)* On the house.

*LUNA drinks. She looks at the SPY RECRUIT, who is sitting against a wall, white-faced, being tended to by KUU.*

LUNA: *(quietly, to the STALL-HOLDER)* Tell me something.

STALL-HOLDER: *(sitting down beside her, creaking)* If I can.

LUNA: How much is a favour worth?

*The STALL-HOLDER considers this for a long time.*

STALL-HOLDER: *(finally)* A person.

LUNA: *(blinking)* A person?

STALL-HOLDER: One favour is worth one person. Exactly one. Not a credit more, not a credit less. That's the rate. It's the oldest rate there is.

*LUNA stares at the flask in her hand.*

LUNA: *(slowly)* So when I paid him...

STALL-HOLDER: *(gently)* You bought a person.

LUNA: *(very quietly)* I didn't want to buy a person.

STALL-HOLDER: *(kindly)* Nobody does. But it's still the only currency that doesn't devalue.

## Episode 7 - The Broker's Terms

*The Exchange's upper gallery, a private office with a view of the floor. The BROKER, at a rosewood desk, hands folded. LUNA sits across from him, alone.*

BROKER: *(pouring tea)* You surprise me.

LUNA: *(flatly)* Do I?

BROKER: Most operators in your position treat their people as expenses. *(he sets down the pot)* You're treating yours as liabilities.

LUNA: Is that worse?

BROKER: *(mildly)* It's more expensive. *(he lifts his cup)* But it's more loyal.

*LUNA doesn't touch her tea.*

BROKER: I'll make you an offer. A partnership. You supply the people. I supply the funds. We split the margin.

LUNA: *(very still)* What margin?

BROKER: *(smiling)* On the war.

*A silence. Below, the sound of stalls being rebuilt.*

LUNA: *(slowly)* You're selling both sides.

BROKER: I sell what's wanted.

LUNA: *(standing)* And what do I want?

BROKER: *(lightly)* That's the question, isn't it.

*She does not answer. She turns, and her hand, in her pocket, finds the notebook. She turns, and walks out.*

BROKER: *(to her back, calm)* The offer stands.

LUNA: *(without stopping)* So does the table.

## Episode 8 - The Broker (Boss)

**[Boss: The Broker. Arena: the Exchange floor, collapsed and burning. Phase 1: abacus-beads as projectiles; ledger-drones as shields. Phase 2: the Broker "buys" nearby enemies, turning them to his side; the player must "outbid" by clearing them. Phase 3: he unfolds a many-armed accounting frame; break the frame's counting beads.]**

BROKER: *(from the gallery, with a sigh)* You could have been so rich.

LUNA: *(from the floor, cheerful, blood on her teeth)* I'd rather be on time.

*The fight is a ballet of coins and collapsing stalls. PLACK uses ledger-throws to pin his abacus-chain. KUU, unexpectedly, hauls the SPY RECRUIT's ledger like a shield and bashes a drone with it.*

*At the end, the BROKER sits on the shattered gallery rail, his monocle cracked, his coat torn.*

BROKER: *(mild, a little breathless)* You've disrupted a very delicate market.

LUNA: *(looking up at him)* Good.

BROKER: *(smiling faintly)* You'll lose, you know. Eventually. Everyone does. It's only a question of the exchange rate.

LUNA: *(quietly)* Then I'll make sure it's in people.

### Stinger

*Night, the Exchange. The stalls are closing. LUNA sits alone on an upturned crate, the stall-holder's flask in her hand. The SPY RECRUIT's cheque, torn in half, is pinned under a coin on the counter, his name on both pieces.*

*The STALL-HOLDER brings her a second cup, and sits.*

STALL-HOLDER: *(gruff)* Still thinking about the rate?

LUNA: *(looking at the coin)* It's a terrible rate.

STALL-HOLDER: *(quietly)* It's the only honest one.

*A long silence. LUNA takes out the notebook. Under "WHAT WAS HE BEING PAID?", she writes, in small letters: "A sister. Eight."*

*She closes the notebook, and for the first time, she does not put it away. She holds it, in both hands, in her lap.*
