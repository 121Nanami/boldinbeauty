# Ch 29 - Sponsor Night - Script

**Arc 2 / 2012 / SIDE STORY: Myrcia and Fujita.** Two three-star operators hired as "talent" for a sponsors' gala in Paradise. The
gala is built around an incident. A rescue that does not happen.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A convention hall's loading dock, 6 p.m. Two figures in dress uniforms that do not fit, each holding a plate of canapés they cannot
afford to put down. A wall of lights; a rig of cables running under the stage; faint, from underneath, a sound like a very large
animal breathing.
MYRCIA: (politely, to the canapé) Excuse me.
FUJITA: (not looking up, tilting her head at the stage) Hear that?

## Episode 1 - "Talent"
**Scene:** Backstage. **Present:** Myrcia (playable guest), Fujita (guest).

MYRCIA: We're meant to stand near the champagne.
FUJITA: And smile.
MYRCIA: And be *seen.*
FUJITA: By whom?
MYRCIA: By *the cameras.* (she lowers her voice) Fujita, I am very sorry, but I think I'm not very good at this.
FUJITA: At what?
MYRCIA: At being *decorative.*
FUJITA: (grinning) That's the best qualification.
*(A producer in a headset passes with a clipboard and a face like a stopped clock.)*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Myrcia's kit (polite stun), Fujita's (lockpick); a backstage hub.
**Beat:** Introduce the pair and the unease.

## Episode 2 - "The Rig"
**Scene:** Under the stage. **Present:** both.

*(Beams, wires, sandbags. A long low hum. Fujita lifts a floor-panel and puts her hand on cold steel.)*
FUJITA: That's not scenery.
MYRCIA: It's a *rig.*
FUJITA: It's a rig for a *thing.* (she raps the steel; something inside answers) A *large* thing.
MYRCIA: (very faint) ...Oh dear.
FUJITA: Don't say "oh dear." That's what a person says right before the thing.
MYRCIA: I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I'll say "well."

**Gameplay:** Under-stage exploration; light combat (cage handlers).
**Beat:** The stage is built over a cage.

## Episode 3 - "The Cage Vault"
**Scene:** The cage vault. **Present:** both.

*(Row after row of cages, each holding a crouched angel, dim, shackled at the wings.)*
MYRCIA: (hand to her mouth) They're... they're *angels.*
FUJITA: Twenty-six.
MYRCIA: Why are they --
FUJITA: Look at the tags.
*(Each cage has a paper tag with a number and the name of a factory.)*
MYRCIA: (reading) "Line Four. Batch eleven."
FUJITA: These are *the same angels.* (a whisper) From the tunnel.
MYRCIA: How do you know?
FUJITA: (touching a cage) They're holding something. Look at their hands.
*(A faint glow in each cell: a shard, like a candle. They're still carrying them.)*

**Gameplay:** Vault exploration; lock puzzles (timing); caged angels as non-hostile NPCs (neutral).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The captive angels are the same as those in the Intersection.

## Episode 4 - "The Plan For The Evening"
**Scene:** The producer's booth. **Present:** both.

*(They listen at a vent. The Showrunner's voice, warm as a bath, speaking to a headset.)*
SHOWRUNNER: At nine o'clock, lights up, cages open. Three angels, medium aggression. Hero squad enters from stage left. It's a rescue.
A short one. Seven minutes. We have the sponsors' logos on the angels' chests, for the *close-ups.*
MYRCIA: (very small) They're going to *release* them. Into the crowd.
FUJITA: On live television.
MYRCIA: So their own heroes can *rescue* them.
FUJITA: (flat) A staged attack.
MYRCIA: It's... it's an *incident.*
FUJITA: It's an *advert.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the booth scene; sneak.
**Beat:** The gala is built around a staged angel attack.

## Episode 5 - "Quietly Breaking Locks"
**Scene:** The cage vault. **Present:** both.

*(Fujita, on her knees, a lock-roll open. Myrcia, behind her, with a handful of canapés and an expression of terrible politeness, facing three
guards.)*
GUARD: Ma'am, this area is restricted.
MYRCIA: (sweetly) I do beg your pardon. I seem to have got turned around. I'm *terribly* sorry. Could you tell me where the lavatories are?
GUARD: Ma'am --
MYRCIA: Because I don't want to be any trouble, but I've had *quite* a lot of champagne, and --
*(She is *lying beautifully.* Behind her, a lock clicks.)*
FUJITA: (under her breath) One.
MYRCIA: (still talking, on a slightly higher pitch) -- and I think it's the *vol-au-vents.*
FUJITA: (a click) Two.

**Gameplay:** Stealth: Fujita picks locks while Myrcia distracts guards (the player toggles between them).
**Beat:** The pair free the cages quietly.

## Episode 6 - "Applause"
**Scene:** The stage. **Present:** both.

*(Lights up. The curtain draws back on a stage full of heroes in sponsor colours, a rig of cables... and empty cages.)*
SHOWRUNNER: (a hair's breadth off script) And now... a rescue! (a smile; a glance at the wings) ...A *rescue!*
*(The audience claps. They are applauding an empty stage.)*
FUJITA: (in the wings, to Myrcia, delighted) They're *applauding.*
MYRCIA: (hoarse) They think it's the *show.*
FUJITA: It's *a* show.
*(The Showrunner, off-mic, white, makes a small sharp gesture toward the wings.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the stage scene.
**Beat:** The audience applauds a rescue that never happens.

## Episode 7 - "A Contract"
**Scene:** The producer's booth. **Present:** both.

SHOWRUNNER: (catching them by the wrists; smiling) Ladies. A word.
MYRCIA: (a small, terrified squeak) We're so sorry --
SHOWRUNNER: Don't be. (he slides a contract across a console) It's a very good *offer.* Two years, exclusive. A salary. A flat. A
*name.*
FUJITA: (flat) What would we do?
SHOWRUNNER: Exactly what you did tonight.
MYRCIA: ...Sorry?
SHOWRUNNER: *Be decorative.* (a sunny, dreadful smile) You were *wonderful.*
*(Fujita looks at the contract, at him, at the glowing, dim cages below.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Showrunner's cameramen (a booth-to-stage chase).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The producer offers them the thing they've never had.

## Episode 8 - "The Showrunner"
**Scene:** The stage. Boss fight. **Present:** both.

*(Boss: the Showrunner: a producer in a headset with six floating cameras. Phase 1, spotlights and clapper arms; Phase 2, he calls "cut" and the stage resets.)*
SHOWRUNNER: And... *action!*
MYRCIA: (taking the contract, folding it neatly) I'm afraid we must decline.
SHOWRUNNER: Cut! (the stage resets; the floor reconfigures)
FUJITA: Again with the *cut.*
MYRCIA: (drawing a very small, very polite breath) I'm *so* sorry.
*(She takes his headset in a single, graceful, devastating gesture, and speaks into it:)*
MYRCIA: Ladies and gentlemen, there will be no rescue this evening, because nobody was in danger. The cages were opened and the
angels *walked out.* Thank you for coming.
*(A silence. Then, from a hundred screens, a sound like the sea.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge spotlights; take the headset; broadcast the truth (a timed interaction).
**Beat:** The Showrunner falls. The gala collapses, and the sponsor's ratings fall with it.

**Stinger:** Packing up, Fujita finds a cage tag in her pocket. A number, and the name of a factory. She reads it twice, and
passes it to Myrcia, who reads it three times. "That's the factory," Myrcia says, quietly. "A.R.C TECH," says Fujita. "It's running
again."
