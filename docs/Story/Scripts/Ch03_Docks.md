# Ch 03 - Docks (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Map `L_Ch03_Docks` is LOCKED and untouched. Owner brief: semi-ruined area by the sea, waves, heavy fog, the only structure is a lighthouse.

Chapter promise: the angels face west because something west of them is holding them there.

---

## Episode 1 - Fog

*Dawn that never quite arrives. A broken quay, planks sagging into black water. Waves slap pilings. Fog so thick the lighthouse is a pale smear on the horizon.*

**[Cold open. Walk along the quay in near-zero visibility. Footsteps and wave sound; no music.]**

KUU: *(a dull thump)* Sorry! Sorry, bollard.

PLACK: You apologised to a bollard.

KUU: It was in my way. It's rude to hit things and not say.

LUNA: *(from three steps ahead, invisible)* I can hear you, but I can't see you. This is the closest I've ever felt to being a podcast.

COMMANDER: *(radio, crackling)* Visibility is under four metres. Stay together. Hold position if you lose sight of each other.

LUNA: Commander, how many metres is "within shouting distance"?

COMMANDER: ...Four.

LUNA: Good. Then I'll shout.

*She doesn't. She hums.*

*PLACK finds a mooring ring and ties a rope between herself and KUU.*

PLACK: Tie yourself to Luna.

KUU: *(tying, with a perfect bowline)* There.

PLACK: *(tugging)* That's actually good.

KUU: Thank you. It's not my fault I'm good at knots.

**[Tutorial: fog mechanic. Enemies appear only within 6 m. A ringing sound on the left means an angel is near.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Boats That Aren't

*A boatyard. Hulls on stilts, rusted skeletons of fishing boats, a crane arm sunk into the water. Luna is convinced a boat exists.*

LUNA: There's a boat.

PLACK: There is no boat.

LUNA: There's a boat that works. I can feel it in my kneecaps.

KUU: Luna, your kneecaps have been wrong before.

LUNA: My kneecaps are never wrong. They just have strong opinions about timing.

*They check six hulls. Five are empty or sunk. The sixth has a name painted on the stern: MARU-3. A mooring line still attached. A crew locker with a lantern, a coil of rope, and a sealed tin of biscuits.*

LUNA: *(grinning)* A boat that doesn't float is still a boat.

PLACK: A boat that doesn't float is called a shed.

**[Combat: dock angels, first "wave" mechanic: tides rise every 40 seconds, turning part of the level into shallow water that slows movement.]**

*After the fight, KUU opens the biscuit tin and offers one to PLACK, who takes it, sniffs it, and pockets it.*

PLACK: That's for the cat.

KUU: What cat?

PLACK: The one at the police station.

KUU: *(a beat)* You noticed it too?

PLACK: Of course. It was the only living thing there.

*KUU smiles to himself for a long while.*

---

## Episode 3 - Stepping Rocks

*The shoreline ends. A scatter of black rocks leads across open water toward the lighthouse island. A rope bridge hangs, half-collapsed, between two of them.*

**[Traversal level. Jump puzzle across rocks. Option: swim the full distance, longer but safe from tidal spray.]**

LUNA: Rocks.

PLACK: Rocks.

KUU: *(looking at the water)* Is it, um. Deep?

PLACK: Deep enough.

KUU: I should mention I'm not a strong swimmer.

LUNA: *(turning slowly)* Kuu. We have been friends for years. You've mentioned that you can't swim exactly once.

KUU: I didn't want to make a fuss.

LUNA: You're on the edge of the sea. It's the one time to make a fuss.

*She crouches in front of him.*

LUNA: Jump. I'll catch you.

KUU: Is that a promise?

LUNA: It's a pun. *(beat)* Yes. It's a promise.

**[Gameplay: Luna's black-hole movement option lets the player pull a squadmate across a gap. Used in tutorial form here.]**

*PLACK, ahead, is already swimming. She surfaces with a calm, unbothered expression, hair plastered, ledger sealed in a bag on her back.*

PLACK: I've swum across worse for less.

LUNA: *(to KUU)* She's a legend.

KUU: She's terrifying.

LUNA: *(fondly)* Both.

## Episode 4 - The Beam (Midpoint)

*The lighthouse island. The fog is thinner here, and the beam sweeps over them every eight seconds, white, steady, mechanical.*

**[Gameplay: the beam is a moving hazard: stand in it and enemies freeze, stand out and they move. Learn to use it.]**

KUU: Luna. Look.

*Along the shore, dozens of angels stand shoulder to shoulder at the water's edge, all facing the lighthouse, utterly still. The beam passes over them. They don't flinch. They don't turn. They don't move.*

PLACK: *(very quiet)* It's holding them.

LUNA: Holding?

PLACK: Look at the beam. It's not just light. See how the fog bends around it.

*She points. The fog parts, very slightly, where the beam passes, as if repelled.*

LUNA: What's it do?

PLACK: Whatever it does, they stop.

*A pause. The beam passes. The angels stand frozen.*

LUNA: So the angels on the surface, the ones facing west...

PLACK: They're not facing west. They're facing this.

KUU: The lighthouse?

PLACK: The beam. They're being held in place by it.

*LUNA looks up at the tower, then at the ring of frozen angels.*

LUNA: Who put it here?

**[Gameplay beat: from this point, enemies near the beam are frozen unless it's turned off. Teaches the final puzzle.]**

## Episode 5 - Eighty-Four Steps

*The spiral staircase. A narrow iron stair clinging to the outside of the tower, rain-slick, windy. The beam swings past every eight seconds.*

**[Level: vertical climb. Combat is minimal: angel archers on the gallery above, wind gusts that push the player. Stairs must be climbed in rhythm with the beam.]**

LUNA: *(counting)* Eleven. Twelve. Thirteen.

KUU: Why are you counting?

LUNA: To feel progress.

PLACK: How many are there?

LUNA: Eighty-four.

PLACK: How do you know?

LUNA: It's on a sign at the bottom.

*They climb. Forty steps in, LUNA is breathing hard.*

LUNA: Sixty-one.

PLACK: Fifty-two.

LUNA: ...Sixty-one.

PLACK: You've lost count.

LUNA: *(with great dignity)* I've lost count in a way that is, spiritually, moving us forward.

*KUU tugs her sleeve gently.*

KUU: Luna, there's a door.

*A small, rusted iron door at the top, a government seal on its lock.*

**[Puzzle: the lock is a three-number combination. The numbers are hidden in three places on the stair: a scratched mark, a painted number, a bolt count.]**

## Episode 6 - The Lamp Room

*Inside the lamp room. A huge Fresnel lens in the centre, brass-framed, humming with trapped light. A sponsor plate on the base: three feathered lines in a wing shape. Beneath it, a government serial number.*

PLACK: *(running a gloved finger across the plate)* This is government-issued.

LUNA: Of course it is.

PLACK: A lighthouse isn't a lighthouse. It's a unit. See the cable?

*A bundle of thick cables runs from the base of the lens through the floor.*

PLACK: This beam is deployed. It was installed. By people who knew what they were holding.

KUU: *(very quietly)* Why would they hold angels?

PLACK: Because they're afraid of what the angels would do if they weren't.

*A voice from the stairs.*

KEEPER: You shouldn't have come up here.

*A figure in oilskins, thin, grey-bearded, a lens-polishing cloth in one hand, a worn wrench in the other. Eyes sunken, steady.*

KEEPER: Please. Go back down. It's not safe.

LUNA: *(politely)* We're from the Ark.

KEEPER: Then you've come to take it off.

LUNA: Take what off?

KEEPER: The light. *(he laughs, small, tired)* They said someone would come. For years, I've waited. Technicians. Inspectors. I thought it would be someone with orders.

LUNA: We have lunch.

KEEPER: *(blinking)* I... beg pardon?

LUNA: We're just very hungry. And a bit lost.

*A beat. Then the KEEPER, against all expectation, smiles.*

---

## Episode 7 - What the Light Does

*The lamp room, later. A kettle on a camping stove. Four tin cups. The KEEPER pours.*

KEEPER: I've been here since 2003. My name is Abe. I'm a lighthouse technician. Or I was. Now I'm a keeper.

PLACK: What does the beam do?

KEEPER: It holds them. The angels. It doesn't kill them. It doesn't even hurt them. It just... *(he gestures)* ...pins them. Like a moth on a card.

KUU: How long?

KEEPER: Since the first year. Before the Ark. Before the sponsors.

*A pause.*

KEEPER: I had a partner. Mei. She stood the night watch, I did the day. Then one night the lamp flickered, and the angels at the shore began to move.

*He looks into the cup.*

KEEPER: Mei went out to relight it. The lamp came back on. She didn't come back in.

*Silence. KUU puts his cup down.*

KUU: I'm very sorry.

KEEPER: Thank you, young man.

LUNA: *(carefully)* What would happen if the lamp went out?

KEEPER: *(without hesitation)* They'd go west.

PLACK: Why west?

KEEPER: *(for the first time, looking afraid)* I don't know. But I've watched them for six years. Every time the beam flickers, they turn. Every single one. And they walk toward the sea. *(beat)* As if something's calling.

**[Low point. A siren sounds. The beam stutters. The angels on the shore begin to twitch.]**

KEEPER: The lens! It's failing!

---

## Episode 8 - The Keeper's Lens (Boss)

*The lens cracks along one edge. Light spills in jagged white shafts. The mechanism turns on its own, rotating faster, eyes of light blinking open across the glass. The lamp room becomes the arena.*

**[Boss: The Keeper's Lens. A turret-like boss that fires beams in rotating patterns. Phase 1: dodge the sweeping beam; destroy three conduits. Phase 2: the lens rotates faster, the arena flips orientation as the tower shudders. Phase 3: the Keeper helps from the side, holding a cable steady; keep him alive.]**

LUNA: It's overloading!

PLACK: It's trying to hold too many!

KUU: *(shouting over the noise)* What do we do?

LUNA: *(to the KEEPER)* If I pull the energy out, will it still hold?

KEEPER: *(clutching a cable)* I... think so. But you'll have to be fast.

*LUNA raises her hand. Black hole opens above the lens. The beam bends, light spiralling inward. For a moment, the whole room is silent.*

*Then the white hole opens, and the light is pushed out in a single clean pulse, rolling across the sea.*

*The cracked lens steadies. The angels on the shore sway, then go still.*

*The KEEPER sinks to the floor, breathing hard.*

KEEPER: *(softly)* You've done something nobody's done in six years.

LUNA: *(wiping her brow)* I just rearranged the furniture.

**[Reward: a spare lens from the cabinet. LUNA takes it.]**

*PLACK watches her pocket it.*

PLACK: What's that for?

LUNA: *(innocent)* Safekeeping.

### Stinger

*The squad on the gallery, watching the beam sweep the sea. The angels below are motionless.*

KUU: We could turn it off.

LUNA: *(without looking at him)* Or on.

KUU: *(after a pause)* I don't like the second one.

LUNA: *(still looking at the sea)* Neither do I. That's why it's interesting.

**[Chapter 3 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 4. Seeds: government beacon units hold the angels; "they go west, as if something's calling"; Mei's disappearance; the spare lens.]**
