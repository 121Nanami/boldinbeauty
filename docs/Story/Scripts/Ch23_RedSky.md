# Ch 23 - Red Sky (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Side story: **Weather** and **Stargazer** (both 5-star, MAIN section 5). Characterisation not documented; PROPOSED here. Weather: restless, blunt, reads storms by feel, swears at instruments. Stargazer: patient, precise, speaks in constellations and does not trust feelings, only patterns.

Chapter promise: two people who cannot agree on how to read the sky find the one place the sky points to, and the one place it avoids.

---

## Episode 1 - The Crown

*The Observatory at the arc's crown, where the sixteen hatches to the surface converge in a central tower. A great white dome, a ring of instrument galleries, wind turbines turning on the roof. WEATHER, hair wild, coat flapping, shakes a barometer at the sky.*

**[Cold open. Wind and music. The sky through the dome's slit is a faint, uneasy red.]**

WEATHER: *(to the barometer)* You're *lying*.

STARGAZER: *(from a telescope, not looking up)* It is not lying. It is calibrated.

WEATHER: It says fair!

STARGAZER: It says fair because it is fair.

WEATHER: *(pointing at the slit)* That is not fair. That is not fair in the slightest.

*STARGAZER takes her eye from the lens, turns, regards the red glow through the slit with a mild, patient expression.*

STARGAZER: *(softly)* It is not weather.

WEATHER: *(a beat)* ...Then what is it?

STARGAZER: *(returning to the telescope)* I would prefer to measure it first.

**[Tutorial: dual-protagonist. Weather: wind-and-lightning crowd control; Stargazer: long-range precision with "star-lock" marks.]**

## Episode 2 - Galleries

*The instrument galleries, a ring of long glass corridors. Anemometers spinning, rain-gauges full of black water, chart-recorders scratching long lines on paper. A map, pinned to every wall.*

STARGAZER: *(walking slowly down the line of charts)* Here. Here. Here. And here.

WEATHER: *(irritated, following)* What am I looking at?

STARGAZER: A year of angel movement.

*She presses a finger to a chart. A thin line, drawn in pencil, curves across the map, a long, faint thread.*

STARGAZER: This is one angel. This one. This one.

*A hundred lines, a thousand, all in fine pencil, all curving gently, like grass in a wind.*

WEATHER: *(slowly)* They're all going the same way.

STARGAZER: Of course.

WEATHER: *(looking closer)* To the west?

STARGAZER: *(a small, cool smile)* Further. Look.

*She turns a chart ninety degrees. In the corner of it, a single point, where the lines converge, faintly marked in red.*

**[Gameplay beat: clue board. The player may place and connect pins on the map to reveal the point.]**

## Episode 3 - Front

*The roof galleries. Rain drives sideways. Lightning flickers silently in the red cloud. Instruments hum. The squad's radio, WEATHER's own, crackles with static.*

WEATHER: *(into the radio)* Storm front, bearing two-seven-zero! Forty kilometres and closing!

STARGAZER: *(bent over a telescope, reading)* It is not a front.

WEATHER: *(shouting over the wind)* It's got a *leading edge*!

STARGAZER: It has a *lattice*. *(she straightens)* Look at the lightning. It is not random. It forms a grid. *(a pause)* A Fibonacci grid.

*WEATHER squints. Beyond the glass, the lightning flickers, and for one frame, the bolts form perfectly spaced lines.*

WEATHER: *(slowly)* That's... not nature.

STARGAZER: *(quietly)* No.

*The glass shudders. A shape moves in the cloud, huge, slow, many-winged.*

**[Combat: first wave of Storm Angels. Weather's wind pushes them off the galleries; Stargazer pins them from range.]**

## Episode 4 - Converge (Midpoint)

*The map room, a circular chamber under the dome. A vast, hand-drawn chart of the whole arc and the surface above, covered in a mesh of pencilled lines. In the middle, a brass pointer and a lamp.*

STARGAZER: *(laying out a final sheet)* All of them. Every angel recorded in the last three years. Their final heading before disappearance.

*WEATHER, hands on the table, stares.*

WEATHER: *(very quietly)* They're not wandering.

STARGAZER: They are going to a place.

*She places the brass pointer. On the chart, every pencil line, from every corner of the map, curves toward a single mark far west of the arc: a small black square, labelled in her neat hand, "?"*

WEATHER: *(softly)* What's there?

STARGAZER: *(carefully)* A village. A valley. A *stone*.

WEATHER: *(a long pause)* The monolith.

STARGAZER: *(nodding)* I believe the operators who've gone there call it that.

*WEATHER straightens.*

WEATHER: Then that's the Centre.

STARGAZER: *(gently)* That is what I would call it, too.

## Episode 5 - The Hole

*Later. WEATHER is asleep in a chair, boots on a desk. STARGAZER, in lamplight, is working. She has drawn a second map, overlaying the first. Where the lines converge, a dark point. And, in another corner, a place where no line goes, a perfect circle, a pinprick void.*

*WEATHER wakes, rubs her eyes.*

WEATHER: ...What's that?

STARGAZER: *(without turning)* A hole.

WEATHER: A hole in what?

STARGAZER: In the pattern.

*She taps it with a pencil.*

STARGAZER: Every angel, in the entire arc, for three years, has avoided this place. Not a single heading crosses it. Not one.

WEATHER: *(coming to look)* That's... *(a pause)* ...that's a long way. Where is it?

STARGAZER: *(very quietly)* I have not yet been able to place it on the surface.

WEATHER: *(softly)* It could be anywhere.

STARGAZER: *(a long moment)* Or nowhere.

*They look at each other, and neither speaks.*

**[Gameplay beat: the hole appears on the map. The player can place a pin; a hidden achievement unlocks in Ch 39.]**

## Episode 6 - Names

*A small room on the observatory's lowest floor, a kitchen of sorts: a kettle, two chipped cups, a plate of crackers. WEATHER, restless, paces. STARGAZER sits, composed.*

WEATHER: *(abruptly)* I want to name it.

STARGAZER: *(pouring tea)* The Centre.

WEATHER: *(nodding)* The Centre. *(a pause)* And the other.

*A silence. STARGAZER stirs her tea.*

STARGAZER: You do not wish to.

WEATHER: *(after a moment)* No.

STARGAZER: *(neutral)* May I ask why?

WEATHER: *(not looking at her)* Because a name is a handle. *(she grips the back of a chair)* And you only put a handle on something you mean to pick up.

*STARGAZER sets down her spoon.*

STARGAZER: *(a long pause)* I understand.

*WEATHER sits, finally, and pulls a cup toward her. She looks at the map on the wall, at the hole. Her voice, when it comes, is quiet.*

WEATHER: I've never been afraid of a storm in my life.

STARGAZER: *(very softly)* Nor have I.

---

## Episode 7 - Stormfront

*The roof. A wall of cloud, red-black, boiling, a mile high. Lightning, in perfect lattice, strikes the observatory's lightning rods in unison, one-two-three. Wind enough to tear rivets. A shape in the cloud, shifting, resolving, unfolding wings of mist.*

**[Low point. The Stormfront reaches the observatory.]**

WEATHER: *(shouting)* It's here!

STARGAZER: *(fixing a final telescope into its mount)* I observe that.

*The STORMFRONT's face resolves: no face, a swirl of cloud with a halo of lightning, a thousand storm-angels in its wake. It lifts one great arm, and the dome's glass cracks.*

WEATHER: *(grinning wildly, soaked, coat in tatters)* Right, then.

*She flings her arms wide. The wind, to the squad's astonishment, changes direction.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Stormfront (Boss)

**[Boss: The Stormfront. Phase 1: lightning grid; stand on safe nodes. Phase 2: it shreds the observatory floor by floor; Weather redirects gusts, Stargazer pins weak points. Phase 3: it condenses into a single, brilliant storm-core; combine wind and star-lock for the finishing blow.]**

*The fight is a symphony of weather. WEATHER carves cyclones. STARGAZER, calm at the centre, calls out coordinates in her flat, patient voice.*

STARGAZER: *(without raising her voice)* Left, thirty degrees. Hold. *(a pause)* Now.

*WEATHER sweeps a wall of wind through the cloud-lattice. The lightning, for one instant, aligns, and STARGAZER's star-lock closes on its core.*

*A single bolt, vertical, perfect. The STORMFRONT unravels, from the top down, a column of dispersing mist, and the sky, for the first time in a month, clears.*

*Sunrise, grey and thin, through the broken dome.*

WEATHER: *(panting, laughing, soaked)* That was a *good* one.

STARGAZER: *(adjusting her glasses)* It was acceptable.

### Stinger

*The map room, dawn. STARGAZER is alone, pinning the final sheet to the wall. She draws a small, careful red circle around the hole. In the margin, she writes two words, then, after a moment, a third.*

*WEATHER, from the doorway, reads over her shoulder.*

WEATHER: "Do not."

STARGAZER: *(not turning)* "Do not go."

WEATHER: *(softly)* Who's that for?

*STARGAZER takes a long moment.*

STARGAZER: *(finally)* Anyone who will be asked to.
