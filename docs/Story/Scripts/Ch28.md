# Ch 28 - The Commanders' Table - Script

**Arc 2 / 2011 / NEW.** Main cast. The government tower. Seven commanders under a ceiling of wings. The truce is the most dangerous part.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
An elevator, mirrored, forty storeys of it, with a jingle that is a sponsored jingle. Three people in a lift, each pretending not to
look at the others' reflections. A ceiling speaker: "Your ascent is brought to you by..."
LUNA: Don't.
KUU: I wasn't going to say anything.
LUNA: You were going to *apologise* to it.
KUU: ...I was going to apologise to it.

## Episode 1 - "Truce Rules"
**Scene:** Tower lobby. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. A guard (BLUE).

GUARD: (reading from a card, hands visibly shaking) Under the terms of the truce... you will not draw weapons. You will not...
you will not speak to the staff. You will not attempt to... (he turns the card over) ...to *leave.*
LUNA: To leave?
GUARD: ...That's what it says.
PLACK: (low) That's not a truce. That's a *check-in.*
LUNA: (pleasantly) Do you mean to say we're guests?
GUARD: (a long swallow) You're... *expected.*
KUU: (to the Commander, very quietly) Please tell me this was a good idea.

**Gameplay:** Truce tutorial: no combat until the elevator; a guard checks for weapons (the player surrenders them).
**Beat:** The squad enters unarmed by design.

## Episode 2 - "Sponsorships"
**Scene:** Office floors. **Present:** all three. An intern (BLUE).

INTERN: (a lanyard, a tablet, a suit two sizes too big) Hello! Are you here for the careers fair?
KUU: ...Sorry?
INTERN: We have a sponsorship opportunity! It's *very* low commitment!
KUU: I'm -- I'm not sure --
INTERN: (offering a tablet) Just sign here. Your likeness will be used for *positive* outreach.
PLACK: (taking the tablet) What's the exit clause?
INTERN: (blank) ...Exit?
PLACK: Of the *deal.*
INTERN: (very small) It's... *lifetime.*
LUNA: (walking on) Kuu, put the intern down.
KUU: I'm *not holding him.*

**Gameplay:** Floors with guards (RED, light); an optional intern minigame; YELLOW pickups.
**Beat:** The tower is a sponsored place.

## Episode 3 - "A Thousand Logos"
**Scene:** The executive corridor. **Present:** all three.

*(Framed logos line the walls, wing after wing, each slightly different: a hundred sponsors, one shape.)*
PLACK: Look at the wings.
KUU: They're all *different.*
PLACK: They're all the *same.* (she steps back) They're the same wing at different *scales.* (she counts) A hundred and twelve companies.
One *symbol.*
LUNA: A hundred and twelve masks.
PLACK: On the same face.
LUNA: (quietly) I'd like to see its face.
KUU: Luna, it's the *government's* tower.
LUNA: It's the *wing's* tower. They just *work here.*

**Gameplay:** Corridor stealth; a short non-combat conversation; codex update.
**Beat:** All sponsors are one symbol.

## Episode 4 - "Seven Seats"
**Scene:** The council chamber. **Present:** all three. The seven commanders (BLUE).

*(A round chamber under a ceiling of fan-shaped wings. A table the size of a boat. Seven seats; seven men and women in tailored uniforms.)*
COMMANDER (high collar): Operator Luna.
LUNA: Commander.
COMMANDER: You made a statement.
LUNA: I made *a* statement. I made it with *feeling.*
COMMANDER (cigar): (a low laugh) The arc heard.
COMMANDER (cane): The arc heard *half.*
LUNA: Somebody cut it.
COMMANDER (high collar): (a very small pause) ...Did they?
*(Plack looks at Kuu. They both see it.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the Table scene; seven character moments (each commander gets a line).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The commanders did not cut the feed; they didn't know. Someone else did.

## Episode 5 - "Terms"
**Scene:** The chamber. **Present:** all three.

COMMANDER (high collar): Here are the terms. The charge is dropped. The war crime is struck. In return, you bring the Secrets to heel.
LUNA: Under your command?
COMMANDER (high collar): Under *a* command.
LUNA: And the operators?
COMMANDER (cigar): The operators remain... *operators.*
LUNA: (flat) That is the *problem,* Commander.
COMMANDER (cane): (leaning forward) And what would you have in its place?
LUNA: (a flicker; she looks at Plack, who gives her the smallest nod) A Charter.
COMMANDER (cane): ...A *what?*
LUNA: I haven't written it yet.
*(A silence. One of the younger commanders, to the left, very slightly smiles.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); Luna names the Charter for the first time.
**Beat:** The Charter is announced.

## Episode 6 - "Hardliners and Reformers"
**Scene:** The chamber. **Present:** all three.

*(The Table argues. It does not argue like people with a common aim. It argues like a family at a funeral.)*
COMMANDER (young): She is *right.* The sponsors are bleeding us.
COMMANDER (high collar): The sponsors are the *reason* we are not *dead.*
COMMANDER (young): Then why are we all so afraid of them?
COMMANDER (cigar): (quietly) I am not afraid.
COMMANDER (young): You are *sweating.*
*(Three of the seven are looking at Luna. Four are looking at the ceiling.)*
PLACK: (low, to Luna) It's split.
LUNA: Hardliners.
PLACK: And reformers.
LUNA: And I'm the *only person in the room they all agree to hate.*

**Gameplay:** A chamber argument scene; the player picks which commander to watch (cosmetic).
**Beat:** The Table is divided.

## Episode 7 - "The Door"
**Scene:** The chamber's door, the truce ends. **Present:** all three.

COMMANDER (high collar): The truce expires at the door.
KUU: (hoarse) The door's *right* there.
COMMANDER (high collar): Then I suggest you use it.
*(The Sovereign Guard, six of them, fan out from the walls.)*
LUNA: (pleasantly) It's a *very* nice door.
PLACK: Luna.
LUNA: I'm only *admiring* it.
*(Six hands rise. Six sidearms. A hum.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Sovereign Guard's captains; an arena inside the chamber.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The truce ends the moment they stand.

## Episode 8 - "The Sovereign Guard"
**Scene:** The tower stair. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Sovereign Guard: a captain with a tower shield bearing the wing, and six guards. Phase 1, shield wall; Phase 2, the shield opens and fires.)*
CAPTAIN: Stand down.
LUNA: Ah. No.
CAPTAIN: Then *we* shall.
*(The shield wall closes. Luna and Plack take the flanks; Kuu, shaking, runs straight through the middle with a toaster in his coat.)*
KUU: (hitting the shield with it) I'M *SORRY!*
*(The shield dents. The line breaks.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: break the shield wall (hit from flank, centre, flank); a descent-escape.
**Beat:** The squad escapes with the reformers' names.

**Stinger:** On the street, in the rain, Luna unfolds a scrap of paper. Three names. "The reformers," she says. Plack: "They're afraid."
Luna: "Of what?" Plack: "Of the wing." Luna looks up at the tower, its ceiling-of-wings lit from within. "Then they have good taste."
