# Ch 26 - Death's Door (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Recruits **Death** (6-star Secret, MAIN section 5; later one of the two strongest, with Angel, to declare the unworthy). Death's voice: still, courteous, unhurried; counts rather than comforts; never threatens.

Chapter promise: a ledger with their names in it, and a question Luna cannot answer.

---

## Episode 1 - Tiers

*The Mortuary Level: a tiered cemetery under the old city, ring upon ring of stone terraces descending into dim, a spiral of niches and plaques. Faint lantern-light, blue. No wind. The air is cool and clean and smells of cut stone.*

**[Cold open. A held, low organ note. Footsteps echo.]**

LUNA: *(too loud)* It's very... tidy.

PLACK: *(low)* Don't.

LUNA: *(lower)* I'm not doing anything.

PLACK: You're doing a voice.

LUNA: *(a pause)* I'm doing my "reverent" voice.

KUU: *(very quietly)* It's good.

LUNA: *(relieved)* Is it?

KUU: It's terrible. But it's trying.

*They descend. Each tier, row on row of names, hundreds, thousands. The dates all start with the same year.*

COMMANDER: *(radio, hushed)* Unit Luna. This is the most restricted site in the Ark. The government has no map of it.

LUNA: *(under her breath)* Of course it doesn't.

**[Tutorial: quiet level. Few enemies. The squad's footsteps change volume with the surface.]**

## Episode 2 - Names

*The upper tier. PLACK stops at the first wall and reads. She reads aloud, softly, steadily, the names, one after another. KUU, after a moment, takes her hand.*

PLACK: Aizawa. Baba. Chiba. Daimon.

KUU: *(whispering)* You don't have to read them all.

PLACK: *(not stopping)* Endo. Fujita. Gomi.

LUNA: *(very low)* Plack.

PLACK: *(finally)* I know. *(a pause)* I'm just... *(she swallows)* ...somebody should say them.

*A silence. LUNA, after a moment, steps up beside her, and starts at the other end of the wall.*

LUNA: Hara. Ichikawa. Jinno.

*Their voices meet in the middle.*

**[Gameplay beat: a collectible side activity. Name plaques can be read; each unlocks a small memory fragment (a line of diary).]**

## Episode 3 - Lanterns

*A circular crypt, its ceiling lost in dark. A ring of stone lanterns, six of them, unlit. At the centre, a locked door of black iron, a single keyhole.*

KUU: *(squinting at an inscription)* "Relight them in the order they were put out."

PLACK: How are we supposed to know?

*LUNA, moving around the ring, finds small brass plaques at the base of each lantern: dates. 2002. 2003. 2004. 2006. 2007. 2008.*

LUNA: *(slowly)* The years.

PLACK: *(thoughtful)* Then which was first?

*LUNA lights the 2002 lantern. A soft blue flame blooms. A faint whisper, hardly a sound, as of many voices breathing out.*

*She lights the next. And the next. By the sixth, the whole ring is lit, and the black iron door unlatches with a long, quiet sigh.*

KUU: *(shivering)* They're all so... thankful.

LUNA: *(her voice odd)* Don't say that.

KUU: Sorry.

LUNA: *(a beat)* ...No. It's true.

**[Puzzle: lantern order. Reward: a mortuary key and a lore entry.]**

## Episode 4 - Visited (Midpoint)

*The hall of records, at the bottom of the spiral: a long, low chamber, vaulted, lit by a single blue lamp. A lectern at the far end, carved of black stone. On it, an enormous ledger, open. And, standing beside it, a tall figure in a long black coat, hands folded, head bowed, as if in thought.*

DEATH: *(without turning, in a voice of immense calm)* You are the Trio.

LUNA: *(carefully)* Some people call us that.

DEATH: *(a pause)* I have called you that since 2002.

*A silence. DEATH turns. A pale, composed face, ageless. Dark eyes, very still. A pen, silver, tucked behind one ear.*

DEATH: Come. Look.

*They approach the ledger. The left page is a long, ruled list of names, a column, small and exact, in a clear, even hand. The right page, headed "VISITED", has only three entries, and the last three are in a different ink:*

*"Luna." "Plack." "Kuu."*

*Beside each, a date: a day in 2002, the same day. And a small, neat tick.*

KUU: *(whispering)* That's... us.

PLACK: *(her voice flat)* Visited. When?

DEATH: *(gently)* The day the sky fell.

*LUNA's hand goes to her mouth.*

LUNA: *(very low)* We were twelve.

DEATH: *(softly)* Yes.

*A long, empty silence.*

**[Cutscene. Gameplay beat: the ledger is a pivotal lore object. The "VISITED" column returns in Ch 35 and Ch 39.]**

## Episode 5 - The Count

*DEATH turns a page. A single sheet, headed "CASUALTIES SINCE 2002". A figure at the foot of the page, in the same clear hand.*

PLACK: *(reading)* Four hundred and... *(she stops)* That's not right.

DEATH: *(gently)* It is correct.

PLACK: The government says forty thousand.

DEATH: *(with grave courtesy)* The government has rounded.

*The number is of a size none of them can quite take in.*

KUU: *(barely audible)* That's... that's most of us.

DEATH: *(softly)* Yes.

*LUNA turns away, a hand pressed to her forehead. When she turns back, there are tears on her face, and she does not wipe them.*

LUNA: *(almost a whisper)* Why are you showing us?

DEATH: *(simply)* Because you asked.

LUNA: *(a bitter half-laugh)* I didn't.

DEATH: *(a gentle inclination of the head)* You have been asking since the first night.

**[Gameplay beat: a heavy conversation scene. The player picks Luna's responses; each yields a quiet reaction from Plack and Kuu.]**

## Episode 6 - A Joke That Doesn't Land

*Silence. A long one. LUNA, hands trembling, tries.*

LUNA: *(thinly)* I'd like to say that this is the most depressing filing system I've ever seen.

*Nobody laughs. The lantern light shivers.*

LUNA: *(quieter)* I meant it kindly.

DEATH: *(without offence)* I know.

LUNA: *(a long breath)* I don't know what to say.

DEATH: *(gently)* It is not required.

*PLACK, at her side, takes LUNA's hand.*

PLACK: *(quietly)* You don't have to be funny.

LUNA: *(a ragged exhale)* It's all I've got.

PLACK: *(softly)* It's not.

*A silence. DEATH regards the three of them, with the kind of attention one gives to something rare.*

DEATH: *(after a long moment)* I am told you are building a table.

LUNA: *(wiping her face with her sleeve)* ...Yes.

DEATH: *(with the faintest warmth)* Then I shall attend.

---

## Episode 7 - Whether a Count Is a Life

*DEATH closes the ledger, very gently.*

DEATH: I ask only one thing.

LUNA: *(steadying)* Anything.

DEATH: *(without hesitation)* Before the table is set, tell me: is a count a life?

*A silence like a held breath.*

LUNA: *(slowly)* I... don't understand.

DEATH: *(patiently)* I keep the count. Every name. Every date. Every cause. *(he gestures to the ledger)* In all this time, I have not been able to determine whether I am keeping the dead, or keeping *death*. Whether a name written down is the person, or the thing that remains when the person has gone.

*He looks at her, with enormous, calm attention.*

DEATH: You will be asked this by many people. I would like you to answer me first.

*LUNA opens her mouth. Closes it. For the first time in her life, nothing comes.*

LUNA: *(finally, very small)* I don't know.

DEATH: *(nodding)* That is an honest answer.

LUNA: *(a flicker of anger, at herself)* It's not a good one.

DEATH: *(gently)* No. *(a pause)* But it is the first.

## Episode 8 - The Pallbearers (Boss)

*A tolling. Deep, from the walls. The black iron door slams open, and six figures enter, hooded, grey, in a slow procession, bearing a coffin on their shoulders. They place it, with great ceremony, at the centre of the hall.*

**[Boss: The Pallbearers. Six enemies, each carrying a lantern pole. Phase 1: they circle, swinging lanterns; extinguish them with the white hole. Phase 2: the coffin opens, releasing Grave Walkers; fight in the crypt aisles. Phase 3: the pallbearers merge into a single giant; strike the coffin at its heart.]**

DEATH: *(softly, not intervening)* They are my guards. *(a faint apology)* They do not know you.

LUNA: *(blocking a swung lantern)* Neither do I!

*The fight is solemn, ritualistic, lit by drifting blue flame. PLACK's ledger-throws extinguish lanterns, one by one. KUU, to his own surprise, is a rock.*

*At the end, the last PALLBEARER kneels, sets down its pole, and bows.*

*The coffin opens. Inside, nothing. Just a single white lily, perfectly fresh.*

*DEATH takes it, and sets it on the ledger.*

DEATH: *(gently)* It is for you.

*LUNA stares at it.*

DEATH: *(to the three of them)* I shall attend the unification. *(a pause)* I will ask again then.

### Stinger

*The Mortuary steps, dawn. The squad climbs, in silence, into thin gold light. At the top, KUU stops, looks back down the long spiral of names.*

KUU: *(quietly)* Luna.

LUNA: *(without turning)* Mm.

KUU: *(carefully)* What would you say?

*A very long silence. The wind lifts the lily in her hand.*

LUNA: *(finally, in a voice nobody has heard from her before)* I think... a count is how you know someone was there.

KUU: *(hesitantly)* Is that a yes?

LUNA: *(shaking her head)* It's not an answer. It's a... *(she turns the lily in her fingers)* It's a start.
