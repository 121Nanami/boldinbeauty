# Episode scripts (NIKKE-style) - voice guide and format

**Status: PROPOSED.** Written 2026-10-11 for the owner's approval. Every chapter has eight episodes (= its eight campaign levels).
Format per episode: a scene line, a dialogue block, a gameplay line (what the player does), and a beat line (what changed).
Chapter summaries and reveals are in `../Chapters/ChXX.md`; these scripts are the performable version.

## Rules of the house
- **The Commander is the player and never speaks on screen.** Characters talk to them over the radio ("Yes, Commander", then answer
  themselves). Nothing here decides who the Commander is among the rulers (MAIN: OPEN).
- **Comedy and dread share a scene.** The joke lands, the floor drops. Never two dread beats in a row; never two jokes after a death.
- **Every episode ends on something that changed**, even if it is one line.
- **Archive documents** close each chapter: short found texts that carry lore. They are YELLOW pickups in the levels.
- Secrets are never named or shown in Chapters 1-13; only hints (marked `[HINT]`).
- Verbatim owner text is never altered: the Chapter 9 screen, the Chapter 10 Morse lines, the Chapter 13 voice.

## Voices
| Who | Voice |
| --- | --- |
| **Luna** | Informal, half-bored, kind to Plack and Kuu and curt to everyone else. Says things that sound profound and are nonsense ("A door is only a wall that has made a decision."). Opportunist. Never apologises; sometimes pays. |
| **Plack** | Asks the question under the question. Precise, dry, a caretaker who keeps accounts. Warm in one-line bursts. Reads everything. |
| **Kuu** | Apologises to people, objects and weather. Afraid of heights. Brave in the clutch and embarrassed after. Loyal to the point of cost. |
| **Judge** | Formal, old-country cadence, full sentences, no contractions. Contempt delivered politely. |
| **Media** | Fast, sarcastic broadcast voice, narrates her own life like a segment. Cereal. |
| **Narrator** | Speaks of people in the third person and past tense, even to their faces. |
| **Jester** | Riddles, card talk, delighted by rules. |
| **Timekeeper** | Never rushes; counts. Precise, a little amused. |
| **Death** | Short sentences. Numbers. Never raises their voice. |
| **Angel** | Courteous, serene, absolute. He never insults; he describes. |
| **Adapt** | Coach cadence: "Again." Warm and merciless. |
| **Gambler** | Odds and house rules; honest about the edge. |
| **Alchemist / Blood** | Giddy chemist / one-word gruff porter. |
| **Weather / Stargazer** | Complaining poet / dreamy exactness. |
| **Ishii / Shiraishi / Arita** | Deadpan counter / grumpy ledger-keeper / upbeat tinkerer. |
| **Myrcia / Fujita** | Polite and nervous / mischievous. |
