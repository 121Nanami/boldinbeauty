# Ch 06 - The Moon Kingdom (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch06_MoonKingdom`; owner brief: a second arc, fully liveable, not a single living creature, everything covered in flora, a massive Moon inside casting deep darkness.

Chapter promise: a whole city that was left in the middle of dinner.

---

## Episode 1 - Population: 0

*A cavern wall opens onto a vista. A city, stacked in terraces up a slope toward a huge dim Moon that hangs from the ceiling of the rock. Roofs are green. Streets are green. A sign at the gate: WELCOME TO THE KINGDOM OF THE MOON. POPULATION: 0.*

**[Cold open. A slow dolly shot over the gate. Low strings. The Moon pulses faintly, once.]**

COMMANDER: *(radio)* Unit Luna, a government survey drone entered this site three times and did not return. You are authorised to investigate. Do not, I repeat, do not bring anything back.

LUNA: Define "anything".

COMMANDER: Anything.

LUNA: *(to herself)* Okay, but what about souvenirs.

PLACK: *(reading the gate sign)* Somebody painted "zero" by hand.

KUU: *(in a very small voice)* Why would you write zero?

PLACK: To be sure.

*They pass under the gate. A single wind chime, somewhere high, rings once and falls silent.*

**[Tutorial: low-light mechanic. The Moon's light dims visibility; carry a lantern (Kuu), or use light-sources in the environment. Flora enemies appear only in the dark.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Dinner

*A residential terrace. Doors open. Windows lit by moss that glows faintly. In a kitchen, a table is laid for four. Steam does not rise from the bowls because there is no steam; the soup is dust.*

PLACK: *(softly)* Somebody was about to eat.

LUNA: Somebody was about to eat, and then somebody wasn't.

*KUU moves quietly through the next room. A child's bed, the sheets turned down. A music box on the sill. He doesn't touch it.*

KUU: *(whispering)* I'd like to say sorry.

PLACK: To whom?

KUU: I don't know. To the bed.

LUNA: *(from the doorway)* Kuu.

KUU: I know.

LUNA: It's allowed.

*PLACK writes: "Beds made. Plates laid. No bodies. No struggle. No signs of evacuation."*

PLACK: Whoever lived here didn't leave. They stopped.

LUNA: *(looking at the Moon through the window)* Or someone stopped them.

**[Optional: collect "Household Items" for lore: a calendar with all days crossed out, a child's drawing of the Moon with a smiling face, a letter addressed to "the next ones".]**

---

## Episode 3 - Market

*A market square. Stalls with canvas awnings, fully stocked: fruit, spices, thread, lanterns. Coins on counters, in small neat piles.*

KUU: *(counting)* Forty-two. Fifty. Sixty-one. *(he stops)* They're all here.

PLACK: What?

KUU: Nobody took the coins. Not a single one. Nobody even stole.

LUNA: *(a faint chill)* Not in... however long.

*She picks up a single lantern from a stall. A tag on it: "Moon-oil, mild." She sets it back.*

LUNA: It's the cleanest market I've ever seen.

PLACK: It's the quietest.

KUU: *(whispering)* I don't like that nobody stole.

LUNA: *(gently)* That's the right instinct.

**[Combat: the first Flora enemies emerge from stalls when the player picks up too many items. Moss Sentinels and Vine Walkers. They don't attack unprovoked.]**

*After the fight, LUNA notices something odd: every stall has a small, hand-lettered sign in the same careful script: "TAKE WHAT YOU NEED. LEAVE WHAT YOU CAN."*

LUNA: *(slowly)* This place was kind.

PLACK: It was.

LUNA: *(quietly)* That's worse.

---

## Episode 4 - The Cemetery Wall (Midpoint)

*A long wall at the base of the cliff, set with stone niches, each with a name plaque and a small vase. Hundreds of them. Every one open.*

**[Gameplay: the player explores the wall. Each plaque is a short lore entry. A few of them have written messages. The names are ordinary, local. Some of them are children.]**

PLACK: *(reading)* Itou. Nakamura. Saito. *(beat)* There are no remains.

KUU: Grave robbers?

PLACK: Nobody took the coins. Remember?

*A long silence. LUNA kneels at one of the niches. The vase, still holding withered flowers, has a ribbon tied round it. A tag, in careful handwriting: "Come back."*

LUNA: *(very quietly)* Come back.

PLACK: Luna?

LUNA: *(standing, her voice odd)* They're not dead. Or they weren't. They went somewhere. And they left the doors open.

KUU: *(whispering)* Why would you leave the doors open?

LUNA: *(looking up at the Moon)* So they could come back.

*The Moon pulses, once. A soft wind lifts the ribbons on every vase along the wall, all at once.*

---

## Episode 5 - Lake

*A black lake at the centre of the city, with lily-pad stepping-stones leading across to an island under the Moon's light. Fireflies drift above the water.*

**[Traversal level: stepping stones sway; Moss Sentinels ambush from underwater; stones sink when stood on too long.]**

LUNA: *(arms folded, looking up)* I'd like to have a word with the Moon.

PLACK: It's not going to answer.

LUNA: *(raising her voice)* Excuse me! I'd like to discuss the lighting!

*The Moon says nothing. A ripple crosses the lake.*

LUNA: Rude.

KUU: Luna, please don't provoke it.

LUNA: I'm not provoking it. I'm networking.

*She steps onto the first lily pad. It holds. She steps onto the second. It holds. The third, it sinks. She leaps to the fourth, arms windmilling, lands on the fifth, and turns, grinning.*

LUNA: *(shouting back)* I'm winning the argument!

PLACK: The Moon isn't arguing!

LUNA: That's what makes it a strong position!

*KUU crosses the whole lake in a single terrified leap, lands in a heap, and lies there with his eyes closed.*

KUU: *(muffled)* I'm fine.

---

## Episode 6 - Names in the Frame

*A house at the top of the island. The door is ajar. Inside, a hallway, a small shrine. On the door-frame, carved in a child's hand and then an adult's, three names. They have been carved again and again, new lines beside old, over what seem to be years.*

PLACK: *(reading, her voice dropping)* Luna. Plack. Kuu.

*A silence. LUNA, very slowly, steps forward and lays her palm on the carving.*

LUNA: These are ours.

KUU: *(whispering)* That's not possible.

PLACK: *(tracing the lines with a gloved finger)* There are twenty-three entries. Twenty-three years of marks, from the smallest, high up on the left, to the newest, at eye level.

KUU: *(unsteady)* We're nineteen.

PLACK: *(softly)* I know.

*A sound behind them. The three turn. A bowl on a table, with three spoons laid beside it. In the bowl, steam.*

*It's the only warm thing in the entire city.*

**[Gameplay: no combat. This is a non-combat exploration beat. After a minute, ambient music cuts out. The Moon's light dims by half.]**

LUNA: *(to herself)* Somebody's been expecting us.

---

## Episode 7 - The Weeding

*The island's summit. A garden, wild and green, rising in terraces around a pale tree. A single figure moves among the beds: tall, slow, made of vines and flowers, tending a row of small white blossoms with great delicacy.*

**[Low point. The Gardener does not attack. It continues its work. The player must decide whether to engage.]**

KUU: *(very quietly)* It's gardening.

PLACK: It's weeding.

LUNA: It's very good at it.

*The GARDENER pulls a small weed, sets it in a basket, and moves on. It does not look up.*

LUNA: *(softly)* Hello.

*The figure pauses. Its head turns, a bud of white petals, no face. It regards them for a long time.*

GARDENER: *(a voice made of rustling leaves, hardly a voice)* You are late.

*A pause. KUU takes a half-step back.*

LUNA: *(calmly)* How late?

GARDENER: *(a gesture of its long hand, toward the empty city)* All of them.

*The wind stirs. A drift of blossoms falls from the pale tree, brushing the squad's shoulders.*

GARDENER: They went to wait. I stayed to keep the beds.

PLACK: Wait where?

GARDENER: *(with great gentleness)* West.

---

## Episode 8 - The Gardener (Boss)

*The garden darkens. The GARDENER rises, and the vines of the whole island rise with it, curling into a vast spiral of green. The Moon above begins to dim.*

**[Boss: The Gardener. Phase 1: vines sweep the island; use lanterns to burn them back. Phase 2: it summons Moss Sentinels from the beds; protect Kuu while he lights three lanterns. Phase 3: the Moon begins to fall; stand in the unshaded light to be healed.]**

LUNA: *(to the others)* It doesn't want to fight!

PLACK: It's not choosing to!

KUU: *(lighting a lantern)* It's protecting something!

*LUNA stops, listens. The GARDENER's attacks are slower than they should be, as if each one costs it something.*

LUNA: *(realising)* It's keeping the city from falling.

*She does not strike. She raises her hand, and the black hole opens above the island, drawing the Moon's weight in, gently, a little at a time, while the white hole holds it from falling.*

*The GARDENER sags, relieved, a single long breath. The vines settle.*

GARDENER: *(very quietly)* Thank you.

*It kneels in the bed, sets down its basket, and begins to unravel, leaf by leaf, into the earth.*

*LUNA reaches out. The last petals fall into her palm. A single white blossom, perfect, glowing faintly.*

**[Reward: Moon Blossom. A cosmetic item and a lore item.]**

### Stinger

*The squad at the gate. The Moon above them, dim. As they walk out, the Moon flickers, for exactly one second, a violent, unmistakable yellow, the exact yellow of the cubes in Chapter 5. Then it settles.*

*No one speaks. PLACK takes out her ledger, writes a single line, and closes it.*

LUNA: *(in a very small voice)* I'd like a different route home.

KUU: *(whispering)* I'll take it.

**[Chapter 6 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 7. Seeds: empty tombs; the three names carved in a door-frame (twenty-three years); the Moon's yellow flicker; "they went west to wait"; "you are late".]**
