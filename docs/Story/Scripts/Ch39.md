# Ch 39 - Nowhere, Inhabited - Script

**Arc 3 / 2017.** Main cast. Nowhere again, and not empty. Death's seat. The echoes of the squad's whole story, in reverse. Luna answers.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A drawer, opened. A map with a hole in it, a small blank circle with the words *do not* beside it in red. A hand, Stargazer's, closes
the drawer. Luna, in the door, with Plack and Kuu behind her. "I'd like to go," she says. Nobody argues.

## Episode 1 - "The Entry"
**Scene:** The edge of Nowhere. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

*(A frame, pale grey, at the end of a hallway. Beyond it, grey.)*
KUU: I've been in this one.
PLACK: We all have.
LUNA: It's not as long as I remember.
KUU: It's *exactly* as long.
LUNA: (very quietly) Then I've got *shorter.*
*(They step through. A click of static. A second. A third.)*
PLACK: (a small, unsurprised voice) Counted.
KUU: ...Again?
PLACK: (flat) Again.

**Gameplay:** Walking intro; no HUD; grey post-process (like Ch 11).
**Beat:** The squad is counted again.

## Episode 2 - "The Crossing"
**Scene:** Echo of Neo Tokyo. **Present:** all three.

*(A grey crossing. At each corner, a figure: a grey Luna with her umbrella over her face; a grey Kuu with a toaster; a grey Plack with a
sandwich. They do not look up.)*
KUU: There's... *us.*
PLACK: They're echoes.
KUU: Of *when?*
PLACK: (a beat) Of the first night.
*(A grey Luna, on a water tank, sits up. She says nothing. A grey pager goes off against her ribs.)*
LUNA: (watching herself) ...I was *very* tired.

**Gameplay:** Echo combat: grey replicas of Ch 1 enemies; each defeated echo drops a "left-behind" item.
**Beat:** The echoes are the squad's own history.

## Episode 3 - "The Counted"
**Scene:** The middle of Nowhere. **Present:** all three.

*(A field of rows. Thousands of figures in everyday clothes, standing in grey, facing the same way. They do not look at the three.)*
KUU: (a whisper) They're so quiet.
PLACK: They're *counted.* (very low) Aoyama. Hayashi. (she recognises a name) Nakagawa.
LUNA: (very still) Plack.
PLACK: It's... all of them. All the ones from the wall.
KUU: (thick) They're *here?*
PLACK: (nodding) *Here.*
*(A figure at the front of a row lifts one hand: a small, shy wave.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): an eerie walk; a quiet puzzle.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The counted stand in rows in Nowhere. The dead are *here.*

## Episode 4 - "The Lighthouse, Grey"
**Scene:** Echo of the Docks. **Present:** all three.

*(A grey lighthouse with no beam, a grey pier, a grey sea of white noise. At the stair's foot a grey cardboard sandwich.)*
KUU: Is that --
PLACK: "Ask who is paying for the rain."
LUNA: (a thin laugh) I *ate* that.
PLACK: (reading the note on the grey wrapper) You said you'd think about the lunch.
LUNA: I've been thinking.
*(Each echo holds something they left behind: Luna's umbrella; Kuu's boot; Plack's recorder.)*

**Gameplay:** Echo combat; pickups (YELLOW): the items they left.
**Beat:** The squad picks up what they left.

## Episode 5 - "The Chain, Grey"
**Scene:** Echo of the Hanging City. **Present:** all three.

*(A grey chain, rising out of the ground like a root, links the size of houses. At its foot, a grey child, on a grey lift, with a clock
that has no hands.)*
CHILD: (blank, bright) What time is it?
KUU: (very small) ...Four-thirteen.
CHILD: (a flicker of a smile) ...It's always four-twelve.
KUU: It *moved.*
CHILD: (gently) Everywhere else.
*(The child lifts a small grey hand and, with one finger, points to the end of the field.)*

**Gameplay:** Echo traversal (vertical); a chain-climb in grey.
**Beat:** The child points the way.

## Episode 6 - "The Screen"
**Scene:** Echo of the data centre. **Present:** all three.

*(A grey screen, small, in a grey hall. It shows text. It is not the Ch 9 text. It is a list.)*
PLACK: (reading) "Luna. Plack. Kuu." (a beat) "Visited." (a beat; a third line, newer) "Returned."
KUU: (very quietly) *Returned?*
LUNA: (reading over her shoulder) That's *new.*
PLACK: It's *Death's.*
LUNA: Death doesn't... (she stops) ...Death *adds* things.
*(From the end of the hall, a very level voice.)*
DEATH: I add things *when they happen.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** Death's ledger recorded their return.

## Episode 7 - "The Question"
**Scene:** Death's gallery. **Present:** all three.

*(A long, low room. A desk, a lamp. Death, at the desk, no longer in a plain dark coat; a pale cloak of ledger-pages, a red glyph column
running down it, as down the page.)*
DEATH: You have come to answer.
LUNA: (very quietly) I have.
DEATH: I will ask again. Is a count a life?
*(Luna looks at Plack. She looks at Kuu. She looks at the thousand figures in the rows behind her.)*
LUNA: No.
DEATH: (a very long silence) ...No?
LUNA: A count is a *record.* A life is what the record is *of.* (she takes a breath) You've been keeping the one and mistaking it for
the other. It's the most understandable mistake in the world. It's what *I* do with recruits.
DEATH: ...And that is your answer.
LUNA: It's the only one I've got. (a beat) It's not good.
DEATH: (the red column on the cloak flickers) It is *correct.* That is a different thing.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the answer. (The player's input is a single confirming press.)
**Beat:** **Low point.** The answer does not end the fight. It begins it.

## Episode 8 - "Death"
**Scene:** Death's gallery. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: Death, three phases. Phase 1, the ledger: names fall as hazards. Phase 2, the cloak unfurls: the glyph column attacks. Phase 3, the
counted stand and the arena fills with figures; the squad must not hit them.)*
DEATH: (not fighting; weighing) The column was *written* by another. I cannot unwrite it.
LUNA: (dodging) Then cross it out!
DEATH: I have *tried.*
PLACK: (in a gap) Death. What's the heading?
DEATH: "Unworthy."
PLACK: (shouting) *Add a line!*
DEATH: ...What?
PLACK: Add a *line.* Under it. "Of whom?"
*(Death stops. For the first time, the red column falters. A hesitation, a very long one. The pen drops.)*
DEATH: ...Of whom.
*(The glyphs on the cloak curl; the cloak goes grey. The counted, in their thousand rows, lie quietly down.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: three phases; the "Add a line" interact; the counted as non-targetable.
**Beat:** Death yields. The counted lie down.

**Stinger:** Death, in plain grey, sits at the desk. "I do not know," says Death. Luna: "That's all right." Death: "It is not." Luna
puts a hand on the desk, very lightly. "It's a start." On the screen of the gallery's only lamp, a new line has appeared in the ledger,
under *Unworthy*: **Of whom?**
