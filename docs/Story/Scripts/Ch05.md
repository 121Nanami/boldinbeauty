# Ch 05 - A.R.C TECH INCORPORATED - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A fully functional factory with no visible workers, making tiny cubes that glow insanely bright yellow.
Conveyors, robot arms, ten stampers, coolant pools, the cube core.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
An intake door the width of a street. Over it, in letters a metre high, A.R.C TECH INCORPORATED. Below, a smaller sign: *Visitors
Welcome. Please Do Not Touch The Product.* Inside, a thousand machines moving in perfect order, and not one person.
VOICE (warm, unhurried): Good evening, visitors. Please enjoy the line.

## Episode 1 - "Please Enjoy The Line"
**Scene:** Intake hall. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. The Voice (loudspeaker, always present, never seen). *(HINT: Narrator)*

VOICE: Once upon a time, three visitors came to the factory that made the light. They were tired. They were wet. They were,
the factory thought, a little hungry.
KUU: It's narrating us.
LUNA: It is *rating* us. (to the loudspeaker) What is my rating?
VOICE: Luna. Four stars. Interesting. Slightly bored.
LUNA: (delighted) I have been seen.
PLACK: Who runs this place?
VOICE: Nobody runs the factory, dear. The factory runs.
PLACK: That is not an answer.
VOICE: It is the answer the factory has.

**Gameplay:** Intake hall exploration; the Voice comments on each action. Basic security automata (RED).
**Beat:** The squad learns they are being observed, politely.

## Episode 2 - "Rate Kuu Twice"
**Scene:** Conveyor hall. **Present:** all three. Cube hauler automata.

LUNA: Voice. Rate Kuu.
VOICE: Kuu. Three stars. Apologises to the equipment.
KUU: Sorry -- I mean -- *sorry.*
LUNA: Rate him again.
VOICE: Kuu. Three stars. Apologises to the equipment.
LUNA: It's the same!
VOICE: Consistency is a virtue.
PLACK: Why are we rating things?
LUNA: It is the closest I have come to being praised in months. (to the loudspeaker) Rate Plack.
VOICE: Plack. Five stars. Asks the right question. Rarely gets the right answer.
PLACK: ...Thank you?
VOICE: You're welcome.

**Gameplay:** Conveyor-line combat (belts as moving platforms); first glowing cubes on pallets.
**Beat:** A tonal tease: the factory is polite. That's what makes it frightening.

## Episode 3 - "A Sunrise In Cubes"
**Scene:** Loading dock. **Present:** all three.

*(Hundreds of pallets of thirty-centimetre cubes, each glowing a violent sunrise-yellow, strapped and waiting.)*
KUU: It's so bright.
PLACK: It's beautiful.
LUNA: It is an opportunity.
PLACK: Don't touch it.
LUNA: I'm not touching it. I'm *evaluating* it.
*(Plack lifts a cube anyway, very carefully, and puts it back as if it were a sleeping animal.)*
PLACK: It's warm.
KUU: Like it's alive?
PLACK: Like it's been *held.*

**Gameplay:** Pallet puzzle (move cubes to open a gate); a lull in the combat. YELLOW pickup: a stamped pallet tag.
**Beat:** The cubes feel recently handled. By whom?

## Episode 4 - "One Percent"
**Scene:** Packaging bay. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: Look at the stamp.
KUU: "1%".
PLACK: On every pallet. Not a batch number. Not a date. "1%".
LUNA: (a beat; the bored expression slips) One percent of what?
PLACK: (quietly, not looking at either) I know a number like that.
KUU: ...Plack.
PLACK: Operators. Fifty percent human, forty-nine percent machine --
KUU: -- and one percent --
PLACK: (very flatly) They're not *packing* it. They're *dosing.*
*(The loudspeaker, kindly: "Please do not read the product. The product is not for reading.")*

**Gameplay:** Packaging line: crate-stack puzzle; an automaton ambush; the pallet stamp (YELLOW) is a key.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The cubes are what makes an operator an operator. The 1% is manufactured.

## Episode 5 - "The Press Hall"
**Scene:** Ten stampers. **Present:** all three.

KUU: There are ten.
PLACK: There are always ten.
KUU: Which is a good number to be flattened by?
LUNA: Seven. It's lucky.
KUU: Why seven?
LUNA: I don't know. It has a nice *shape.* (she goes through on seven; the stamper drops behind her) See? Lucky.
*(Plack counts the cycle under her breath, then goes through four, five, six, with a flat calm.)*
KUU: (to the empty hall) I'm going last. I'd like that noted.
VOICE: Noted. Kuu goes last.

**Gameplay:** Timing run under ten stampers; a timed robot-arm gauntlet; coolant-pool shortcut (swim).
**Beat:** A hard environmental set piece; Luna quietly pockets a stray cube as she passes a conveyor.

## Episode 6 - "Past Tense"
**Scene:** Robot-arm halls. **Present:** all three. The Voice narrates death in advance.

VOICE: The arm came down on the left. Luna said, "Missed me." The arm rose. Kuu apologised. The arm, perhaps embarrassed, went on
its way.
KUU: It's narrating the thing it hasn't done yet.
PLACK: It's narrating the thing that has happened to someone.
KUU: ...Who?
VOICE: Another group. Another evening. They were tired too.
LUNA: (flat) How did it end?
VOICE: They were rated. They were not a very good group.

**Gameplay:** Combat through arm sweep lanes; the Voice's predictions are tells: its narration warns the next hit.
**Beat:** The factory has done this to others. The cube core is close.

## Episode 7 - "The Foreman Knows Your Names"
**Scene:** Cube core antechamber. **Present:** all three.

*(A person-shaped automaton with a clipboard stands in the middle of the glass-walled core. It turns at their approach.)*
FOREMAN: Luna. Plack. Kuu.
KUU: (very small) How does it --
FOREMAN: You were on the manifest.
PLACK: What manifest?
FOREMAN: The visitor manifest. It is a long document. It has a great many names. (a click of the pen) You're all present. Good.
Now: product reviews.
LUNA: (to the Commander, quietly) Something is not right with this tone.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Foreman's lieutenant (a heavy automaton), arena with conveyors.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The manifest knew they would come.

## Episode 8 - "The Foreman"
**Scene:** Cube core. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

FOREMAN: (starting its routine) We will begin with a safety briefing. In the event of a fire --
LUNA: There is a fire.
FOREMAN: -- in the event of a fire, please leave by the *nearest exit*, which is not the exit you came in by, and is not marked.
PLACK: That's the weak point. It's talking us to the wrong door.
KUU: It's very polite.
LUNA: So is a knife.
*(Boss phases: 1, press and arm sweeps; 2, the Foreman fuses with the stampers; cubes scatter.)*
FOREMAN: (as it falls) ...Thank you for visiting. Your feedback is... valued...

**Gameplay:** Boss: safety-briefing patterns (follow the exit signs to dodge); stamp-drops; cube scatter.
**Beat:** The Foreman falls. The cubes keep glowing.

**Stinger:** Outside, rain. Plack and Kuu argue gently about what they saw. Luna walks a few steps behind, one hand in her pocket,
the cube warm against her palm. She does not tell them. *(HINT: Luna's leverage.)* The factory's lights do not go out behind them.
The line keeps running.
