# Ch 13 - A Secretive Soul (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Map `L_Ch13_SecretiveSoul` is LOCKED and untouched. Owner brief: a small Japanese-looking countryside town in consistent low to medium rain; in the middle a pure black monolith with red hieroglyphics in an undecipherable language; when you get close the voice of the angel god says, verbatim: **"They wree purity, Impure sin, Thats all"**.

The voice line is kept exactly as written (spelling included). The chapter ends Arc 1.

New voice: **THE SHEPHERD**, the village's guardian operator, soft-spoken, immovable.

Chapter promise: quiet people, a stone that listens, and the end of the Plan as they understood it.

---

## Episode 1 - Rain on a Roof

*A valley ringed by hills, thin rain. A river, wooden bridges, rice paddies shining like glass. A village of tiled roofs, a red torii, a shrine at the top of a long stone stair. Far in the middle, a black line against the sky.*

**[Cold open. Gentle, almost folk-like score. The first calm place since Paradise.]**

KUU: *(breathing out)* It's quiet.

PLACK: It's very quiet.

LUNA: *(tipping her face up to the rain)* It's the first rain I've liked.

COMMANDER: *(radio, faint, warm)* Unit Luna, the government has no record of this settlement. Treat as unregistered. Observe. Do not engage unless provoked.

LUNA: *(to the others)* Observe, they say. As if we've ever once done that.

*They cross the first bridge. A villager at the far end, in a straw hat and a patched coat, stops what she's doing and watches them. She doesn't wave. She doesn't run.*

VILLAGER: *(quietly)* You're not from here.

LUNA: *(polite)* No.

VILLAGER: *(a nod)* Then wipe your feet.

**[Tutorial: no combat. The player may explore the village. NPCs respond to the squad with polite, minimal courtesy.]**

## Episode 2 - Deserters

*A communal house, tatami floors, a low table. A pot of tea steams. Twelve villagers sit in quiet pairs, mending tools, shelling beans. No one is in uniform. Several wear the faded stripes of operators.*

PLACK: *(under her breath)* Operators.

KUU: *(looking at their hands)* Retired.

PLACK: *(very low)* Deserted.

*An older villager, grey-bearded, pours tea into three cups without being asked.*

ELDER: You'll be wanting to know why we're here.

LUNA: I'd like to know why you don't mind that we're here.

ELDER: *(a ghost of a smile)* We've learned not to mind much. It's easier.

*He passes the cups. KUU takes his with both hands, carefully, as though it might spill.*

ELDER: Most of us were sent to the Intersection. Or the Docks. Or the Hanging City. We came home from the jobs one day and we couldn't do it again.

PLACK: *(quietly)* Home?

ELDER: *(gesturing out at the paddies)* This is the only place that ever felt like one.

*A silence. LUNA looks down at her tea.*

LUNA: *(slowly, sincerely)* I'd like to stay a bit.

ELDER: *(nodding)* Then you'll learn to plant rice.

**[Hub level: the village has quests: help with the paddies, repair the bridge, collect firewood.]**

## Episode 3 - Planting

*A paddy at dusk. The three of them, trousers rolled, up to the shin in mud. An old woman demonstrates a planting technique with great patience. LUNA fails, repeatedly.*

OLD WOMAN: *(for the fifth time)* Three stalks. Not four.

LUNA: *(holding up a small, sodden bundle)* It's three.

OLD WOMAN: It's five.

LUNA: *(counting)* ...It's five.

*PLACK, two rows over, is a natural. She plants quickly, evenly, without comment. KUU, beside her, is humming a tune and, for once, apologising to nothing.*

KUU: *(after a while, quietly)* This is nice.

PLACK: *(without looking up)* It is.

KUU: I'd like to do this more.

PLACK: *(a pause)* So would I.

*LUNA, up to her ankles in mud, leans on her bundle of stalks and looks across the paddy at the two of them. A softness crosses her face. She glances, once, at the black line in the middle of the valley.*

*The softness fades.*

**[Pickup: Rice Seedlings. A cosmetic. Plack gains a "farmer's hat" accessory.]**

## Episode 4 - The Kneeling (Midpoint)

*Dusk, the village square. The rain is thin. Lanterns are being lit along the eaves. One by one, the villagers leave their homes and walk, without speaking, to the centre of the square.*

*They kneel. All of them. In rows. Old and young. Facing the same direction: toward the middle of the valley.*

KUU: *(whispering)* What are they doing?

PLACK: *(very low)* Praying.

KUU: To what?

*The black line stands on the horizon, a vertical slash against the grey sky.*

LUNA: *(very softly, almost to herself)* Ah.

*No one speaks. The only sound is the rain. A bell, somewhere, once. A moment later, as if answered, a low hum rises from the valley floor, a familiar note, the one from the data centre vent.*

KUU: *(grabbing PLACK's sleeve)* That's it. That's the same sound.

PLACK: *(her voice flat)* I know.

*The villagers begin, softly, to murmur, a long slow chant, a single word repeated. It is almost the word "purity".*

*LUNA, standing very still, watches the monolith.*

LUNA: *(under her breath)* They're not praying *to* it.

PLACK: *(turning)* What?

LUNA: They're praying *at* it. Like you'd pray at a grave.

---

## Episode 5 - The Monolith

*The valley floor. A field of short grass, wet, dark. In its centre, fifty metres tall, a pure black slab, matte, absorbing the grey. Carved across it in red, in fine, spidery strokes, are glyphs: not letters, not pictures, not anything that can be read.*

**[Gameplay: free exploration around the monolith. Enemies are absent. The player may touch the stone; a faint warmth.]**

KUU: *(tilting his head back)* It's very tall.

PLACK: *(reading the glyphs, brow furrowed)* It's not a language. It's... something that looks like one.

LUNA: *(placing her palm against the black)* It's warm.

PLACK: Luna.

LUNA: *(her eyes half closed)* It's like someone's been leaning on it.

*She takes her hand away. The glyphs, for a moment, glow a shade brighter. A faint pulse, once, in time with her heartbeat.*

PLACK: *(softly)* It knows you.

LUNA: *(steadying her voice)* It knows everyone. That's the point of it.

*A beat. KUU steps back, and, one by one, the glyphs on the stone begin to fade, leaving a smooth black surface.*

---

## Episode 6 - The Voice

*The three stand at the foot of the monolith. The rain is almost nothing now. A hush has fallen over the valley.*

*From the stone, a voice, neither male nor female, neither loud nor soft, a voice as if the air itself had spoken:*

VOICE: **They wree purity,**
**Impure sin,**
**Thats all.**

*The words hang. No echo. The rain stops entirely.*

*LUNA does not move. PLACK's hand finds hers. KUU, behind them, stands with his duck against his chest.*

LUNA: *(after a long moment, in a very small voice)* Is that all?

*The glyphs glow, bright and slow, like the lamp of a lighthouse. The voice does not answer.*

PLACK: *(tightly)* It's saying we're impure.

LUNA: *(quietly)* I heard.

PLACK: It's saying... *(she can't finish)*

LUNA: *(still looking at the stone)* That we're the impure ones. The ones who sinned. And the angels... *(a pause)* ...are the pure.

KUU: *(very, very small)* They were pure.

*A silence.*

LUNA: *(her jaw tightening)* They *wree* pure. Past tense.

**[Gameplay beat: a trigger zone around the monolith plays the line when entered. Subtitle uses the owner's exact spelling.]**

## Episode 7 - The Shepherd

*The village stair, at dawn. A tall figure stands at the top, in a long grey robe, a plain staff in one hand, perfectly still. Behind it, the shrine. Below, the whole village, watching in silence.*

**[Low point. The Shepherd bars the way.]**

SHEPHERD: *(in a deep, even voice)* You've heard.

LUNA: *(slowly)* We heard.

SHEPHERD: Then you've been judged.

KUU: *(quietly)* We didn't ask to be.

SHEPHERD: Nobody does.

*A beat. The SHEPHERD descends two steps, the staff touching each stone with a small, careful tap.*

SHEPHERD: We came here to be quiet. To let it be. To listen, and not to answer. There is a peace in that.

PLACK: *(coldly)* You're afraid.

SHEPHERD: *(a long pause)* Yes.

LUNA: *(gently)* Then let us pass.

SHEPHERD: *(shaking his head)* You'll go to the stone. You'll raise your hand to it. And it will answer. *(he looks at her, with grave, almost pitying, eyes)* It will wake. And we've worked so hard to let it sleep.

*The staff's tip glows red.*

SHEPHERD: I can't let you leave unjudged.

## Episode 8 - The Shepherd (Boss)

**[Boss: The Shepherd. Arena: the shrine stair, the torii, the paddies. Phase 1: he uses his staff to draw glyph-circles on the ground; avoid them. Phase 2: the villagers become spectators; their murmured chant buffs him, silence them with a pulse. Phase 3: he opens his robe; the staff splits into a bow; dodge long-range glyph arrows.]**

*The fight is slow, dignified, almost ritual. Each strike is a bow. Each parry, a kneel.*

LUNA: *(blocking, breathing hard)* You don't want this!

SHEPHERD: *(without anger)* It's not about what I want.

PLACK: *(from behind)* Then what is it?

SHEPHERD: *(a flicker, genuine)* Duty.

*KUU, to everyone's surprise, steps between the two and holds up both hands. His umbrella, forgotten, hangs open from his elbow.*

KUU: *(shaking, steady)* Please stop. Sorry. Please. I don't want anyone to be hurt. *(a swallow)* I know what it's like to wait. To wait for someone to say you're allowed to go.

*The SHEPHERD hesitates.*

KUU: *(very softly)* You're allowed.

*A long pause. The staff drops from the SHEPHERD's hand and clatters down the steps.*

SHEPHERD: *(a long breath)* ...Then you'll have to carry it.

*He bows to LUNA, deeply, and does not rise.*

*Behind him, on the shrine's wall, a single glyph, red, glows. It is, unmistakably, the shape of an open hand.*

### Stinger

*The squad at the top of the stair, looking out over the valley. The rain has begun again, softly. Below, the black slab stands in the paddies.*

LUNA: *(after a very long silence)* "Is that all."

PLACK: *(her hand in hers)* What?

LUNA: It's what I said. *(quietly)* It's what it said.

*The glyphs on the monolith brighten. The voice doesn't answer.*

*LUNA turns away, her coat in the wind.*

LUNA: *(clearly)* I'm tired of being quiet.

**[Chapter 13 ends. Arc 1 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 14. Seeds: the voice of the angel god; "purity" and "sin"; the village of deserters; the Shepherd's open-hand glyph; Luna's resolve to go loud.]**
