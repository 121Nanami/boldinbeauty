# Ch 11 - Return to Nowhere (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED, 2026-10-11. Map `L_Ch11_ReturnToNowhere` is LOCKED and untouched. Owner brief: nowhere, a mashup of all ten past maps pasted together, pure grey, no post-processing, nothing in it, white noise; no levels, no bosses; a single walk of about five minutes; nothing happens; horrifying the whole time.

This chapter has **almost no script on purpose**. Everything below is direction for what is *not* said. The design rule: no combat, no pickups, no HUD, no music, no dialogue prompts, no objective marker. The commander's radio is silent. The squad barks are disabled.

---

## The walk (five minutes)

*Grey. White noise. No horizon.*

**0:00.** Fade in. The squad stands at the start of a path, flat grey, in flat grey light. No shadows. No sky. The three of them are the only colours: Luna's two coats, Plack's satchel, Kuu's umbrella.

The first sound is a lifted breath. It is the player's own character. There is no other sound under the noise.

**0:20.** *A crossing from Neo Tokyo, grey.* Traffic lights hang from nothing. A zebra crossing, no road. Nobody speaks. LUNA stops at the kerb, as she would, and waits for a light that never changes. After a long moment she steps on to the crossing anyway.

**1:00.** *The toppled tower from Chapter 2, across grey.* The squad walks along it. The hatch numbers on its side are all 9.

**1:30.** *The lighthouse from Chapter 3.* Its beam does not move. It lights nothing. No angels stand on the shore. PLACK looks at it for a long time and does not write anything down.

**2:00.** *Chain links rise from the ground, as in Chapter 4.* Each is the exact size of a person. KUU stays very close to LUNA. He does not apologise to any of them.

**2:30.** *A conveyor that does not move, with a single grey cube on it.* PLACK touches the cube. It is cold. She puts it back.

**3:00.** *A house under a low moon.* Three names are carved in its door-frame. There are more names now. LUNA does not read them.

**3:30.** *Rows of desks from T.O.M.B.S, then a wall too tall to see over.* A bell on one of the desks. LUNA reaches for it, stops, lowers her hand.

**4:00.** *A tunnel segment. A dune. A server rack with no lights.* The white noise has a shape now, very faint, like someone saying a name very slowly and repeating it.

**4:30.** *A floating island, a giant teacup, a chair.* All grey. There is no Morse on the floor.

**5:00.** *The exit.* A plain rectangular frame, standing alone, grey on grey. Through it: the same grey.

---

## Exit

*The squad steps through, one at a time.*

**LUNA** passes through the frame. A single soft click of static.

**PLACK** passes through. A single soft click of static.

**KUU** hesitates for a moment on the threshold, looks back along the path, then passes through. A single soft click of static.

*Nobody remarks on it.*

*Fade to the ordinary rain of the real world.*

---

## Design notes

- **Never explained in-game.** The three clicks are counted by Death (Ch 26: "visited"; Ch 39: the counted).
- **No stinger.** The next chapter starts on a warm, bright, noisy place, deliberately jarring.
- **Camera:** locked behind the squad, eye-level, no cuts.
- **Optional hidden detail (PROPOSED):** if the player walks off the path by more than 30 metres, they are returned to the path with no message. Nothing else happens.
- **Audio:** white noise only; its level rises by 3 dB per minute; at the exit it is cut to silence for one second.
- **Rule:** this is the only chapter without a boss, a mini-boss, or an enemy. It is also the only chapter with no dialogue.
