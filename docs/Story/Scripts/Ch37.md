# Ch 37 - The Alliance Breaks - Script

**Arc 3 / 2017.** Main cast. The Ark's districts, the Operators' dome, the Secrets' garrison, the government quarter. The Charter's
last copy under Luna's arm.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
An empty hall. A great deal of marble. A pen on a stand. Paper everywhere, trodden. A Charter, torn at the middle, lying on the dais;
a hand, in a taped armour glove, picks it up. A voice: "Item one."

## Episode 1 - "After"
**Scene:** The Great Hall, empty. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: They just *left.*
PLACK: They walked out.
KUU: Nobody stopped them.
LUNA: (flat) Nobody was *sure they were allowed.* (she looks at the dais) Three hundred and four people in this hall and not one of them
knew who was allowed to stop the two *strongest beings in the arc.*
PLACK: (quietly) Luna.
LUNA: I know. I know what I'm saying.
PLACK: The Charter's in pieces.
LUNA: (gathering a page, then another) Then we'll *piece* it.

**Gameplay:** Quiet walk; a short fight with remnant loyalists.
**Beat:** The aftermath; the Charter is gathered.

## Episode 2 - "The Council Demands"
**Scene:** The Operators' dome. **Present:** all three. Council (BLUE).

ISHII: (tight, counting) The Secrets are armed.
SHIRAISHI: They are *all* armed. (she turns the ledger) The Council demands that the Secrets be disarmed.
ARITA: (not looking up from a taped weapon) I'd just like the kettle fixed.
LUNA: Ishii. Shiraishi. There's a *Charter.*
SHIRAISHI: The Charter has been *violated.*
LUNA: By two people.
SHIRAISHI: (steely) By the two with the *most power.* (she shuts the book) It's a fair *point.*
LUNA: (a small, appalled breath) ...It's a *very* fair point.

**Gameplay:** Council scene (BLUE); a district defence.
**Beat:** The Council demands.

## Episode 3 - "The Secrets Demand"
**Scene:** The Secrets' garrison. **Present:** all three.

*(A quiet fortress of whitewashed rooms. Media, at a window; Jester, on a stair, swinging a leg; Timekeeper, with a watch; Narrator, in a
chair; Adapt, running drills.)*
MEDIA: The government should dissolve.
NARRATOR: He said that the government should dissolve.
JESTER: *Obviously.*
TIMEKEEPER: (a pause) It is the *logical* ... *position.*
LUNA: (very slowly) I'd like to ask a question. Did *any* of you *intend* to sign it?
JESTER: Of course! (a card, flicked) We all did. And then...
TIMEKEEPER: And then two of us didn't.
ADAPT: (stopping her drill) That changes things.
LUNA: (quietly) ...That's the thing about a *Charter.*

**Gameplay:** Garrison scene (BLUE); a combat gauntlet through the garrison.
**Beat:** The Secrets demand.

## Episode 4 - "The Dome Burns"
**Scene:** The Operators' dome. **Present:** all three.

*(A shot. A flare. Smoke curls from the dome's eastern face. Within a minute, the dome is a column of fire.)*
KUU: Who *lit it?*
PLACK: (running) It doesn't *matter!*
KUU: (hoarse) Yes it does!
*(The operators stream out in coats and blankets. A child, wrapped in a flag. Arita at the gate with a fire hose of tape.)*
LUNA: (stricken) Get everyone *out.*
SHIRAISHI: (in her ear) Forty-three missing.
LUNA: (a very small, very deliberate breath) *Find them.*

**Gameplay:** Evacuation: rescue civilians from the dome (timed); fire hazards.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The dome burns, and nobody will say who lit it.

## Episode 5 - "Carried"
**Scene:** The dome, interior. **Present:** all three.

*(Plack, kneeling by a boy in a doorway, a beam over both. She puts her shoulder to it.)*
PLACK: Kuu.
KUU: I'm here.
PLACK: Take him.
KUU: (taking the boy) And *you?*
PLACK: (grunting, the beam dropping, her hairline bleeding again) I'm *fine.* Go.
*(The beam falls. A silence. Then Kuu's voice, in the smoke, very steady.)*
KUU: Plack?
PLACK: (very quiet) ...I'm *fine.*
*(Kuu carries them both out. She does not let go of the boy's hand.)*

**Gameplay:** Rescue sequence; a quick-time carry.
**Beat:** Plack is hurt shielding a civilian; Kuu carries them out.

## Episode 6 - "One Door Open"
**Scene:** The Ark's central stair. **Present:** Luna.

*(Luna, running the length of the Ark with the Charter's last copy under her arm. At each gate a delegate; at each delegate a no.)*
GOVERNMENT DELEGATE: The government will hold the quarter.
COUNCIL DELEGATE: The Council will hold the dome.
SECRETS' ENVOY: The Secrets will hold the garrison.
LUNA: (breathless; to each) A door. *One* door. Leave *one* door open.
GOVERNMENT DELEGATE: To *what?*
LUNA: To *talking.*
GOVERNMENT DELEGATE: (a long beat) ...The east gate.
COUNCIL DELEGATE: ...The south.
*(Two doors. It is not nothing.)*

**Gameplay:** Run through three districts; short fights; delegate dialogue (BLUE).
**Beat:** Luna keeps two doors open.

## Episode 7 - "The Colossus Walks"
**Scene:** The government quarter. **Present:** all three.

*(A tremor. A boom in the street. A figure, not a mech, not a person: a colossal humanoid, built, like a weapon the arc has never used.)*
KUU: It's... *walking.*
PLACK: (staring, bandaged) It's never been *switched on.*
LUNA: Somebody switched it on.
*(The thing's shoulder guns rotate. They are pointed at the Operators' dome.)*
LUNA: (very quietly) ...No.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Colossus's escort (interceptors, brutes).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The government's weapon has been awakened.

## Episode 8 - "The Hardliner Colossus"
**Scene:** Government quarter. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Hardliner Colossus. Phase 1, shoulder guns; Phase 2, the colossus drops to its knees and uses its hands; climb it.)*
PLACK: (limping, determined) Aim for the joint.
KUU: Which one?
PLACK: *The big one.*
LUNA: (hurling a black hole at the shoulder gun) I'll take the *guns!*
*(The colossus falls like a tower in slow motion. The dome, behind them, still burns.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: climb the colossus; disable the guns; a final collapse.
**Beat:** The Colossus falls.

**Stinger:** On the surface, the hatches, every one of them, open. The angels stand in rows, facing the arc, from horizon to horizon. A
tall man in white stands at the head of the nearest rank. He raises one hand, and the whole row lifts its wings.
