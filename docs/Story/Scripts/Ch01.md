# Ch 01 - Neo Tokyo - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / LOCKED map (events authored).** Tutorial chapter. Luna is granted automatically; Kuu comes from the tutorial summon;
Plack joins after the boss. Rain, fog, rooftops, neon.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 40 s)
Rain on a million windows. Neo Tokyo from above: signs in three languages, a billboard of a smiling woman holding a umbrella-shaped
bottle of something. Down the side of a tower, past a vending machine that is also a shrine, to a rooftop. A girl is asleep on a
water tank with a black umbrella over her face. A pager goes off against her ribs.

## Episode 1 - "Pay Is Lunch"
**Scene:** Rooftop above the Tanabe Arcade. **Present:** Luna. Voice: Dispatch (pager).

DISPATCH (pager): Operator Luna. Job 4471. Guard one crate until the drop. Pay: lunch.
LUNA: Lunch.
DISPATCH: Lunch.
LUNA: Is it a good lunch or a lunch lunch?
DISPATCH: ...Lunch.
LUNA: (sits up, drops the umbrella over her shoulder) Fine. Commander, you can hear me? Good. Tell the nice voice I will guard
the crate and I will think about the lunch.
LUNA: (to herself) There is nothing under this sky I am not already bored of. Let us go be bored somewhere with a purpose.

**Gameplay:** Movement tutorial: walk, jump (double jump), climb the fire escape down to the street. Camera, sprint.
**Beat:** Luna takes the job. The first angel lights on a neon sign and doesn't move; a note to remember it.

## Episode 2 - "Please Read The Tutorial"
**Scene:** Back-street, Tanabe Arcade. **Present:** Luna. Angels: three basic.

COMMANDER (radio): *(silent; Luna answers)*
LUNA: Yes, I see them. Yes, I know. "Attack with the button." It says here the attack is called Attack. Whoever wrote this has
never been bored in their life.
LUNA: (hits one with the black hole; it drags in, the white hole pushes out) There. That is the move. You press one and the world
changes its mind.
LUNA: Second one. This one you hold. I would say it is a lot like patience, but I have never had any.
LUNA: Third. This is the one you use when you are in trouble. It is also the one you use when you are not, but you want to be.

**Gameplay:** Combat tutorial: basic attack, ability 1 (Blackhole), dodge, target switch. Three angel waves; the last drops a key (YELLOW).
**Beat:** The first mini-boss on the street (a Heavy angel) is introduced as a trial. The key opens the arcade shutter.

## Episode 3 - "Who Is Late"
**Scene:** Arcade interior, prize counter. **Present:** Luna, Kuu (arrives). The first summon (tutorial).

*(The player is prompted to summon. Light pours out of a claw-machine. Kuu falls out of it.)*
KUU: -- and that is why I said I'd be there at six, so I'm very sorry, I know it's half past, and I know that is *my* six, but --
(sees Luna) ...oh.
LUNA: You're late.
KUU: I know. I'm sorry. I'm sorry to the machine as well. (to the claw machine) Sorry.
LUNA: He came out of the machine. Commander, you are a person of genius and I hate to admit it.
KUU: Luna, it's -- you look well. You look like you haven't slept.
LUNA: I have been sleeping on a water tank since the world ended. It is surprisingly comfortable.
KUU: You can't --
LUNA: I can. I did. (beat) Come on. I have a crate to guard. It pays lunch.
KUU: ...Is it a good lunch?
LUNA: *That* is the question.

**Gameplay:** Summon tutorial; Kuu joins the squad (swap, team control). Short fight in the arcade with two enemies, then a shop (BLUE).
**Beat:** Kuu joins. They share a look that says they have done this before.

## Episode 4 - "The Crate Is Warm"
**Scene:** Loading dock behind the arcade. **Present:** Luna, Kuu. A crate stencilled with a sponsor mark (wing, small).

KUU: It's humming.
LUNA: Good. It means it works.
KUU: Crates don't hum, Luna.
LUNA: Crates are what we say they are.
*(A knock from inside. Three knocks. A pause. A fourth, annoyed.)*
KUU: ...Luna.
LUNA: I heard it.
KUU: Someone is --
LUNA: I *heard* it. (she pries the lid with a crowbar) Hello. I'm Luna. This is Kuu, he is apologising to you already.
*(A person in a courier jacket sits up inside the crate, pale, furious, with a ring on a chain around their neck.)*
PLACK: You are doing it wrong.
LUNA: Doing what wrong?
PLACK: All of it. (they bite Luna's glove) Who are you?

**Gameplay:** Environmental puzzle: unlock the crate. A short non-combat dialogue scene (PURPLE) with the first choice-free reaction cutscene.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The crate was never cargo. Plack, an unregistered courier, was the shipment.

## Episode 5 - "Knots"
**Scene:** Rooftops of the Tanabe district, rain. **Present:** Luna, Kuu, Plack. Angels: mixed.

KUU: A bowline is the one that doesn't slip. You make a rabbit hole --
PLACK: I know what a bowline is.
KUU: Then you know the rabbit.
PLACK: There is no rabbit.
KUU: There's always a rabbit.
LUNA: (below, hanging off a pipe) I would like to say I am impressed that you are both discussing knots over a drop of forty
metres. I am not impressed. I am bored. Climb faster.
PLACK: (climbing) Who sent the crate?
LUNA: The dispatcher.
PLACK: And who pays the dispatcher?
LUNA: ...The city?
PLACK: The city doesn't pay anyone anymore. Someone pays the *city*.

**Gameplay:** Climb tutorial: fire escapes, ledges, a rope-and-hook segment. Roof-to-roof jumps; a hanging sign as a bridge.
**Beat:** Plack refuses to name the sender. Luna notices the question and puts it in her pocket.

## Episode 6 - "Lunch Is Lunch"
**Scene:** Water tank on a rooftop (Luna's), evening. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: I am not going to the drop.
LUNA: It is on the way to lunch.
PLACK: The drop is where they kill the shipment.
KUU: *Kill* -- Luna, did you know that?
LUNA: I did not know that, Kuu, which is the only reason I am not shouting at it. (to Plack) Who is "they"?
PLACK: The people who pay the city.
LUNA: And what are you?
PLACK: Someone who does not want to be a crate.
*(A silence. Rain. A far-off siren.)*
LUNA: I am going to say a thing and I want you to take it as a thing I mean. We do not go to the drop.
KUU: Then where do we go? We have a job.
LUNA: We go to the drop *late.*
KUU: ...That's -- Luna, that is the same --
LUNA: It is a different drop. It is a drop with a plan in it.

**Gameplay:** A short stealth segment across a market roof; a BLUE NPC (a noodle seller) hints at the drop's location.
**Beat:** They choose to go to the drop, on their own terms. Plack joins the squad as a temporary escort (not yet playable).

## Episode 7 - "Someone Told Them"
**Scene:** Sponsor kiosk plaza; the drop point. Dense fog. **Present:** all three.

KUU: There's nobody here.
LUNA: Good.
PLACK: There should be nobody here.
KUU: ...Good?
PLACK: The kiosk is sponsor-owned. Sponsors keep staff. (looks up) There's none because they were told to leave.
*(A choir of angels rises on the roofs around the plaza, haloed in neon, singing one soft note.)*
LUNA: Oh. They *did* send someone.
KUU: They're -- they're singing --
PLACK: They were told where to be. Somebody told them.
LUNA: Somebody told the angels where a courier would be. (pause) Commander, this is the first interesting thing that has
happened to me this year, so please do not get us killed.

**Gameplay:** Wave defence (two waves); escort Plack across the plaza; mini-boss: a Brute with a sponsor banner.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The drop was a trap, and the trap is run by someone who can summon angels.

## Episode 8 - "The Neon Choir"
**Scene:** Plaza. Boss fight. **Present:** all three. Boss: the Neon Choir (a ring of neon-haloed singing angels).

KUU: It's not an attack, it's a *hymn*.
PLACK: Then it has a conductor. Find the one that isn't singing.
LUNA: (laughing, delighted) A boss with a theory! Plack, you're in.
*(Boss phases: Phase 1, choir ring; Phase 2, the conductor dives. Plack is unlocked as a playable operator when the boss falls.)*
PLACK: (after) ...Fine. I'll go with you. For now.
KUU: For now?
PLACK: Until I find out who pays.

**Gameplay:** Boss: ring phase, conductor phase. Plack becomes playable (MAIN: unlocked after the Chapter 1 boss).
**Beat:** The squad is three.

**Stinger:** Dawn. The pay arrives in the form of a cold sandwich, left on the water tank. Inside the wax paper there is a note in
a tidy hand: *Ask who is paying for the rain.* Luna reads it twice, puts it back in the wrapper, and eats the sandwich anyway.
