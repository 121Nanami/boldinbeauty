# Ch 25 - Adapt (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Side story: **Adapt** (6-star Secret, MAIN section 5). Characterisation not documented; PROPOSED here. Adapt's voice: calm, even, short sentences, a coach who never raises their voice and never lets a cadet get hurt on a course they cannot learn.

Chapter promise: the course adapts to the one running it, and the sponsor changes the ammunition.

---

## Episode 1 - Whistle

*The Academy Range: a steel-and-concrete campus of training halls, live-fire courses and a control tower, ringed by spectator stands. A banner: "CADET CUP: SPONSORED BY..." and the wing logo. ADAPT, in a visor and a track jacket, a whistle on a lanyard, stands at the starting line of a course.*

**[Cold open. Crowd murmur, bright morning, sports-commentary music.]**

ADAPT: *(to the cadets lined up, in an even voice)* First rule.

*Twelve cadets in branded armour, nervous, fidgeting.*

ADAPT: The course will change. That is its job.

CADET: *(raising a hand)* How do we know where it'll be?

ADAPT: *(a nod)* Good question. You do not. You will know where it *is*.

*They blow the whistle. A klaxon sounds. The course begins to shift, plates sliding, obstacles rising and sinking, floors tilting.*

**[Tutorial: Adapt's combat style: the player's moveset mutates with each enemy defeated, a "stack" of three adaptive stances.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Hall One

*A training hall. Targets pop up in rows. The player controls Adapt, demonstrating the course to a group of cadets.*

ADAPT: *(softly, to the nearest)* Watch the shoulder, not the muzzle.

CADET: *(whispering)* How do you know where it's going to be?

ADAPT: *(a faint smile)* I do not. I change when it does.

*A target swings. ADAPT shifts stance, and the cadets gasp. The target explodes in a burst of foam.*

ADAPT: *(without turning)* You try.

*The cadet takes a stance. The target swings. They miss. The course, to everyone's surprise, slows by a notch.*

CADET: *(blinking)* It... waited.

ADAPT: *(nodding)* It adapts to you. That is the point.

**[Gameplay beat: the course's difficulty matches the player's performance. Mechanically, enemy speed scales ±20% by hit rate.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Live Fire

*The second course, an open yard with a ring of targets and a row of live-fire turrets, painted to look like toys. A yellow sign: "LIVE ROUNDS: FOAM." A smaller sign beneath it, in marker: "Trust me. -A."*

*The cadets file in. A SPONSOR REPRESENTATIVE in a neat grey suit and a wing-logo lapel pin stands at the rail, clipboard in hand, watching.*

SPONSOR REP: *(to ADAPT, quietly)* Ratings are good.

ADAPT: *(without turning)* They are cadets.

SPONSOR REP: *(smoothly)* They are *talent*.

*ADAPT's jaw does not move.*

ADAPT: *(evenly)* They are learning.

SPONSOR REP: *(with a small smile)* Of course.

*He glances at his wrist-watch. A moment later, in the control tower, a small light changes from green to amber.*

**[Gameplay beat: a subtle cue. The player may notice the turrets' ammunition indicators flicker from "FOAM" to "---".]**

---

## Episode 4 - Swap (Midpoint)

*The yard, mid-run. A cadet, a girl no older than sixteen, sprints toward the final target. The turrets sweep. A shot rings out. Not foam: a flat crack, and a chip of concrete spits from the wall.*

*She freezes. The crowd's murmur falters.*

ADAPT: *(sharply)* Halt course.

*Nothing. The course continues. Turrets keep sweeping.*

ADAPT: *(louder, to the tower)* HALT!

*A response, over the PA, in a smooth, unhurried voice: the SPONSOR REP.*

SPONSOR REP: *(over the PA)* We're live. Nothing's wrong. It's a *real* course.

ADAPT: *(very quietly)* You changed the rounds.

SPONSOR REP: *(pleasantly)* We *upgraded*. Authenticity is what the audience wants.

*In the yard, a second shot. A cadet cries out, clutching an arm.*

*ADAPT's whistle drops on its lanyard. They look at the girl in the middle of the course, who is looking back, frozen, wide-eyed.*

ADAPT: *(softly)* Do not move.

*They step onto the course.*

---

## Episode 5 - The Course Adapts

*The yard. The turrets sweep. ADAPT walks the course, shielding the girl, deflecting rounds with a flicker of the arm, each time changing stance. The course, designed to adapt, adapts to them.*

**[Gameplay: escort. The player controls Adapt, shielding cadets across a live-fire course while the turrets learn the player's habits.]**

ADAPT: *(quietly, to the cadets)* On my count. Three. Two. *(a pause)* The course will adapt to my pattern.

CADET: *(whispering)* Then what do we do?

ADAPT: *(not turning)* Do not follow my pattern.

*Rounds crackle past. A turret locks on, swivels, fires. ADAPT's stance flows, their body pivoting, the shot ricocheting harmlessly.*

*The course, noticing, shifts: the turrets cease sweeping and begin to predict.*

*ADAPT's eyes narrow.*

ADAPT: *(very softly)* Good.

*They stop. Stand perfectly still, in the very centre of the yard.*

*Every turret, for a long, long second, aims. And then, uncertain, wavers.*

---

## Episode 6 - The Control Tower

*The control tower: a glass-walled chamber atop the range. A console, banks of switches, a window onto the yard. Two technicians cower in a corner. The SPONSOR REP stands in the middle, hands folded.*

ADAPT: *(entering, a thin smear of blood on their jacket)* Stand down the course.

SPONSOR REP: *(calmly)* I'm afraid I can't.

ADAPT: *(evenly)* That was not a request.

*A beat. ADAPT, with a faint, dangerous precision, tears up the Cup's printed rulebook, a thick pamphlet in a plastic sleeve, and lets the halves fall to the floor.*

ADAPT: *(softly)* These are my rules.

*They put a hand on the main console. Lights turn green, one by one.*

SPONSOR REP: *(for the first time, a trace of concern)* That's a contract violation.

ADAPT: *(not turning)* I am aware.

*Below, the turrets fall silent, one after another, like candles being snuffed.*

---

## Episode 7 - Warden

*The tower door bursts inward. A tall, armoured figure strides in, wing-logo on its shoulder, a long rifle in its arms, a helm without a face. THE SPONSOR'S WARDEN.*

**[Low point. The Warden sweeps the tower, and the player must hold it while cadets are evacuated.]**

WARDEN: *(a flat, voiceless voice)* The contract is binding.

ADAPT: *(squaring up)* The cadets are not.

WARDEN: *(raising the rifle)* They are assets.

ADAPT: *(very quiet)* They are *children*.

*A beat. The WARDEN fires. ADAPT slides, a graceful, impossible pivot, and the shot punches a neat hole in the wall.*

ADAPT: *(under their breath)* Course adapts.

*They snatch the whistle from their lanyard, and blow it, once, sharp.*

*Every cadet, in the stands, on the range, in the corridors, hears it. And moves.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Sponsor's Warden (Boss)

**[Boss: The Sponsor's Warden. Phase 1: long-range rifle; use pillars as cover. Phase 2: it deploys "contract bots", small drones that tether to cadets; free them. Phase 3: it swaps its rifle for a close-range blade-array; the player must adapt the stance stack to counter each attack in sequence.]**

*The fight is the most mechanical in the chapter: each of the WARDEN's attacks is a pattern, each of ADAPT's responses is a counter. The cadets, hiding behind the stands, watch.*

*At last, ADAPT drives a single, perfect strike through the WARDEN's visor. The helm comes apart. Inside, nothing. A hollow suit.*

*ADAPT stands over it, breathing hard.*

ADAPT: *(quietly)* An asset.

*They turn, and sees, at the rail, a small group of cadets, looking at them. The girl from the yard. They are all very still.*

CADET: *(a small voice)* Coach?

ADAPT: *(softly)* Yes.

CADET: *(a pause)* The course adapted.

ADAPT: *(a ghost of a smile)* It did.

### Stinger

*The yard, empty. LUNA, hands in pockets, waits at the rail. ADAPT approaches, jacket torn, whistle in hand.*

ADAPT: You are the one who is uniting us.

LUNA: *(dry)* That's the plan.

ADAPT: *(evenly)* I will join.

LUNA: *(blinking)* On what terms?

ADAPT: *(without hesitation)* One condition.

*They hold out the whistle. LUNA takes it.*

ADAPT: *(softly)* The course must adapt to *you*.

LUNA: *(turning the whistle in her fingers)* What does that mean?

ADAPT: *(a faint smile)* I do not know yet. *(a pause)* That is why it is a condition.

*LUNA, slowly, puts the whistle round her neck.*
