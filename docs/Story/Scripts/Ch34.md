# Ch 34 - The Night Before - Script

**Arc 2 / 2016 / NEW.** Main cast. The Operators' dome on the eve of the signing: parties, apologies, sabotage. The quietest chapter and
the longest. The Narrator's last move.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A dome full of lights. Banners, bunting, a trestle table with a cake that says CHARTER in icing, mis-spelt. A crowd of operators,
Secrets, cooks, reformers, children. Over it all a single, hand-lettered banner: TOMORROW.
KUU: (to the toaster, tenderly) You will be *very* useful tomorrow.

## Episode 1 - "The Cake"
**Scene:** The dome floor. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

ARITA: (rushing up with a knife) We can't cut it yet, it's for the morning!
LUNA: What does it say?
ARITA: "Charter."
LUNA: It says "Chater."
ARITA: (looking) ...It says "Chater."
SHIRAISHI: (arriving, a ledger under one arm) Item three hundred and four.
ARITA: I'll *fix* it!
ISHII: (counting) You'll make it worse.
PLACK: (smiling) Leave it. It's *right.*
LUNA: It's wrong.
PLACK: (gently) It's *ours.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat hub: talk to a dozen NPCs (BLUE), light party minigames.
**Beat:** The eve of the signing, warm and chaotic.

## Episode 2 - "Kitchen Arguments"
**Scene:** The dome's kitchens. **Present:** all three.

*(Reformer A and Reformer B, up to their elbows in dishwater, in an argument that has apparently been going for four hours.)*
REFORMER A: It was *forty-seven.*
REFORMER B: It was *forty-nine.*
REFORMER A: I was *there!*
REFORMER B: *I* was *there!* (to Kuu) Young man, settle this. How many people were in the room?
KUU: (very carefully) ...Sorry?
REFORMER B: (impatient) The room in ninety-eight.
KUU: I'm... not... old enough --
REFORMER A: He's *no use.*
KUU: Sorry.
*(Plack hands both a towel.)*

**Gameplay:** Hub; a dish-washing minigame; BLUE pickups.
**Beat:** Warm, comic; the coalition is a family.

## Episode 3 - "The Roof"
**Scene:** The dome's rooftop. **Present:** Luna, Kuu.

*(Two people on a roof, two bowls of soup, a city a long way below.)*
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: Yes.
KUU: About the third game.
LUNA: (very still) ...Yes.
KUU: I've been trying to think of a way to say it so that it's not about me.
LUNA: Kuu --
KUU: You didn't ask me. You *knew* the price was me. And you didn't ask.
LUNA: (very quietly) I know.
KUU: Would you have asked, if you'd had time?
LUNA: I don't know.
KUU: (nodding) That's... *honest.*
LUNA: It's the only thing I've got.
KUU: (a small smile) It's a start.
*(They finish the soup. They do not settle it. It is a good evening.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a quiet conversation; optional dialogue prompts (cosmetic).
**Beat:** Luna and Kuu talk and do not settle it.

## Episode 4 - "A Thousand Copies"
**Scene:** The dome's undercroft. **Present:** all three.

*(A low roar from below. Through a grille, a printing hall. A forest of presses. A thousand sheets, wet, dropping onto a belt.)*
PLACK: (reading a sheet, her face going still) This is the Charter.
KUU: It's got a typo.
PLACK: It's not a typo. It's a *rewrite.* (she turns the page) Clause four. "Operators shall be *ratified.*" Clause nine: "Subject to
review by the *Narrator's office.*"
LUNA: (very evenly) They've *finished* it for us.
PLACK: A thousand copies. By morning.
KUU: (hoarse) And nobody's going to read it.
*(A voice from the dark, kindly.)*
NARRATOR: They read the *first* one.

**Gameplay:** Infiltration; a printing hall; press guards (RED).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The Narrator has forged a Charter.

## Episode 5 - "Plack Fixes A Page"
**Scene:** Printing hall. **Present:** all three.

*(Plack at the press, with a quill-pen and a bottle of correction fluid, correcting the master plate, one line at a time, with a very steady hand.)*
PLACK: "Operators shall *not* be ratified."
KUU: (holding the lamp) That's not how it works.
PLACK: It's how it works *now.*
LUNA: (watching her) Plack.
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: When this is over, I'd like to say a thing.
PLACK: (not looking up) Say it now.
LUNA: It's too big for a press hall.
PLACK: (a smile at the plate) Then *write* it down.

**Gameplay:** A puzzle: correct the plate in the right order (time pressure); guards (RED).
**Beat:** Plack fixes the forgery by hand.

## Episode 6 - "On The Roof, Again"
**Scene:** The dome's roof. **Present:** Luna, Plack.

*(Under the stars the arc has painted on its ceiling. Plack and Luna on the roof, not talking. Then talking.)*
LUNA: I treated Kuu like a coin.
PLACK: Yes.
LUNA: I treated *you* like one.
PLACK: Sometimes.
LUNA: I'm... trying. (a beat) I don't know how to say it properly.
PLACK: (still looking at the sky) You can say it badly.
LUNA: I love you. I'm *terrible* at this.
PLACK: (a laugh; a short one) You're *very* bad at it.
LUNA: I'd like to be *better.*
PLACK: (turning at last) Then keep going.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** Luna is honest to Plack.

## Episode 7 - "The Final Edition"
**Scene:** The printing hall, again. **Present:** all three.

*(The presses stop. One by one, the forest of machines aligns into a single, vast, house-sized press with dozens of long arms. It has
a face of rolled paper. It speaks in headlines.)*
FINAL EDITION: *EXTRA! EXTRA!* THE CHARTER IS RATIFIED.
KUU: It's got a *voice.*
PLACK: It's got a *deadline.*
FINAL EDITION: *THREE WITNESSES DISSENT.* (an arm slams down) *DISSENT OVERRULED.*
LUNA: Oh, *good.* It's got *editorial opinion.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: press arms (dodge, break).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The press fights for the forgery.

## Episode 8 - "The Final Edition"
**Scene:** The printing hall. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Final Edition. Phase 1, press arms and headline slams (each headline is a warning); Phase 2, it prints a fake squad.)*
FINAL EDITION: *LUNA FALLS!* *PLACK FALLS!* *KUU APOLOGISES AND FALLS!*
KUU: I'm *not* going to --
*(The press prints a headline. A fake Kuu steps out of the paper.)*
KUU: (to the fake) I'm so sorry. (he hits it with the toaster) I'm really, really *sorry.*
*(The fake tears like newsprint. The press's arms, one by one, go slack.)*
FINAL EDITION: (as the last roller stops) ...EXTRA...

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge headline slams; hit fake copies; break the plate.
**Beat:** The Final Edition falls. The forged copies are burnt.

**Stinger:** In the ashes the Narrator, who has been standing quietly behind them the whole time, folds their hands. "She said that she
would write whatever I liked," the Narrator says. Luna: "Under my name." A very long silence. The Narrator, after a very long time: "Yes." The Narrator
picks up one of the surviving sheets, reads it, and, with a small pen, signs it.
