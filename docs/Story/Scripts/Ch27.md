# Ch 27 - Ordinary Hours - Script

**Arc 2 / 2011 / SIDE STORY: Ishii, Shiraishi and Arita.** Three three-star operators go to work. Nobody is a hero. A shift, a heist,
a table with a sign on it. The loudest chapter is the quietest.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A canteen at 5:40 a.m. Three trays. A broken kettle. A sign on the wall: *Please report faults to Facilities.* Under it, in marker:
*We did. It's been 14 months.*
ISHII: (deadpan, counting) Twelve minutes late for breakfast. Eleven for the kettle.
SHIRAISHI: (grumpy) I'm putting that in the book.
ARITA: (cheerful, taping something) Done! It's a very strong tape.

## Episode 1 - "Talent"
**Scene:** The Barracks. **Present:** Ishii, Shiraishi, Arita (playable guests). A manager (BLUE).

MANAGER: Good morning, talent.
SHIRAISHI: We're not talent.
MANAGER: You're *branded.* That's talent.
ISHII: (counting) Third time this week he's said "talent."
MANAGER: Today's shift is the Sponsor Mall. You'll stand in front of a display and be seen. You are not to fight, you are not to speak,
and you are not to *touch* the merchandise.
ARITA: What if the merchandise falls?
MANAGER: Then it's *unfortunate.* (a flicker of a smile) You're branded, not *insured.*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: three-operator party; a manager's dialogue; armour fitting.
**Beat:** Introduce the three; the brand on their armour doesn't fit.

## Episode 2 - "The Mall"
**Scene:** The Sponsor Mall. **Present:** all three.

*(A mall of glass and neon, sponsor logos on every pane, drones with advertising faces drifting between the shoppers.)*
ARITA: (adjusting a plate) This doesn't fit.
ISHII: It was made for someone taller.
ARITA: It was made for someone *posed.*
SHIRAISHI: (writing) Item twelve, ill-fitting armour. Item thirteen, the drone face.
BRANDED DRONE: (in a bright voice) Have you considered a *lifestyle upgrade?*
SHIRAISHI: (without looking up) That's item fourteen.

**Gameplay:** Mall combat (branded drones, RED); a pickup trail (YELLOW).
**Beat:** The mall as a joke and a prison.

## Episode 3 - "The Ledger Of Grievances"
**Scene:** A stockroom. **Present:** all three.

SHIRAISHI: (taking out a worn notebook) One year. Three hundred and sixty-four grievances. Do you know how many have been addressed?
ISHII: Zero.
SHIRAISHI: *Zero.* (she turns pages) One for every day. A good grievance is a thing you can *count.*
ARITA: I'd like to propose a *thing.*
ISHII: A thing.
ARITA: We do not ask Facilities. We ask... ourselves.
ISHII: ...Ourselves.
ARITA: We do *some* of it. The little things first.
SHIRAISHI: (putting her pen down; for the first time, almost smiling) That's *item one.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE); a grievance-list puzzle (which can be fixed by whom).
**Beat:** The three take small action.

## Episode 4 - "A Month's Wages"
**Scene:** The sponsor vault. **Present:** all three.

*(The vault's door ajar. A large steel shelf, a ledger, a stack of pay envelopes tagged "Withheld".)*
ISHII: There are four hundred of them.
SHIRAISHI: That's every operator in the Barracks.
ARITA: (reading a tag) "Withheld pending review."
ISHII: What review?
ARITA: (turning the envelope) ...Doesn't say.
*(A silence. They look at the envelopes, at each other.)*
SHIRAISHI: (quietly) That is a month. That's *our* month.
ISHII: And the one before.
SHIRAISHI: And the one before *that.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); vault exploration; a locked gate.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The wages have been in a vault all along.

## Episode 5 - "The Heist"
**Scene:** The mall, the vault. **Present:** all three.

*(A plan of disarming simplicity: one lift, one mop bucket, and the tape.)*
ARITA: I'll tape the door.
ISHII: Tape's not a lock.
ARITA: It's a very *persuasive* tape.
SHIRAISHI: (reading a list) The lift goes from four to nine in ninety seconds. The guard's rota changes at ten past. We have
eleven seconds.
ISHII: That's not enough.
SHIRAISHI: It's *eleven.* It's *in the book.*
*(They go, very badly, in perfect order.)*

**Gameplay:** A stealth-heist set piece (timed lift ride; a mop-bucket distraction).
**Beat:** A small, loud, over-prepared heist.

## Episode 6 - "COUNCIL"
**Scene:** The canteen, night. **Present:** all three.

*(The envelopes, one per operator, spread out on a long table. A queue of people in stickered armour is filing in. Arita has found a
piece of cardboard and a marker.)*
ARITA: What do we call it?
ISHII: A table.
SHIRAISHI: A committee.
ARITA: (writing) ...COUNCIL.
SHIRAISHI: Too grand.
ARITA: It's *capital letters.* They're not grand; they're *loud.*
ISHII: (looking at the sign) It'll be taken down by morning.
ARITA: (propping it against the sugar bowl) Then we'll put it back.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE); the table scene; the first Council vote (a scripted choice-free).
**Beat:** The Operators have a table.

## Episode 7 - "Compliance"
**Scene:** The canteen, a visit. **Present:** all three.

*(A tall mech in sponsor grey enters. A clipboard in one hand. A very large stamp in the other.)*
COMPLIANCE OFFICER: This is an unauthorised gathering.
SHIRAISHI: It's a canteen.
COMPLIANCE OFFICER: Which is not a *forum.* (it consults the clipboard) You have forty seconds to disperse.
ISHII: We have forty seconds to *finish.*
COMPLIANCE OFFICER: (stamping the table; the stamp leaves a red *DENIED* on the wood) Thirty-nine.
ARITA: (low) Is this a good time to say we have a *plan?*
ISHII: (counting) Thirty-eight.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Compliance's guards (a canteen-arena fight with tables as cover).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The table is denied.

## Episode 8 - "The Compliance Officer"
**Scene:** The canteen. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Compliance Officer. Phase 1, stamp slams that "deny" sections of floor; Phase 2, the clipboard fires paper cutters.)*
SHIRAISHI: (throwing the ledger of grievances like a javelin) Item one hundred and four!
COMPLIANCE OFFICER: *Denied.*
ARITA: (taping the stamp) It's *very* persuasive tape.
*(The stamp stops; the mech's hand jams in mid-air.)*
ISHII: (counting down) Three. Two. One.
*(They hit it all at once. It falls onto the tables, and the sign stays upright.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: three-operator attack synchronisation; tape the stamp; ledger throw.
**Beat:** The Compliance Officer falls. The COUNCIL sign stays up.

**Stinger:** Morning. The three of them go back to work. They are on time. Somewhere across the Barracks Luna's recruiters stop at a
table with a sign on it. "That's the thing," one says. "That's the thing she's been looking for." Behind them, Ishii, not looking up:
"Item two."
