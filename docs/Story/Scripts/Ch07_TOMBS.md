# Ch 07 - T.O.M.B.S, The Old Man's Battings Site (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch07_TOMBS`; owner brief: a structure larger than any man-made structure; inside it looks like a regular office, yet holds many "Angel"-based secrets. Comic register: this is the chapter where the horror wears a lanyard.

Chapter promise: the most dangerous building in the arc has a coffee machine and a visitor's book.

---

## Episode 1 - Reception

*A black shell, taller than the cavern ceiling should allow, with a single door at its base and a sign: T.O.M.B.S. Inside: a lobby with a plant, a desk, a bell, a clock that chimes the wrong hour.*

**[Cold open. A long shot from outside, tilting up to the top of the shell, which is out of frame. Cut to a small, ordinary lobby.]**

RECEPTIONIST: *(without looking up)* Good afternoon. Do you have an appointment?

LUNA: We have a filing box to collect.

RECEPTIONIST: Name?

LUNA: Luna.

RECEPTIONIST: *(typing)* Luna. L-U-N-A. *(pause)* I have you down for Thursday.

PLACK: It's Tuesday.

RECEPTIONIST: Then you're early. *(she smiles, the smile of someone being paid not to feel anything)* Please sign the visitor's book.

*She slides a thick ledger across. LUNA signs "Luna, the Great". PLACK writes her own name with care. KUU hesitates, then signs: "Kuu (sorry)".*

*A bell on the desk. LUNA rings it. Then again. Nineteen times.*

RECEPTIONIST: *(evenly)* Please don't ring the bell.

LUNA: *(still ringing)* Why?

RECEPTIONIST: It's the fire alarm.

*LUNA stops.*

**[Tutorial: a non-combat stealth segment. The player must navigate the office without triggering "Compliance" alarms: don't run, don't ring bells, stay on the carpet.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Open Plan

*The first floor. Cubicles in rows. Computer monitors glow with spreadsheets. Coffee cups on every desk. No people. A copier running on its own.*

KUU: *(whispering)* Where is everyone?

PLACK: Break.

KUU: A very long break.

LUNA: *(sitting at a desk, swivelling)* This is the nicest chair I've ever sat in.

PLACK: Luna.

LUNA: I'm doing reconnaissance.

*She opens a drawer. Inside: a stapler, a framed photo of a cat, and a sticky note: "DO NOT OPEN DOOR 5." She closes the drawer, opens another. "DO NOT OPEN DOOR 4."*

LUNA: *(delighted)* There's a theme.

PLACK: Don't.

LUNA: I'm only reading.

*KUU vaults a desk in a single graceful leap, lands, and looks briefly astonished at himself.*

KUU: I did that.

LUNA: You did that.

KUU: I don't normally do that.

**[Combat: Reception Drones and Cubicle Wardens. The enemies behave like office workers: they're polite, slow, and attack only when you step off the carpet. Optional puzzle: find the coffee machine's missing component.]**

*The coffee machine, when repaired, produces a perfect cup of coffee. LUNA takes it, sips, and goes very still.*

LUNA: *(reverently)* It's the best I've ever had.

PLACK: *(suspicious)* That's what worries me.

---

## Episode 3 - Minutes

*A meeting room, third floor. Glass walls. A long table laid with notepads and water jugs. A projector displays a single slide: "AGENDA: ITEM 1: THE QUESTION."*

KUU: What's the question?

PLACK: *(lifting a printed minute from the table)* "Meeting held 14 November 2002. Present: eleven. Apologies: nil. Item one: the question. Resolved: yes."

LUNA: Yes to what?

PLACK: It doesn't say.

*A pause. LUNA reads the minute herself, upside-down.*

LUNA: That's it? "Resolved: yes."

PLACK: There are no further minutes.

*She checks the filing cabinet, the shelves, a wall calendar. Every other day is blank.*

KUU: *(very quietly)* Eleven people decided something, in one afternoon, and then never wrote anything down again.

PLACK: Or wrote it somewhere else.

*LUNA taps the projector. The slide changes: "ITEM 2: THE ANSWER." A pause. Then: "ITEM 3: THE OPERATORS."*

*The projector clicks off.*

**[Gameplay beat: a clue board in the player's journal begins to fill. Plack's ledger gains a new tab: "Questions".]**

---

## Episode 4 - Five Doors (Midpoint)

*A corridor on the sixth floor. Five plain walls. LUNA, running her fingertips along them, finds a seam. She pushes. A section of wall swings open on a silent hinge.*

KUU: *(whispering)* It's a door.

PLACK: It was a wall.

LUNA: It's a very committed wall.

*Behind it, a narrow room. A desk, a lamp, a single manila folder. Stencilled on the cover in red: ANGEL V1.*

PLACK: *(very softly)* Don't touch it.

*LUNA touches it.*

**[Cutscene: the folder opens in LUNA's hands. We don't see inside. Only the three faces lit from below by a pale glow.]**

KUU: What does it say?

LUNA: *(after a long pause)* It says... *(she closes the folder)* It's too long to read.

PLACK: Luna.

LUNA: *(handing it over)* You do it.

*PLACK reads. Her face doesn't change. Her fingers tighten on the edge of the folder.*

PLACK: It's a project file. Specs. Containment. A list of names.

KUU: Names of what?

PLACK: Of... *(she looks up, very carefully neutral)* of who they're making next.

*Silence. Then LUNA, almost cheerfully:*

LUNA: There are four more doors.

**[Gameplay: the player opens four more hidden doors. Each contains a folder. Two are labelled ANGEL V1, two OPERATOR V!. The fifth is empty, with a ring stain where a folder once sat.]**

---

## Episode 5 - Floor Six

*A conference room, set for a meeting that has just ended. Chairs pushed back. Half-finished cups of tea. A whiteboard, wiped except for the corner.*

PLACK: *(reading the corner)* "Q3 targets. Angel: 100%. Operator: 1%. Reconcile."

KUU: What's reconcile?

PLACK: To make two things agree.

KUU: *(very slowly)* So they're trying to make the operators agree with the angels.

PLACK: Or the angels agree with us.

*LUNA is staring at the whiteboard.*

LUNA: The one percent.

PLACK: It's the same number as the cubes.

LUNA: *(very quietly)* So every operator's one-percent angel.

PLACK: Yes.

LUNA: And the one hundred percent angel...

PLACK: *(flatly)* Is a whole one.

*A silence. The clock on the wall, a standard office clock, ticks. KUU turns away from the whiteboard.*

KUU: I'd like to stop reading things.

LUNA: *(gently)* We're nearly done.

**[Traversal level: elevator shaft climbs. Enemies: Filing Sentries that patrol in shafts. Luna presses every elevator button; the building adds a note to a form.]**

---

## Episode 6 - The Form

*An elevator, jammed halfway between the eighth and ninth floors. A small panel flickers: "ERROR: FORM INCOMPLETE."*

LUNA: *(to the panel)* What form?

PANEL: Please enter your reason for visiting.

LUNA: Curiosity.

PANEL: Please enter a *valid* reason for visiting.

LUNA: *(to PLACK)* Is curiosity not valid?

PLACK: It's valid. It's just not on the list.

*LUNA considers.*

LUNA: Lunch.

PANEL: *(a long pause)* Accepted.

*The elevator lurches upward. KUU holds on to a rail, white-knuckled.*

KUU: Every time I think I understand this place, it's more ridiculous.

PLACK: It's the ridiculous that frightens me.

KUU: Why?

PLACK: *(looking at the closing doors)* Because it's so well-organised. You can't organise something like this by accident.

*The elevator stops. The doors open on the ninth floor.*

**[Low point. A single desk, in the middle of an empty floor. Behind it, a man in a suit, perfectly pressed, white-haired, smiling. A nameplate: AUDITOR.]**

---

## Episode 7 - Compliance

AUDITOR: *(rising)* Operator Luna. Operator Plack. Operator Kuu. Welcome to T.O.M.B.S. I trust you've enjoyed your visit?

LUNA: The coffee was excellent.

AUDITOR: Thank you. It's a point of pride.

PLACK: *(carefully)* What do you audit?

AUDITOR: *(warmly)* Everything. Time, expenditure, compliance. And, of course, *deviation*.

*He gestures, and a cabinet slides open. Inside, three forms, already typed with their names.*

AUDITOR: You have, between you, deviated from the visiting protocol on one hundred and four occasions. This is a significant overage.

KUU: *(whispering)* One hundred and four?

AUDITOR: You rang the bell nineteen times.

LUNA: In fairness, I enjoyed it.

AUDITOR: *(gently)* That is also noted.

*He holds out a pen.*

AUDITOR: If you sign, we can close the matter. If not, I'm obliged to complete the form on your behalf.

LUNA: *(smiling)* You know what? I'll fill it in myself.

*She takes the pen. Writes. Hands the form back. The AUDITOR reads it, and for the first time, his smile falters.*

AUDITOR: *(reading aloud)* "Reason for visit: to see how you did it."

LUNA: *(sweetly)* So, how did you?

*A silence.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Auditor (Boss)

**[Boss: The Auditor. Phase 1: forms in the air; stand in the sign-off circle to lose a buff. Phase 2: he calls "Reception Drones" in waves and files them in a cabinet behind him; destroy the cabinet. Phase 3: he removes his jacket, revealing angel wings folded beneath the shirt; speed and burst windows.]**

AUDITOR: *(coldly, the smile gone)* You were not scheduled for this.

LUNA: *(cheerful)* Neither were you.

*The fight is brisk and absurd: paper blades, filing-cabinet shields, stapler traps. KUU, in the middle of it, sets off the fire alarm on purpose, and for the first time the office lights go red.*

AUDITOR: *(horrified)* That is a *violation!*

KUU: *(a very small, very proud voice)* Sorry!

*The AUDITOR falters. LUNA's black hole draws him into the desk. A thunderous implosion of paper. When the dust settles, a single suit jacket lies on the floor, neatly folded.*

*Silence. Then, throughout the building, every elevator descends at once. Every light flickers. The lobby clock, far below, chimes a time that is not the time.*

PLACK: *(listening)* That's not the hour.

LUNA: What hour is it?

PLACK: *(very quietly)* I don't think it's an hour at all.

### Stinger

*The lobby. The RECEPTIONIST is at her desk. The plant has moved from the left of the desk to the right.*

RECEPTIONIST: *(not looking up)* Thank you for visiting. Please remember to sign out.

*LUNA signs: "Luna, the Great. Will return."*

*As the squad leaves, the RECEPTIONIST's pen continues to move across the page of its own accord, writing a line beneath LUNA's: "We know."*

**[Chapter 7 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 8. Seeds: ANGEL V1 and OPERATOR V! project files; "Reconcile"; the eleven-person meeting in 2002; the fifth empty folder; the Receptionist's last line.]**
