# Ch 02 - The Ruins (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of the existing map `L_Ch02_Ruins`; owner brief: "based on the Outer World, on top of the arc". Voices as in Ch 01. New today: **PLACK** is now a full squad member and carries the ledgers; **THE PEDDLER** is a one-chapter comic NPC.

Chapter promise: a salvage job on the surface becomes the first proof that the angels are not wandering.

---

## Episode 1 - Hatch Sixteen

*The Ark's service lift lets out onto a concrete pad under a bruise-coloured sky. Sixteen round hatches in a grid, numbered in faded paint. Fog, dust, ash that doesn't fall so much as drift.*

**[Cold open: the squad's first step onto the surface. No combat. A long camera pull-back reveals the shape of the city: tilted towers, a school with half a roof, a broken stadium bowl.]**

COMMANDER: *(radio)* Unit Luna, you are now outside. Atmosphere is survivable. Do not take your helmets off. You don't have helmets. Do not take your, uh, faces off.

LUNA: Sixteen hatches. It's like a very depressing advent calendar.

PLACK: Fifteen are sealed from the inside. We came up through number nine. *(she checks the sheet on her clipboard)* The job: recover a field recorder from Operator Hasegawa's last known position. School, then police station, then wherever the signal stops.

KUU: Operator Hasegawa. Is he... I mean, was he...

PLACK: He didn't come back.

KUU: Oh. *(quietly)* Sorry, Hasegawa.

LUNA: *(crouching to the hatch)* Stuck.

KUU: What's stuck?

LUNA: The hinge. It's rusted open and it'll bang every time the wind moves. *(she looks around, finds a spanner, unscrews the hinge pin, pockets it)* There. Quiet.

PLACK: You just stole a hinge.

LUNA: Salvaged. Different job title.

**[Tutorial of surface rules: dust cloud visibility, sound carries. First enemies: three basic angels standing very still on a nearby rooftop, facing the same direction. They don't react.]**

KUU: They're not moving.

LUNA: They're relaxed. Good for them.

*PLACK writes something on the clipboard and doesn't say what.*

## Episode 2 - Roll Call

*The school. Half the roof is gone. Classrooms on the second floor are open to the sky, desks still in rows. A noticeboard in the hall with curling paper.*

**[Exploration level. Combat in two classrooms. Pickups: notes on desks (lore), a lost-and-found box (Kuu's optional quest).]**

PLACK: *(reading the noticeboard aloud)* "Class 2-B, bake sale, Friday. Bring your own bag. The nurse's office will be closed Thursday." *(beat)* This was a Tuesday.

KUU: How do you know?

PLACK: The lunch menu. Tuesday is curry.

LUNA: *(opening a desk)* There's a note in here. "Aiko, I know it was you. Return the pen." *(beat)* This is the most important document I've ever read.

KUU: Please don't read other people's notes.

LUNA: They're dead, Kuu. They've waived it.

KUU: *(very quiet)* That's not how that works.

*LUNA stops. A pause. She closes the desk lid gently.*

LUNA: ...You're right. Sorry.

**[Optional: Kuu hunts a "lost-and-found" box on the first floor. He believes there's a lunchbox in it that someone left behind. He's been thinking about it since the lift.]**

KUU: Sorry, I need to look in the lost-and-found.

LUNA: Why?

KUU: There's a rumour. A lunchbox. With a, um, sandwich in it. Still wrapped. I read it on a forum.

PLACK: There's a forum?

KUU: There's a forum for everything.

*He finds the box. Inside: a single mitten, a library card, a wrapped sandwich. KUU holds it up like a trophy.*

KUU: It's real.

PLACK: Is it safe?

KUU: It's from 2002.

PLACK: Then don't eat it.

KUU: *(clutching it)* I won't. I just wanted to know it existed.

**[Pickup: Lost Lunchbox. Lore: the school's emergency drill sheet, last modified 2002. Everyone evacuated downward. None of the sheet's evacuation routes lead to the Ark.]**

## Episode 3 - The Peddler

*Outside the school gate, a figure is sitting on a pushcart loaded with metal junk, a huge backpack at their feet. It is the Scrap PEDDLER, weathered, grinning, with a smile that has seen better ordinances.*

PEDDLER: Hinges! Valves! Real pre-collapse bolts, none of this post-collapse nonsense. Best prices on the surface.

LUNA: *(delighted)* You're the only person on the surface.

PEDDLER: That makes me both the best and the worst.

PLACK: *(whispering)* Is he an operator?

PEDDLER: I can hear you, miss. I'm a freelancer.

*LUNA gets a look on her face.*

LUNA: How much for a hinge pin?

PEDDLER: *(looks at the spanner)* Funny. I just sold one of those. Fresh, from hatch nine. Forty credits.

LUNA: *(very slowly)* From hatch nine.

PEDDLER: Lovely little thing. Ever so slightly warm.

*A silence. KUU looks at LUNA's pocket. It's empty. The PEDDLER has the pin, somehow, in his palm.*

LUNA: That was in my pocket.

PEDDLER: *(innocent)* Was it?

*A short, extremely petty argument follows.*

**[Dialogue mini-game: haggle. Luna's profound-sounding nonsense reduces the price by 5% each time it's used. Success unlocks a one-time discount at the Ark's shop; failure still gets the pin back.]**

LUNA: In the light of the cosmos, forty credits is a measurement of how much we are willing to be afraid of each other.

PEDDLER: ...Thirty-five.

LUNA: A hinge is merely a door's apology.

PEDDLER: Thirty.

PLACK: *(under her breath)* I hate that it works.

**[Peddler provides the next route hint: "The stadium's where the signal stops. Don't go in the front."]**

## Episode 4 - The Last Entry (Midpoint)

*The police and fire station, partly flooded. An evidence room with a steel door, an actual fire truck that still, impossibly, has a battery. A cat watches them from the hose rack.*

**[Combat: ranged angels in the garage, brutes on the upper walkway. Puzzle: the evidence room lock takes a four-digit code, found on a whiteboard in the break room.]**

KUU: Is that a cat?

PLACK: It's a cat.

LUNA: Is it a cat-shaped angel?

PLACK: It's a cat.

*The CAT blinks, stretches, and vanishes behind a shelf. Nobody pursues it.*

*In the evidence room, a field recorder sits on a table, next to an operator's helmet. PLACK lifts it. Presses play. Static, then a voice, tired and professional.*

HASEGAWA: *(recording)* Entry nineteen. Station's clear. The, uh, angels aren't wandering. I've been watching them since sunrise. They stand in rows, all facing the same direction. West. They're not attacking. They're... counting us.

*A pause. A distant sound, a long soft hum.*

HASEGAWA: *(recording)* Entry twenty. They know I'm here. They're all looking this way now. I think the, uh. I think the counting's done.

*The recording ends.*

KUU: *(very small)* Sorry. Hasegawa.

LUNA: *(after a long moment)* Counting.

PLACK: He said counting.

LUNA: *(looking at the window)* They were facing west when we came up.

PLACK: They're still facing west.

**[Gameplay beat: from this point, all surface angels are placed in rows, facing west. They don't attack unless approached.]**

## Episode 5 - The Hospital

*A hospital. Long white corridors, beds in rows, every bed with a name card clipped to the end. The handwriting on every card is identical.*

**[Horror-lite level. No ranged enemies; the angels here are placed standing at bedsides. Combat is slower, more ambush-based.]**

PLACK: *(walking slowly, reading cards)* Mori. Tanaka. Fujii. Honda. *(she stops)* Same hand. Every one.

KUU: Maybe the nurse...

PLACK: Same pen. Same slant. Same loop on the "k". These were written in one session.

LUNA: *(looking at the beds)* There aren't any bodies.

PLACK: No.

LUNA: Not even bones.

PLACK: No.

*The three move on in silence. Even LUNA doesn't joke. KUU, behind them, whispers to each bed as he passes.*

KUU: Sorry. Sorry. Sorry.

*PLACK turns, and for the first time, speaks gently.*

PLACK: Kuu.

KUU: *(startled)* Sorry!

PLACK: You don't have to apologise to a bed.

KUU: I know. It's just... what else do you say?

*PLACK looks at him a long moment. She doesn't answer. She looks at the cards again, then takes out her ledger and adds a line.*

**[Optional pickup: a clipboard in the nurse's station: "Evacuation order 14-C. All patients to Sublevel 9. Nurses to remain." No Sublevel 9 appears on any Ark map.]**

## Episode 6 - A Reasonable Man

*The stadium approach. A tunnel of cracked concrete, floodlights dead overhead. PLACK has stopped talking. LUNA is humming. KUU is rehearsing something under his breath.*

KUU: Luna.

LUNA: Hm?

KUU: When we're done, can we go home by a different route?

LUNA: Why?

KUU: I don't like the way the hospital looked at me.

LUNA: *(stopping)* It didn't look at you.

KUU: It looked at me.

*LUNA considers this seriously, then nods.*

LUNA: Different route. Done.

*PLACK adjusts her satchel.*

PLACK: There's a problem with the route. The signal on the recorder stops at the stadium. That's where Hasegawa was last. But there's something wrong with the entry numbering. He said "entry twenty" at the station. But the log shows nineteen entries before it.

LUNA: So there's a missing entry.

PLACK: There's a missing entry between the station and the stadium. Someone cut it.

KUU: Cut how?

PLACK: Physically. Look at the casing.

*She holds up the recorder. Along the side, a clean cut where a chip once sat.*

LUNA: So someone's been here since. Someone took the last entry.

PLACK: *(quietly)* Or Hasegawa did.

**[Branch point: the player may spend a "scan" action to examine the recorder casing. Reward: a lore entry with a faint sponsor wing mark etched into the cut. Not explained.]**

## Episode 7 - Standing Room Only

*The stadium bowl. Stands rise in tiers, cracked, tilted. The pitch is overgrown with white grass that doesn't move in the wind. Hundreds of angels fill the seats, sitting shoulder to shoulder, facing west.*

**[Low point. A cinematic: the squad enters from the tunnel. The camera pans slowly across the crowd. No one moves. Then the first angel at the front row stands.]**

KUU: *(whispering)* Luna.

LUNA: *(whispering)* I see it.

KUU: There are so many.

LUNA: There are exactly as many as there are.

*A second angel stands. A third. A wave rolls through the stands, row by row, angels rising in unison like a stadium wave at a match nobody remembers.*

PLACK: They're not attacking.

LUNA: Not yet.

*The angels begin to merge. Limbs fold into limbs. Halos link. The crowd compresses into a single mass the height of the upper tier, hundreds of faces without eyes, hundreds of mouths without voices.*

**[Transition into boss. Camera lifts to the top of the stands, then drops to ground level as the mass finishes forming.]**

PLACK: *(under her breath)* They've been waiting for us.

LUNA: *(a beat)* Then let's not be rude.

---

## Episode 8 - The Crowd (Boss)

*The CROWD, a mass of fused angels, towers over the pitch. Its body sways to a rhythm only it hears. A halo the size of a lamp post spins slowly over its head.*

**[Boss: The Crowd. Phase 1: arms sweep the pitch; climb the stands to reach the glowing weak points on its shoulders. Phase 2: it sheds smaller angels as adds; use the stands as cover. Phase 3: it drops to the pitch and charges; time the dodge.]**

LUNA: *(to herself)* A crowd with no cheer.

KUU: What do we do?

LUNA: What every crowd wants. A reason to leave.

**[Mechanic: black hole pulls adds into the boss, white hole shoves the boss back into the stands. Cinematic finisher: the squad pulls the mass apart.]**

*When the CROWD falls, it falls slowly. The angels peel away, one by one, and lie still on the grass. Each one, as it settles, faces west.*

PLACK: *(picking up Hasegawa's recorder, which has been playing quietly in her bag the whole time)* The last entry came back.

*She presses play. This time, a new voice, clear.*

HASEGAWA: *(recording)* Entry twenty-one. I've found it. They're not counting us. They're counting down.

*The recording ends.*

LUNA: *(after a very long pause)* Which direction is west?

KUU: That way.

LUNA: *(pointing)* Then that's where we go next.

### Stinger

*Back at the school gate, empty now. The peddler's cart is gone. On the ground, where it stood, a single playing card, face down.*

*LUNA picks it up. Turns it over. The face is blank except for a tiny stylised J in one corner. She looks at the others, then at the card, and puts it in her pocket without a word.*

**[Chapter 2 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 3. Seeds: angels face west and "count down"; Hasegawa's recorder; the playing card with a J (Jester); Sublevel 9; Plack's "same hand" handwriting.]**
