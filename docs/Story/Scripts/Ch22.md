# Ch 22 - Narrator's Page - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / NEW.** Main cast. The Library of Unwritten Pages. Luna's enemy; the author of the official record. The Narrator
speaks of people in the third person and the past tense, even to their faces.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A library with no roof. A floor of loose pages, ankle deep, rustling. Shelves that rise into a dark ceiling like the walls of a
canyon. At a reading desk in the middle of it all, a composed figure in a long coat, writing.
NARRATOR: Luna, who had come a very long way, stood in the doorway and wondered whether to knock.
LUNA: (from the door) I'm not wondering.
NARRATOR: She said that she was not wondering. She was, of course.

## Episode 1 - "Third Person"
**Scene:** Library entrance. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu. Archivist (BLUE).

ARCHIVIST: (stained fingers, round glasses) You have to be quiet.
KUU: We are --
ARCHIVIST: (a finger to her lips) *Everything* you say is recorded.
PLACK: By whom?
ARCHIVIST: By the *room.* (she points at the floor; the pages are covered in faint pencil lines, tracing every step) It keeps up.
LUNA: (reading a page at her foot) "Luna said that she was not wondering." (flat) That's from a minute ago.
ARCHIVIST: Yes. It's quicker than it looks.

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: the library's rearranging rooms (environmental); page hazards.
**Beat:** The library writes the squad as it goes.

## Episode 2 - "Reading Rooms"
**Scene:** Reading rooms. **Present:** all three.

*(Each room rearranges as the squad reads its plaque: bookshelves slide aside, a door swings in a wall, a staircase becomes a wall.)*
KUU: Don't read the sign.
PLACK: I have to read the sign.
KUU: You *don't.*
PLACK: (reading) "Do not read this sign."
*(The room rearranges. A corridor of mirrors opens.)*
KUU: ...That's on you.
PLACK: It's in the *nature of a sign.*

**Gameplay:** Rearranging-room puzzles; page-wraith combat (RED).
**Beat:** The rooms respond to reading.

## Episode 3 - "The Charge, In Draft"
**Scene:** The drafting room. **Present:** all three.

*(A long table of drafts, each annotated in red.)*
PLACK: (turning pages) These are the charges. "Luna: war criminal." (turning) "Luna: war criminal, revised." (turning) "Luna: war
criminal, *final*, with a joke." (she stops) ...They've workshopped the joke.
KUU: What was the joke?
PLACK: (reading) "She declared war on a *Tuesday.*"
LUNA: (quietly, taking a draft) That's not even accurate. It was a Thursday.
KUU: Is that the part you object to?
LUNA: It's the part with a *date.*

**Gameplay:** Documents investigation; a chase through drafts; YELLOW pickups.
**Beat:** The charge was written in this room.

## Episode 4 - "The Voice"
**Scene:** The recording archive. **Present:** all three.

*(A glass booth, a tape machine, a label: FACTORY INTAKE, 2008.)*
KUU: (hearing it on the speaker) ...That's the voice.
PLACK: From the factory.
KUU: "Once upon a time, three visitors came to the factory that made the light."
LUNA: (very quiet) Recorded in 2008.
PLACK: Two years before we arrived.
KUU: It was *waiting* for us?
PLACK: It was *scripted.* (she looks at Luna) It wrote the visit before we made it.
*(From the stacks, the Narrator's soft voice, amused.)*
NARRATOR: She noticed. How very quick of her.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a reveal scene; the booth unlocks the central room.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The factory voice is the Narrator's, recorded two years early.

## Episode 5 - "The Reading Room"
**Scene:** The central reading room. **Present:** all three.

*(A desk, a lamp, three chairs. The Narrator stands.)*
NARRATOR: Please. She sat.
LUNA: (not sitting) You write things about me.
NARRATOR: He sat. Plack sat. Luna did not sit.
LUNA: I will in my *own time.*
NARRATOR: (turning a page) In her own time, she said. Which is the sort of thing that *sounds* profound.
LUNA: (a flicker) It *is* profound.
NARRATOR: (gently) It is *nonsense,* Luna. It is charming nonsense, and it has taken you far. But I am the one who has read
the rest of the page.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a long scene; subtle player options (listen / interrupt: cosmetic).
**Beat:** The Narrator and Luna meet.

## Episode 6 - "A Better Ending"
**Scene:** The reading room. **Present:** all three.

NARRATOR: I should like to offer her a better ending.
LUNA: What's wrong with the one I've got?
NARRATOR: It ends badly.
LUNA: They all do. That's how you know they're endings.
NARRATOR: Hers is *specifically* bad. It does not have a happy marriage. It does not have a joke. It has a very long, very quiet,
very *grey* walk to a place where nothing happens.
KUU: (very quietly) ...Nowhere.
NARRATOR: (a small, approving smile) He read ahead.
LUNA: Take it off the table. (a beat) I would like a *worse* one.
NARRATOR: (genuinely surprised) Worse?
LUNA: With *more jokes.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): Luna refuses the offer.
**Beat:** Luna refuses an easy ending and the Narrator notes it.

## Episode 7 - "The Editor"
**Scene:** The stacks. **Present:** all three. The Editor (RED).

*(The Narrator's proxy, a faceless figure in a tailored suit with a red pen, steps out of the dark. Pages fly from its coat like a flock.)*
EDITOR: (a soft, thin, careful voice) Cut. Cut. Cut. Rewrite.
PLACK: It's editing us.
KUU: It's *deleting* things!
*(Where the Editor passes, shelves are blank, a staircase has no steps, Kuu's coat has lost its buttons, and Plack's recorder has no
words.)*
PLACK: (staring at the empty recorder) That was the *only* copy.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Editor's pen-clerks; the environment loses detail as the fight goes on.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The squad is losing things.

## Episode 8 - "The Editor"
**Scene:** The central reading room. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Editor. Phase 1, red-pen strikes (anything crossed out cannot be hit); Phase 2, the Editor rewrites the arena as pages.)*
EDITOR: Strike the line. Strike the line.
LUNA: (hurling the black hole) I don't *do* lines.
*(The hole drags a swirl of pages in; the Editor, tangled in its own revisions, is pulled apart.)*
EDITOR: (in tatters) ...it ... *reads* better...
LUNA: (flat) It did not.

**Gameplay:** Boss: stay on un-crossed ground; hit the pen; page-wall phase.
**Beat:** The Editor falls. The Narrator is gone.

**Stinger:** On the central desk, a single page with Luna's name at the top. Below it, nothing. Plack picks it up and turns it over.
The back is blank too. Luna, very softly: "No ending." Plack: "Or not yet." Kuu: "Is that better or worse?" Luna looks at the empty
shelves. "I don't know," she says. "I'd like to find out."
