# Ch 11 - Return to Nowhere - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / LOCKED map (events authored).** A place simply known as nowhere: a mashup of all ten past maps pasted together,
pure grey, no post-processing, nothing in it, no texture, white noise. No levels, no bosses: a single walk of about five minutes.
Nothing happens, and it should feel horrifying the whole time. **This chapter keeps its rule: nothing happens.**

The "episodes" below are *legs of the walk*, not scenes. There is no dialogue except where marked. There is no combat, no HUD
markers, no enemy, no boss, no mission text. The only authored event is one click of static as each of the three passes the exit.

---

## Cold open
No cold open. Grey.

## Leg 1 - the crossing
A grey Neo Tokyo crossing: signal poles, a zebra of paler grey, no cars, no sound but white noise. The squad's footsteps are the only
rhythm. Nobody speaks.

## Leg 2 - the toppled tower, the lighthouse, the pier
A grey radio tower lying across a grey crater. A grey lighthouse with no beam. A pier that goes nowhere. They walk the tower.

## Leg 3 - the chain
Chain links rise out of the ground like the roots of something. Kuu does not look up. He keeps his eyes on Luna's heels.

## Leg 4 - a conveyor, a house under a low moon
A dead conveyor with grey cubes on it. A house with a grey lamp in the window. A moon, low, flat as a plate.

## Leg 5 - desks, a wall too tall to see over
A hundred office desks in rows, each with a grey chair. A wall at the end of the row too tall to see the top of.

## Leg 6 - a tunnel segment, a dune, the data centre
A grey tunnel mouth. A grey dune. A grey block with a grey screen that shows nothing.

## Leg 7 - a floating island, a giant teacup
A grey island held up by nothing. A grey teacup the size of a house, set down very carefully, as if by a very patient hand.

## Leg 8 - the last stretch
It looks, to the squad, like the first leg. It is not. They do not discuss it.

## The exit
A frame of pale grey in the dark grey. They step through it one at a time.
Luna passes. *Click.* A single soft click of static, no louder than a tongue on a palate.
Plack passes. *Click.*
Kuu passes. *Click.*
Nobody notices. The player may not notice.

*(Optional lines, only if the player lingers at the exit for 20 seconds and speaks to nobody: Kuu, very quietly: "I'd like to go home
now." Luna: "Yes." That is all.)*

**Gameplay:** Walking only. Camera free. No post-processing. The only trigger in the map is the exit frame.
**Beat:** The squad walks out. Nothing happens. *(Death counts them: Ch 26, Ch 35, Ch 39.)*

**Stinger:** None. The next chapter opens without a transition.
