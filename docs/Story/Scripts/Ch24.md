# Ch 24 - The Joke - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / NEW.** Main cast. The Big Top under Neo Tokyo: the Jester's carnival. Three games. Luna cheats. It costs her Kuu.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Under a city, a tent. A carousel that plays a tune Luna half remembers and cannot place. A string of paper lanterns. A sign, hand
painted, with a joker's grin: ALL THE FUN OF THE ARC. Under it, smaller: *(Please play fair. The house will know.)*
A playing card, face up, on the ticket booth. It is the same plain white back as the one in Luna's pocket.

## Episode 1 - "The Midway"
**Scene:** The carnival midway. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: It's very... cheerful.
PLACK: It's very *loud.*
LUNA: It's very *rigged.* (she picks up a ring from a stall; it is too small to fit any peg) You see?
PLACK: Then why are we here?
LUNA: Because the Jester left a card in a *school.* It was a *message.* It said: "Come and find me, but be *funny* about it."
KUU: It didn't say that.
LUNA: (sweetly) It said it to *me.*

**Gameplay:** Midway hub: mini-games for tickets; carnival automata (RED).
**Beat:** The squad enters the Jester's house.

## Episode 2 - "Shooting Gallery"
**Scene:** The gallery. **Present:** all three.

*(Rows of tin ducks, targets with painted smiles, a counter, a rifle on a chain.)*
JESTER: (leaning on the counter, in motley, a half-mask like the back of a card) Three shots. Three prizes. All you have to do is hit what
you can't see.
KUU: (hoarse) How do we hit what we can't *see?*
JESTER: (delighted) By being *honest* about where it isn't!
LUNA: (taking the rifle) Which targets are real?
JESTER: Dear Luna. (a bow) None of them are real. That's why they're *targets.*
*(She fires. A duck falls. Behind it, a smaller duck rises.)*
JESTER: (applauding) She's *got it!*

**Gameplay:** Shooting gallery: a rhythm/aim minigame; hidden targets revealed by sound.
**Beat:** Game one: Luna wins by listening.

## Episode 3 - "Mirror House"
**Scene:** The house of mirrors. **Present:** all three.

*(A maze of mirrors; every reflection a half-second behind.)*
KUU: There are two of me.
PLACK: There are *forty.*
KUU: (to his reflections) Sorry.
*(One of the reflections doesn't apologise back.)*
LUNA: (quietly) Plack. Stay at my left.
PLACK: Why?
LUNA: Because it's the one with the mirror that's *bored.* (she points) That's the real wall.
PLACK: How can a mirror be bored?
LUNA: It's got nothing to *reflect.*

**Gameplay:** Mirror-maze puzzle with a combat gauntlet (mirror images as enemies).
**Beat:** Game two: Luna wins by seeing which mirror isn't playing.

## Episode 4 - "The Third Game"
**Scene:** The ring. **Present:** all three.

JESTER: (on a small stage, a spotlight) The third game is the only game. (he shuffles a deck) We shall play for the whole arc's vote.
LUNA: What are the rules?
JESTER: (smiling) You will lose.
LUNA: (flat) That's a rule?
JESTER: It's the *premise.* (cards flick; one lands in her hand) The third game is rigged. I have *told* you. I will *always* tell you.
(a small, sharp smile) What you do about it is the game.
*(Plack leans in, low.)*
PLACK: It's a test.
LUNA: Of?
PLACK: Of what you do when you're *beaten.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a card game as a dialogue minigame (the rules explained).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The third game is openly rigged; the test is her response.

## Episode 5 - "She Cheats"
**Scene:** The ring. **Present:** all three.

*(Luna sits. The deck is dealt. Her hand is nothing. She turns a card and, slowly, as if weighing it, slides a second card from her
sleeve onto the felt.)*
KUU: (very quietly) Luna.
JESTER: (not looking at the card; at her) Ah.
LUNA: (evenly) You said it was rigged.
JESTER: I did.
LUNA: So I *un-rigged* it.
*(A long, delighted, terrible silence. Then the Jester begins to laugh.)*
JESTER: She *cheated!* She cheated *honestly!* In front of the whole *tent!*
*(The tent applauds. A thousand puppets and clowns, in unison.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the cheat is a scripted choice-free scene.
**Beat:** Luna wins by cheating openly. The Jester respects it.

## Episode 6 - "Kuu Is The Target"
**Scene:** The ring. **Present:** all three.

JESTER: (still chuckling) And the *price,* of course.
LUNA: Price?
JESTER: Every win is a *rental.* You've won my vote. The house takes its commission in *kind.* (to the crowd) Bring up the target!
*(A circle of painted boards swings out. Kuu is tied, gently, to the centre, and a cardboard apple is placed on his head.)*
KUU: (white) Luna...
LUNA: (a very small voice) It's a *joke.*
JESTER: It is! It's the *best* one.
*(Plack stands up very slowly.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): an uncomfortable scene; the player can only watch.
**Beat:** The price is Kuu's dignity, and Luna lets it be paid.

## Episode 7 - "Not Speaking"
**Scene:** The tent's back lot. **Present:** all three.

*(Kuu, untied, rubbing his wrists. Plack, not looking at Luna. Luna, looking at the sawdust.)*
KUU: It's fine.
LUNA: It's not.
KUU: It's *fine,* Luna.
LUNA: You were *scared.*
KUU: Yes. (a pause) I'm *always* scared. That's what I'm *for.* You don't have to --
LUNA: I *did,* Kuu.
KUU: (looking at her, softly) Did what?
LUNA: I let them tie you up and I told myself it was a joke.
*(Plack, a little way off, does not turn her head. She does not speak to Luna for the rest of the night.)*
*(A roar from the tent: the Ringmaster Machine has risen.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Tent Brute (a tent-pole slam arena).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The cost of winning is that nobody is speaking.

## Episode 8 - "The Ringmaster Machine"
**Scene:** The ring. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: the Ringmaster Machine. Phase 1, whip arms and drum-roll pulses; Phase 2, the tent collapses into a carousel and the machine spins.)*
JESTER: (from the rafters, delighted) The *house* is displeased!
LUNA: It can send a *note.*
JESTER: It sent a *machine.*
*(Plack, without looking at Luna, aims her shot at the machine's drum. Kuu, bruised, finally swings in from the rope.)*
KUU: (to the Ringmaster) I'm sorry. (he hits it) I'm *really* sorry.
*(The Ringmaster falls. The carousel tune stops.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: dodge whips; the drum is the weak point; the carousel phase rotates the floor.
**Beat:** The Ringmaster falls. The Jester's vote is Luna's.

**Stinger:** At the tent flap the Jester, folding a card, says: "A vote is a vote. A joke is a joke. A *price* is a price." He tucks the
card into Luna's sleeve. It is blank. "One for the road," he says. "Fill it in when you know what you owe." Luna looks over her shoulder
at Kuu, who is walking slightly away from her. She puts the card away.
