# Ch 19 - House Rules - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / SIDE STORY: Gambler.** The Deck, a casino on the arc's upper terrace where everyone has a price. A mysterious
backer is fixing the book against Luna. Odds, honesty, a card that is not from any house.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A casino at night, under a ceiling of artificial stars. A woman at a table dealing herself a hand she does not look at. Every
chip on the felt has a small wing stamped in the rim; she turns one over with a fingertip and does not smile.
GAMBLER: (softly, to the empty chair) House rules.

## Episode 1 - "The Book"
**Scene:** The Deck, main floor. **Present:** Gambler (playable guest), the Bartender (BLUE).

BARTENDER: The board's moved.
GAMBLER: By how much?
BARTENDER: Luna. It was one-to-nine. It's one-to-forty.
GAMBLER: (reading the board) Someone has taken every bet against her. All at once.
BARTENDER: Whose money?
GAMBLER: (a pause) That's the question.
*(Above the bar, the board flickers. NINE becomes FORTY. A small light blinks beside it, in the shape of a wing.)*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial (Gambler's kit: a prediction minigame; a first chip toss).
**Beat:** Someone is moving the odds.

## Episode 2 - "Odds"
**Scene:** The floor. **Present:** Gambler.

GAMBLER: (to a croupier) Who bought into the market this morning?
CROUPIER: Nobody I know.
GAMBLER: Money does not walk in on its own. Somebody *sent* it. (she picks up a chip; the wing is crisp, new) Count the stamps.
CROUPIER: There are two hundred.
GAMBLER: Count the *backs.*
CROUPIER: (counting) ...They're all the same.
GAMBLER: That's a house that doesn't exist.

**Gameplay:** Casino floor combat (enforcers); a chip-matching puzzle.
**Beat:** The money has a stamp and no name.

## Episode 3 - "House Rules"
**Scene:** A private room. **Present:** Gambler and the backer's enforcer.

ENFORCER: Madam. The house's friend would like a word.
GAMBLER: I have a rule. I do not speak to friends of the house without the house.
ENFORCER: He is the house.
GAMBLER: (still dealing) Then he may come in and sit.
ENFORCER: He does not sit.
GAMBLER: (without looking up) Everyone sits. It is the only thing a table is *for.*

**Gameplay:** A tense dialogue duel: Gambler's responses unlock timing windows in a short fight.
**Beat:** Gambler refuses to be bought.

## Episode 4 - "The Vault"
**Scene:** The Deck's vault. **Present:** Gambler.

*(A vault of a thousand drawers, each a different bet. One has been emptied.)*
GAMBLER: (taking a ledger from the empty drawer) This was my private fund.
BARTENDER: (arriving) Who...?
GAMBLER: Three hundred thousand. And in its place...
*(A single chip with a wing, and a card, face up.)*
GAMBLER: (reading the card) "The house thanks you for your custom."
*(She turns the chip over; the wing is stamped on the back. She looks at it for a long time.)*

**Gameplay:** Vault exploration; combination puzzle; guards (RED).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The backer robbed the house to fix the book.

## Episode 5 - "The Visitor With A Card"
**Scene:** The bar. **Present:** Gambler and Luna.

LUNA: (sitting) A glass of whatever you serve people you don't like.
GAMBLER: (without turning) That's the house red.
LUNA: Wonderful.
GAMBLER: You're very calm for a woman at one-to-forty.
LUNA: I've had worse odds. (she slides a playing card across the bar) Recognise this?
*(Gambler turns the card over. It is a plain white back with a thin black border and no wing, no logo.)*
GAMBLER: (a pause) That is not mine.
LUNA: It isn't?
GAMBLER: I deal on blue. I deal on *red.* I have never in my life dealt on *white.* (very quietly) That's not a house card.
LUNA: Whose is it?
GAMBLER: There's only one in the arc who leaves cards behind.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the card scene (the Ch 2 card, finally identified).
**Beat:** The Ch 2 card is the Jester's calling card.

## Episode 6 - "The Dealer"
**Scene:** The Deck, rooftop. **Present:** Gambler, Luna.

LUNA: The backer is paying people to bet against me.
GAMBLER: The backer is paying people to bet against you. The money is *real.* It has a source. It just has no name.
LUNA: (flat) The wing.
GAMBLER: The wing.
LUNA: It's on the chips.
GAMBLER: It's on the *chips.* It's on the *board.* It's on every bet. (she turns a chip over) I would not bet *my life* the
backer's money is government.
LUNA: Then whose?
GAMBLER: (a long pause) Luna, I run the book on a war. I know every hand that bets. And there is a hand I have never seen.

**Gameplay:** Rooftop combat (enforcers); a shortcut: the elevator.
**Beat:** The backer is not the government.

## Episode 7 - "Flipping The Table"
**Scene:** The Deck, high-roller floor. **Present:** Gambler, Luna.

*(The Backer's Dealer, in a long black tuxedo, a deck of cards spread like a fan in his hands, steps out onto the felt.)*
DEALER: Madam. The house would like its money back.
GAMBLER: The house stole it.
DEALER: The house *borrowed* it.
GAMBLER: Then the house may *ask.*
DEALER: (a thin smile) The house does not ask.
*(He flicks the deck; the cards cut through the air; the first table splits in two. He kicks the next over.)*
GAMBLER: (flatly) Table's flipped.
LUNA: (delighted) Of *course* it is.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Dealer's aides; a flaming casino set piece.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The casino is on fire.

## Episode 8 - "The Backer's Dealer"
**Scene:** High-roller floor. Boss fight. **Present:** Gambler, Luna.

*(Boss: the Backer's Dealer. Phase 1, card-blade volleys; Phase 2, the table becomes a roulette; the wheel decides which areas are safe.)*
GAMBLER: (placing a chip on a section) Red nineteen.
DEALER: (flinching as that section lights) That's *impossible* --
GAMBLER: It's *odds.* (she places another) Black seven.
*(The roulette spins; the chips settle; the Dealer lands, with a thin, astonished sound, on his own numbers.)*
GAMBLER: (quietly) Never bet against the house you can't see.

**Gameplay:** Boss: roulette mechanic (place chips to mark safe areas); card cuts.
**Beat:** The Dealer falls. The book is righted.

**Stinger:** The board over the bar rewrites itself: LUNA 1:9. Gambler reads it. Next to the wing-stamp on the nearest chip, she
writes with a grease pencil: *Not mine.* Luna, at the door: "What are the odds on me now?" Gambler: "I don't tell friends." A beat. "One
to nine." Luna smiles. "That's higher than I expected."
