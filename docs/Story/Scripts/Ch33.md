# Ch 33 - The White Room - Script

**Arc 2 / 2015 / SIDE STORY: Judge.** Judge goes alone to the sanctum below T.O.M.B.S, where the unlisted Secret lives. She expects a
prisoner. She finds a host.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A lift at the bottom of an office, the doors parting on a corridor that is whiter than white. A woman six feet five in a power dress, a
metal gavel, steps out. She does not look at the walls.
JUDGE: (to herself, formal, dry) It is a courtesy of the unlisted to be prompt.

## Episode 1 - "The Corridor"
**Scene:** The sanctum corridor. **Present:** Judge (playable guest).

*(A corridor of white stone, polished, without seam. Footsteps do not echo. Behind her, at an exact distance, the Hush walk.)*
JUDGE: (without turning) You may walk beside me, if you prefer.
*(The Hush, a row of white-robed soldiers with sealed mouths, do not answer. They continue at an exact distance.)*
JUDGE: (dry) I thought not.

**Gameplay:** Slow corridor: the player walks; the Hush follow (non-hostile).
**Beat:** The sanctum is a pressure.

## Episode 2 - "The Hall of Silence"
**Scene:** Honour-guard hall. **Present:** Judge.

*(A vast hall of white columns; rows of the Hush standing in ranks, each with a mouth sealed with a seam of white thread.)*
JUDGE: (aloud, to the room) Is this the court?
*(The ranks, as one, tilt their heads. A single column of pale light rises where she stands.)*
JUDGE: (very dry) I take that to be a *no.*
*(An ambient mechanic: any sound the player makes (attacks, abilities) draws the Hush; the safest route is silence.)*

**Gameplay:** Silence mechanic (stealth through a hall; light combat if noticed).
**Beat:** Judge declines to use her voice; she is tested.

## Episode 3 - "A Host"
**Scene:** The white room. **Present:** Judge, Angel (BLUE).

*(A white room, a small table, two chairs, a vase with a single white flower. At the table, a tall, serene man in a plain white
robe, a faint ring of light at the back of the head. He rises.)*
ANGEL: Judge. (he inclines his head) I have been looking forward to this.
JUDGE: (a half-second pause; the first she has allowed) You are not what the file described.
ANGEL: What did the file describe?
JUDGE: A *prisoner.*
ANGEL: (a gentle, very faint smile) I have been a guest for a very long time. It is the same, from outside.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the meeting scene.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Angel is not a prisoner; he is a host.

## Episode 4 - "A Day"
**Scene:** The white room. **Present:** Judge, Angel.

*(They sit. They talk. The scene is a time-lapse: the vase's flower closes, opens; the light in the room changes; a clock on the wall, which
does not exist, passes a day.)*
ANGEL: You find operators a distasteful constituency.
JUDGE: I find them *irregular.*
ANGEL: You are very precise.
JUDGE: It is the only courtesy a judge may afford.
ANGEL: (nodding) I find them... *loud.*
JUDGE: (a very small pause) Is that all?
ANGEL: I find them *numerous.*
JUDGE: (flat) You find them a *burden.*
ANGEL: (gently) I did not say it. You did.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a long dialogue scene (time-lapse).
**Beat:** Angel is courteous in every sentence and contemptuous in every pause.

## Episode 5 - "A Verdict In Advance"
**Scene:** The white room. **Present:** Judge, Angel.

JUDGE: I have come on behalf of the Charter.
ANGEL: I know.
JUDGE: It requires your signature.
ANGEL: Of course.
JUDGE: (a flicker of surprise, quickly hidden) You will sign.
ANGEL: I will. (he takes a pen; he weighs it) A verdict, Judge, is a thing a wise judge writes *before* the argument. It is only *spoken* afterward.
JUDGE: You are speaking of yourself.
ANGEL: I am speaking of the Charter. (he signs; his hand does not move at all) It is *already decided.* I merely *attend.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** Angel agrees to sign "in advance and without surprise."

## Episode 6 - "A Mark"
**Scene:** The white room. **Present:** Judge.

*(In the margin, beneath the signature, a small mark: a black circle with fine red glyphs round the rim. It was not there a moment ago.)*
JUDGE: What is this?
ANGEL: My mark.
JUDGE: I have seen this mark. (very evenly) In a village.
ANGEL: (smiling, faintly) It is on a great many things.
JUDGE: (slowly, a statement of fact) You are the *monolith's* voice.
ANGEL: (after a moment) I would put it differently. I am what it *speaks through.*
*(A silence. The flower in the vase has closed.)*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** Angel's mark is the monolith's glyph circle.

## Episode 7 - "Weighed"
**Scene:** The corridor, leaving. **Present:** Judge.

*(The Hush, now in a column, bar her way. Not hostile. Merely *there.*)*
JUDGE: Stand aside.
*(They do not.)*
JUDGE: (her gavel rises, wired, heavy) You have been *weighing* me.
*(One of the Hush raises a hand; a long, pale sound, like a held note, fills the corridor.)*
JUDGE: (very quietly) ...I see. It was a *trial.*
*(The soldiers draw white blades from their sleeves.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: the Hush's honour guard (a corridor fight).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The sanctum tests Judge in a way she did not agree to.

## Episode 8 - "The Hush"
**Scene:** The corridor. Boss fight. **Present:** Judge.

*(Boss: the Hush captain: a tall white guard with a sealed mouth. Phase 1, silent blade sweeps; Phase 2, the corridor is stripped of sound and the HUD loses its audio cues.)*
JUDGE: (swinging) You have nothing to say.
*(The captain does not answer. The gavel meets the blade; the sealed mouth, under its white thread, trembles.)*
JUDGE: (very low) Then I shall say it for you. *Guilty.*
*(The captain falls. The thread on its mouth unpicks itself, quietly, and a breath comes out of it, a long one, like relief.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: parry silent strikes (visual cues only); audio drop; a gavel finishing move.
**Beat:** The Hush falls.

**Stinger:** Back in the lift Judge checks the Charter's last line. The signature is in *her* handwriting, not his. She looks at the
black circle. Back in the sunlight she tells Luna the Secret's seat is filled, and that she would like it recorded that she said so
under her own name. Luna records it. Neither of them says what they think the circle means.
