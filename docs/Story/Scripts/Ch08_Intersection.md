# Ch 08 - Intersection (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch08_Intersection`; owner brief: a simple tunnel drenched in rain and seemingly flooding, brimming with angels, the main transport path of the angels from their assumed headquarters.

WORKING (touches DEFERRED angel behaviour, flagged for the owner): the angels travel one way and carry shards.

Chapter promise: a demolition job the squad quietly refuses to finish.

---

## Episode 1 - Damp

*A tunnel mouth, six lanes wide, arched in dirty concrete. Rain pours through the roof grates in sheets. The floor is a shallow stream. Distant, a low hum, like traffic that never ends.*

**[Cold open. Tracking shot from behind the squad as they walk in. The rain sound is the entire score.]**

COMMANDER: *(radio)* Unit Luna. Your task: demolish the Intersection. Charges are in your pack. Detonation will collapse the tunnel and stop angel transit.

LUNA: *(looking into her pack)* There's a lot of putty in here.

COMMANDER: Plastic explosive.

LUNA: It's very squishy.

PLACK: *(taking the putty, examining it)* It's damp.

COMMANDER: It's... what?

PLACK: The charges are damp. Water's got into the casings. *(she presses one)* They won't detonate.

COMMANDER: *(a pause)* ...Understood. Proceed with the survey. Report conditions.

LUNA: Conditions are wet.

KUU: *(wringing out a sleeve)* Very wet.

**[Tutorial: wading speed reduced in water; rain obscures long-range targets; rooftop pipes may be used as an overhead route.]**

## Episode 2 - Lanes

*Deeper in. Painted lane markings on the floor, long faded arrows. Angels walk in lines along them: one direction, evenly spaced, never overtaking, never speaking.*

KUU: *(whispering)* They're in lanes.

PLACK: Six lanes. All northbound.

LUNA: Where's northbound?

PLACK: *(consulting the compass on her wrist)* Toward the Ark.

*They crouch behind a concrete divider and watch. The angels pass like pedestrians at a crossing. One glances at the squad, not hostile, not curious, a blank glance, and keeps walking.*

LUNA: They're not attacking.

PLACK: They're not stopping either.

KUU: Is that good?

LUNA: *(softly)* It's the most orderly thing I've ever seen.

*PLACK writes: "No southbound. Not one. In forty minutes."*

**[Combat: only when the player steps into a lane. Lane Angels attack the player alone; they ignore squadmates. Teaches "lane break" mechanic, which makes an angel stagger.]**

*After the fight, LUNA crouches next to a fallen angel. It has a small glowing fragment embedded in its chest, shaped like a shard of glass.*

LUNA: What's this?

PLACK: *(leaning in)* A shard.

KUU: Of what?

PLACK: *(quietly)* I don't know yet.

---

## Episode 3 - Junction

*A flooded junction where a cross-tunnel meets the main bore. Water to the waist. A control booth on stilts, windows broken. A single working light.*

KUU: I'll check the depth.

LUNA: Kuu, no.

KUU: It's fine. I'm very careful.

*He wades in. Three steps, four. The floor drops away. He goes under with a soft glug. A moment of silence.*

PLACK: *(dryly)* Kuu.

*A bubble. Then KUU surfaces, spluttering, hair plastered, holding a small, yellow rubber duck.*

KUU: *(triumphant)* I found a duck.

LUNA: *(a long, delighted pause)* Is it... a government duck?

KUU: *(examining it)* It's a regular duck.

PLACK: Put it down.

KUU: *(clutching it)* I'm keeping it.

*PLACK exhales slowly. LUNA, grinning, helps KUU out of the water.*

LUNA: I've never been so proud.

KUU: *(a bit breathless)* It's a good duck.

**[Pickup: Rubber Duck. A cosmetic and a running gag: it appears in later chapters on Kuu's backpack.]**

---

## Episode 4 - Cargo (Midpoint)

*A stopping bay, a wide alcove in the tunnel wall. Hundreds of angels stand in rows, each carrying a glowing shard in its folded hands. As the squad watches, a stream of shards are passed hand to hand along the rows and stacked in a freight cage at the back.*

PLACK: *(very quietly)* They're not attacking us because they're not here for us.

KUU: What are they here for?

PLACK: *(pointing)* They're delivering.

*The cage is full. A gantry arm descends, lifts it, and carries it away on rails into the dark. A new empty cage rolls into place. The line begins again.*

LUNA: Delivering to who?

PLACK: Up the line.

LUNA: *(very quiet)* To the Ark.

*A silence. The rain beats on the roof.*

KUU: *(almost inaudible)* Then the Ark knows.

PLACK: *(her face hard)* The Ark's been receiving them.

*LUNA stares at the freight cage. Inside, hundreds of shards glow faintly, the same pale light they saw in the cube on the conveyor in Chapter 5.*

LUNA: It's the same colour.

PLACK: I noticed.

**[Gameplay beat: ledger entry updated: "Angels carry shards. Shards go north. North is home."]**

## Episode 5 - Pump Room

*The tunnel floor ends. A hatch, rust-red, opens onto a fully flooded pump room. A single valve wheel at the far end. Swim.*

**[Level: underwater segment. Air meter. Pipes and rungs to rest on. Enemies are a few Lane Angels that stand motionless in the water, like statues, until woken.]**

KUU: I'll go.

LUNA: You can't swim.

KUU: I... *(he lifts the duck)* ...have a flotation device.

LUNA: Kuu.

KUU: I want to. I'd like to be useful.

*PLACK studies him for a moment, then clips a rope to his belt.*

PLACK: Three tugs means come back.

KUU: *(nodding, solemn)* Three tugs.

*He dives. The squad waits. The rope runs out slowly through PLACK's hands. A minute. Two. Then three tugs. PLACK hauls. KUU surfaces, gasping, holding a small steel box.*

KUU: *(wheezing)* I got it. I think it's a... a flight recorder? It has a label.

*On the box, in stencilled capitals: INTERSECTION, TRANSIT LOG 2002 TO PRESENT.*

PLACK: *(reading, hands tightening)* Every shipment. Every date.

LUNA: *(helping KUU up)* That's the bravest thing I've seen a duck do.

**[Reward: Transit Log. A lore pickup. The first entry is dated 14 November 2002, the day of the meeting in Chapter 7.]**

## Episode 6 - Damp, Again

*A quiet moment, in a dry alcove. The squad sits on crates. Rain drums on the roof. KUU is drying the duck with a handkerchief. PLACK is reading the log. LUNA is staring at the demolition kit.*

COMMANDER: *(radio)* Unit Luna, report status.

LUNA: We've found the Intersection.

COMMANDER: And the charges?

LUNA: *(a long pause)* Damp.

COMMANDER: Damp.

LUNA: Extremely damp. A bit mouldy, actually.

COMMANDER: *(a very long pause)* I see.

*PLACK looks up from the log, her eyes narrowing.*

PLACK: Luna. They aren't damp.

LUNA: They're a bit damp.

PLACK: They're perfectly dry. I checked.

*LUNA doesn't look at her. She snaps the kit shut and puts it in her pack.*

LUNA: If we blow the tunnel, they'll stop delivering.

PLACK: That's the point.

LUNA: *(quietly)* But then we'll never know where to.

*A silence. PLACK closes the log.*

PLACK: ...Fine. They're damp.

KUU: *(looking between them, relieved)* They're very damp.

---

## Episode 7 - Toll

*The tunnel's far end. The lanes converge on a vast gate of rusted iron, three storeys tall. A toll booth squats in front of it, glass fogged. Inside, a tall angel in a clerk's cap, seated, a roll of pale tickets in one hand.*

**[Low point. The Toll Collector. The lanes of angels pass through him one by one; he punches each ticket, and the angel steps through.]**

TOLL COLLECTOR: *(a voice made of light, kind and bored)* Ticket.

LUNA: We don't have one.

TOLL COLLECTOR: Everyone has one. *(he looks at the three of them)* You are... *(a long pause)* ...unmarked.

PLACK: *(carefully)* We're passing through.

TOLL COLLECTOR: No one passes through. They arrive.

KUU: *(under his breath)* Arrive where?

TOLL COLLECTOR: *(a gentle gesture at the gate)* There.

*The gate shifts slightly, a seam of white light along its edge. A hum rises.*

LUNA: What's the fare?

TOLL COLLECTOR: *(softly)* One percent.

*A long silence. The rain beats on the roof. PLACK takes a step back. KUU grips the duck tighter.*

LUNA: *(very calm)* I think I'll pass.

TOLL COLLECTOR: *(sadly)* Then I'll have to collect it.

---

## Episode 8 - The Toll Collector (Boss)

**[Boss: The Toll Collector. Phase 1: ticket-punch strikes along the lanes; stay off marked lanes. Phase 2: he calls lane angels as adds in formation; break the formation with a lane break. Phase 3: the gate opens, pulling the squad toward it; use white hole to resist.]**

LUNA: *(shouting over the rain)* I'm not paying!

TOLL COLLECTOR: *(sadly)* Everyone does.

*The gate yawns open. A pale light spills out, pulling at the squad's coats, their hair, their breath.*

KUU: *(clinging to the duck)* It's trying to take us in!

PLACK: Anchor! Use the pipes!

*LUNA turns, throws a hand out. The white hole opens, a long wavering lens of light, and pushes against the pull. The squad is held, trembling, between two forces.*

LUNA: *(through gritted teeth)* Now!

*PLACK drops a charge, a real charge, dry, hidden in her coat all along, at the foot of the booth. The explosion is small and exact. The TOLL COLLECTOR staggers, his ticket-roll unspooling, and the gate trembles and begins to close.*

*Silence. Water drips. The lanes of angels, uninterrupted, resume their slow march. A few pause, glance at the squad, and move on.*

PLACK: *(quietly)* I said they weren't damp.

LUNA: *(without turning)* And I said I didn't hear you.

### Stinger

*The tunnel's mouth. Dawn light. The three walk out, drenched. Behind them, the angels stream on.*

*LUNA stops at the last drain. A single angel passes in the lane beside her, carrying a shard. She watches it go, its gait slow and certain, the light in its hands the exact colour of the Moon's flicker, the cube, the pocketed thing in her coat.*

*She puts her hand on her pocket.*

LUNA: *(very quietly)* Where are you going?

*The angel doesn't answer. It never does.*

**[Chapter 8 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 9. Seeds: one-way angel traffic; the shards; the Transit Log (2002 to present); "the fare is one percent"; Plack's dry charge; Luna's lie about the charges.]**
