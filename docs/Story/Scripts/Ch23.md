# Ch 23 - Red Sky - Script

**Arc 2 / 2010 / SIDE STORY: Weather and Stargazer.** The observatory at the arc's crown, where the surface hatches meet. A storm that
does not behave like weather. A map with a hole in it.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A dome of iron and glass, perched where the arc's ceiling touches the surface. Through its panes, a sky the colour of a bruise. Inside,
a woman in a rain-coat, cursing a barometer in four languages; and beside her, in a long star-scarf, a quiet figure drawing a star on a
chart that has no star there.
WEATHER: (to the barometer) You *liar.*
STARGAZER: (not looking up) It's not lying. It's *estimating.*
WEATHER: It's lying *in an estimating voice.*

## Episode 1 - "Estimating Voice"
**Scene:** Observatory floor. **Present:** Weather (playable guest), Stargazer (guest).

STARGAZER: (still drawing) Three pressure systems are converging on the same point.
WEATHER: (at the window) That is not how pressure behaves.
STARGAZER: No.
WEATHER: Pressure goes *around* things. It is *polite.*
STARGAZER: This one is not polite. This one has somewhere to be.
*(Outside, the sky's colour deepens. A very distant sound like an enormous door being slowly closed.)*

**Gameplay:** Tutorial: Weather's kit (wind push), Stargazer's (a ping that marks).
**Beat:** The storm is behaving like a thing that knows.

## Episode 2 - "Instrument Galleries"
**Scene:** Galleries. **Present:** both.

*(Instruments on every wall, spinning wildly; a map pinned to the ceiling showing a month of angel movements as thin red threads.)*
WEATHER: The anemometer's *crying.*
STARGAZER: It's not crying, it's *screaming* politely.
WEATHER: Same thing.
STARGAZER: (touching a thread on the map) Look at this. Every thread ends in the same place.
WEATHER: Of course they do. They're angels. They go home.
STARGAZER: (very softly) Then home has a very *precise* shape.

**Gameplay:** Gallery combat (instrument drones, gale wardens); gust-climbing; a map puzzle.
**Beat:** The map is the clue.

## Episode 3 - "Storm Angels"
**Scene:** The storm balcony. **Present:** both.

*(Angels formed of cloud, lightning veined through them, spiral up the dome.)*
WEATHER: (laughing, delighted and appalled) They are *made* of it!
STARGAZER: They're *wearing* it.
WEATHER: It's the *vulgarity* of it, that's what I can't forgive. (she throws a gust) Come on! Come *on!*
STARGAZER: Not so loud. They'll notice.
WEATHER: I *want* them to notice!

**Gameplay:** Aerial combat on a balcony; wind and lightning as environmental tools.
**Beat:** The angels are using the storm as a body.

## Episode 4 - "The Hole"
**Scene:** The map room. **Present:** both.

*(Stargazer lays a clear sheet over the map. Every red thread converges on a single point in a far-off valley.)*
STARGAZER: There. The Centre.
WEATHER: That's a village.
STARGAZER: That is a *village with a black spire.*
WEATHER: (quiet) The monolith.
STARGAZER: (moving the sheet) And here. (a patch where no thread passes, where every red line bends around a small blank circle) A hole.
WEATHER: An absence.
STARGAZER: A *place nobody goes.*
WEATHER: What's there?
STARGAZER: (a long breath) I don't know. I have never had to draw a place where *nothing* converges. It's so *deliberate.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the map reveal.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The angels converge on one point and avoid another.

## Episode 5 - "Names"
**Scene:** A quiet gallery. **Present:** both.

WEATHER: I'm going to name it.
STARGAZER: Which?
WEATHER: The convergence. I'm going to call it "the Centre."
STARGAZER: That's not a name. That's a *position.*
WEATHER: It's a name for a position. (she pins a tag) The Centre.
STARGAZER: And the other?
WEATHER: (looking at the hole for a very long time) ...No. I won't name that one.
STARGAZER: Why?
WEATHER: Because naming a thing means you expect to visit it.
STARGAZER: (very gently) Weather.
WEATHER: *Don't.*

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (BLUE); a map-annotation minigame.
**Beat:** Weather refuses to name the hole.

## Episode 6 - "The Front"
**Scene:** The roof. **Present:** both.

*(The sky is a wall of purple and red. A single front, a hundred miles wide, advancing at a walking pace.)*
STARGAZER: It's not a storm. It's a *procession.*
WEATHER: It's got a *rhythm.*
STARGAZER: (counting) One. Two. One. Two.
WEATHER: It's walking.
STARGAZER: It's *marching.* (she lowers the chart) It's marching at *us.*
WEATHER: (to the dome, almost fond) Of course it is. We're the highest thing in the arc.

**Gameplay:** Defensive stand; lightning rods; wind-gust to deflect.
**Beat:** The observatory is the storm's target.

## Episode 7 - "Floor By Floor"
**Scene:** The observatory, failing. **Present:** both.

*(The front reaches the dome. Glass explodes in a line across the ceiling. The Stormfront's wings spread across the sky like a bruise being
unfolded.)*
WEATHER: It's taking the floors!
STARGAZER: (backing, mapping) It's going *down.* Floor by floor.
WEATHER: It wants the *map.*
STARGAZER: It wants *us.* (a beat) No. It wants the *drawing.*
WEATHER: Then give it a *bad* one.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: lightning drake; floors collapse in sequence.
**Beat:** **Low point.** The observatory is being unmade.

## Episode 8 - "The Stormfront"
**Scene:** The storm balcony. Boss fight. **Present:** both.

*(Boss: the Stormfront. Phase 1, wall of rain and lightning; Phase 2, the eye opens and the sky shows a hole.)*
WEATHER: (calling a wind) I know *you.*
STARGAZER: (aiming a ping) Eye! Left!
WEATHER: I've been *predicting* you for six years!
*(Weather and Stargazer fight as a single motion, her wind shaping the front, his ping marking where it will break.)*
STARGAZER: Now!
*(One enormous clap of thunder. Then a long, ordinary, grateful rain.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: alternate wind-and-mark attacks; the eye; thunder finish.
**Beat:** The Stormfront breaks.

**Stinger:** In the wet quiet Stargazer circles the hole on the map in red and writes, beside it, in very small print, *do not.* Weather,
reading over his shoulder, says nothing. Down in the arc, a bell tolls once; neither of them can say whose.
