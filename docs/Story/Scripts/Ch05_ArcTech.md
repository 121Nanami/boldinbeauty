# Ch 05 - A.R.C TECH INCORPORATED (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch05_ArcTech`; owner brief: a fully functional, undamaged factory with no visible workers, making small cubes that glow insanely bright yellow.

New voice: **THE VOICE**, a warm, courteous loudspeaker narrator who speaks about people in the third person and in the past tense, as if telling a bedtime story. (Later revealed: the Narrator, Ch 22.)

Chapter promise: a machine that is polite about everything and never says what it makes.

---

## Episode 1 - Welcome, Visitors

*A hangar door the size of a cathedral slides open. Inside, conveyors, robot arms, press stampers, all running in silence. Floors polished. No dust. No people.*

**[Cold open. A tracking shot through the intake hall. The machines move in perfect rhythm. The only sound is a faint hum.]**

THE VOICE: *(warm, unhurried)* Once upon a time, three visitors came to A.R.C TECH INCORPORATED. They were tired, and a little wet, and quite certain they would not like it.

LUNA: *(to PLACK)* It's narrating us.

THE VOICE: The first, a young woman with two coats, thought the hall was very large.

LUNA: *(looking down)* I do have two coats.

THE VOICE: The second, a courier with a ledger, wondered who owned the walls.

PLACK: *(under her breath)* Yes.

THE VOICE: The third, a boy with an umbrella, was sure he had forgotten something.

KUU: *(checking his pockets)* I... did I?

COMMANDER: *(radio, uncertain)* Unit Luna, we have a government work order: verify output at A.R.C TECH. Please proceed. Er. Please also, uh, note any anomalies.

LUNA: Does a polite wall count as an anomaly?

COMMANDER: ...Yes.

**[Tutorial: the factory has no enemies yet. The player may walk freely. The Voice comments on movement and item pickups in a gently teasing way.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Five Stars

*The intake hall, deeper in. Overhead rails carry crates; below, on a console, a screen flicks to life with a smiley face and a row of five empty stars.*

THE VOICE: Please rate your visit. For the visitors' convenience, we have rated you in advance.

*The stars fill: four for LUNA, five for PLACK, three for KUU.*

KUU: *(a small gasp)* Three?

THE VOICE: The boy with the umbrella was thoughtful. But he apologised too often, and it made the machines nervous.

KUU: *(devastated)* The machines are nervous?

LUNA: *(delighted)* I got four. Why four?

THE VOICE: The first visitor was charming, but she took things.

*LUNA's hand, halfway to a loose cube on a pallet, freezes.*

LUNA: I was just looking.

THE VOICE: Of course. Please continue looking.

*She puts it back. PLACK, watching, writes in her ledger: "Machine knows what you touch. Machine knows what you intend. Machine does not stop you."*

**[Optional: the player may attempt to rate the Voice. Different responses give different barks. One hidden response: Luna asks it to rate Kuu twice; it adds a sixth star. No reward but a cheerful line.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Cubes

*A conveyor line, a hundred metres long. On it, thousands of small cubes, each the size of a fist, each glowing a violent, almost painful yellow.*

PLACK: *(squinting)* What are they?

THE VOICE: Product.

PLACK: Product of what?

THE VOICE: *(warmly)* Of care.

*PLACK crouches and, very gently, lifts one. It's warm. Bright enough to light her face from below.*

KUU: Is it safe?

PLACK: It doesn't feel like anything. *(she tilts it)* There's a hairline seam. It opens.

*She doesn't open it. She sets it back, carefully, in its slot.*

LUNA: *(watching the line)* Where do they go?

THE VOICE: To those who need them.

LUNA: Who needs them?

THE VOICE: *(gently)* Everyone, eventually.

**[Gameplay: conveyor traversal; timing platforms as the cubes ride the lines. Hazards: stamper arms. First combat: security automata, which behave like very polite guards: they warn the player before attacking.]**

*Over the loudspeakers, THE VOICE hums a lullaby, softly, as the fight ends.*

---

## Episode 4 - One Percent (Midpoint)

*A loading dock. Pallets stacked three high, each one wrapped in plastic, each stamped with a single figure in the same typeface: 1%.*

PLACK: *(reading)* One percent.

KUU: One percent of what?

PLACK: *(very quietly)* The same number as the angel share in every operator.

*Silence. A cube's glow flickers across the three faces.*

KUU: That's a coincidence.

PLACK: *(not looking at him)* Kuu.

KUU: It's a coincidence. It's a number. Numbers repeat.

*He steps back from the pallet.*

KUU: *(very small)* I'd like to go home.

LUNA: *(gently)* In a minute.

*She puts a hand on his shoulder. He leans into it.*

LUNA: Plack. Open one.

PLACK: Are you sure?

LUNA: *(her eyes on the pallet)* No. Open it anyway.

*PLACK slits a seam. Inside the cube, a finger-width of pale light, humming. For a long moment, nobody moves. Then PLACK reseals the cube.*

PLACK: It's what they give us. In the shots.

KUU: *(whispering)* The ones we take every year?

PLACK: The ones we take every year.

**[Gameplay beat: ledger entry unlocked in Plack's journal. The Voice pauses mid-sentence for a full second, then resumes.]**

THE VOICE: *(softly)* The courier was clever. It was a pity.

---

## Episode 5 - The Press Hall

*A press hall. Ten stampers drop in sequence along a corridor. Each is a block of steel the size of a van. Timing windows are narrow.*

**[Level: platforming run. Each stamper has a different rhythm, but they share a master beat. Learn it and the run is easy; ignore it and Kuu's barks get desperate.]**

KUU: *(clutching his chest)* I'm going to die.

LUNA: *(from the far side)* You're not.

KUU: How do you know?

LUNA: Because I'm going to be sad about it.

KUU: *(a long breath)* That's not reassuring.

LUNA: It's true, though.

*KUU takes three steps, ducks the first stamper, rolls, ducks the second, leaps, hits the third's edge, lands on the fourth's flank, and clears the corridor in a heap, wheezing, utterly unharmed.*

KUU: *(lying on the floor)* I did it.

PLACK: You did.

KUU: *(to the ceiling)* I've never been so tired.

LUNA: *(offering a hand)* You were brilliant.

KUU: *(taking it)* Was I?

LUNA: Obviously.

*A faint line from THE VOICE: "The boy with the umbrella, it must be said, did very well."*

*KUU goes pink.*

---

## Episode 6 - Arms

*The assembly floor. Robot arms swing in huge arcs over the heads of the squad. Each one is perfectly synchronised with the others. And each one, as the squad passes, tilts slightly, as if watching.*

**[Gauntlet: arm patterns, plus automaton waves. Narration reaches its peak here: the Voice describes each step before it happens, then, on the third, is wrong.]**

THE VOICE: And then the first visitor ducked to the left.

*LUNA ducks to the right.*

THE VOICE: ...And then she ducked to the right.

*A pause. The arms stutter, one beat late.*

PLACK: It's predicting us.

LUNA: It's narrating us.

PLACK: Same thing, from the inside.

*The arms recover. The VOICE returns, slightly more careful.*

THE VOICE: And then the three visitors reached the foreman's office, where a tall automaton stood, with a clipboard, and a very polite smile.

*A door at the end of the floor slides open on its own.*

KUU: *(whispering)* It's holding the door for us.

LUNA: That's what I'm afraid of.

---

## Episode 7 - The Foreman

*The foreman's office. A desk, a coat rack, a tall automaton in a faded high-visibility vest, with a clipboard where its left hand should be. A name badge: FOREMAN.*

FOREMAN: Good evening. Operator Luna. Operator Plack. Operator Kuu.

*A silence. KUU grips LUNA's sleeve.*

LUNA: *(carefully)* How do you know our names?

FOREMAN: You were registered. *(a smile, perfectly pleasant)* We know everyone's names. It's our job.

PLACK: Whose job?

FOREMAN: The Arc's.

LUNA: A.R.C TECH is the Arc?

FOREMAN: A.R.C TECH is a *department* of the Arc. *(he consults the clipboard)* One of the departments you've been asked not to ask about.

**[Low point. The Foreman does not attack. He offers a form.]**

FOREMAN: There's a form. It's very short. It asks for your name and your signature. By signing, you consent to be counted.

PLACK: Counted for what?

FOREMAN: *(patiently)* For the purposes of the count.

LUNA: *(slowly)* And if we don't sign?

FOREMAN: Then I will have to complete it for you.

*He extends the clipboard. A pen hangs from it. Three lines. Three names, already typed.*

KUU: *(whispering)* Luna.

LUNA: *(very still)* I know.

---

## Episode 8 - The Foreman (Boss)

**[Boss: Foreman. Phase 1: the clipboard fires paper-sharp forms; stand in the forms to be "counted" and lose a buff. Phase 2: the arms of the factory become his; dodge cargo drops. Phase 3: he removes his vest, reveals a cube core; strike it during the stamp sequence.]**

LUNA: *(shouting)* I decline!

FOREMAN: *(patiently)* Declining is a form.

LUNA: *(throwing a punch)* Then I'll fill it in with my fist!

*The FOREMAN blocks with the clipboard. A flurry of exchanged strikes. PLACK slides under his guard, KUU, to everyone's surprise, tackles his legs.*

KUU: *(with a roar of pure panic)* SORRY!

*The FOREMAN topples. His chest plate opens. Inside, a single cube, glowing white. LUNA's black hole snaps around it, pulls, and the FOREMAN's body folds inward with a long mechanical sigh.*

*The factory goes quiet. Every conveyor slows, then stops. For the first time, silence.*

*Then, over the loudspeakers, THE VOICE, very softly:*

THE VOICE: ...And the three visitors left. And the factory did not mind. The factory had, after all, many more stories.

*A distant mechanical thunk. Lights flicker. The conveyors start again, one by one.*

PLACK: *(looking up)* It's still running.

LUNA: Of course it is.

### Stinger

*Outside, at the hangar door. Rain. KUU and PLACK walk ahead, arguing quietly about the pallets. LUNA lingers, one hand in her coat pocket.*

*She withdraws it. In her palm: a single cube, glowing faintly yellow. She looks at it for a long time.*

*She puts it back, very carefully, and catches up.*

**[Chapter 5 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 6. Seeds: the 1% stamp; the Voice; Luna's pocketed cube (not shared with the others); A.R.C TECH as a department of the Ark.]**
