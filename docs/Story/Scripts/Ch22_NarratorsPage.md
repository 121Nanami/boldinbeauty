# Ch 22 - Narrator's Page (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Introduces the **Narrator** (6-star Secret, Luna's enemy per `Luna.md`). Narrator's voice: the same warm third-person bedtime voice as THE VOICE in Ch 5; speaks of everyone in the past tense; polite to the point of menace.

Chapter promise: the author of the official record offers Luna a better ending, and she asks for a worse one with more jokes.

---

## Episode 1 - No Roof

*The Library of Unwritten Pages: a cavernous hall below the old city, roofless, open to a dim painted sky of rolling grey cloud. The floor is a drift of loose paper, ankle-deep. Shelves rise in spiralling stacks, further than eyes can follow.*

**[Cold open. A wind that makes a sound like pages turning.]**

KUU: *(wading through paper)* It's so *quiet*.

PLACK: *(picking up a sheet, reading)* It's blank.

LUNA: *(lifting another)* This one's blank.

PLACK: *(a third)* All of them.

KUU: *(whispering)* Are they all blank?

*A single page, drifting down from above, settles on LUNA's sleeve. She turns it over. A single line, in a small, neat hand: "Luna, who had come a long way, did not yet know what she wanted."*

LUNA: *(very still)* That's not blank.

*She looks up. A hundred pages, falling gently, each with a line, in the same neat hand. A rain of sentences.*

**[Tutorial: page-hazard mechanic. Pages can be caught, and read, for lore. Others stick to the player, slowing them.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Reading Rooms

*A corridor of reading rooms, each with a glass door. Inside each, a desk, a lamp, a chair. A figure sits at each desk, an Archivist, reading. They don't look up.*

*As the squad passes, the rooms rearrange, walls sliding, doors swapping, lamps hopping from desk to desk.*

KUU: *(startled)* It's moving!

PLACK: *(calm)* It's responding.

KUU: To what?

PLACK: To being *read*. *(she nods at a desk, where a figure has just turned a page)* The more anyone reads, the more it reshapes.

LUNA: *(nodding at the lamp)* So we don't read.

PLACK: *(a faint, resigned smile)* We read *selectively*.

**[Puzzle: the player must navigate reading rooms by choosing which pages to read. Reading the wrong ones shifts walls. A "bookmark" mechanic lets them fix a room's layout.]**

*An old ARCHIVIST, with ink-stained fingers and round glasses, looks up from her desk as they pass.*

ARCHIVIST: *(a whisper)* You shouldn't be here.

LUNA: *(politely)* We're looking for the author.

ARCHIVIST: *(blinking)* Of which book?

LUNA: Of the lot.

ARCHIVIST: *(after a pause, a sad, small smile)* Straight on. *(a pause)* But he writes you in the past tense. Don't be surprised.

---

## Episode 3 - Drafts

*A reading room, larger than the rest, a stack of folders on a mahogany table. Each folder has a tab: "LUNA: CHARGES." LUNA, brow furrowed, flips through them.*

*Drafts. Pages upon pages. Margin notes in red. A preliminary indictment.*

LUNA: *(reading aloud)* "The accused, who had been known to cheerfully consume a stranger's meals..." *(she pauses)* That's not a crime.

PLACK: *(at her shoulder)* It's characterisation.

LUNA: *(turning a page)* "...and who, with the indifference of the very young, had made light of the grief of others." *(a pause)* That's not entirely fair.

KUU: *(whispering)* It's not entirely wrong.

LUNA: *(with a glance)* Thank you, Kuu.

*PLACK picks up a stray margin note. A quill scribble: "Too sympathetic. Rewrite. Add the wedding."*

PLACK: *(low)* He's adding things.

LUNA: *(very quiet)* To make me worse.

*PLACK taps another note: "Consider: should she be funnier? Funny is harder to forgive."*

*A silence.*

LUNA: *(flatly)* I hate him.

---

## Episode 4 - The Voice (Midpoint)

*A corridor lined with speakers, cone-shaped, brass, hung from the ceiling like chandeliers. A soft, warm voice, one the squad has heard before.*

NARRATOR: *(over the speakers)* And so the three visitors came, at last, to the heart of the Library.

*KUU freezes.*

KUU: *(whispering)* That's the voice from the factory.

PLACK: *(very quiet)* That's the Voice.

*A pause. LUNA's face, for the first time in the chapter, is entirely still.*

LUNA: *(carefully)* You wrote A.R.C TECH.

NARRATOR: *(warmly, pleased)* A small piece. Early work. I confess I was fond of it.

LUNA: You *narrated* us.

NARRATOR: Naturally. *(a gentle laugh)* Somebody had to.

*The speakers fall silent. A door at the end of the corridor opens, silently, onto a lit room.*

NARRATOR: *(softly)* Do come in. I've been looking forward to this chapter.

**[Gameplay beat: reveal. The voice's actor switches from "narrator tone" to "conversation tone". The player's controls lock for the next scene.]**

---

## Episode 5 - The Central Reading Room

*A circular room, high-ceilinged, quiet. At its centre, a round table, a green-shaded lamp, a single chair. Standing behind it, a tall, slim figure in a long dark coat, one hand on a thick book, the other holding a quill. Not old. Not young. A face of calm, kindly regard.*

NARRATOR: Please. Sit.

*They don't.*

NARRATOR: *(unoffended)* As you like. It is, I'm told, a very comfortable chair.

LUNA: *(flatly)* You're the Narrator.

NARRATOR: I am *a* narrator. I have made it my vocation.

PLACK: *(low)* Of whom?

NARRATOR: *(turning a page)* Of everyone. *(a faint smile)* Of the Ark. Of its events. Of its causes. *(he glances at LUNA)* Of its heroines and its villains. And, from time to time, of its war criminals.

LUNA: You wrote my charges.

NARRATOR: I composed them. It is a different art.

*PLACK steps forward, a hand tight on her ledger.*

PLACK: *(coldly)* Why?

NARRATOR: *(a note of genuine surprise)* "Why?" *(he considers)* Because a story without a villain is a pamphlet.

---

## Episode 6 - The Offer

*The Narrator draws a chair out from the table with his foot, and sits.*

NARRATOR: Let us be frank. I have read what you intend. A table of the willing. A Charter of the improbable. A great, cheerful uprising, with a song at the end.

LUNA: *(shortly)* Yes.

NARRATOR: It will not work.

*He says it very gently, as if giving news of a death.*

NARRATOR: I do not say so to be cruel. I say so as one who has read the final chapter.

*A pause.*

NARRATOR: I can offer you a better ending.

LUNA: *(a slow, narrowing look)* How?

NARRATOR: I will write you the epilogue you deserve. Quiet. Loved. Remembered. A small house by the sea. Your wife. Your brother. A long, soft decline into old age, with a garden. *(he opens his hands)* No trial. No war crimes. No Death.

LUNA: *(very still)* And what do you want?

NARRATOR: *(gently)* Your silence.

*KUU's hand finds LUNA's sleeve. She doesn't look at him.*

LUNA: *(after a long moment)* Can I ask for something else?

NARRATOR: *(politely)* Of course.

LUNA: I'd like a worse ending.

*A beat.*

NARRATOR: *(blinking)* I beg your pardon?

LUNA: *(warming)* A worse one. With more jokes.

---

## Episode 7 - The Editor

*The Narrator's pen scratches once, sharply, across the page. The room shudders. Shelves shift, rolling on hidden tracks, pages lifting into a spiral. A figure, faceless, pale, with a long red pen in place of a right hand, steps from the stacks.*

**[Low point. The Editor attacks, the walls fold in.]**

NARRATOR: *(mildly, as to a servant)* Edit the interruption.

*The EDITOR bows, and with a single stroke of its pen, a wall of paper slashes through the room. PLACK, diving, is not quick enough: a long red mark cuts across her ledger, and a page, her page, her one-page summary of the Ark's casualty count, is struck through.*

PLACK: *(a cry, small, furious)* That was *mine!*

*She lunges. The EDITOR parries with the pen. Pages fly.*

LUNA: *(shouting)* Plack!

*KUU, scrambling, catches a falling folder, finds it labelled "KUU: TO BE REVISED." He stares at it a moment, and then, very deliberately, shuts it and sits on it.*

KUU: *(to the room, shaking)* I don't consent to revision!

---

## Episode 8 - The Editor (Boss)

**[Boss: The Editor. Phase 1: the red pen strikes lines through the arena; avoid struck lines. Phase 2: it "edits" platforms out of existence; use Plack's ledger to restore them. Phase 3: it tries to rewrite the player's party; the player must "cancel" the edits by pressing a defined key at the right moment.]**

*The fight is as much literary as physical. Walls of text, margins, redactions, footnotes that fall like anvils. PLACK's ledger-throws restore deleted floor. LUNA's black and white holes tear paragraphs from the air.*

*At last, the EDITOR is on its knees, pen cracked, a single drop of red ink pooling on the floor.*

*It raises its head. Where a face should be, a blank page. And on it, slowly, in faint ink, letters appearing: "Thank you."*

*Then it falls apart, a drift of loose paper, and is gone.*

*The room is silent. The NARRATOR, at the table, closes his book, a little wearily.*

NARRATOR: *(quietly)* A good scene. *(a pause)* Not what I'd planned.

LUNA: *(very polite)* That's what I'm here for.

*The NARRATOR looks at her for a long, searching moment. Then, with great courtesy, he rises, bows, and, as the lamp flickers, steps backward, into the dark. When the light steadies, he is gone.*

### Stinger

*The central desk. On it, a single page, face up. LUNA's name at the top, in elegant script. Beneath it, nothing. The rest of the page is blank.*

*She reads it. Reads the empty space below. Looks at PLACK.*

LUNA: *(very quietly)* He didn't write an ending.

PLACK: *(her hand on LUNA's shoulder)* He doesn't know it yet.

*KUU, peering over her arm:*

KUU: *(whispering)* So who does?

*LUNA picks up the page, folds it, once, twice, and puts it in her notebook.*
