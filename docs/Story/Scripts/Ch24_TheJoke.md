# Ch 24 - The Joke (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Recruits the **Jester** (6-star Secret, MAIN section 5). Jester's voice: bright, giddy, sincere in a way that unsettles; speaks to the audience; loves cheating that admits itself.

Chapter promise: Luna must win three games honestly, and cheats at the third, and that is the only honest move on offer.

---

## Episode 1 - Calliope

*Under Neo Tokyo: a vast hidden chamber hung with pennants, strings of bulbs, a patched big-top the size of a stadium. A carousel plays a tune. It is a tune LUNA half-recognises, and she cannot place it. Smell of sugar and old canvas.*

**[Cold open. Warm, slightly wrong carnival music; the melody is the music box from Ch 10 slowed down.]**

LUNA: *(stopping)* I know this song.

PLACK: *(looking at her)* Do you?

LUNA: *(slowly)* ...I don't know where from.

*She takes the blank-faced playing card from her coat. A tiny J on one corner. It is faintly warm.*

KUU: *(watching her)* Luna?

LUNA: *(putting it away)* It's nothing. *(a beat)* It's a good tune.

*A voice, high and bright, from somewhere above.*

JESTER: *(over the PA)* LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, BOYS AND GIRLS, OPERATORS OF ALL AGES! TONIGHT, FOR ONE NIGHT ONLY, THE ARC'S MOST UNLIKELY GUESTS!

*A spotlight snaps on, circling the three.*

**[Tutorial: carnival level. Rules are posted as signs. Small mini-games reward tickets.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Midway

*The midway: a long avenue of striped booths, a shooting gallery, a ring-toss, a strongman's mallet. Lanterns. Painted clowns. Prizes hang from the awnings: plush rabbits, tin trumpets, a single enormous brass key.*

KUU: *(looking at a booth)* Sorry. May I?

*A clown automaton at the booth bows, holds out a ring.*

KUU: *(tossing)* ...Three out of three!

AUTOMATON: *(dully)* Congratulations.

KUU: *(to PLACK, delighted)* I won a plush rabbit!

PLACK: *(dry)* You did.

KUU: *(with great dignity)* His name is Mr. Bun.

*LUNA, meanwhile, has wandered to the shooting gallery. A row of tin ducks. She fires three. Misses all three. The rifle's bell chimes cheerfully.*

LUNA: *(to the clown)* Does that count?

CLOWN: *(a flat, painted smile)* Everything counts.

**[Combat: carnival automata attack when the player cheats at booths. Learn to read tells.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Game One

*The mirror house, a labyrinth of glass. Every wall shows a dozen LUNAs, each slightly different: one grinning, one frowning, one in a wedding dress, one with her hair white.*

JESTER: *(over the PA, delighted)* GAME ONE! FIND THE REAL ONE!

*A hundred reflections, all moving a fraction late, all subtly off. LUNA walks, stops. The reflections stop. She tilts her head. The reflections tilt theirs, a half-beat behind.*

LUNA: *(thoughtfully)* They're not trying very hard.

PLACK: *(from behind her, in a dozen mirrors)* How do you know?

LUNA: They're *bored*. *(she points)* That one. She's leaning on the glass. Nobody leans on glass unless they've nothing better to do.

*She walks up to the reflection, and, without hesitation, knocks.*

*The mirror swings open, revealing a door. Beyond it, daylight, well, bulb-light.*

JESTER: *(a slightly crestfallen squeak)* ...How?

LUNA: *(over her shoulder)* I'm excellent at being bored.

**[Puzzle: the player must pick the "bored" mirror by observation. Optional: collect reflections as lore.]**

---

## Episode 4 - Game Three (Midpoint)

*The ring. A circular arena of sawdust under a glass-roofed tent. Bleachers filled with ranks of clown automata, silent, painted grins. A table at the centre: a deck of cards, a single candle. A figure sits behind it.*

*The JESTER: slim, mobile, a mask of cards fanned across the face, a coat of mismatched patches, a feathered cap. They lean forward on their elbows.*

JESTER: *(delighted, in a warm and unexpectedly sincere voice)* Luna. At last.

LUNA: *(sitting, folding her hands)* You left a card.

JESTER: *(clapping)* I leave lots of cards!

LUNA: In a school.

JESTER: *(a small, pleased wriggle)* Did you like it?

LUNA: It was *very* subtle.

JESTER: *(whispering)* Wasn't it?

*They shuffle, a blur.*

JESTER: *(normal voice)* This is the third. You've won two. If you win this one, you have the Secrets' vote. *(they smile behind the mask)* If you lose, well, we'll have a lovely time.

*They deal a single card, face down, in front of LUNA. It is blank.*

JESTER: *(softly)* One card. One chance. One question.

LUNA: What's the question?

JESTER: *(leaning forward, whispering)* Is it rigged?

*A silence. PLACK, at the rail, closes her eyes. KUU grips Mr. Bun.*

---

## Episode 5 - The Honest Move

*The table. The candle gutters. LUNA looks at the blank card. She looks at the JESTER's mask. At the clowns in the bleachers, grinning. At the sawdust.*

LUNA: *(carefully)* Is it rigged?

JESTER: *(brightly)* You tell me!

*LUNA turns the card. It is the Joker. Of course it is.*

LUNA: *(very mildly)* It's the Joker.

JESTER: *(clapping)* It is!

LUNA: *(without looking up)* It's always the Joker.

JESTER: *(a pause, a little less sure)* ...Is it?

LUNA: *(looking up, finally)* You've never lost a game of cards in your life. Not because you're lucky. Because the deck is *yours*.

*A hush. The clowns do not move.*

LUNA: So I'm not going to play it.

JESTER: *(softly)* What?

LUNA: *(standing)* I'm going to cheat.

*She takes the deck from the JESTER's hands, shuffles it once, and with a flick, sends the entire deck, fifty-two cards, spinning upward in a cloud, fluttering down around them like snow.*

*In the confusion, one card lands face-up on the table, between the two of them: a blank, with a tiny J in the corner.*

*LUNA puts her finger on it.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* I win.

*Silence. A long, long silence, and then, from the bleachers, a single automaton claps. Then another. And then the whole tent.*

---

## Episode 6 - Kuu Is the Target

*Afterwards. The JESTER, delighted beyond measure, lifts the mask to reveal a pale, sharp, laughing face, and claps LUNA on both shoulders.*

JESTER: *(shrieking)* It was the only honest move! The only *one*! Everyone else tries to win!

LUNA: *(dry)* It's a rigged game.

JESTER: *(wiping a tear)* The *finest*.

*They twirl to face KUU, who, to everyone's surprise, has been standing at the edge of the ring, holding Mr. Bun.*

JESTER: *(brightly)* And you!

KUU: *(startled)* Me?

JESTER: Every game needs a target.

*A spotlight snaps onto KUU. A line of clown automata step forward, each holding a tin trumpet. KUU, white as paper, takes a step back.*

LUNA: *(turning)* Wait...

JESTER: *(cheerful, firm)* It's in the rules. The one who cheats owes a forfeit, and the forfeit's a friend.

*KUU looks at LUNA. LUNA, for the first time, realises what she has done.*

KUU: *(very quietly)* It's all right.

LUNA: *(a low voice)* Kuu...

KUU: *(smiling, a very brave, small smile)* I'll be quick.

*The trumpets sound.*

**[Gameplay beat: a humiliating but harmless "forfeit" scene: Kuu is pelted with confetti and pie. The player controls only the camera.]**

---

## Episode 7 - The Silence After

*A backstage tent, afterwards. KUU, hair full of confetti, a smear of custard on his cheek, sits on a stool. PLACK, silent, is dabbing his face with a handkerchief. LUNA stands at the tent flap, arms folded.*

LUNA: *(finally)* I didn't mean to.

PLACK: *(not looking up)* I know.

LUNA: *(a bite in the words)* I said it was a game.

PLACK: *(evenly)* I know you did.

*A pause. KUU, quietly:*

KUU: It really was fine.

LUNA: *(turning)* It wasn't.

KUU: *(a small shrug)* I've had worse.

LUNA: *(very low)* That's the part that's wrong.

*KUU looks at her. For a long moment, neither moves.*

*PLACK stands, folds the handkerchief, tucks it away.*

PLACK: *(without a glance at LUNA)* I'm going to find some tea.

*She leaves. The tent flap drops.*

*LUNA, alone with KUU, sits down on the sawdust, an arm's length away.*

LUNA: *(after a long time)* I'm sorry.

KUU: *(softly)* I know.

LUNA: I always say it's a joke.

KUU: *(a pause)* I always laugh.

*That sits between them.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Ringmaster Machine (Boss)

*The ring, again. The clowns in the bleachers have left. The big top is lit by a single carbon arc. In the centre, hauled by cables from the roof, a huge brass automaton in a top hat, a drum for a torso, a whip in each of four arms: THE RINGMASTER MACHINE.*

**[Boss: The Ringmaster Machine. Phase 1: whip-cracks across the ring; leap them. Phase 2: it summons clown automata to attack in rings; fight inside the ring edge. Phase 3: it fires confetti cannons and fireworks; stay moving.]**

JESTER: *(from the rafters, dangling by one knee, wide-eyed)* Oh dear. It's *glitching*.

*The MACHINE swings its whips. KUU, still in his confetti, dodges every strike. LUNA, grim, doesn't joke at all. PLACK, wordless, pins a whip with her ledger.*

*At last, LUNA, face set, steps forward, raises her hand, and a black hole opens, a small, silent disc, at the MACHINE's drum. It folds, once, and is gone.*

*The tent falls silent. The lights flicker.*

JESTER: *(softly, dropping from the rafters)* That was not in the programme.

LUNA: *(quietly)* It is now.

### Stinger

*The JESTER hands LUNA a folded paper, tied with a ribbon.*

JESTER: The Secrets' vote.

*LUNA takes it. It is surprisingly heavy.*

JESTER: *(gently)* One thing.

LUNA: *(wary)* Mm.

JESTER: *(whispering, behind a hand)* You didn't pay the forfeit. *(a nod at KUU, across the tent)* He did. *(a pause)* That's the thing about the honest move.

*They bow, and vanish between two flaps of canvas, leaving only the faint smell of sugar and the tune.*

*LUNA looks at KUU, who is standing, sleepy, hair a mess, holding a plush rabbit. He smiles at her.*

*She does not smile back. She cannot.*
