# Ch 15 - Dead Air (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Side story: **Media** (6-star Secret, ally of Luna per `Luna.md`). Playable: Media (story-forced); the trio appear in cutscenes. Character design for Media is not documented; voice and manner here are PROPOSED.

Media's voice: dry, fast, nocturnal, talks to equipment, eats cereal at all hours, hates silence and loves a clean signal.

Chapter promise: the person who cut Luna's speech is the one who saved her life.

---

## Episode 1 - 4 A.M.

*A subway station that was shut in 2002. Tiled walls, dripping ceiling, a wall of salvaged monitors, wires like ivy. At a mixing desk made of three consoles and a door, MEDIA, in oversized headphones, spoons cereal from a mixing bowl.*

**[Cold open. The player controls Media at the desk. Tutorial: switching between four channels.]**

MEDIA: *(to the desk)* Channel one. Government weather. "A pleasant day, with scattered sunshine." *(spoon)* Liar. Channel two. Sponsor ad. A man selling umbrellas to people who don't have rain. *(spoon)* Channel three. Static. *(she leans in)* Channel three is breathing.

*She holds up one finger, listens. A faint rhythmic pulse under the hiss.*

MEDIA: Hm.

*She writes a number on the wall with a marker. There are hundreds of numbers on the wall.*

MEDIA: Channel four. *(a click)* The desert's still talking.

*A single green light flickers, repeating the same signal, over and over.*

MEDIA: *(softly)* I told you. *(she taps the desk)* I told all of you.

**[Gameplay: the player can tune channels to unlock lore. Channel four plays the damaged Morse from Ch 9, her own rebroadcast.]**

## Episode 2 - Studio Static

*Her studio. A bunk made of a broken escalator, shelves of cassettes, a kettle on a Bunsen burner. A hand-painted sign: STUDIO STATIC. ON AIR (ALWAYS). On a table, a single clean cup.*

MEDIA: *(to a bobble-head of a cat)* Don't judge. It's the only company I've got.

*A rattle from the ceiling. A jammer drone, dish-shaped, drops through a vent, red light blinking.*

MEDIA: *(a pause, then calm)* That's a sponsor drone.

*It scans the room. Its light finds her tapes. A second drone follows, then a third.*

MEDIA: *(in a low voice)* No. No no no no.

**[Combat tutorial: Media's combat style is signal-based: she throws "static bursts" that disrupt enemy sensors. Player learns to jam drones with timing.]**

*She vaults a console, slams a switch. The room lights go red, then blue.*

MEDIA: *(shouting at the drones)* You don't just walk into someone's studio! There's etiquette!

*One drone buzzes a retreat. Another doesn't. A static burst fells it.*

MEDIA: *(panting, to the cat)* I was going to get to bed.

---

## Episode 3 - Patch Panel

*A server room in the old signal tunnel. A patch panel the size of a barn door, a thousand sockets, each labelled with a masking-tape tag in small, fast handwriting.*

MEDIA: *(squinting at a tag)* "Weather, 2008." "Ambulance, secret." "Cathedral organ, no idea." *(a thoughtful pause)* I was very tired.

**[Puzzle: re-tune the patch panel by listening. Three channels play overlapping audio; the player must match them to their sockets by ear.]**

*She plugs in a cable. A fragment of audio: a woman's voice, calm, official: "...the rain is natural. The rain is natural. The rain is..." Another plug: a child, giggling. A third: a deep hum.*

MEDIA: *(under her breath)* Come on, come on.

*A new cable, and a voice breaks through, clear: "Unit Luna, this is your assigned commander."*

*MEDIA freezes.*

MEDIA: *(whispering)* Oh, that's lovely. That's the *commander* channel.

*She listens for a moment, then, very gently, unplugs it.*

MEDIA: No. Not my business.

## Episode 4 - The Kill Order (Midpoint)

*A monitor shows the replay of the declaration, a frame at a time, metadata overlaid in green columns. MEDIA, scrolling, stops on a line she's read a hundred times.*

MEDIA: *(to the screen)* There you are.

*She highlights a sub-string. It's a single word, encoded, embedded in the carrier wave, not meant to be seen.*

MEDIA: A trigger. *(quietly)* Hidden in the signal. If she said the word "oppression" in the middle of a sentence, it'd arm a device in the spire. Not at her. *(she swallows)* At everyone in the building.

*She sits back. For the first time all chapter, she is not eating.*

MEDIA: *(very softly)* Luna, you silly, silly, brilliant woman.

*A flashback frame: the night of the declaration. MEDIA at this same desk, hands hovering over the controls, watching LUNA's face fill the screen. At the word "grateful", LUNA's lips shape the next word, and MEDIA, with the faintest sound, cuts the line.*

MEDIA: *(in the present, to nobody)* I didn't want to.

**[Gameplay beat: Media's reveal. The player has the option to replay the moment she cut the feed.]**

---

## Episode 5 - Signal Tunnels

*The old maintenance tunnels. Dripping pipes, old cables, a trolley track. A relay box at the far end, dark. MEDIA, with a headlamp and a toolbox, is crossing the dark.*

**[Level: run-and-gun through jammer-infested tunnels. Media reconnects three relays in sequence; each activation sends a pulse that stuns nearby drones.]**

MEDIA: *(whispering, to a pipe)* Don't mind me.

*A dish-shaped drone drifts overhead. She presses herself to the wall. It passes. She exhales.*

MEDIA: *(to the cat bobble-head in her pocket)* I'm a coward. It's fine. It's a coping strategy.

*She reaches the relay. Inside, the signal is burnt. She opens her toolbox, finds a fuse, replaces it.*

MEDIA: *(to the relay, soothing)* There you go. There. It's not your fault.

*The relay hums, a green light. Down the tunnel, a distant answering hum.*

MEDIA: *(to herself, a faint smile)* Two more.

## Episode 6 - Soup

*A sudden knock at the studio's iron door. MEDIA freezes, a cassette in one hand. A second knock, polite.*

PLACK: *(from the other side)* It's me.

*MEDIA opens the door a crack. PLACK, soaked, holds a flask and a paper bag.*

PLACK: We needed somewhere. And Luna said you'd be awake.

MEDIA: *(opening it, wary)* I'm always awake.

*PLACK steps in, looks around the studio, and does not comment on the cat bobble-head. She sets down the flask.*

PLACK: Soup.

MEDIA: *(staring)* I don't... take food from strangers.

PLACK: *(dry)* I'm not a stranger. I'm a delivery person.

*A pause. MEDIA, after a long moment, picks up the flask and sips it. Her shoulders drop.*

MEDIA: *(muffled)* That's good.

PLACK: It's lentil.

MEDIA: *(still sipping)* I don't like lentils.

PLACK: *(settling in a chair)* No. You don't. But you like this one.

*A pause. MEDIA, very quietly:*

MEDIA: Is Kuu all right?

PLACK: *(a little surprised)* He's sleeping. He'll be fine.

MEDIA: *(nodding, relieved)* Good. *(a beat)* I wasn't trying to... I didn't mean for him to be shot.

PLACK: *(gently)* I know.

## Episode 7 - The Jammer Choir

*The studio's main hall. The doors burst inward. A formation of jammer drones, thirty-strong, hovering in a ring, each with a dish antenna, each emitting the same sustained, rising whine. In the middle, a single larger drone, wearing a crown of dishes.*

**[Low point. Tapes burn. The studio is overrun.]**

MEDIA: *(shouting)* Not the tapes!

*A drone fires. A shelf of cassettes goes up in a burst of sparks. MEDIA dives, shielding a box with her body.*

MEDIA: *(to the choir, furious)* Do you know how long it took to file those?

*The choir does not answer. It tightens. The whine rises to a shriek, and the monitors on every wall go dark at once.*

*In the silence, the only light is the soft glow of the soup flask. MEDIA, on her knees, looks at PLACK. PLACK, at her side, has a hand on her shoulder.*

PLACK: Media.

MEDIA: *(whispering)* I can't hear anything.

PLACK: *(firmly)* Then make a noise.

*MEDIA looks up. A slow, wild grin spreads across her face.*

MEDIA: *(rising)* Oh. Oh, you've done it now.

---

## Episode 8 - Jammer Choir (Boss)

**[Boss: The Jammer Choir. Phase 1: the choir's whine disables UI icons; Media's static bursts shatter formations. Phase 2: drones dive-bomb, use the studio's ductwork for cover. Phase 3: the crown drone becomes a hovering turret; Media hijacks the studio's PA and blasts a counter-frequency.]**

*MEDIA flicks a switch. The studio's entire speaker array, a salvaged, mismatched wall of cones, roars to life with a single, glorious, over-driven chord.*

MEDIA: *(shouting over it)* THAT'S THE SOUND OF A SIGNAL YOU CAN'T OWN!

*The drones convulse, their antennas folding. The crown drone shudders and falls, spinning, onto a console. A final, long, descending note.*

*Then: silence. And in the silence, the faint, regular hiss of a clean carrier wave.*

MEDIA: *(panting, quietly)* There we are.

### Stinger

*Dawn, the station platform. The studio is a ruin, scorched, smoking, but standing. MEDIA sits on a bench, a bowl of cereal in her lap, a scorched cassette beside her.*

*LUNA arrives down the stair, hood up, two coffees in her hands, one of which she is plainly drinking.*

LUNA: *(offering the other)* I got you a coffee.

MEDIA: *(looking at it)* You didn't pay for it.

LUNA: *(a beat)* ...How do you know?

MEDIA: I know everything that goes through that till.

LUNA: *(a long pause, sheepishly)* I owe you.

MEDIA: *(taking the coffee)* You owe me *nothing*. *(a pause)* You cut my line.

LUNA: *(blinking)* You cut mine.

MEDIA: *(a small, tired smile)* I know.

LUNA: *(sitting beside her)* I'll pay it all back.

MEDIA: *(sipping)* With interest.

LUNA: *(clinking her coffee against Media's)* With interest.
