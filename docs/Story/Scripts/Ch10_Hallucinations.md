# Ch 10 - Hallucinations (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch10_Hallucinations`; owner brief: the whole chapter is a hallucination, a nonsensical pastel drawing-look dreamscape juxtaposed with hyper-realistic terrifying things, and Morse messages telling a small story. The four Morse lines are kept verbatim:

1. "The angels bring mercy for all"
2. "Not those stupid pure ones you call angels"
3. "but TRUE ANGELS THE ONE"
4. "The rest are all filth compared to its existence"

Chapter promise: each of them is alone with the thing they do not say.

---

## Episode 1 - Pastel

*Waking, in a field of pale pink grass under a lilac sky. A teacup the size of a house sits to the left. A chair the size of a church sits to the right. The clouds are drawn in crayon.*

**[Cold open. Soft music box melody. The art style shifts to a hand-drawn look: thick outlines, flat colours.]**

LUNA: *(sitting up, blinking)* I've been poisoned.

KUU: *(beside her, equally dazed)* Or we've died.

LUNA: That's the same thing with better lighting.

*PLACK is already on her feet, one hand on her ledger, the other held out as if testing the air.*

PLACK: It's not real.

LUNA: Obviously.

PLACK: I mean it. *(she pinches her own arm)* Nothing's reporting back. No pain. No weight. This is a construct.

KUU: *(looking at the teacup)* A well-made one.

*The teacup tilts, politely, and a small waterfall of pink tea rolls down its side. A rainbow forms in the spray.*

COMMANDER: *(radio, distant, as if through a seashell)* ...Unit Luna... if you can hear this... *(a pause)* ...don't eat anything.

LUNA: Noted!

**[Tutorial: no combat in the first ten minutes. The player explores. The drawing look makes depth hard to read; ledges are marked with white chalk.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Luna's Island

*A floating island in the shape of a waiting room. Plastic chairs in rows, a coffee table with magazines, a television on a stand. The television is off. A sign above the door: NOW SERVING: 0.*

LUNA: *(sitting down in the nearest chair)* Oh. It's mine.

KUU: *(arriving at the edge of the island, uncertain)* Luna?

LUNA: *(not looking up)* It's fine. It's a waiting room.

KUU: For what?

LUNA: *(picking up a magazine, flicking through)* I don't know yet. That's what makes it a good one.

*She sits, perfectly still, for a very long moment. The television does not come on. A clock on the wall ticks.*

*KUU, standing at the edge, speaks softly.*

KUU: Luna. You're not reading it.

LUNA: *(turning the page)* I am.

KUU: It's upside down.

*A beat. LUNA looks at the magazine, then lets it fall.*

LUNA: *(in a voice smaller than usual)* I keep thinking someone's going to call my number.

KUU: *(sitting beside her)* I'll wait with you.

LUNA: You don't have to.

KUU: I know.

**[Combat: Clock Faces, apparitions with analogue clocks for heads, attack in waves as the numbers on the sign rise from 0.]**

*The sign flickers to NOW SERVING: 1. The television clicks on, showing a flat grey screen. LUNA stands.*

LUNA: *(brightly, too brightly)* Right. That's me.

---

## Episode 3 - Kuu's Street

*A long, narrow street in warm lamplight. Every shop sells one thing. There is a bakery, but it sells apologies in paper bags. A tailor sells apologies by the metre. A florist sells bouquets of apologies, wrapped in cellophane.*

KUU: *(very quietly)* Oh no.

PLACK: *(from a rooftop, calling down)* What is it?

KUU: *(looking around)* It's everything I've ever said.

*He walks slowly down the street. A shopkeeper, a figure in an apron with no face, holds up a small paper bag. Written on it, in neat handwriting: "Sorry I'm late."*

KUU: *(taking it)* That was in the third year.

*The next shop: "Sorry I didn't call." The next: "Sorry for being in the way." Hundreds of them, an endless row.*

*PLACK drops down from the roof, lands beside him.*

PLACK: Kuu.

KUU: *(flatly)* It's okay. I know.

PLACK: You don't have to buy them all.

KUU: *(looking at the bag in his hands)* I don't know how to stop.

*A silence. PLACK takes the bag from him. She opens it, looks inside, and tips it upside down. Nothing falls out.*

PLACK: See? It's empty.

KUU: *(staring)* It is.

PLACK: They're not worth anything. You're allowed to put them down.

*KUU, slowly, lets the rest of the bags fall from his arms.*

**[Optional dialogue: a short exchange with PLACK in which she offers, quietly, to hold his umbrella for a while.]**

## Episode 4 - The Message (Midpoint)

*A bridge of chalk-white stairs connects the islands. At its centre, a stone slab set into the path. Carved into it, in dots and dashes: a line of Morse.*

PLACK: *(crouching, tracing it with a finger)* It's clean. Clearer than the screen.

LUNA: *(arriving, hands in pockets)* Can you read it?

PLACK: *(without looking up)* Can *you*?

LUNA: *(a pause)* ...No.

*PLACK glances up, surprised, then back at the slab.*

PLACK: *(slowly)* "The angels bring mercy for all."

*A silence. A breeze, impossibly gentle, lifts the hem of KUU's coat.*

KUU: That's nice.

PLACK: *(very quietly)* Is it?

*Below the line, a second, smaller line is carved, as if added later in a hurry: "Not those."*

LUNA: *(reading it, quietly)* Not those.

*The stairs begin to drift. Slowly, gently, as if someone pulled a thread, the whole bridge rotates a quarter-turn, and a new island comes into view.*

**[Gameplay: the player must read all four Morse slabs, one on each island, in order to unlock the final bridge. Each slab is a short, tense moment. The text appears in-game as the player decodes it through a minigame.]**

---

## Episode 5 - Plack's Island

*A wedding cake as tall as a tower. Tiers of white icing, dripping red. Around it, a banquet table, long, lit with candles. Place cards in front of each seat. They are written in teeth.*

*PLACK stands at the foot of the table, quite still.*

LUNA: *(arriving, softly)* Plack?

PLACK: *(not turning)* It's all right.

LUNA: *(looking at the cake)* It's not.

PLACK: It's my island. It's mine to be in.

*She walks to the head of the table. The place card reads: "BRIDE." The next seat: "GROOM." The next, "MOTHER." The next, "FATHER." The last, "WITNESS."*

*PLACK touches the last card.*

PLACK: *(very quietly)* I've never been to one.

LUNA: A wedding?

PLACK: A table where someone would have saved me a seat.

*LUNA doesn't answer. She crosses the grass, pulls out the chair beside the BRIDE card, and sits in it. Then, after a moment, she lifts the card and turns it over. On the back, in her own hand: "Luna."*

*PLACK stares at it.*

PLACK: ...You wrote that?

LUNA: I haven't yet.

*A very small, very private silence.*

**[Combat: Cake Wraiths, shapeless creatures made of icing and teeth, attack in tiers. Plack's barks are quieter than usual throughout.]**

---

## Episode 6 - Horror Passing By

*A long stairway of impossible angles. At the corners, between the pastel clouds, hyper-realistic figures walk by. A man with too many teeth. A woman with her face pressed flat against a pane of glass. A child with a hundred-eyed face, standing perfectly still.*

KUU: *(very small)* Don't look.

LUNA: I'm not looking.

KUU: You're looking.

LUNA: *(cheerfully, firmly)* I'm looking *through*.

*The three keep walking. A figure steps out from behind a cloud, a tall thing, wrong in every proportion, and stops in front of them. It tilts its head. Its mouth opens, and out of it comes a note of the music box melody from the first scene.*

LUNA: *(with great politeness)* I'm sorry, you're very upsetting. Could you move?

*The figure considers this. Then, as if embarrassed, it steps aside.*

PLACK: *(slowly)* Did that just work?

LUNA: Rudeness is a great weapon against things that expect to be feared.

KUU: *(clutching his coat)* I didn't expect it to be that easy.

LUNA: *(a faint, thoughtful pause)* It wasn't.

**[Gameplay: apparitions may be ignored if the player does not look at them. Looking at them accumulates "Dread", a cosmetic meter that distorts the screen. Nothing mechanical.]**

---

## Episode 7 - The Mimic

*The final island: a perfect copy of the squad's starting field, with a tiny house at its centre. In the doorway, a figure in a long coat, hair in the same style, the same two coats. Her face is Luna's.*

MIMIC: *(in LUNA's voice, softly)* Hello.

LUNA: *(very still)* Hello.

MIMIC: You came a long way.

LUNA: *(managing a smile)* I like walking.

MIMIC: *(tilting her head)* You don't, though.

*A silence. PLACK and KUU move to either side, wary. The MIMIC does not glance at them.*

MIMIC: You say you do. But you never walk anywhere you haven't been sent.

LUNA: *(lightly)* That's not nice.

MIMIC: *(gently)* It's not meant to be nice. It's meant to be true. *(she smiles, almost sadly)* You don't know why you're doing this.

*LUNA doesn't answer. The wind picks up. The MIMIC's coat flutters.*

MIMIC: You're going to do it anyway. Because it's easier to keep walking than to stop and ask.

*LUNA's jaw tightens. Her hand twitches toward her coat pocket, and stops.*

PLACK: *(softly)* Luna.

LUNA: *(very quietly)* I know.

*The MIMIC, with a sudden, strange tenderness, takes Plack's face. Then Kuu's. Then Luna's. Each in turn flickers across the figure, like a slide reel.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Mimic (Boss)

**[Boss: The Mimic. Phase 1: it wears Luna's face and copies her abilities. Phase 2: it wears Plack's face and copies her ledger-throws. Phase 3: it wears Kuu's face and fights defensively. The player must break each phase by speaking to it, a choice-driven boss mechanic. All three must "agree" to defeat it.]**

LUNA: *(addressing the MIMIC in LUNA's face)* I don't know why I'm doing it.

*The MIMIC stops.*

LUNA: *(steadily)* That's true. I don't. But it's *mine*, the not-knowing. You don't get to have it.

*The MIMIC flinches. Its face flickers.*

*Next: PLACK steps forward.*

PLACK: *(to the MIMIC in PLACK's face)* I've never been invited. That's true too. But I'm here, and I'd rather be here without one than anywhere with.

*The MIMIC recoils. KUU, last, steps forward, shaking.*

KUU: *(to the MIMIC in his own face)* I'm sorry! *(a breath)* No. Wait. I'm not. Not about this. I'm not sorry about this.

*The MIMIC's face cracks, three faces overlapping, then none.*

*A long silence. A distant music-box note, rising, and stopping.*

*On the nearest slab, a new line of Morse appears, carved by an unseen hand: "THE REST ARE ALL FILTH COMPARED TO ITS EXISTENCE."*

*PLACK reads it aloud. Then, quietly:*

PLACK: *(softly)* "True angels. The one."

LUNA: *(very quiet)* There's a one.

### Stinger

*The three wake in the same field. Actual rain on actual grass. The sky is a normal grey. The teacup is gone.*

*KUU sits up, rubbing his eyes. He reaches into his coat pocket, and withdraws a small paper bag. It is empty. He looks at it for a long time, then folds it, carefully, into a small square, and puts it back.*

*The hum from Chapter 9 is gone.*

**[Chapter 10 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 11. Seeds: "TRUE ANGELS THE ONE"; each squad member's private wound; the Mimic's "you don't know why"; Kuu's folded paper bag; the hum's end.]**
