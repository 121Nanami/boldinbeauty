# Ch 21 - Fixed Dose (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Side story: **Alchemist** and **Blood** (both 5-star, MAIN section 5). Characterisation not documented; PROPOSED here. Alchemist: brisk, cheerful, dangerously curious, talks to her experiments. Blood: huge, quiet, practical, carries everything, apologises to nobody and checks on everybody.

Chapter promise: the 1% is not a dial. It is a wall.

---

## Episode 1 - Burnt Sugar

*A greenhouse on the farm terraces, glass walls fogged with steam, rows of tomatoes, ferns, glowing mushrooms. A smell of burnt sugar and warm earth. ALCHEMIST, in a canvas apron, goggles pushed up, adds a drop of liquid to a yellow cube in a glass dish.*

**[Cold open. Warm lights, birdsong from a speaker, then a faint sizzle.]**

ALCHEMIST: *(to the cube)* There you are. Not so scary, hmm? A bit of nitrate. A bit of love.

*The cube fizzes, brightens. She claps her hands.*

ALCHEMIST: *(over her shoulder)* Blood! It took the drop!

*BLOOD, in the doorway, a crate of flasks balanced on one shoulder, glances at the dish with mild suspicion.*

BLOOD: It's glowing.

ALCHEMIST: It's *supposed* to be glowing.

BLOOD: *(setting the crate down very carefully)* It wasn't glowing yesterday.

ALCHEMIST: Yesterday it was incomplete.

BLOOD: *(a pause)* Or it was safe.

ALCHEMIST: *(waving a hand)* You worry.

BLOOD: Someone has to.

**[Tutorial: dual-protagonist chapter. Player controls Alchemist (ranged, chemical effects) and Blood (melee, protective). Swap with a single key.]**

---

## Episode 2 - Terraces

*The farm terraces, stepped hillsides of crops under artificial sun. Workers in straw hats bend among the rows. ALCHEMIST and BLOOD walk the path.*

ALCHEMIST: *(counting on her fingers)* Every operator burns out within three years. It's a *pattern*. The angel share in us, it's a hundredth of the whole, and it's not enough to sustain the load. We *fade*.

BLOOD: *(lugging a sack of fertiliser)* Fading isn't burning.

ALCHEMIST: It's the same thing. A candle's a slow fire.

BLOOD: *(grunting)* I don't like when you talk about us like candles.

ALCHEMIST: *(cheerfully)* You're a bonfire, dear.

*A young operator, thin, tired, with a cough, waves from a row of beans.*

OPERATOR: Is it ready?

ALCHEMIST: *(softly, to BLOOD)* That's Test Subject One.

BLOOD: *(low)* I know who it is.

ALCHEMIST: He volunteered.

BLOOD: *(quietly)* Everyone volunteers.

## Episode 3 - Test One

*A greenhouse annex. A cot, a drip, a row of monitors. TEST SUBJECT ONE sits on the edge, sleeves rolled, a cube of yellow light in a glass sleeve pressed to his arm. ALCHEMIST adjusts a dial. BLOOD stands at the door, arms folded.*

ALCHEMIST: *(gently)* On three.

TEST SUBJECT: *(nodding)* Okay.

ALCHEMIST: One. Two. *(a pause)* Three.

*A hiss. A soft pulse of light travels up the subject's arm. His eyes close. His breathing slows. The monitors flicker, then settle.*

*A long moment. Then, very quietly, he sits up and opens his eyes.*

TEST SUBJECT: *(puzzled)* ...Could I have some tea?

*ALCHEMIST lets out a long breath, and begins, helplessly, to laugh.*

ALCHEMIST: *(wiping her eyes)* Blood. Blood, he wants tea!

BLOOD: *(a slow, rare smile)* I'll put the kettle on.

**[Gameplay beat: a brief, joyful non-combat scene. The player makes tea as a mini-game.]**

---

## Episode 4 - Test Two (Midpoint)

*The same annex. A new cot. A new subject, a woman in her thirties, composed, calm. The same pulse, the same dial. The same hiss.*

*This time, the monitors do not settle. The lines rise, climb, flatten. A low hum builds, a note both familiar and wrong.*

ALCHEMIST: *(very quietly)* Blood.

BLOOD: *(already moving)* I see it.

*The woman opens her eyes. They are the wrong colour, bright, bleached, unfocussed. She does not ask for tea.*

SUBJECT TWO: *(in a voice that is not entirely hers)* ...Counted.

*A silence. ALCHEMIST's hands go still on the dial.*

SUBJECT TWO: *(softly)* ...Counted. Counted. Counted.

*Her skin begins to glow from within, a pale gold that spreads across her face. The glass in the cot's frame cracks.*

BLOOD: *(grabbing ALCHEMIST by the shoulders)* Back!

*The subject sits up, slowly, and the cot buckles beneath her.*

**[Gameplay beat: the first combat of the chapter. The player controls Blood, pushing Alchemist out of the line of fire.]**

---

## Episode 5 - The Ceiling

*The laboratory, in lockdown. Red lights. A blast door sealed. ALCHEMIST and BLOOD, at a console, scrolling through a wall of formulae.*

ALCHEMIST: *(muttering)* It should have worked. The dosage was within tolerance. The share was calibrated to...

*She stops.*

BLOOD: What?

ALCHEMIST: *(very quietly)* It isn't a dial.

BLOOD: ...What isn't?

ALCHEMIST: The one percent. *(she turns, her face white)* It's not a setting. It's a *ceiling*.

*BLOOD looks at the monitor, at the line where the percentage, in clean white numerals, stops at 1.00 and every curve above it turns to noise.*

BLOOD: *(slowly)* So if you go past it...

ALCHEMIST: *(softly)* You don't become stronger. You become... *(she looks for the word)* ...something that isn't an operator any more.

BLOOD: *(after a very long pause)* Angel.

ALCHEMIST: *(a flat whisper)* Or a thing that's trying to be.

**[Combat: lab-wide fight. Plant Creatures and Lab Drones, plus corrupted cubes that spawn minor enemies.]**

*In the glass of the console, her reflection looks very small.*

---

## Episode 6 - Cold Store

*The cold store, a long refrigerated hall with racks of frozen samples and ranks of coolant tanks. Mist rolls along the floor. BLOOD carries ALCHEMIST on his back through knee-high haze, a rifle across his chest.*

ALCHEMIST: *(muffled)* I can walk, you know.

BLOOD: *(not slowing)* You're trembling.

ALCHEMIST: It's cold.

BLOOD: *(a pause)* It isn't that.

*A silence. The mist swirls. Somewhere ahead, a slow, regular dripping.*

ALCHEMIST: *(very softly)* I killed her, Blood.

BLOOD: *(without turning)* You didn't.

ALCHEMIST: I turned the dial.

BLOOD: *(evenly)* And I put her in the cot. *(a beat)* And she volunteered. *(a longer beat)* None of us knew. That's all.

*ALCHEMIST says nothing. Her arms tighten around his neck.*

BLOOD: *(very quietly)* We stop it now.

ALCHEMIST: *(a pause)* How?

BLOOD: *(a long breath)* The way we should have started.

---

## Episode 7 - Overdose

*The reactor annex, at the heart of the greenhouse. A vast bell-jar of glass, ten metres high, in which a figure floats, curled, suspended in yellow light. SUBJECT TWO, huge now, her shape elongated, her skin like beaten gold.*

**[Low point. The jar shatters.]**

ALCHEMIST: *(softly)* She's still in there.

BLOOD: *(grimly)* I know.

*The glass cracks, a single clean line, then a lacework. The figure uncurls.*

ALCHEMIST: *(a half-sob)* I'm so sorry.

*A voice, gentle, enormous, like a church bell heard through water.*

OVERDOSE: ...*thank you for the dose.*

*BLOOD steps in front of ALCHEMIST. He drops the rifle.*

BLOOD: *(quietly)* Stay behind me.

---

## Episode 8 - Overdose (Boss)

**[Boss: Overdose. Phase 1: it swings long golden limbs; Blood tanks, Alchemist dispenses buffs. Phase 2: it exhales a cloud of "dose", which empowers nearby plants; Alchemist's anti-dose clears them. Phase 3: it drops to its knees; the player must use the cubes in the lab, set off in sequence, to "draw" the angel share back out.]**

*The fight is slow, mournful, almost tender. OVERDOSE does not hate them. Each of its attacks is a plea. BLOOD takes the hits, one after another, a wall of muscle between her and the others.*

*In the final phase, ALCHEMIST, shaking, takes up a cube, and with a soft click, sets it to a single, specific frequency.*

ALCHEMIST: *(through tears)* One percent. *(a breath)* One percent. One.

*The cube sings, a clear, quiet note. OVERDOSE hesitates. Slowly, like a tide, the gold drains from her limbs, back into the cube.*

*A single woman lies on the floor of the annex, thin, exhausted, breathing.*

BLOOD: *(kneeling)* She's alive.

ALCHEMIST: *(very quietly)* ...Is she?

*SUBJECT TWO opens her eyes. They are grey, ordinary, tired.*

SUBJECT TWO: *(in a thread of a voice)* ...Could I have some tea?

*BLOOD, wordless, with a hand that shakes, brushes the hair from her forehead.*

### Stinger

*The greenhouse, morning. The glass is broken. Sunlight, the artificial kind, slants in. ALCHEMIST, at a bench, writes a number on a paper label: "1". She sticks it to the cube, and places the cube in a drawer.*

*She slides the drawer shut. Turns the key. Hangs the key on a nail by the door.*

BLOOD: *(from the doorway)* That's it?

ALCHEMIST: *(not looking at him)* That's it.

*A pause. LUNA, in the doorway behind him, with a bag of oranges.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* I'll file this under "not worth it".

ALCHEMIST: *(a ghost of a smile)* File it under "never again".
