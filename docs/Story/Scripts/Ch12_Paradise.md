# Ch 12 - Paradise (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch12_Paradise`; owner brief: a beautiful neo-tropical city, not ruined, bright vibrant colours, constant good vibes, quite heavy rainfall. Owner decision applied: **Luna and Plack marry in 2009**, in this chapter.

New voice: **THE AMBASSADOR**, a celebrity operator who is warm on camera and brittle off it. **CAMERA DRONES** record everything; some scenes have a "broadcast" label meaning the whole arc is watching.

Chapter promise: the best day of their lives is on television, and the sponsor owns the rights.

---

## Episode 1 - Guests of Honour

*After Nowhere's grey, the colour hits like a slap. Coral, turquoise, lime and sunflower buildings, palm trees, canals, waterfalls. A brass band plays. Rain falls warm and heavy and nobody carries an umbrella. Banners everywhere: three feathered lines in a wing shape.*

**[Cold open: saturated, loud, joyous. A reverse of Ch 11's silence.]**

AMBASSADOR: *(arms wide, a white smile, on a balcony)* Welcome, welcome, welcome to Paradise! Our guests of honour have arrived!

*A crowd cheers. Confetti, warm rain. CAMERA DRONES wheel overhead, each carrying a small wing logo.*

KUU: *(flinching from the noise)* Are they cheering for us?

PLACK: They're cheering for something.

LUNA: *(tipping an imaginary hat)* Thank you, thank you. You're too kind.

PLACK: *(under her breath)* Luna.

LUNA: *(under hers)* I'm adapting.

COMMANDER: *(radio, apologetic)* Unit Luna, this is a government showcase. You've been invited as "operators of merit". Smile. Eat something. Don't cause an incident.

LUNA: Define "incident".

COMMANDER: ...Anything on television.

**[Tutorial: social hub level. No combat. The player may talk to NPCs, eat at stalls, and ride the canal boats.]**

## Episode 2 - The Menu

*A canal-side restaurant. A waiter with a wing-logo apron sets down plates: five dishes, steaming, each more colourful than the last. LUNA eats three of them in the space of a minute.*

LUNA: *(through a mouthful)* This is the best food I've ever had.

KUU: *(timidly sampling)* It's very good.

PLACK: *(not eating, reading the menu)* There's a logo.

LUNA: *(mouth full)* There's a logo on everything.

PLACK: No, I mean there's a logo on *everything*. The plates. The cutlery. The napkins. The sauces. *(she turns the menu over)* The ink on the menu is a pattern of tiny wings.

KUU: *(looking up)* Is that bad?

PLACK: *(very evenly)* It means someone designed the meal, the plate, the menu and the napkin in one go. It's not a restaurant. It's an advertisement you can eat.

*LUNA lowers her fork, looks at the plate, then at the logo.*

LUNA: ...It's still very good food.

PLACK: I know.

LUNA: *(to herself)* Annoyingly.

**[Pickup: Wing-Logo Menu. Lore item. A line of text on the back: "Property of the Patron."]**

## Episode 3 - The Question (Midpoint)

*A rooftop garden, high above the canals. Waterfalls pour off the terraces into rainbows. A glass pavilion. No one else is there.*

*PLACK has been quiet since lunch. She stops at the rail, looking over the city.*

LUNA: *(joining her)* You've gone quiet.

PLACK: I've been thinking.

LUNA: That's dangerous.

PLACK: It is.

*A pause. The rain softens to a drizzle. PLACK turns, and takes something from her satchel, a small ring on a chain, worn smooth.*

PLACK: I've carried this for four years. I didn't know what for. It was my mother's, probably. Someone left it in my first delivery bag, no note.

LUNA: *(very still)* Plack.

PLACK: I'd like to ask you something. And I'd like you to be honest.

LUNA: I'm always honest.

PLACK: You're never honest, Luna. You're charming. It's different.

*LUNA, for once, has nothing to say.*

PLACK: I've been in three jobs and four cities and I've never had a place I wanted to come back to. And then you opened a crate.

*She holds out the ring.*

PLACK: Will you marry me?

*A silence. A long one. A drone buzzes overhead, and PLACK, without turning her head, says:*

PLACK: Go away.

*The drone, apologetically, retreats.*

LUNA: *(her voice not quite steady)* Plack. I... *(she laughs, a sound halfway to a sob)* I've never made a plan that didn't involve a way out.

PLACK: I know.

LUNA: This one doesn't.

PLACK: *(softly)* I know that, too.

*LUNA takes the ring. Looks at it. Looks at her.*

LUNA: *(finally, a whisper)* Yes.

**[Gameplay beat: a quiet cinematic. No UI. The camera pulls back, slowly, to reveal KUU watching from behind a pillar, both hands clasped over his mouth, silently weeping.]**

## Episode 4 - The Wedding (2009)

*A rooftop garden decked with flowers. Waterfalls, lanterns, a long white carpet. A small crowd of residents from the Hanging City, in their best mismatched layers, brought up by the sponsors as "extras". CAMERA DRONES hover in a ring. A banner: "THE WEDDING OF THE YEAR, brought to you by..." The wing logo.*

**[Broadcast: the entire Ark watches. Small, in-world TV overlay in the corner of the screen.]**

KUU: *(in a slightly too-large formal jacket, holding a ring box)* I have the rings.

PLACK: *(in white, hair braided)* Don't lose them.

KUU: I won't.

PLACK: Kuu.

KUU: I won't!

*He loses them. Twice. The first time, a canal boat. The second, a fountain. Each time, LUNA, in a flowing white dress with a veil of silk, watching it all with a grin, puts a hand over her mouth.*

*At the rail, the registrar clears her throat.*

REGISTRAR: *(a little desperately)* Dearly beloved, we are gathered, on this beautiful, rain-washed day...

LUNA: *(sotto voce)* She's reading from a sponsor's script.

PLACK: *(sotto voce)* Ignore it.

REGISTRAR: ...to witness the union of two exceptional operators, blessed by the generosity of...

LUNA: *(sweetly, to the nearest drone)* I'd like to say my own vows.

*The REGISTRAR freezes. A drone buzzes in.*

LUNA: *(to PLACK, not the camera)* I'm not good at promises. I make things up as I go. But I've never once made you up. *(a small breath)* You're the only real thing I've ever found in a box.

*PLACK, eyes bright, says nothing. Then, very quietly:*

PLACK: I'd have bitten anyone else.

*The crowd laughs. The rain softens. KUU, at the back, blows his nose.*

**[Gameplay: no combat. Player controls the walk down the aisle. A bark system lets the player pick Luna's vows from three lines; each is short, none is wrong.]**

## Episode 5 - The Service Tunnels

*Evening. The reception thrums above. KUU, slipping away to refill a punch bowl, finds a stairwell behind a palm. He goes down.*

*A concrete corridor, harshly lit. Pipes, ducts, trolleys of food. A queue of people in grey uniforms, waiting at a window. Above the window: RATIONING OFFICE. Beneath it: "Show your card."*

*KUU watches. A woman at the front takes a small paper bag, no logo. Inside: a bread roll and a cup of water. She nods and moves aside.*

KUU: *(whispering to himself)* They cook it. And they eat this.

*A cook in a stained apron sees him, a wing-logo badge on the pocket of the uniform. She does not look afraid. She looks tired.*

COOK: You're one of the guests.

KUU: *(embarrassed)* I got lost. Sorry.

COOK: *(not unkindly)* Go back up, honey. They'll notice.

KUU: I... *(he looks at the bread roll in her hand)* Can I help?

*She considers him, then, almost against her will, smiles.*

COOK: You can carry the trays.

**[Optional sequence: help the cooks. Each task unlocks a short vignette. Reward: Kuu's "service tunnel" backpack charm.]**

## Episode 6 - Receipts

*The bridal suite at midnight. LUNA, shoes off, in a robe, sits cross-legged on the bed with a stack of papers. PLACK, hair down, brings tea.*

PLACK: What's all this?

LUNA: Receipts.

PLACK: For what?

LUNA: For everything. *(she holds up a pink slip)* This is the florist. *(a yellow one)* This is the caterer. *(a blue one)* This is the sponsor's "licence fee for use of likeness at an authorised ceremony". *(she looks at it, grimly)* They're charging us.

PLACK: *(setting down the tea)* Of course they are.

LUNA: It's a hundred and forty thousand credits.

PLACK: *(sitting)* We don't have a hundred and forty thousand credits.

LUNA: No. But *they* do. And every one of these has a signature.

*She spreads them across the sheets. Names, stamps, a wing at the top of each.*

LUNA: I've been bad at being nice. I'm going to be good at this.

PLACK: Collecting receipts?

LUNA: *(tapping one)* Evidence.

*PLACK looks at her for a long moment, then picks up one receipt, reads it, and quietly begins sorting by date.*

PLACK: *(without looking up)* Start with the oldest ones.

## Episode 7 - Bless the Union

*The lagoon, dawn. The beach has been dressed for a "sunrise blessing ceremony". A white pavilion, floral arches, a wing-logo sunrise on a backdrop. CAMERA DRONES in formation. The AMBASSADOR in a white linen suit, wading through shallow surf.*

**[Low point. The Ambassador's mask slips on live broadcast.]**

AMBASSADOR: *(to the cameras, radiant)* Friends! On this joyous morning, we celebrate, not just a marriage, but the harmony between operators and the government!

*A smattering of applause. LUNA, at the edge of the surf, in a plain white dress, arm in arm with PLACK, does not clap.*

AMBASSADOR: *(to LUNA, pleasantly)* Would you like to say a few words?

LUNA: *(carefully)* Thank you.

AMBASSADOR: *(smiling wider)* It's an honour to have you.

LUNA: I'd like to ask a question. Who paid for the rain?

*A silence. A drone hovers closer. The AMBASSADOR's smile does not move.*

AMBASSADOR: I'm sorry?

LUNA: The rain. It's heavy. It's warm. It's been falling for eleven days. In the Ark, no one has seen a cloud since 2002. *(a pause)* I think someone's making it. And I think someone's selling it.

*A murmur runs through the crowd. The AMBASSADOR laughs, a brittle, practised trill.*

AMBASSADOR: A joke! Our guest is a wit!

LUNA: *(very softly, almost kindly)* Who's paying for the rain?

*The smile drops. For a single frame, the AMBASSADOR's face is cold, assessing, entirely human. Then it's back, wider than ever.*

AMBASSADOR: *(through his teeth)* Cut the feed.

## Episode 8 - The Ambassador (Boss)

**[Boss: The Ambassador. Phase 1: choreographed "performance" attacks with camera-drone shields; destroy drones to expose him. Phase 2: wades into the lagoon, summoning Beach Guards and Brand Elites. Phase 3: he removes his jacket and wings of pure sponsor-light unfold; dodge spotlights.]**

*The lagoon becomes an arena. Surf, sand, floating pavilion. The fight is, by design, absurdly photogenic: slow-motion shots, confetti, perfectly composed.*

KUU: *(ducking a flying floral arch)* I think they're still filming!

PLACK: *(catching a drone with her satchel)* Of course they are!

LUNA: *(grinning, hair whipping)* Then give them something to watch!

*The AMBASSADOR, enraged, spreads his wings.*

AMBASSADOR: *(to the camera, no longer smiling)* I am the face of this arc!

LUNA: *(punching him squarely)* Then wear it better!

*He goes down in a spray of surf. The crowd gasps. The drones, uncertain, descend, and for a long moment no one speaks.*

*Then, from the crowd, one of the Hanging City guests begins to clap. Slowly. Then another. Then the cooks, from the back, and the lift clerk, and all the extras. It spreads. A warm, rising roar.*

*The drones, confused, record it.*

### Stinger

*A back road, dusk. The squad walks out of Paradise, soaked and glowing, carrying a bag of mangoes. PLACK holds LUNA's hand. KUU walks a step behind, the duck on his pack.*

*LUNA tears a cheque in half. It is the sponsor's honorarium: two hundred thousand credits, a wing at the top.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* I'm done being nice.

PLACK: *(squeezing her hand)* I know.

*She lets the pieces fall into the gutter.*

**[Chapter 12 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 13. Seeds: the Patron's wing logo; the rationing office; Luna's receipts; "who pays for the rain"; the wedding (2009); Luna's resolve.]**
