# Ch 06 - The Moon Kingdom - Script

**Arc 1 / 2009 / REWORK.** A second arc built like the first, with no living creature, covered in flora, and a vast dim Moon inside
the cavern that casts a deep darkness.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
Black. A drip. Then, slowly, a pale circle: a Moon where no sky can be, hanging in the middle of a cavern the size of a city. Under it,
a town: gates, a market, houses with roofs of green. A sign at the gate reads POPULATION and under it, in neat paint, 0.

## Episode 1 - "Population: Zero"
**Scene:** Gate plaza. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: It's lovely.
PLACK: It's empty.
KUU: It's *lovely* and empty.
LUNA: It is a second arc.
PLACK: A government drone came three times and didn't return. That's why we're here.
LUNA: Then it is a very quiet place to lose a drone.
*(In the doorway of the nearest house, a bunch of flowers has grown through the door-frame and out the other side.)*
KUU: (very softly) Sorry. (to the flowers) Sorry.
LUNA: You apologise to flowers now.
KUU: They were *there first.*

**Gameplay:** Dark exploration (the Moon's light is the only light), flora enemies.
**Beat:** The place is real, lived-in, and empty.

## Episode 2 - "Plates Laid"
**Scene:** A row of houses. **Present:** all three.

PLACK: The table is laid.
KUU: With plates?
PLACK: Four. Four plates. There is a candle that has burnt down to a stub.
LUNA: Someone was about to have supper.
PLACK: Someone was *having* supper. The chairs are pushed back. (she touches one) They stood up all at once.
KUU: Maybe the alarm went.
PLACK: There's no alarm bell in the street.
LUNA: Then it was not an alarm.
KUU: What was it?
LUNA: Something that made everyone stand up and leave their plates. (a pause) I want to know what it *was.*

**Gameplay:** House-by-house exploration (each house a short combat); a pickup chain of letters (YELLOW).
**Beat:** The town was emptied in a moment.

## Episode 3 - "Every Coin"
**Scene:** The market. **Present:** all three.

KUU: There are coins on every counter.
LUNA: Good. We will take them.
KUU: Luna, there are *coins on every counter.* All of them. Not one is missing. Not one is *taken.*
LUNA: Maybe it was a very honest market.
PLACK: Nobody leaves a market with the coins on the counter.
LUNA: They were interrupted.
PLACK: All of them? At the same moment?
LUNA: (picks up a coin; looks at both sides) ...This one is warm.
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: It's warm, Kuu. It is *warm.*

**Gameplay:** Market combat; flora creatures guarding stalls; a shortcut across the rooftops via vines.
**Beat:** The place is not old. It was emptied recently, or is not yet finished.

## Episode 4 - "Empty Tombs"
**Scene:** The cemetery wall. **Present:** all three. *(HINT: Death)*

*(A long wall of crypts, each with a name carved on the door. Every door is open. Every crypt is empty.)*
PLACK: (reading) Aoyama. Hayashi. Nakagawa. Kono. (she stops) No bodies.
KUU: Maybe they were taken somewhere.
PLACK: Not one is missing.
KUU: ...Wait. Not one is *missing,* or not one is *here?*
PLACK: (flatly) Every crypt was opened from the *inside.*
*(A silence. The Moon is very large.)*
LUNA: (quietly, genuinely) That is not what I came for.

**Gameplay:** Crypt-wall exploration; light puzzle: relight the lanterns in order; flora creatures emerge.
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The dead of the second arc are not accounted for.

## Episode 5 - "Lily Pads"
**Scene:** The lake. **Present:** all three.

LUNA: I would like to argue with the Moon.
PLACK: You can't argue with the Moon.
LUNA: I can argue with anything that doesn't leave.
*(Luna stands on a lily pad and talks at the Moon for the whole crossing. The Moon, a large indifferent disc, does not respond.)*
LUNA: It's winning.
KUU: It's not even *participating.*
LUNA: That is how it's winning. (she hops to the next pad) It has the confidence of a thing that doesn't need to turn up.

**Gameplay:** Lake crossing on lily pads (jump); a swim option; a mini-boss: a Vine Walker.
**Beat:** A light moment before the darkest house.

## Episode 6 - "Three Names"
**Scene:** The last house, an old one. **Present:** all three.

*(Carved into the door-frame at a child's height, three names, each in a different hand: Luna. Plack. Kuu.)*
KUU: ...That's us.
PLACK: It's not us. It's *names.* It's someone else called Luna.
LUNA: It's in my handwriting.
KUU: (very quietly) It's... *older* than your handwriting.
*(Luna touches the carving. For a second the moss under her thumb is dry.)*
LUNA: The date.
PLACK: There's no date.
LUNA: There's always a date. (she turns away) I will not think about it. I will think about lunch.
PLACK: Luna --
LUNA: *Lunch,* Plack.

**Gameplay:** Short non-combat (PURPLE) scene; a locked door opens with a key found on the lake.
**Beat:** A personal shock. Luna refuses to discuss it.

## Episode 7 - "The Gardener Is Weeding"
**Scene:** The island under the Moon. **Present:** all three.

*(The Gardener, a tall figure of vines and blossoms, bends over a flower bed with a trowel. It does not look up.)*
KUU: It isn't guarding anything.
PLACK: It's *weeding.*
LUNA: It is *weeding the graves.*
*(Flowers grow back as it plucks them. It is patient. It is very patient.)*
PLACK: It's been doing this since --
LUNA: Since before us.
*(The Gardener straightens. It has no face; it has a rose where the face should be.)*
GARDENER: (a voice made of rustling) You are standing on the bed.

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Gardener's hands (stay out of the flower beds; a stealth-timing puzzle).
**Beat:** **Low point.** The place is tended. By someone.

## Episode 8 - "The Gardener"
**Scene:** The island. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

GARDENER: Please. Leave the bed.
KUU: We are *trying --*
GARDENER: The bed is for the ones who are coming.
PLACK: Who is coming?
GARDENER: (the rose turns to her) The ones for whom the beds were laid.
LUNA: (flat) And what happens to the ones who are here already?
GARDENER: ...They are the ones who are *in* the bed.
*(Boss: the Gardener. Phase 1, trowel and vines; Phase 2, the beds grow around the squad; the Moon flickers.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: avoid the flower beds; cut the roots; the Moon's light pulses and exposes the weak point.
**Beat:** The Gardener falls. The Moon flickers the colour of the cubes in Chapter 5, for one second.

**Stinger:** The squad walks out under the Moon in silence. Kuu says, finally, "That was the yellow." Plack says, "I know." Luna says
nothing. Behind them the gate sign has changed. It reads POPULATION: and then, in smaller letters, *pending*.
