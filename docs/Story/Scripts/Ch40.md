# Ch 40 - They Were Purity - Script

**Arc 3 finale / 2017.** Main cast. The village, the monolith, Angel. Luna's arc ends. Epilogue: Neo Tokyo in the rain; Judge's courtroom.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 45 s)
A village at dawn in the rain, the same one: paddies, a torii, a stair. A cart-track. Three travellers, walking, with a very large
number of people behind them, in silence, in coats and blankets and armour, as far as the eye can see. The rain, for the first time in
seventeen years, stops.
KUU: (looking up) It's *stopped.*
PLACK: It's *waiting.*

## Episode 1 - "Dawn"
**Scene:** The cart-track and the torii. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu.

KUU: Do you remember the first time?
LUNA: I had a bag of fruit.
KUU: And a sock.
LUNA: And a sock. (she touches the torii) It's smaller.
PLACK: *You're* bigger.
LUNA: (a flicker; she looks at her hands) ...I think I am.
*(Behind them, the army of the arc: Council, Secrets, government, reformers, cooks, children. Judge, at the back, with her gavel at her side.)*
LUNA: (without turning) Is everyone here?
SHIRAISHI: (arriving, with a ledger) Item *four thousand and six.*
LUNA: ...Item four thousand and six?
SHIRAISHI: Everyone's here.

**Gameplay:** Walk-in (no combat); the torii; the host's gathering on the horizon.
**Beat:** The final approach.

## Episode 2 - "The Villager"
**Scene:** The village street. **Present:** all three. The Old Man (BLUE).

*(The same very small, very patient old man from Chapter 13, on the same bank, in the same coat.)*
OLD MAN: You've learned to plant.
LUNA: (a half-laugh) Not really.
OLD MAN: You've learned to *stand* in water. That's most of it.
KUU: (to Plack, low) He remembered.
PLACK: Everyone remembered.
OLD MAN: (to Luna, kindly) They kneel at dusk. They've stopped. (a pause) They stopped when *you* stopped being afraid of it.
LUNA: (a long beat) ...I'm still afraid.
OLD MAN: (patiently) That's not what I said.

**Gameplay:** Village hub (no combat); optional conversations (BLUE).
**Beat:** The village has changed.

## Episode 3 - "The Paddies"
**Scene:** The rice paddies. **Present:** all three. The angel host (RED).

*(The sky fills. Angels descend over the paddies in waves, each one the same: a halo, a pale face, a blank, serene look. They land in the
water without a splash.)*
PLACK: (reading it as a pattern) They're not attacking. They're *arriving.*
KUU: Is that worse?
PLACK: (flat) It's *much* worse.
LUNA: (a very small, very dry breath) Good. I'd hate for it to be easy.

**Gameplay:** Mass combat: angel host waves in the paddies (shallow water); allied NPCs fight alongside (the army of the arc).
**Beat:** The final wave.

## Episode 4 - "Through Angel"
**Scene:** The monolith's foot. **Present:** all three. Angel (BLUE).

*(The monolith, fifty metres, black, red glyphs. At its foot, serene, in a plain white robe, Angel, his hands folded.)*
ANGEL: Luna.
LUNA: Angel.
ANGEL: I did not think you would come.
LUNA: I always do. It's very annoying.
ANGEL: (gently) It is not annoying. It is... (he considers) *persistent.* I have noticed it. It is a rare quality, in your kind.
*(Behind him the glyphs flare. The voice speaks, not to Luna: through him. His lips move a half-second after the words.)*
VOICE (through Angel): *They wree purity, Impure sin, Thats all.*
LUNA: (very quietly) That's... that's the *same* thing.
ANGEL: It is the *only* thing. I have been trying to find another way of saying it for some time.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** The monolith's voice speaks through Angel.

## Episode 5 - "The River"
**Scene:** The river bridge. **Present:** all three.

*(The bridge, wooden, wet, cracking under the host's weight.)*
KUU: It's going.
PLACK: It's *going.*
LUNA: Hold it.
KUU: *How?*
LUNA: (a very small smile) Rope.
KUU: (hoarse, delighted, terrified) *Rope!*
*(He drops from the rail with a coil and a hook. Below, the water rushes. He does not look down.)*
KUU: (to himself, very low) I'm afraid of heights. It's a different fear.
*(The bridge holds. He lets out a long, shaking breath.)*
PLACK: (from the rail, soft) Kuu.
KUU: ...Yes?
PLACK: You *didn't* apologise.

**Gameplay:** Bridge-defence set piece (a QTE-lite hold).
**Beat:** Kuu holds the bridge, and doesn't apologise.

## Episode 6 - "The Glyphs Go Out"
**Scene:** The monolith's face. **Present:** all three.

*(The glyphs, hundreds of them, red on black, begin to dim one by one as the black hole turns on its face. Luna holds it at arm's length.
It's not a weapon this time. It's a *question.*)*
LUNA: (strained, to Plack) It's *eating* it.
PLACK: It's *reading* it.
LUNA: (a half-laugh) Same thing.
PLACK: (low) Don't let it *finish* reading.
LUNA: I won't. (a breath) *Hold* the white one. I need a push.
*(Plack takes the white hole, in both hands, and shoves. For a moment Luna's black hole and Plack's white hole are one; the glyph-face
shatters like glass in light.)*

**Gameplay:** Cooperative set piece (two operators, one ability); a rhythm segment.
**Beat:** The glyphs go out, one by one.

## Episode 7 - "Certain"
**Scene:** The monolith's foot. **Present:** all three.

ANGEL: (stepping forward; he is, for the first time, not entirely serene) You have broken the voice.
LUNA: Yes.
ANGEL: It was *kind* to speak to you.
LUNA: It was *loud.*
ANGEL: (almost sadly) You are certain.
LUNA: I'm not certain at all. (she looks at him) That's the *difference* between us.
ANGEL: (very gently) It is the difference between a mistake and a *verdict.*
LUNA: (quietly) That's what I'm *afraid* of.
*(He raises his hand. The ring behind his head becomes a wheel of light. The wings unfold.)*
ANGEL: Then let us *attend.*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: Angel's honour guard (the Hush) and a half-phase of Angel.
**Beat:** **Low point.** Angel is certain, and Luna is not.

## Episode 8 - "Angel"
**Scene:** The monolith's foot. Boss fight. **Present:** all three.

*(Boss: Angel, three phases. Phase 1, the host: light-blade and wings; Phase 2, glyph cracks run across his body and the arena becomes the monolith's
face; Phase 3, the voice speaks through him and the squad's abilities are muted.)*
ANGEL: (as he fights) You were *purity.* All of you. Once.
LUNA: (taking a blow) I was *bored.*
ANGEL: Impure.
LUNA: (laughing, bleeding) Yes! That's *much* more like it!
*(In phase three the voice is the only sound. The squad can no longer hear each other.)*
PLACK: (mouthing, across the arena) *Now.*
KUU: (mouthing) *Which?*
LUNA: (mouthing, a bright, wicked grin) *The wrong one.*
*(She throws the white hole instead of the black. The monolith's face, which has been waiting for the other, has nothing to read.)*
ANGEL: (as the glyphs fall) ...Was any of it... purity?
LUNA: (quietly, kneeling to him) No.
ANGEL: (almost relieved) ...Thank you.

**Gameplay:** Boss: three phases; a muted phase (no audio cues); the "wrong hole" finish.
**Beat:** Angel falls. The war ends.

## Epilogue - "Spring, 2017"
**Scene:** Neo Tokyo, rain; Judge's courtroom. **Present:** all three, Judge.

*(Neo Tokyo, a thousand windows, a rain that is only rain. A courthouse. A bench. Luna in plain clothes, hair tied, no insignia.)*
JUDGE: Luna of the arc. You have been charged as a war criminal.
LUNA: Yes.
JUDGE: You have surrendered your command.
LUNA: Yes.
JUDGE: (dry) You are *aware* the court is not obliged to be gentle.
LUNA: I'd be disappointed if it were.
*(She looks over her shoulder. Plack, beside her, with a ring on a chain. Kuu, at the door, with a toaster.)*
JUDGE: (a pause) The court will hear the evidence.
LUNA: (quiet) Thank you. (a beat) I'd like to enter a receipt.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): the epilogue.
**Beat:** Luna asks to be tried.

**Stinger / ending:** It is raining in Neo Tokyo. On a rooftop, a water tank with a black umbrella over it. A pager goes off against
an empty space. A line on its screen: *Job 4472. Guard one crate. Pay: lunch.* Nobody answers. Somewhere below, three people, laughing,
are walking home.
