# Ch 09 - Remnant Remains (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch09_RemnantRemains`; owner brief: an empty desert with violent winds and constant downpour, a data centre in the middle hosting a small screen that states (verbatim):

`Angel//V1 Success Line down Operator//V! Faillllllllllllllllll ...---... ////// .- -.--. . .-.. ... / .-- .. .-.. .-.. / -.- .. .-.. .-.. / ..- ... / .- .-.. .-..`

The screen text is kept exactly as the owner wrote it. In-fiction, the Morse is damaged; Luna claims to read it, and cannot.

Chapter promise: the screen is a message, and Media is the one who sent it.

---

## Episode 1 - Wind

*A desert under a bruise-purple sky, dunes bent flat by wind, rain falling sideways in sheets. Visibility: twenty metres. A distant point of light, steady, the only fixed thing.*

**[Cold open. Rain and wind dominate the audio. Dialogue is half-shouted.]**

COMMANDER: *(radio, crackling)* ...copy... Unit Luna... recover what you can... from the site... *(static)*

LUNA: *(shouting)* "Recover what you can"?

COMMANDER: *(broken)* ...discretion...

LUNA: *(to PLACK)* That's government for "take whatever you like".

PLACK: *(shouting back)* It's government for "don't tell us what you took".

KUU: *(clutching his coat)* Can we go back?

LUNA: *(turning to him, gentle despite the volume)* Kuu. Look at me.

KUU: *(looking)* I'm looking.

LUNA: It's just weather.

KUU: It's a lot of weather.

LUNA: Then we'll take it a bit at a time.

*A gust shoves all three of them a metre sideways.*

LUNA: ...I take it back.

**[Tutorial: wind pushes the player; leaning into it conserves stamina. Dune ridges offer cover. Rain hides sound: enemies cannot hear you, and you cannot hear them.]**

## Episode 2 - Socks

*The dunes. A long trudge. Small things stick out of the sand: a bicycle wheel, a bucket, a traffic cone, a single boot.*

KUU: *(holding up the boot)* It's one boot.

PLACK: It's a boot.

KUU: Whose?

PLACK: Nobody's.

*He studies it a moment, then carefully sets it upright, as if in a small shrine.*

KUU: Sorry, boot.

LUNA: *(kicking sand)* I've lost a sock.

PLACK: Where?

LUNA: Somewhere in the last mile. It was a good sock.

PLACK: How does a person lose one sock without losing the shoe?

LUNA: I'd like it noted that the universe is extremely good at specific theft.

*PLACK reaches into her pack, pulls out a sock, and hands it over.*

LUNA: ...Is this mine?

PLACK: It was in the sand. About a hundred metres back.

LUNA: You noticed?

PLACK: Of course.

LUNA: *(quietly, genuinely)* Thank you.

PLACK: *(shrugging, adjusting her satchel)* It's what I do.

**[Combat: sand-drifter angels emerge from the dunes and drag the player down if the player stands still. Teaches the sprint-in-wind input.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Cables

*The first black cable emerges from the dune, thick as a tree trunk, running straight toward the horizon. Then another. Then eight, converging.*

PLACK: *(dropping to one knee, brushing sand)* Power trunk.

KUU: To what?

PLACK: Whatever's in the middle.

*They follow the nearest cable. It rises out of the sand at intervals into low arches, and they climb along it, balancing.*

**[Traversal: cable climbs, with rain-slick sections. Combat: Cable Crawlers that slide down the trunks. Optional: collect fused connectors for upgrade materials.]**

LUNA: *(from atop a trunk, arms spread)* It's a very long, very thin road!

KUU: *(clutching it)* It's slippery!

LUNA: That's what makes it exciting!

KUU: I disagree!

*PLACK crosses with steady, practised steps.*

PLACK: Weight on the centre line. Don't look at the wind.

KUU: *(gritting his teeth)* I'm not looking at it. It's looking at me.

*A gust. KUU slips. LUNA, without looking, grabs his collar and hauls him back up.*

LUNA: *(cheerfully)* I've got you.

KUU: *(clutching her sleeve)* You always do.

*A small silence. LUNA, for a moment, says nothing.*

LUNA: ...Obviously.

---

## Episode 4 - The Warm Room (Midpoint)

*The data centre. A tiered block rising out of the dune, four cooling towers venting soft steam. Lights on in every window. The air around it is calm, as if the storm doesn't dare.*

KUU: It's lit.

PLACK: It's warm.

LUNA: *(touching the wall)* It's not just warm. It's tidy.

*The main door opens at their approach. Inside, a lobby of pale carpet, humming server rows, a faint smell of ozone and coffee. A single armchair in the corner, the cushion dented.*

PLACK: Somebody sits here.

LUNA: Somebody sat here this morning.

*A cup on the arm of the chair, still faintly steaming.*

KUU: *(whispering)* Is it a trap?

PLACK: *(glancing at the ceiling)* Everything's a trap.

LUNA: *(sitting in the armchair)* Then we may as well be comfortable.

*A beat. Somewhere above, a cooling fan changes pitch, a note lower, and the lobby lights dim by exactly one step.*

PLACK: It knows we're here.

LUNA: *(still seated)* Good. It's rude to arrive unannounced.

**[Gameplay beat: the data centre is a "safe zone" until the squad draws a weapon. The player explores it freely for a minute before the first server-rack sentry unlocks the combat.]**

---

## Episode 5 - Vent

*The cooling towers. A spiral stair up the inside of a vast chimney, steam rising in slow clouds. At the top, a vent hums a long low note, a single sustained tone.*

**[Level: climb inside the tower; steam jets scald; fans can be timed. Sound design: the vent note rises in pitch as the player climbs.]**

KUU: *(wincing)* I can feel it in my teeth.

PLACK: It's resonance. Probably.

KUU: It's in my fillings.

LUNA: *(cheerfully)* Mine too! Do you think it's playing a song?

PLACK: *(sharply)* Don't listen for it.

LUNA: *(puzzled)* Why not?

PLACK: *(after a pause)* I don't know. It's just... a feeling.

*She presses a fist to her sternum. The note drops, for a moment, below the threshold of hearing, then returns.*

KUU: *(holding his head)* My head hurts.

LUNA: *(concerned, stopping)* Kuu?

KUU: It's fine. It's just... it sounds like somebody saying my name. Very slowly.

*A silence. The steam parts around them.*

LUNA: *(lightly, carefully)* Let's leave the vent alone.

**[Gameplay: environmental storytelling. The player can optionally stand in the vent's range and take small damage for a lore entry: a recorded voice reading a list of names, a pause, then "Operator, Kuu. Registered."]**

## Episode 6 - The Small Screen

*The server hall. Rows of racks, blinking, humming. At the far end, a single small screen on a metal stand, green text glowing, no bigger than a cookbook.*

PLACK: *(reading, slowly)* "Angel two slashes V1, success. Line down. Operator two slashes V, exclamation, fail..." *(she stops)* There's a string of ells.

KUU: Fail-llllll. I count thirty.

PLACK: *(continuing)* "...three dots, three dashes, three dots." That's SOS.

LUNA: *(delighted)* So it is a message.

PLACK: Then the slashes. Then a line of Morse.

*She takes her recorder from her pack, presses a button, and holds it to the screen. The device chirps and offers a damaged transcription: ".- (... .-.. ... / .-- .. .-.. .-.. / -.- .. .-.. .-.. / ..- ... / .- .-.. .-.."*

PLACK: *(frowning)* It's corrupted. The fourth letter is a bracket.

KUU: What does it say?

*LUNA, peering at the screen, nods very slowly, as if reading.*

LUNA: It says what you'd expect.

PLACK: Which is?

LUNA: *(waving a hand, vaguely)* "Angels will kill us all." Something like that.

PLACK: *(very still)* ...You can read Morse?

LUNA: *(after a very long pause)* Obviously.

*PLACK says nothing. She makes a note in her ledger, then, with a faint movement of her pen, underlines it.*

**[Gameplay: the screen text is shown verbatim on a close-up, including the owner's exact characters. Luna's translation is a cutscene line; the actual correct decoding is not given.]**

---

## Episode 7 - The Uptime

*The server hall's lights go out, one row at a time. A low, rising whine. The racks around the squad shudder, then lift, bolting themselves together into a hunched, many-armed shape the height of a bus.*

**[Low point. The Uptime assembles from server racks. Audio: hard drives spinning up like a choir of wasps.]**

KUU: *(backing away)* It's standing up.

LUNA: It's very tidy about it.

*Overhead, the hall's status screens all flash a single message, over and over: "OPERATOR V! FAILED. OPERATOR V! FAILED. OPERATOR V! FAILED."*

PLACK: *(very quietly)* Operator V exclamation.

LUNA: *(still cheerful, but tighter)* I don't like that it's taking attendance.

*The UPTIME raises one arm. Blue lights run along the cables, lighting each rack in turn, a ripple of data along its body.*

PLACK: It's a monitor. It's watching for failure.

LUNA: *(a low, wry note)* Then let's give it something to see.

---

## Episode 8 - The Uptime (Boss)

**[Boss: The Uptime. Phase 1: it fires packet-bolts at a fixed rhythm; learn the pattern. Phase 2: it "reboots" sections of the hall; the floor flickers between safe and unsafe tiles. Phase 3: it merges the hall's fans into a cyclone; use the cooling vents to counter it.]**

*The fight is a battle of rhythm: bursts, pauses, flickers. KUU, in the thick of it, stumbles upon the weak point, a loose cable behind the Uptime's spine, and yanks it free with both hands, shouting.*

KUU: *(at the top of his lungs)* SORRY!

*The UPTIME locks up. Its arms freeze mid-swing. Then, all at once, every light in the hall goes out.*

*A long darkness. A single click.*

*A small green light comes on. The screen, the little one on its stand, reads: "..."*

*Then, one line at a time, it types: "THANK YOU."*

*PLACK stares.*

PLACK: *(very softly)* Who's typing?

*The screen goes dark.*

**[Gameplay beat: a second, hidden cutscene triggers if the player has found the three "audio logs" earlier: a voice, young and tired, hums a half-remembered tune as the screen fades.]**

### Stinger

*Outside, the desert. The storm has thinned to drizzle. The squad stands at the foot of the data centre, looking back. The vent hum has not stopped. It follows them across the dunes, faint, patient.*

KUU: *(rubbing his temple)* It's still going.

LUNA: *(very lightly)* It's just the wind.

PLACK: *(not turning)* Luna.

LUNA: *(a beat)* It's just the wind.

*The three walk on. The hum walks with them.*
