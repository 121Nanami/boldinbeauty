# Ch 31 - Fracture - Script

**Arc 2 / 2013 / NEW.** Main cast. Three commanders defect. An armoured train to Terminus Nine. Luna's methods cost her Plack for a night.

---

## Cold open (cinematic, 30 s)
A railway terminal at the bottom of the arc, the last platform of Terminus Nine, lit by one lamp. A train with iron plates bolted over
its windows. On the platform, three commanders in a row, each with a single suitcase. A fourth, on the roof, with a rifle.
LUNA: (arriving) Gentlemen. Madam.
COMMANDER (cane): We would like to be *taken somewhere.*
LUNA: Anywhere?
COMMANDER (cane): Somewhere the hardliners cannot reach.
LUNA: (lightly) That is a very short list.

## Episode 1 - "Seats"
**Scene:** Terminus Nine platform. **Present:** Luna, Plack, Kuu; three reformers (BLUE).

REFORMER A: I have a bad back. I should have the window.
REFORMER B: *You* have a bad back? I was *shot,* in nineteen ninety-six.
REFORMER A: It's not a competition.
REFORMER C: (small, nervous) Is there a... a sleeping car?
KUU: (to Plack, low) They're *bickering.*
PLACK: They're frightened. It's what frightened people do.
LUNA: (arriving with a clipboard) Ladies and gentlemen, you have been *rated.* Window to the cane, middle to the cough, the back to the one
who hasn't spoken. (to Reformer C) You're sleeping *upright.*
REFORMER C: ...That's *fine.*

**Gameplay:** Boarding tutorial: assign seats (a comic management scene); first guards (RED).
**Beat:** The coalition is awkward and fragile.

## Episode 2 - "Station One"
**Scene:** The first station. **Present:** all three.

*(A tunnel junction. A signal that will not change. A pair of rail guards in armour, backs turned.)*
KUU: There's a lever.
PLACK: It's *connected.* (she looks closer) To the signal. And the track beyond it.
KUU: So we throw it.
PLACK: And divert the *entire line.*
LUNA: Which line?
PLACK: Theirs.
LUNA: (a flicker) Then it's a *good* lever.

**Gameplay:** Station sabotage (stealth or fight); a signal puzzle; mover platforms (train cars).
**Beat:** The squad clears the first station.

## Episode 3 - "The Price"
**Scene:** A carriage, between stations. **Present:** all three + Reformer B.

REFORMER B: (blunt, a hand on a cane) You wish to know why we've come.
PLACK: We'd like to know what it costs.
REFORMER B: *Nothing.* It costs *nothing.* (a tight smile) We have come because we're afraid.
LUNA: Of the hardliners?
REFORMER B: Of the *wing.* (very quietly) The line is restarted. We have seen the new product. We were not told.
PLACK: (low) You were afraid of the Patron.
REFORMER B: We are all afraid of the Patron. That is what makes it *work.*
LUNA: And your price?
REFORMER B: Protection. And a seat at the Charter.
LUNA: You're not defecting out of principle.
REFORMER B: (a dry sound) Principle's for the young.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a carriage conversation.
**Beat:** The reformers are cowards with reasons.

## Episode 4 - "Ends And Means"
**Scene:** Station two. **Present:** all three.

*(The train has stopped. Outside, in the dim of the platform, Luna and Plack face each other.)*
PLACK: They're the people who wrote the contracts.
LUNA: They're the people who can *rewrite* them.
PLACK: They're the people who *signed* the ones that --
LUNA: They are *useful.*
PLACK: (very evenly) They're *people.*
LUNA: (a flash) Yes, Plack. They are people. And I need people who can *sign.* I do not have the luxury of liking the ones I use.
PLACK: That's --
LUNA: The ends justify the means. I have said it a hundred times. I mean it.
*(A long silence. Plack takes off her sash, folds it, and puts it on the bench.)*
PLACK: I'm going to walk.
LUNA: Plack --
PLACK: I'm going to walk the tunnel. I'll be back.
LUNA: (a very small voice) When?
PLACK: When I know.

**Gameplay:** Non-combat (PURPLE): a tense scene; Plack leaves the squad (she becomes a guest-away: not playable this chapter).
**Beat:** **Midpoint swerve.** Plack leaves the train at station two.

## Episode 5 - "Without Plack"
**Scene:** The train. **Present:** Luna, Kuu.

*(The carriage is quiet. Kuu is looking at the empty seat.)*
KUU: Luna.
LUNA: Don't.
KUU: She'll come back.
LUNA: (very tight) I don't need her to come back. I need her to be *right.*
KUU: ...That's not the same thing.
LUNA: It's very *close.*
KUU: (looking at her) It's the farthest apart two things can be.
*(She says nothing. After a while she takes Plack's sash from the bench and puts it round her own neck.)*

**Gameplay:** Two-person play; a shorter mission; a defend-the-carriage.
**Beat:** Kuu stays. It costs him something.

## Episode 6 - "The Hardliners' Train"
**Scene:** The tunnel between stations. **Present:** Luna, Kuu.

*(A long, iron, steaming locomotive behind them, a plume of black smoke, a single wing painted on its prow.)*
KUU: There's another train.
LUNA: Yes.
KUU: It's *our* train's size.
LUNA: It's *bigger.* (she squints) They have turrets.
KUU: They have *turrets.*
LUNA: I wonder what *our* train has.
REFORMER A: (leaning in the doorway, wheezing) Coffee.
LUNA: (a flicker; almost a laugh) That's *something.*

**Gameplay:** Chase: train-to-train (moving platforms, gunfire); the player jumps carriages.
**Beat:** The hardliners catch up.

## Episode 7 - "The Coupling"
**Scene:** Between carriages. **Present:** Luna, Kuu.

*(A sudden, terrible jolt. The coupling between Luna's carriage and the reformers' snaps. The reformers' carriage begins to drift
backward, slowly, at walking pace, toward the hardliners.)*
REFORMER C: (a scream from inside) *Luna!*
KUU: (leaning out, hauling) We have to *re-couple!*
LUNA: How?
KUU: (hoarse) I'll *hold* it.
LUNA: You can't *hold* a --
KUU: I'll hold it with a *rope.*
LUNA: (very quickly) Kuu, you're afraid of --
KUU: (with a small, wild, surprising smile) Heights. This is a *moving* thing, Luna. It's a different fear.
*(He drops from the platform with a coil of rope.)*

**Gameplay:** Mini-boss: coupling sequence (a QTE-lite platforming set piece).
**Beat:** **Low point.** A person has to hold the train together.

## Episode 8 - "The Hardliner Train"
**Scene:** The third station. Boss fight. **Present:** Luna, Kuu, and Plack (returning).

*(Boss: the Hardliner Train: a locomotive with turrets. Phase 1, the train fires; Phase 2, carriages swing around to ram.)*
KUU: (clinging to the coupling) I'm *not* afraid!
LUNA: You're *quite* afraid!
KUU: Yes!
*(A shot rings out from the platform. A figure, in a courier jacket, with a ring on a chain, standing in the headlights with a rifle.)*
PLACK: (calmly) I said I'd be back.
LUNA: (a long, shuddering breath) ...You said "when I know."
PLACK: (reloading) I know.
*(The three of them take the locomotive between them.)*

**Gameplay:** Boss: three-way attack on the locomotive; coupling timing; Plack rejoins mid-fight.
**Beat:** The train falls. The reformers are safe.

**Stinger:** In a stopped carriage, Plack comes back with a sandwich. She hands it to Luna without a word. Luna takes it without a
word. Behind them the reformers watch two people settle an argument in silence, and decide, privately, that this is a woman they
can do business with. Luna doesn't like that either. The train pulls out of the third station before the dust settles.
