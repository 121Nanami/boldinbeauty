# Ch 20 - The Hour (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. New chapter, Arc 2. Main cast. Recruits the **Timekeeper** (6-star Secret, MAIN section 5). Timekeeper's voice: formal, bored, amused by Plack, speaks in tenses.

Chapter promise: time you aren't counted in can't be taxed, and one stopped clock isn't theirs.

---

## Episode 1 - Same Minute

*The old district, a quarter of narrow lanes and blackened stone. Every clock on every wall, in every window, in every tower, shows the same minute: 9:41. A pigeon sits on a lamp-post, perfectly still, mid-ruffle.*

**[Cold open. A quiet, unsettling ticking, many clocks slightly out of phase, then one by one falling silent.]**

KUU: *(whispering)* It's like the Hanging City.

PLACK: It's not. In the Hanging City, they didn't know. *(she touches a shop window, where a clock-face shows 9:41)* Here, it's been done *to* them.

LUNA: *(looking at the pigeon)* Hello, you.

*The pigeon does not respond. She taps its beak. It remains perfectly still.*

LUNA: *(to herself)* Rude.

*A shop door opens. A woman in an apron steps out with a basket of loaves, stops, blinks.*

BAKER: *(politely)* Are you lost?

PLACK: We're looking for the Cathedral.

BAKER: *(nodding)* Straight on. Mind the step. *(she hesitates)* And try not to be late.

KUU: *(whispering)* Late for what?

*The BAKER looks puzzled, then shrugs, then walks on. Her footsteps are slightly too slow.*

---

## Episode 2 - Nave

*The Clock Cathedral. A vast, dim nave, ribbed with iron, lit by a rose window of glass cogs. Pews of brass, a pulpit made of a clock-face the size of a wagon wheel. Dust motes hang in the air, motionless.*

*At the altar, a tall figure in a long coat, back to them, hands clasped, head tilted as if listening.*

LUNA: *(clearing her throat)* Excuse me.

*The figure turns slowly. A face of ageless calm. A collar of clock-hands. Pocket-watches hanging at the breast, each stopped at a different minute.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(in a voice like a metronome)* You are early.

LUNA: *(blinking)* We... didn't have an appointment.

TIMEKEEPER: *(patiently)* You will have had. It is now the past, and you were early.

PLACK: *(under her breath)* Tenses.

TIMEKEEPER: *(turning to her, faintly interested)* Ah. *(a pause)* You are the courier. The one who asks the questions.

PLACK: *(guardedly)* Some of them.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a faint smile)* Then perhaps you will be the one I find interesting.

**[Tutorial: Timekeeper's mechanic: "Pause" zones. Stand still and enemies freeze; move and they advance. This chapter teaches how to use stillness.]**

---

## Episode 3 - Gears

*The gear halls, beneath the nave. Wheels the size of houses turn slowly, interlocking, mounted on iron beams. Catwalks, ladders, pendulums swinging like bells.*

**[Level: platforming through rotating gears. The environment pauses when the player stands still; this is the core puzzle.]**

TIMEKEEPER: *(floating a pace ahead, hands behind her back)* You must not hurry.

LUNA: *(climbing a ladder)* I'm very good at hurrying.

TIMEKEEPER: And it will make you late.

LUNA: *(pausing)* How does that work?

TIMEKEEPER: *(without turning)* The more you move, the more you spend. The more you spend, the less is left. *(a beat)* It is a very simple economy.

*PLACK, on a catwalk, stops. Around her, the gears hesitate, then freeze.*

PLACK: *(softly)* It's like a stopped clock.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a nod of approval)* Precisely.

*PLACK steps forward. The gears resume. She steps back. They stop.*

PLACK: *(half to herself)* It's reading intention.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a note of delight)* You are *quick*.

---

## Episode 4 - Uncounted (Midpoint)

*The Cathedral's back chapel: rows of pocket-watches in glass cases, each ticking out of rhythm. A single, plain stool. The TIMEKEEPER sits, folds her hands.*

KUU: *(looking at the cases)* Why's the district stopped?

TIMEKEEPER: *(evenly)* It is not stopped. It is *excused*.

KUU: Excused?

TIMEKEEPER: A people who are not counted cannot be taxed. *(she nods toward the window, the stalled lane beyond)* The commanders tax time. Per hour. Per citizen. Per second, in certain districts. It is an elegant form of theft.

*A silence.*

TIMEKEEPER: So I stopped the hours that were not on their books. In the Hanging City. In the Old District. In the places no one was counting.

LUNA: *(slowly)* You did that on purpose.

TIMEKEEPER: *(inclining her head)* Of course.

*PLACK, arms folded, glances sideways at LUNA.*

PLACK: She's been protecting them.

TIMEKEEPER: *(with dignity)* I have been *declining to invoice*.

*LUNA starts to laugh. After a moment, KUU joins in. The TIMEKEEPER watches them, mildly puzzled, and then, very faintly, almost smiles.*

---

## Episode 5 - Offer

*The nave. LUNA stands before the TIMEKEEPER, hands in pockets. PLACK and KUU are a pace behind, quiet.*

LUNA: I'd like you to come to a meeting.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a pause)* You are building a table.

LUNA: A very large one.

TIMEKEEPER: *(thoughtfully)* With whom?

LUNA: Operators. Secrets. Eventually, the government.

TIMEKEEPER: *(very dry)* How ambitious.

LUNA: I'd call it "optimistic".

TIMEKEEPER: *(considering)* And what do I receive in exchange?

*LUNA looks at PLACK. PLACK, very slightly, shrugs.*

LUNA: *(honestly)* Nothing I can promise.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a long look)* That, at least, is truthful.

*She turns to the great clock-face above the altar.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(softly)* I will attend. *(she turns back)* I will give you an hour that does not count against you.

LUNA: What does that mean?

TIMEKEEPER: *(a faint smile)* When you need it, you will know.

**[Gameplay beat: Timekeeper grants "A Free Hour", a one-time revive/stall in a later chapter. UI item: a small pocket watch.]**

---

## Episode 6 - The Stopped Hall

*Behind the chapel, a narrow door the TIMEKEEPER has not mentioned. LUNA, passing it, stops. She pushes. It swings open on silence.*

*A hall of clocks, hundreds, floor to ceiling, every face stopped at 4:12. Not the same 4:12 as the Hanging City, a deeper one, with a dust of centuries on it.*

PLACK: *(from the doorway, low)* Luna.

LUNA: *(stepping in)* I know.

KUU: *(whispering)* It's the same time.

PLACK: *(quietly)* It's the *same time*. *(she turns)* Timekeeper.

*The TIMEKEEPER has arrived in the doorway, hands behind her back. For the first time, her face holds an expression, and it is unease.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(evenly)* I would prefer that you leave this hall.

LUNA: Why?

TIMEKEEPER: It is not mine.

*A silence.*

PLACK: *(slowly)* You didn't stop these.

TIMEKEEPER: *(after a long pause)* No.

LUNA: *(looking at the faces)* Then who did?

*The TIMEKEEPER looks at the clocks, one at a time, and her gaze seems to travel a very long way.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(very softly)* I do not know. *(a pause)* And that is the first time in two hundred years that I have said so.

**[Gameplay beat: the 4:12 hall is a visual callback to Ch 4. A lore entry.]**

---

## Episode 7 - Minute Hand

*The nave. A sound like a great bell tolling backward. The altar's clock-face rotates; its hands, immense, bronze, spin. From the vault above, the Cathedral's guardian descends: a column of fused clock-hands, ten metres tall, a sweeping arm like a scythe.*

**[Low point. The guardian wakes, summoned by the intrusion into the stopped hall.]**

TIMEKEEPER: *(sharply)* That is the Minute Hand.

LUNA: *(backing)* Is it yours?

TIMEKEEPER: *(a note of real alarm)* It has never moved in my time.

PLACK: *(very quiet)* Then who's moving it?

*The MINUTE HAND sweeps. The air itself seems to thin. KUU, grabbing LUNA's sleeve, is yanked backward as a blade of bronze passes where she stood.*

KUU: *(shaken)* It doesn't want us here!

TIMEKEEPER: *(her composure returning, grimly)* It wants you to *keep moving*. *(she raises her hand; the pocket-watches on her coat flicker)* Do not stop. If it catches you at rest, you will be... *(a pause)* ...paused.

---

## Episode 8 - The Minute Hand (Boss)

**[Boss: The Minute Hand. Phase 1: a sweeping hour-beam; always keep moving. Phase 2: it "pauses" sections of the arena; stand in unpaused circles. Phase 3: it spins faster; the Timekeeper offers one "Free Hour", letting the player slow time to strike its core.]**

*The fight is a race against a clock. LUNA, PLACK and KUU run laps of the nave, hurdling pews, ducking the bronze arm. KUU, to everyone's surprise, is the fastest.*

KUU: *(over his shoulder)* I'm good at running away!

PLACK: *(keeping pace)* It's a skill!

*At the climax, the TIMEKEEPER lifts a watch and, with one fingertip, stops it. The MINUTE HAND, mid-swing, freezes.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(softly)* Now.

*LUNA's black hole opens at the core. The guardian crumples inward, a spiral of bronze, and the Cathedral falls silent.*

*Dust drifts down through the rose window's light, no longer motionless.*

### Stinger

*The Cathedral steps, dusk. The city beyond is still stalled, but a single clock, on a tower far below, ticks forward one minute. 9:42. A pigeon on a lamp-post shakes out its wings and takes off.*

*The TIMEKEEPER watches it go.*

TIMEKEEPER: *(to LUNA)* I never stopped that one.

LUNA: *(following her gaze)* Which one?

TIMEKEEPER: The hall behind the chapel. *(she turns)* Someone else did.

*A silence.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* We'll find them.

TIMEKEEPER: *(a faint, strange look)* I should prefer that we did not.
