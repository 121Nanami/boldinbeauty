# Ch 01 - Neo Tokyo (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Map `L_NeoTokyo` is LOCKED and untouched. Voices: **Luna** drifts between bored and profound-sounding nonsense, kind only to Plack and Kuu. **Plack** is exact, dry, asks the question nobody wants asked. **Kuu** apologises first and acts second. **COMMANDER** is the player's radio voice (never seen). Stage directions in *italics*. Gameplay beats are in `[brackets]`.

Chapter promise: three people take a job for one sandwich, and the job turns out to be a person.

---

## Episode 1 - The Job

*Rain over Neo Tokyo, 2009. A rooftop beside a broken sign that used to say PARADISE NOODLES. LUNA lies on a water tank with a coat over her face.*

**[Cold open, no combat. Camera tilts down through the rain to the street, then back up.]**

PAGER: *(buzzing, tinny)* Contract 4471. Escort one crate, Shibuya Underpass to Drop Point Seven. Compensation: lunch.

LUNA: *(from under the coat)* Lunch.

PAGER: Compensation: lunch.

LUNA: *(sits up)* Thirty seconds ago I was a person with no plans. Now I have a destination and a meal. That is how you know the universe is listening, it just has terrible handwriting.

*She hops down. KUU is already waiting on the fire escape, soaked, holding an umbrella over a bag.*

KUU: You're late. Sorry, that's rude. You're not late, I'm early. I'm always early, it's a character flaw, please ignore it.

LUNA: Kuu, you've been standing in the rain for an hour.

KUU: Forty minutes. I brought you a coat.

LUNA: I'm wearing a coat.

KUU: A dry one.

*She takes it, drops it over the first coat, and looks pleased with herself.*

LUNA: Two coats. Strategic.

COMMANDER: *(radio)* Unit Luna, this is your assigned commander. Do you copy?

LUNA: Loud and clear. Quick question: when you say "assigned", did you pick us, or did you lose a bet?

COMMANDER: ...Both.

LUNA: Honest. I like you already. Where's the crate?

**[First movement tutorial: roof to roof along three rooftops, jump gaps, rain obscures distance. No enemies.]**

*Halfway across, KUU stops and looks down at the street.*

KUU: Luna, there's nobody down there. Shibuya Underpass on a Tuesday should have a hundred people.

LUNA: Then we're alone. Pleasant.

KUU: Or they know something we don't.

LUNA: Same thing, with better manners.

*They reach the underpass entrance. The street lamps flicker once, in sequence, as if something walked under them.*

## Episode 2 - Please Hold

*Shibuya Underpass. Closed shutters, vending machines humming, a puddle that reflects the wrong sky.*

**[Combat tutorial: light attack, dodge, special. Three basic angels. Tutorial prompts are voiced by COMMANDER.]**

COMMANDER: Press the light-attack input to strike. Hold to charge. Try it on the angel.

LUNA: Which angel?

COMMANDER: The one that is currently about to hit you.

*An ANGEL drops from the ceiling grate. Pale, faceless, a ring of light for a halo. LUNA dodges without looking.*

LUNA: Rude. Didn't even say hello.

*She punches. The air folds around her hand, pulls the angel inward, and shoves it away: black hole, white hole.*

COMMANDER: Excellent. Note the pull-and-push pattern. It is, uh, not in the manual.

LUNA: The manual was written by people who've never been hit. *(another angel)* Kuu, left.

KUU: Left! Sorry! *(he catches the angel's wrist, throws it into a vending machine)* Sorry!

**[Tutorial: Special attack unlocked. Prompt on screen. Luna's black-and-white-hole move, delivered as a cutscene flourish the first time.]**

*After the fight a vending machine rattles and drops a can of cold coffee. LUNA looks at it, at the angels, back at it.*

LUNA: I'd like it noted that the machine was on our side.

KUU: Machines don't have sides.

LUNA: Then it has excellent taste.

**[Tutorial ends. Hint: the crate is at the end of the underpass, behind the third shutter.]**

## Episode 3 - The First Summon

*The underpass dead end. A pile of cargo pallets under tarps. A glowing summoning console, bolted to a pillar, a government stamp worn off its side.*

KUU: Luna, there's a terminal here. It says "Reinforcement request: one slot." Do we, um, press it?

LUNA: What happens if we don't?

KUU: Nothing.

LUNA: And if we do?

KUU: ...Something. I think?

LUNA: Then we press it. Nothing is a terrible roommate.

**[Tutorial summon: the player pulls once, guaranteed result: Kuu. Not a gacha animation, a short cinematic.]**

*The console whirs. Light pools on the floor. KUU, already standing next to LUNA, steps into the light, looks at it, steps out.*

KUU: Oh. That's me. Why is it me?

LUNA: Because they didn't have anyone better.

KUU: *(wounded)* Thank you.

LUNA: I meant for the console. You're the best one I've got.

*He blinks, then goes pink.*

KUU: I'm, uh. Sorry. Okay. Thank you. I'm going to go stand over there.

COMMANDER: Registering Operator Kuu to the squad. Congratulations. You now have a team of two.

LUNA: Three, if you count Kuu's umbrella.

**[Squad management tutorial: swap between Luna and Kuu, show cooldowns.]**

*A noise from the cargo pile. Both look. One tarp has a hand-shaped dent in it.*

## Episode 4 - The Crate (Midpoint)

*The crate. A wooden box the size of a washing machine, stencilled with a sponsor mark: three feathered lines in a wing shape. Air holes along the sides.*

KUU: There are air holes.

LUNA: It's a very well-ventilated lunch.

KUU: Luna.

LUNA: ...Fine.

*She pries the lid with her fingertips. Inside: a young woman in a courier jacket, knees to chest, one eye open, absolutely furious.*

PLACK: *(hissing)* Close the lid.

LUNA: Hello.

PLACK: Close the lid, please. There are three more drones outside.

*LUNA takes her time closing it.*

LUNA: Lunch is a person.

KUU: Luna, we can't just close it on a person.

LUNA: She asked politely.

*The lid pops. PLACK climbs out, brushes herself off, checks the satchel at her hip, then looks at each of them in turn, appraising.*

PLACK: I'm Plack. Courier. I'm unregistered, I'm not a criminal, and I would like to go home.

LUNA: Where's home?

PLACK: Currently? Here.

*A beat.*

KUU: We're supposed to deliver you to Drop Point Seven.

PLACK: I know. I read the sponsor stamp. *(to LUNA)* If you deliver me, I get handed to the people who stamped it. They don't want a courier. They want what I'm carrying.

LUNA: What are you carrying?

PLACK: *(pause)* Not lunch.

**[Squad choice screen: three portraits. Plack is added as a "story-locked" ally for the next two episodes. She does not join permanently until the boss.]**

LUNA: *(looking at the sponsor stamp)* Rain for the whole city, a crate full of a person, and a wing painted on the box. Something expensive is happening and nobody's saying who's paying.

## Episode 5 - Roof Rules

*Rooftops again. Wet gravel, antennas, laundry lines nobody took in. PLACK leads, LUNA behind, KUU last.*

**[Traversal chapter: climbing, rope-swing, ledge shimmy. Light combat: ranged angels first appearance in tutorial form.]**

PLACK: Left foot on the brace, not the gutter. The gutter's glued with sponsor paint, it peels.

KUU: How do you know that?

PLACK: I've used every roof in this district to make deliveries I wasn't supposed to make.

LUNA: *(from behind)* I like her. Can we keep her?

PLACK: I'm not a stray.

LUNA: Everyone's a stray, some of us just have good coats.

*KUU stops at a ledge.*

KUU: Sorry, I need to, um. Tie a knot. Hold on. *(he ties a bowline in a rope he's carrying)* This is for, you know, if anyone slips.

PLACK: *(softens, slightly)* That's a good knot.

KUU: Thank you! I practise. It's, um. A thing.

LUNA: He ties them at night when he can't sleep.

KUU: Luna!

**[Optional: three NPC pigeons to pet. Reward: Kuu's bark variants.]**

*A ranged ANGEL stands on a distant water tower, holding a bow of light.*

PLACK: Don't look at it.

LUNA: Why?

PLACK: They don't like being looked at.

LUNA: Rude of them, they're glowing.

**[Combat: angel archers, cover on rooftop vents. First "Break" shield tutorial.]**

*After the fight, PLACK looks at LUNA's glove, where the crate lid pinched her earlier.*

PLACK: You're bleeding.

LUNA: Occupational.

PLACK: *(wraps it with a strip of cloth from her jacket)* Don't punch things with it for a minute.

LUNA: *(looking at the wrap)* ...I'll consider it.

## Episode 6 - The Wrong Drop

*A ramen stall on a lower roof, shuttered. PLACK sits on an overturned crate, eating a protein bar. KUU is trying to hold the umbrella over all three.*

COMMANDER: Unit Luna. Drop Point Seven is four hundred metres ahead. Stand by to hand over the package.

LUNA: She's not a package.

COMMANDER: Sponsor documentation lists her as a package.

LUNA: Sponsor documentation lists lots of things as lots of things. Commander, tell me what's in her satchel.

COMMANDER: ...I'm not cleared to know.

LUNA: Then you're in the same boat as me.

*PLACK looks up from the bar.*

PLACK: Why are you doing this? You don't know me.

LUNA: I'm doing it because I got paid in sandwiches and nobody ever offers the sandwich to the person in the box.

PLACK: That's not a reason.

LUNA: It's the best I've got.

*A long pause. PLACK folds the bar wrapper into a small square.*

PLACK: I'm carrying records. Delivery ledgers. Dates, routes, who signed for what. Things the sponsor would rather aren't in a drawer.

KUU: So the angels aren't after the crate.

PLACK: The angels are doing what they're told. Someone's telling them where to go.

KUU: Who tells an angel anything?

PLACK: *(quietly)* That's the question, isn't it?

**[Branch: dialogue choice for the Commander, "Hand her over" or "Protect her". Both lead to the same fight. The line changes the text of the stinger.]**

---

## Episode 7 - Drop Point Seven

*A rooftop pad with a small loading beacon and a sponsor kiosk: a booth with a cartoon wing and a vending slot labelled FREE LUNCH WITH SIGN-UP.*

**[Low point. The beacon is already surrounded. Four waves of angels, a heavy angel mini-boss. No cover from rain.]**

KUU: Luna. This is wrong.

LUNA: Which part?

KUU: The part where the angels are standing in a circle around a kiosk.

*The ANGELS are not attacking. They stand in a ring. One turns its head, slowly, to LUNA.*

LUNA: ...Hello.

*It does not answer. The ring begins to close.*

PLACK: Told you. Someone told them.

LUNA: Then someone's going to hear me say "no".

*She takes a step forward. The ring tightens. A heavy ANGEL steps out of it and lifts a fist.*

**[Mini-boss: Heavy Angel. Plack fights alongside as a guest; Kuu is the tank.]**

*At 50% health the heavy angel stops, looks at the kiosk, and the kiosk's screen lights: a face, smiling, generic, a sponsor mascot.*

KIOSK: Congratulations! You have reached Drop Point Seven. Please hand over your parcel.

LUNA: Can the kiosk see us?

KIOSK: Please hand over your parcel.

*KUU, shaking, steps in front of PLACK.*

KUU: No. Sorry, no. Please. No.

*The kiosk stutters. A beat. Then the ring of angels breaks, all at once, and they charge.*

---

## Episode 8 - The Neon Choir (Boss)

*The rooftop dissolves into a bigger platform: a decommissioned billboard stage, neon tubes running down its sides. The angels are fusing, one by one, into a column of singing faces. A choir.*

**[Boss: the Neon Choir. Two phases. Phase 1: sound waves, dodge the rings. Phase 2: the column splits into four lanes, one of which is the weak point. Tutorial of Break and Burst.]**

LUNA: *(over the music)* It's a choir!

PLACK: It's a call and response!

KUU: *(shouting)* Is that bad?

PLACK: It's answering something!

*The CHOIR holds a single sustained note. LUNA's coat lifts in an updraft. She looks up.*

LUNA: It's calling. And someone's answering. Right?

PLACK: *(realising)* The kiosk. It's a relay. The signal goes through the kiosk to the angels.

LUNA: Then we shut the relay.

**[Phase 2 mechanic: destroy the kiosk by pulling the black hole into it, then use the white hole to push it through the choir. Cinematic finisher.]**

*The CHOIR collapses into pale dust, which the rain washes into the gutters. Silence. Just rain. The kiosk is a crater.*

PLACK: *(quietly)* I've been delivering for three years and I never once asked who was paying.

LUNA: Now you're asking.

PLACK: Yes.

*A beat. PLACK looks at the two of them.*

PLACK: If I stay with you, I stop being unregistered.

LUNA: Then you stop being unregistered. We can make something up.

PLACK: Like what?

LUNA: Wife?

KUU: *(choking)* LUNA!

LUNA: I'm joking. Mostly. Sidekick?

PLACK: ...Teammate.

**[Plack joins the squad permanently. Summon prompt: Plack added. Chapter clear.]**

### Stinger

*Dawn, thin. They walk down the fire escape. At the street, a delivery drone drops a paper bag at their feet and flies away. Inside: one cold sandwich and a note, handwritten.*

NOTE: *Ask who is paying for the rain.*

KUU: *(reading)* It's not signed.

LUNA: *(taking the sandwich)* Everything important isn't.

*She tears it in three pieces and hands them out.*

**[Chapter 1 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 2. Seeds: sponsor wing mark; "who is paying for the rain"; Plack's ledgers.]**
