# Ch 04 - The Hanging City (script)

**Status:** PROPOSED full script, 2026-10-11. Rework of `L_Ch04_HangingCity`; owner brief: a city hanging over an endless void, held by a single chain whose top cannot be seen.

Chapter promise: the first place where nobody has ever been counted, and a clock that only knows one minute.

---

## Episode 1 - Vertigo

*A service tunnel opens onto a narrow platform. Below: nothing. Above: a chain, each link the size of a bus, climbing into cloud. Across the gap: a disc of streets and buildings, hanging on cables, lit by lanterns.*

**[Cold open. A slow reveal shot. Wind noise only.]**

KUU: *(whispering)* No.

LUNA: Kuu.

KUU: I'm not doing it. I'll wait here. I'll hold the, um, the door. There isn't a door. I'll hold the air.

PLACK: The lift's on the far side. We need to cross.

KUU: *(very fast)* I'm going to make a statement and I need you both to not laugh. I'm afraid of heights. Not a little. A lot. Like, properly.

*A pause. LUNA and PLACK look at each other. Neither laughs.*

LUNA: Okay.

PLACK: Okay.

KUU: *(startled)* You're not going to make a joke?

LUNA: I'm going to make several, but not today.

*PLACK checks the plank bridge, a line of weathered boards over the void.*

PLACK: Eyes on Luna's back. Don't look down. Count the boards.

KUU: How many?

PLACK: Forty-one.

KUU: *(breathing)* One. Two. Three.

**[Tutorial: bridge crossing. Wind gusts shift planks. Kuu's panic meter is cosmetic but shapes his barks. Falling = kill Z.]**

## Episode 2 - Registrar's Day

*The city. Streets of patched iron, paper lanterns, washing lines strung between balconies. A crowd of residents stops to look at them. Everyone is wearing mismatched layers.*

RESIDENT: *(to another)* Are they government?

OTHER: They have the coat.

RESIDENT: Which coat?

OTHER: The one the commanders give everyone.

*A hush. A woman at the front, apron, clipboard, steps forward.*

LIFT CLERK: Papers?

LUNA: *(cheerfully)* We don't have any.

LIFT CLERK: *(a pause, then she grins)* Wonderful.

PLACK: Wonderful?

LIFT CLERK: Nobody here has any. We're the Unregistered. If you'd had papers I'd have had to ask you to leave.

*A cheer goes up. A child pulls on LUNA's sleeve.*

CHILD: Are you the ones who're going to fix the lift?

LUNA: Is the lift broken?

CHILD: Since last Thursday.

LUNA: *(to PLACK)* We're fixing the lift.

PLACK: We came for a survey.

LUNA: Surveys are just lifts you haven't met yet.

**[Hub level: four hanging districts reachable by lifts and bridges. Quests: fix the north lift; escort a cat; collect lanterns. Enemies: Chain Drifters in the outskirts.]**

## Episode 3 - Rota

*The north lift. A rusted gondola, a jammed gear housing. PLACK crouches in the machinery, hair tied up, oil to her elbows.*

PLACK: Pass me the pliers.

KUU: Which ones?

PLACK: The ones that look like pliers.

KUU: *(handing over the wrong tool)* Sorry.

PLACK: Those are tongs.

KUU: Sorry! *(he finds the right ones)* How do you know how to do this?

PLACK: Couriers ride lifts. Sometimes lifts need riding differently.

*A brief, comfortable silence. The gear catches. The lift groans, shakes, lifts four feet and stops.*

LIFT CLERK: *(from the platform, delighted)* That's the first time in six days!

PLACK: It'll need weekly maintenance.

LIFT CLERK: Then you're the rota captain.

PLACK: I'm not staying.

LIFT CLERK: *(not listening)* Everyone! The rota's captain is here!

*The crowd cheers. PLACK stares.*

LUNA: *(delighted)* They elected you.

PLACK: I did not consent.

LUNA: That's how all the best governments start.

*A small girl hands PLACK a clipboard with a hand-drawn schedule on it. PLACK looks at it. A very small, very private smile.*

PLACK: ...Fine. For this week.

**[Optional: schedule mini-game: arrange lift timings so residents arrive on time. Reward: bark variants and a lantern charm.]**

## Episode 4 - 4:12 (Midpoint)

*A plaza at the centre of the disc. A clock tower with a huge face. All its hands point at 4:12. Every other clock in sight, every window, every storefront: 4:12.*

LUNA: That's a very committed clock.

CHILD: It's always 4:12.

KUU: Is it broken?

CHILD: No. It's right.

KUU: How can it be right? The sun's... *(he looks at the sky, which has no sun)* ...there's no sun.

CHILD: *(patiently)* It's always been 4:12. The clocks don't lie. It's the rest of the world that's confused.

*PLACK crouches to the child's level.*

PLACK: Do you remember a different time?

CHILD: What's a different time?

*A silence. PLACK straightens. She looks at LUNA, who is looking at the clock.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* Plack. You said everyone in the Ark's counted.

PLACK: Registered.

LUNA: Is that the same?

PLACK: ...Not here. Here, nobody's counted. *(she looks at the clock again)* And nobody's late.

**[Gameplay beat: the clock tower is a hub landmark; interacting with it unlocks a minimap overlay of the stopped zone. Later chapter pays off the Timekeeper.]**

*In the clock-face's glass, for one half-second, the second hand twitches. Then it settles.*

## Episode 5 - The Drift

*The outer rim. A cable-and-plank walkway running around the edge of the disc, open to the void. Angels drift down the chain from above, long trailing wisps of light behind them.*

**[Combat level. Chain Drifters descend from the sky; fight on swaying platforms. Kuu climbs links to bypass groups.]**

KUU: *(climbing, wheezing, narrating)* I want it on record that this is the worst thing I've done.

LUNA: *(from the walkway)* You're doing great!

KUU: I'm climbing a chain over an infinite drop! I'm doing it at a speed I'd call "geological"!

PLACK: *(from below)* Link twelve! Left hand!

KUU: *(grabbing)* Got it! *(a beat)* I think I've died. I'm pretty sure I've died and this is the afterlife.

LUNA: *(delighted)* Kuu, I've never been more proud.

*A drifter swoops. KUU, panicking, kicks it. It falls into the void, a long pale streak.*

KUU: *(silence)* ...I did that.

PLACK: You did that.

KUU: Can I do that again?

LUNA: Please.

**[Unlock: climbing XP for Kuu. His barks gain confidence variants.]**

## Episode 6 - Recruits

*A rooftop garden. Twelve unregistered operators sit in a circle, passing a pot of tea. LUNA addresses them from a crate, hands in her coat pockets.*

LUNA: I'd like to make an offer.

RESIDENT A: What kind?

LUNA: A good one. A small one. A thing that doesn't exist yet.

RESIDENT B: Sounds like a scam.

LUNA: It is. But it's a scam with excellent intentions.

*A ripple of laughter. LUNA smiles.*

LUNA: I'm going to take the government's job and do something else with it. I need people who aren't on any list. People who aren't on anyone's side.

RESIDENT C: And what do we get?

LUNA: *(thinking)* A lift schedule that works. A friend who'll answer the door. A cause.

RESIDENT C: *(dryly)* A cause isn't a salary.

LUNA: *(sincerely)* No. But it's something to be late for.

*A silence. The residents look at each other. The LIFT CLERK, at the back, speaks.*

LIFT CLERK: You're doing it wrong, you know.

LUNA: *(blinking)* Doing what?

LIFT CLERK: Recruiting. You're pitching to the group. You don't recruit a group. You recruit one person at a time, and you ask what they need before you say what you want.

*A pause. LUNA is, for the first time in the chapter, quiet.*

LUNA: ...Noted.

**[Mini-game: one-on-one conversations with six residents. Each reveals a need; fulfil it to recruit them as a cosmetic supporter. Plant for Chapter 16.]**

## Episode 7 - The Anchor

*The chain, near its base, where it meets the city. A vast iron ring, groaning. Cables fray at its edges. Dust sifts down from above.*

**[Low point. An earthquake-like tremor. The city sags a foot. Every lantern flickers.]**

LIFT CLERK: *(shouting)* It's the anchor! It's slipping!

PLACK: It's not slipping. Something's holding it.

*Above the ring, a shape in the cloud: a huge hunched angel, wings folded, arms wrapped around the chain itself. It isn't attacking. It's gripping.*

KUU: *(very small)* It's holding up the city.

LUNA: *(slowly)* It's the anchor.

PLACK: It's been the anchor the whole time.

*A silence. The angel turns its head slowly, no eyes, no mouth, a halo the size of a house. It looks at the three of them, then at the city below.*

LIFT CLERK: *(whispering)* We've fed it for years. Scraps. Lantern oil. We thought it was... a ghost.

LUNA: *(carefully)* Does it want something?

PLACK: *(watching)* It wants us not to let go.

*The city shudders. The angel's grip tightens. Cables snap.*

## Episode 8 - The Anchorite (Boss)

**[Boss: The Anchorite. A giant angel at the chain anchor. Phase 1: it swings the city; use platforms as they shift. Phase 2: it sheds fragments; destroy them before they hit the districts. Phase 3: you must relieve it by climbing the chain and re-securing three bolts while it tries to throw you.]**

LUNA: *(over the wind)* We can't hurt it!

PLACK: We don't have to! We have to help it!

KUU: *(shouting)* How do you help a thing that's holding a city?

LUNA: *(to herself)* You let it let go.

*She jumps. Black hole beneath her, white hole above, a short arcing flight that lands her on the chain. She climbs. Each link rings.*

*At the top, three massive bolts, slowly turning. She and PLACK drive them home, one by one, while KUU, below, shouts directions from a plank bridge.*

*The ANCHORITE shudders, loosens its grip, then, very gently, lets go. The chain holds. The angel drifts upward on a thread of light, folds into the cloud, and is gone.*

*The city settles. A long silence, then a single lantern flares green. Then the others follow.*

LIFT CLERK: *(whispering)* Thank you.

*LUNA climbs down, shaking.*

LUNA: I'd like it noted that I did not fall off.

PLACK: Noted.

KUU: *(hugging her)* Noted! Noted! I can't believe you did that!

**[Reward: unregistered operator allies as passive support in later chapters.]**

### Stinger

*The clock tower. The hands are still, still at 4:12. As the squad leaves, the minute hand ticks forward. 4:13.*

*A child's voice, off-screen: "It moved!"*

*LUNA stops, turns, looks. The clock face is utterly normal. The hand does not move again.*

LUNA: *(quietly)* Plack. Write down the time.

**[Chapter 4 ends. Unlocks: Chapter 5. Seeds: the Unregistered; the stopped clocks (Timekeeper); the Anchorite's origin; Luna's recruiting method fails and is corrected by the Lift Clerk.]**
