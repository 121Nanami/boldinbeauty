# Platform discovery and community marketing

Sources checked 2026-09-23. “Requirement” below means a platform rule or technical prerequisite; “advice” means a platform's recommendation or a project inference. The local project memory names possible desktop/mobile targets but does not confirm a Steam release, public date, marketing budget, or available gameplay capture.

## What can the team do now while storefront and release scope remain open?

### Takeaway
Prepare an honest reveal package and a sustainable feedback loop. Use short gameplay or development clips and community spaces only when there is material safe to show and someone to moderate replies; avoid a Steam wishlist call until a live Steam page exists.

### Cited Findings
- **Advice, Steam:** Valve recommends public Coming Soon communication when the team is ready to talk, and cautions that a changing art direction or feature set can confuse early wishlisters. A page needs branding images, a written description, and ideally a gameplay trailer. — [Steamworks Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- **Advice, YouTube:** Shorts can be uploaded from phone or computer; YouTube recommends trying a test Short and then following a schedule the team can maintain. Square or vertical uploads up to three minutes are classified as Shorts under current rules. — [YouTube Shorts upload tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12921536?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en); [YouTube three-minute Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)
- **Measurement, YouTube:** Since 2025-03-31, a Shorts “view” counts starts/replays; YouTube retains “Engaged views” to compare how many viewers chose to continue watching. Do not use raw views alone as proof of audience intent. — [YouTube Shorts overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en)
- **Advice, TikTok (dated 2022):** TikTok's gaming guidance suggests organic clips such as gameplay, walkthroughs, tutorials, and fandom content, and recommends a consistent brand voice. This is platform marketing advice, not a guarantee of reach. — [TikTok for Business gaming guide, 2022-08-04](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/gaming-marketers-ultimate-guide-tiktok-content)
- **Tool, TikTok (updated 2026-07):** Its public Creative Center offers trend, keyword, and ad examples for creative research; using it does not imply buying ads. — [TikTok Creative Center help](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-center)
- **Requirement, Discord Community status:** Discord says Community servers must have accurate descriptions, posted rules, and a moderator team; its enablement flow requires safety settings. Announcement channels and onboarding are optional tools available to Community servers. — [Discord Community Server Guidelines](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035969312-Community-Server-Guidelines); [Enabling a Community Server](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851-Enabling-Your-Community-Server)
- **Advice, Discord:** Discord recommends verification, AutoMod, and human moderation; automation does not replace contextual decisions. — [Discord moderation and community support](https://discord.com/community/automating-moderation-community-support)

### Inferences
- Prepare a small approved content bank: clear one-sentence premise, truthful description of the play loop, short footage showing actual combat/control once it exists, characters/world art identified as such, and a current “release timing to be announced” line. Test separate hooks (combat, operator personality, setting) on a manageable cadence; track useful comments and engaged viewing as well as reach.
- Before opening a public Discord, assign moderation ownership, rules, safety settings, an announcement/FAQ structure, and an answer for repeated release/platform questions. If that staffing is unavailable, collect feedback through a lower-maintenance channel first.
- Build a creator/press research list by audience fit, language, coverage style, and whether a playable preview would be relevant. Draft a factual one-page press kit and capture/asset provenance; outreach can wait for a clear hook or playable milestone.

### Gaps
- No verified project-owned store page, public social accounts, footage, creator contact list, approved campaign spend, first playable, or reveal approval was provided; these are prerequisites for a concrete schedule or channel forecast.

## What changes if Steam is confirmed as a release channel?

### Takeaway
A Steam Coming Soon page becomes the main wishlist destination once the release and store materials are credible. A wishlist is a useful interest and notification signal, but neither a sales forecast nor a universal discovery ranking threshold.

### Cited Findings
- **Requirement, Steam:** A Coming Soon page requires a new app, completed store-presence checklist, Valve review/approval, then explicit publication. Steam recommends submitting the page for review at least seven business days before it should go live; it must be public at least two weeks before release. — [Steamworks Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- **Requirement, Steam onboarding:** Steam Direct currently lists a US$100 fee per product, a 30-day wait after paying before release of initial titles, and a review of store page/build (normally 1–5 days). These are conditional costs/timings, not an approved Boldinbeauty budget. — [Steamworks Partner Program](https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect)
- **Requirement, store assets:** Steam requires at least five actual gameplay screenshots; it disallows concept art, pre-rendered stills, and marketing copy in screenshot slots. Base capsules are limited to game artwork, name, and official subtitle. — [Steam store graphical assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard); [Steam graphical asset rules](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules)
- **Trailer distinction:** Steam says a trailer is required as part of the *game release* process and strongly recommends the first store trailer primarily show gameplay, preferably from the player perspective, with UI when useful. The Coming Soon overview calls a gameplay trailer “ideal,” so do not misstate it as an absolute prerequisite for an early Coming Soon page. — [Steamworks trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer); [Steamworks Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon)
- **Release-date display:** Steam requires an internal intended date but lets the public page display only “Coming Soon.” Such pages sort behind more specific dates on upcoming lists; date changes are limited within two weeks of the intended date. — [Steamworks release dates](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/release_dates)
- **Wishlists and visibility:** Valve says there is no minimum wishlist count for Steam to show a game. Wishlists help with launch notifications and some areas such as Popular Upcoming, but generally are not a factor in algorithmic visibility; Valve also says wishlist totals cannot accurately predict sales. — [Steamworks wishlists](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist); [Steamworks visibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility)
- **Measurement:** Steam's UTM/traffic reports can attribute store visits and wishlist conversions from external links after the page exists. — [Steam store/platform traffic reporting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/traffic_reporting)

### Inferences
- Confirm Steam, product business model, intended internal date, supported OS at launch, and claim-ready feature set before paying/onboarding or promising a wishlist destination. Then build the page around a representative gameplay loop, legible key art, honest tags, actual screenshots, and a public date display matching uncertainty.
- Once live, use one Steam link with channel-specific UTM tags in video descriptions/profile links and press kit. Judge experiments by qualified wishlist additions and feedback, alongside viewing and community activity. Do not invent a target wishlist count or conversion rate.

### Gaps
- Whether Steam will carry this game, whether it launches free to play, which operating systems ship first, and when release can be estimated are unconfirmed. No project store analytics or comparable conversion baseline was available.

## When and how should press and creators be approached?

### Takeaway
Research relevant outlets and creators now; send targeted pitches when there is a clear news hook, approved media, and a credible way to experience or discuss the game. Steam's Curator Connect is an option only after a public Coming Soon page and playable uploaded build exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Steam platform tool:** Curator Connect lets developers filter curators by tags, language, OS, and audience, then send a brief personalized message and playable review copy. It requires a public Coming Soon page or released game, uploaded build, and “Generate CD Keys” permission. Curators have no obligation to accept or review. — [Steamworks Curators and Curator Connect](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/curators)
- **Steam advice:** Valve places Curator Connect/review copies near release when the team wants press, reviewers, streamers, and influencers to try the game; Steam keys are another review-copy path for those outside Curator Connect. — [Steamworks marketing tools](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/tools)
- **Steam advice:** Valve notes that press and streamers benefit from knowing whether a title is weeks or years away; the store date display can remain broad while plans are uncertain. — [Steamworks release dates](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/release_dates)

### Inferences
- Segment a contact sheet for anime/action RPG, character collecting, Unreal development, and indie discovery coverage; record why each audience fits. Prepare a brief pitch with one distinct hook, current footage or screenshots, a press kit, contact details, and an explicit status/date caveat. No mass outreach or paid creator deal is justified by these sources alone.
- Use a reveal, playable demo, playtest, or confirmed launch window as a pitch moment. If Steam is confirmed and a suitable build exists, Curator Connect can supplement direct outreach; match by audience and language rather than follower count alone.
