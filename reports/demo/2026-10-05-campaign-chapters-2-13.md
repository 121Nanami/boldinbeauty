## Decisions made by the assistant (all reversible, OPEN for the owner)

1. 8 levels in every chapter (MAIN does not say; Chapter 1 has 8).
2. "The stage before the boss" read as the section before the boss section of a chapter's last level (MAIN section 7 now records this as WORKING).
3. **Rewards are one monotone curve** (Gold 5,000 + 120 per stage, Bytes half, Angel hearts 40 + 5 per stage, every clear). The old Chapter 1 placeholder (5,000 x level Gold, 40,000 at level 8) made Chapter 1 the best farm of a 104-stage game, which would have made every later stage pointless to replay for; Chapter 1's rewards fell. Diamonds and the ticket are first-clear only (Chapter 1: 300 x level Diamonds as before; later 150 per level, 1,500 for a chapter's last level). The equipment drop stays on every clear.
4. The new enemies' numbers and behaviours, the chapter mix themes (ranged in 2, mages in 3, assassins in 4, then one or two types leaned on), the labels "Ranged angel", "Mage angel", "Assassin angel".
5. Chapter 1's plans and enemy numbers are untouched; level 8 keeps mini boss and boss in the same section (the new rule is applied from Chapter 2).
6. The reference player's assumptions (team Luna, Plack, Kuu; about 3,040 team dps with ultimates from the kit balance table; 60% / 80% uptime; dodging shares; 85% of the currency reaches the team; about one replay per level, two to three before a boss).
7. Only the Chapter 1 boss gives an operator (Plack); later chapter bosses pay a Special ticket.
