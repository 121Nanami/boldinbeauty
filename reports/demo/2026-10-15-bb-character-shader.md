## Texture inputs (all optional; off by default)
| Input | Format | Packing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BaseMap` | sRGB colour | albedo |
| `NormalMap` | tangent normal | applied by hand (unlit materials have no Normal pin) |
| `ILMMap` | linear, Masks compression | R specular mask · G shadow bias (0.5 neutral; AO / forced shadow below) · B hair-band jitter · A identity mask (keeps colour under dread / horror) |
| `RampMap` | sRGB, 256×N | U = light (0 shadow → 1 lit), V = `RampRow` (one row per material) |
| `FaceSDF` | linear, Masks, 1 channel | authored for light from the head's left: lit where value > light angle (0 front … 1 behind); mirrored automatically |
| `MatcapMap` | sRGB sphere map | view-space normal |
| `EmissiveMap` | sRGB | R = emissive mask |
Head axes (`HeadForwardAxis`, `HeadRightAxis`) are the mesh component's local axes; a skeletal head needs its head-bone direction
fed by code (OPEN, out of this shader-only scope).
