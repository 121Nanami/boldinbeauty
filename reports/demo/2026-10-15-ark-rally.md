## Legal approach: clean room

Extracting or decompiling Big Bang Racing and then rewriting its code or reusing its assets would produce a derivative of Traplight's copyrighted work, so it would not be "copyright free". Game mechanics, rules and feel are not protected by copyright. So:

- **Followed:** only publicly described gameplay (store listings and 2016 press coverage): a 2D side-view physics dirt bike, gas and reverse on one side, lean back and forward on the other, coins, checkpoints and hazards, racing other riders (here, ghosts), and tracks that players build and share.
- **Not done:** no download, decompilation or extraction of the app. No code, art, sound, level layout, name or text of Big Bang Racing was used.
- **Original:** the physics model, the track code format, the tracks, the names (Ark Rally, Ark Outskirts, Neo Tokyo Rooftops, Halo Ruins), the visuals (Boldinbeauty palette, operators as rivals) and every number.

Sources consulted for the public gameplay description: TouchArcade (2016-07-18 release coverage), GameSkinny (one million downloads), APKMirror / AppFollow store listings.

## Completed work and checks

**Rules (pure C++, no Unreal), `BIBRules/BIBRally.h/.cpp`:**
- **Physics** (fixed 1/240 s steps): a rigid chassis and two wheels on anisotropic spring-dampers with bump stops. Wheels have grip and spin; the rear wheel is driven with a torque curve (strong from a standstill, half at top speed). The brake works on both wheels, then reverses.
- **Leaning:** lean torque in the air, plus a strong wheelie torque when leaning back on the gas with a wheel down (held too long, the bike loops over).
- **Collisions:** the frame scrapes the ground. The rider's head touching the ground, or falling into a gap, is a crash: 1.2 s, then a respawn at the last checkpoint. `Respawn()` (key R) frees a stuck bike.
- **Track code:** `F` flat, `H` hill, `U` slope, `R` ramp, `G` gap, `W` bumps; `C` coins, `B` bomb, `S` spring, `P` boost pad, `K` checkpoint. At most 600 characters and 2,000 m. Steepness is limited to slope 0.9 so every hill can be climbed from rest. Errors explain which piece is wrong.
- **Three built-in tracks**, one per difficulty.
- **Rivals:** an autopilot drives a reference run when the race starts. Three rivals (random operators with kits) replay it as ghosts at per-difficulty paces. If the autopilot cannot finish a player-made track, the ghosts glide along the ground.
- **Results:** place (1-4), coins, crashes, time limit, stars (finishing 1 + 1 per rival beaten + 1 with no crash) and score.
- **Profile side:** mini-game record slot 4 (`EMiniGame::ArkRally`, `EGame::ArkRally`), 1 arcade token per race, the Rhythm-scale reward table. The player's own track is saved in `FArcadeRecords::RallyTrack` (save key `arcadeRecords.rallyTrack`) and is free practice with no reward or record. Older saves load unchanged: the new record slots are appended at the end.

**Hub (`BIBPagesExtra.cpp`, `BIBPages.h`):**
- An Arcade tile, plus the page `arcade-rally`: three tracks, Write / Edit your track (the text prompt), Ride your track, and Start from a built-in track (asks before replacing).
- `EOverlay::ArkRally`: A = difficulty, B = 1 for the player's own track.

**Unreal layer (not compiled here):**

Owner: "1. Flips don't give nitro/boost 2. We don't have the buggy give the option of 2 cars the dirtbike is faster but more prone to failing by flipping over the buggy is steady and harder to do flips and get nitro but much more sterdy and won't die as easily 3. Base: `main` @ `1fc0031` (which already holds Ultimate Custom Night); branch `claude/funny-keller-yka6n1` was restarted from it.

1. **Flips give nitro.**
   - A landed flip fills the nitro meter by 0.34, so three flips fill it. A flip counts as whole turns in the air, landed within about 35 degrees, with no crash in the next 0.3 s.
   - Hold Space, Shift or the on-screen NITRO button to burn it: +14 m/s per second, up to 7 m/s past top speed.
   - Each flip scores +150. A flip that ends in a crash pays nothing.
   - The HUD has a nitro meter, a flip counter and "FLIP! +NITRO" pop-ups.
2. **Two vehicles** (`ERallyVehicle`, `FRallyVehicleSpec`, `RallyVehicleSpec()`), chosen on the Ark Rally page and saved (`arcadeRecords.rallyVehicle`). The rivals ride the same vehicle.
   - **Dirt bike:** top speed 22 m/s, light, flips easily.
   - **Buggy:** top speed 17.5 m/s.
     - Heavy, wide, big wheels; it turns about 35% slower in the air, so it flips only off big jumps.
     - It self-levels in the air while you are not leaning, and it can't wheelie.
     - Roll cage: it crashes only after lying on its roof or side for 1.2 s.
   - The overlay draws the buggy with a roll cage.
3. **Less punishing dirt bike:**
   - The head only counts once the bike is tilted past about 77 degrees from the ground.
   - A held wheelie balances (the wheelie push fades out and the rider pulls the nose back past about 45 degrees), so it never loops over on the ground.
   - A 0.35 s landing assist caps the spin after a touchdown.
   - A crash comes back on the last safe ground from at least 1.5 s before (further back on quick repeats), with 85% of the speed it had there, after 0.9 s. Before, it was the last checkpoint at a standstill after 1.2 s.
4. **Night Shift removed from the arcade:** the tile, the `arcade-night` page, the `arc:night` / `arc:nightgo` actions, the Ark station text and the profile line. Its rules, overlay, key bindings and saved records stay, because Ultimate Custom Night is built on them.
