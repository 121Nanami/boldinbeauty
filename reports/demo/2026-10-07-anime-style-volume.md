## What was built
| Piece | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ABIBAnimeStyleVolume` | `Source/.../BIBGame/BIBAnimeStyle.h/.cpp` | Unbound post-process volume. Adds the anime material as a blendable, turns off motion blur, film grain, chromatic fringe and lens flares, soft bloom, light vignette. Owns the frame-hold extension below. |
| Frame hold (12 fps) | `FBIBFrameStepExtension` in the same file | A scene view extension. After the whole frame is rendered and post-processed, it keeps a copy on each new visual step and writes that copy back on every other frame. The game, physics, animation and input keep running at full rate; only what is shown on screen updates 12 times a second. |
| `M_BIBAnimeStyle` + `MI_BIBAnimeStyle` | `/Game/BIB/Materials`, generated by `Tools/build_assets.py` | Post-process material after tonemapping: thick ink lines from depth (silhouettes), normals (creases) and colour (VFX / translucency that have no depth), 3 px at 1080p and scaled with resolution, thinner and fading with distance; 4 soft cel bands that keep hue; cool shadow tint; +20% saturation. 18 parameters, tune them in `MI_BIBAnimeStyle`. |
| Damage numbers | `BIBArcadeOverlay.cpp` (`SBIBDamageLayer`) | Re-placed only on a new visual step, so they move at 12 fps with the world, and drawn with a thick ink outline. |
| Placement | `build_assets.py` (`MapBuilder.anime_style`), `greybox_common.py` (`GreyboxMap.save`) | One volume in each of `L_Chapter1_Stage1..8`, `L_NeoTokyo`, `L_Ark`. **None in `L_Hub`** (the main menu). |
| Code-built stages | `ABIBGameMode::UpdateAnimeStyle` | Chapters 2-13, the tower, the arena and Boss Rush run inside `L_Hub`. The game mode spawns the volume there when a stage starts and switches it off when the player is back on the menus. |

Because the hold happens on the finished image, **everything the 3D view draws** — characters, enemies, world text, lights,
fog, translucent and particle effects, camera shake — is both stylised and stepped. Screen UI (hub, HUD, pause, results,
Night Shift / Rhythm and the other 2D arcade overlays) is drawn after the 3D view and stays smooth; this is deliberate so
menus and buttons do not feel laggy. Damage numbers are the one UI layer that is stepped, because they belong to the world.

**Controls:** `VisualStepRate` (default 12) and `bStepVisuals` on the volume; console `bib.VisualStepRate <n>` overrides it
for a session (`0` = smooth, `-1` = use the volume). In the editor the stylisation shows in the viewport of any map with a
volume; stepping only happens in Play.

## Update 2 (2026-10-07, branch `claude/anime-style-build`): heavier, more unique look + standalone Mac build
**Owner request:** push everything to a new build; make the post-process more stylised, mainly more shaky with heavier
shading, a very unique look. **Owner follow-up (same day):** remove the shake or make it less jarring.
- **Shading:** 3 hard bands (was 4 soft); a deep lavender shadow tier (`ShadowLevel` 0.38, darkened 25%); diagonal
  hatching in shadows and cross-hatching in the deepest shadows; a warm flat highlight tier (`HighlightLevel` 0.82);
  saturation 1.3; light paper grain.
