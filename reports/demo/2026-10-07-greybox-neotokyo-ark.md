## Owner request (2026-10-07, summarised)
Greybox levels from the two Fab kits **SCI-FI BUILDINGS CITY KIT** (1) and **SCI-FI BUILDINGS CITY KIT V.2** (2); two actual maps:
1. **Neo Tokyo:** lots of buildings, heavy rain and fog; the main way to travel is climbing up buildings and going through the
   city's many streets; placeholders for neon signs and other dressing.
2. **The arc** (main map): the **operators' area**, a large dome with many identical operator rooms distinguishable only by wear
   and tear, a central area used each day for roll call, and the only way out being **two catwalks with minimal railing across a
   massive hole**; the **town square** with everything a city needs (shops, restaurants, gyms ...), the **government building**
   striking in the middle, the **armoury** and the unmarked **"task" room** with many chutes up to the surface (how operators reach
   it), **ladders along the edges** down to the **Underworld** (criminals and "trade"), and an **unmarked building with a small red
   stripe**: the resistance meeting point; the **civilian area**, a 2050s utopia with fake lush grass and trees and mansion-like
   houses (only the ultra rich survived); the **secret dome**: a long catwalk into the sky with a single long staircase to a
   large dome the Secrets live in.

## What was built
| Map | Path | Actors | Kit meshes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neo Tokyo | `/Game/Greybox/Maps/L_NeoTokyo` | 5,119 | 103 buildings |
| The Ark | `/Game/Greybox/Maps/L_Ark` | 7,627 | 83 town buildings, government, armoury, resistance, 36 mansions |

All generated by script, nothing hand-placed: `Demo/BoldInBeautyDemo/Tools/build_greybox.sh` runs `build_greybox.py`, which
imports the kits (`greybox_import.py`, 62 + 60 FBX from the Fab vault cache, untextured, complex-as-simple collision so every
building surface can be stood on), makes greybox materials (`greybox_common.py`, `M_GB` + 27 instances) and builds both maps
(`greybox_neotokyo.py`, `greybox_ark.py`). About 4 minutes for the first import, then about 30 s per rebuild. `BIB_GREYBOX_ONLY=neotokyo|ark`
rebuilds one map. It never touches `/Game/BIB` or the demo's game mode. Both maps override the game mode with the engine
`GameModeBase`: **Play spawns a free-flying DefaultPawn** (WASD + mouse, E/Q up/down) at the PlayerStart.

**Colour code:** orange = climbable (pipes, ledges, fire-escape landings, rooftop bridges); yellow = ladders; neon colours =
sign / billboard / holo-ad placeholders; dark brown patches = wear-and-tear placeholders; red = the resistance stripe.
Editor labels (TextRender) are **hidden in game**: unmarked buildings stay unmarked in play. Shop signs, GOVERNMENT and ARMOURY
are visible in game. Actor tags (`Climbable`, `ClimbRoute`, `RooftopBridge`, `NeonSignPlaceholder`, `OperatorRoom`, `Wear_0..3`,
`Catwalk`, `Chute`, `UnderworldLadder`, `TradeStall`, `Mansion`, `FakeTree`, `SecretStaircase`, `AreaMarker` ...) let later
gameplay code find them. The Ark has a `TargetPoint` marker per area.

### Neo Tokyo
8 x 8 city blocks of 60 m; streets of 14 m, 24 m and a 36 m avenue each way, meeting at a scramble crossing (player start, four
holo-ad screens). The outer ring is a Kit 2 skyline (up to about 185 m); the inner blocks hold Kit 2 mid-rises, flat Kit 2 "city
tile" low-rises or clusters of four Kit 1 towers with alleys between them (overhead cables, bins, steam-vent placeholders).
Climbing: every building has one or two facade climb routes (drain pipe, a hand ledge every 4 m, a fire-escape landing every
8 m, up to 48 m) and 54 rooftop bridges cross streets at 12 / 24 / 36 m. 812 neon blade signs, 101 billboards, 4 holo ads,
lamp posts on every kerb, cars, vending machines, puddles. Night lighting, wet low-roughness roads, heavy volumetric height fog.
**Rain is a placeholder**: an invisible volume `RAIN_VOLUME_PLACEHOLDER` covering the map (no Niagara rain system exists in the
project yet). Invisible collision walls ring the city.

### The Ark (west to east along +X)
1. **Operators' dome** (240 m wide): 315 identical rooms (bunk, locker, desk) on 4 tiers around the wall, walkways with rails and
   stairs between tiers; each room has a wear level 0-3 shown only by placeholder stains, cracks, debris, a scorch mark, a tilted
   bunk or a missing lintel. Roll-call platform in the middle (podium, assembly lines, flagpole). The only exit is a gap in the
   dome wall onto **two catwalks** (2 m wide) **across a 200 m wide, 300 m deep hole** that spans the cavern wall to wall; each has a
   single thin rail on one side with sparse posts, about a quarter of them missing.
2. **Town square** (400 m): the **government building** (Kit 2 spire, 131 m) on a stepped round plaza in the middle; 83 Kit 1
   buildings with shop signs (shop, restaurant, gym, clinic, bar, market, cinema, hotel, bank, school, pharmacy, noodle bar, cafe,
   barber, laundry, arcade, tailor, electronics, bakery, library); the **ARMOURY**; the unmarked **task room** (plain box, plain door,
   16 launch pads with chutes rising through the 150 m rock ceiling to the surface); the **resistance meeting point**, an unmarked
   house with a small red stripe by its door, tucked into the far corner. **8 ladders** (4 on each long edge) go down shafts into the
   **Underworld**: a 40 m tall level under the whole square with support pillars, a shanty maze of shacks, red / purple neon and an
   open trade floor of stalls under the government plaza.
3. **Civilian area** (400 m) through a gated wall (booth, barrier): fake-grass ground, a fake-sky ceiling panel, a tree-lined
   boulevard and 36 mansions (Kit 1 houses at 2.2x) on hedged plots with driveways and fake trees.
4. **Secret dome:** a 90 m catwalk leaves through the cavern's east wall, then **one staircase of 500 steps** climbs 200 m into
   the open sky to a landing and a 100 m glass dome on a support cone.
