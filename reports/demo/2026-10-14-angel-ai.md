## Owner request (2026-10-13, `reports/demo/goal-2-angel-ai-brief.md` verbatim)
Every Angel enemy should fight at or above a skilled player's level while staying fair: play smart, never cheat. Behaviours for every
angel: spacing, punishing mistakes (whiffs, heals, ultimate wind-ups), defence (dodge / block / sidestep telegraphed heavies with a
skill-scaled chance, never perfect), feints and mixups, adapting (dodge direction, favourite opener, spam), coordination (shared attack
tokens, flanking, alternating pressure, ranged repositioning for line of sight), self-preservation (retreat / regroup / change tactics
when low or close to a break), the whole kit (distance, angle, cooldowns, player state; never purely random). Fairness: no reading
inputs before they happen, reactions from real perception plus a 150-300 ms delay by tier, every attack keeps a readable telegraph,
difficulty knobs per tier in data (reaction time, aggression, dodge chance, adaptation rate). Only AI, perception and enemy ability
code / data; the player ability code of goal 1 stays as it is. **Owner decisions (2026-10-13):** approved the plan; (1) a read-only
getter on the player for what the angels can see; (2) tiers Regular / Elite / Boss with reactions getting faster by chapter; (3)
ranged shots become dodgeable projectiles; "but i would make the angels overall a bit harder to face" -> `Difficulty = 1.15` on top
of the proposed knobs (reaction / attack interval divided by it, chances multiplied).

## Per angel type
✅ **Angel (grunt, Regular)** — spacing (holds its ring point outside the commander's reach, closes in on a recovering commander),
punish (whiffs / heals / long casts, may borrow a token), defence (sidestep away from its group, guard against light swings, more
guards against spam), feints and combos (2-3 strikes, enders: delayed vs early dodgers, sweep vs side dodgers, unblockable vs
blockers), adapt, coordination (tokens, flank), self-preservation (regroups at the group centre when under 25% or near BREAK), kit:
Strike, Combo, Feint, Unblockable, Blink, Dash strike, Sweep, Anti-air.

✅ **Heavy (brute, Regular)** — as the grunt, with a backstep at half the dodge chance, and its own kit: Strike, Combo, Sweep,
**Charge** (telegraphed armoured rush), Unblockable, Nova.

✅ **Ranged (Regular)** — keeps 800-1300 cm, backs off when pressed or outranged, strafes in its band, repositions for a clear line
when an ally blocks the shot or the commander is behind cover; shots fly and can be sidestepped; held shots (aim holds, second flash)
bait early dodges; aim leans to the favourite dodge side; at most two shooters at once, staggered; backsteps from heavies; regroups.
Kit: Strike (shot), Volley (three shots), Anti-air.

✅ **Mage (Regular)** — keeps 700-1100 cm; blasts are placed where the commander was seen (with the dodge-side lean) and stay there
(step out of the circle); Nova when the commander closes in; held casts; backsteps; regroups. Kit: Strike (blast), Nova, Anti-air.

✅ **Assassin (Regular)** — the unwarned dash is gone: **Lunge** is telegraphed, then rushes and strikes on contact, then backs off;
punishes from up to 5 m with a lunge; feints, combos, blink, unblockable.

✅ **Mini boss (Elite)** — faster reactions (209 ms), more dodges (46% x skill), always holds a token, whole kit (every move but guard /
flank), longer combos, changes tactics near BREAK (fights from range with Nova / Volley), coordinates with its escorts.

✅ **Boss (chapter boss, Boss tier)** — fastest reactions (174 ms, 150 ms from chapter 4), 57.5% x skill dodge chance (never certain),
the slam chosen by the brain with super armour, phase two shortens telegraphs by 10% (never under MinTelegraph) and lengthens combos,
changes tactics instead of running.

## Fairness checks (engine-free, `Tests/angel_brain_tests.cpp`, 4,600 checks, 0 failures)
- Reaction 150-300 ms for every tier and chapter 1-13; later chapters never slower; the floor holds against a bad config.
- No certain dodge / block / punish (caps 0.85 / 0.85 / 0.95); evasions measured per type stay under the knob; one roll per commander action.
- Fuzz (7 types x 5 chapters x 4,000 decisions, fully adapted mind): every attack's telegraph >= MinTelegraph (>= 0.25 s); every feint
  / held strike's real hit >= MinTelegraph after its second flash; combos <= 3 follow-ups.
- Perception: decisions use the delayed snapshot only; hidden commander -> the last known position, forgotten after MemorySeconds.
- **Clean play wins**: a scripted commander that reacts to each flash after a human 0.25 s and dodges (0.3 s i-frames) beats every type
  in chapters 1 and 13 (5 seeds each, bosses pre-damaged to their last stretch) **without taking a hit**; chapter 1: angel 6.5 s,
  heavy 21.6 s, ranged 18.3 s, mage 8.0 s, assassin 3.2 s, mini boss (10%) 11.4 s, boss (4%) 15.1 s at a 500 dps commander.
