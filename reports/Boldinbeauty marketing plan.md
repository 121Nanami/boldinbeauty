# Build Boldinbeauty demand through verified proof

**Working strategic proposal · 23 September 2026.** Boldinbeauty has a distinctive intended setting and combat structure, but the project record does not establish an implemented build, launch platform, release date, campaign budget, audience size, or approved public copy. The marketing job now is to test whether people understand and want the premise, then show representative play only when it exists. Start with private message research and an asset and claim inventory; move to public channels after owner approval and evidence review. If Steam becomes a confirmed release channel, a truthful Coming Soon page can become the wishlist destination. The 90-day sequence below is a relative operating template, activated only when a credible launch window and proof milestones exist. **Publishing, creator or press outreach, store publication, and paid spend each require separate authorization.**

## Source facts set the boundary for every claim

The project's current title is **Boldinbeauty**. Its recorded *intended design* is a 3D AAA-style gacha in a cyberpunk-like 2009 Neo Tokyo, after a 2002 catastrophe. Humanity lives underground in **“the arc”** while non-biblical angels of light and mathematics threaten it. Sentient operators, described as 50% human, 49% robot and 1% Angel, fight the angels. The player is a commander; Luna is the narrative lead. The intended combat lineup has three operators, one active at a time, with button switching and no accompanying AI teammates. These are source decisions, **not evidence that the game is playable or that these features are shipped**. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §§0–1, 6, 8](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L4))

The project lists Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and Apple mobile as platforms, while exact device coverage is OPEN and “Apple mobile means iOS” is WORKING. Single-player/multiplayer scope and first playable milestone are OPEN. No simultaneous launch commitment, release date, price, online requirement, audience demographic, published page, reach baseline, or approved marketing spend is established. The 20-operator roster and Luna, Plack and Kuu's story presence are documented, while biographies, individual goals and move sets are DEFERRED. The gacha design includes specified rarity odds and some Mileage and pity rules, but eligible pools and several counter and carry-over interactions are OPEN. Public economy copy must wait for final rules and review. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §§1–2, 5](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L12))

The project record explicitly requires implemented claims or clearly prospective wording, accurate gameplay and media provenance, and no invented release date, player count or testimonial. This proposal does not change canon or approve any campaign action. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §12.5.32](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L597))

## Test one promise before choosing the public voice

**Proposed positioning for research, not approved copy:** “In Neo Tokyo, command sentient operators against angels of light and mathematics while the government's sponsorship system profits from their fight.” It combines the world threat, operator identity and the government's sponsorship and exploitation premise. It does not claim a genre first, a particular play feel, moral stance on monetization, or a feature already implemented. A shorter alternative to test is “Humanity survives in the arc. Its operators fight what ended the world.” ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §8](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L134) and [government sponsorship context](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L141); [project positioning memo](../research_notes/Boldinbeauty%20marketing%20plan/project_positioning.md))

Use three **audience hypotheses**, not demographic targets: people who follow character collecting action games; people drawn to dystopian science fantasy; and people interested in switching combat. The cited games in the brief are design references, not proven competitors or audience overlap. In short, consent-based interviews or private concept reviews, show each cohort the same one-sentence premise and accurately labelled concept art. Ask what they think the player actually does, what they expect the game to cost, what confuses them, and what they would want to see before following it. Record verbatim objections and platform preference without treating a tiny sample as market size. With an approved representative build, repeat the test after observed play and compare understanding, interest and concerns. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §1](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L12); [project positioning memo](../research_notes/Boldinbeauty%20marketing%20plan/project_positioning.md))

The working message order is: **world and threat → sentient operators → demonstrated switching combat → story tension → finalized acquisition and availability details**. Keep the fiction's exploitative sponsorship system distinct from claims about the real payment system. Advance a line from *hypothesis* to *approved copy* only after the owner accepts it and production can show what the line promises.

## Run a relative 90-day pre-launch sequence

**Activation rule:** use Day −90 only after the owner sets an internal target window, production identifies a credible evidence path, and gate 0 has an owner. Until then, run gates 0–1 as an undated discovery sprint. The sequence creates preparation and decisions, not a promised public release or 90 days of posting. If the build, store, moderation or legal gates slip, move the schedule with them and update any approved public wording.

| Relative window | Work and dependency | Decision/output |
|---|---|---|
| Days −90 to −76 | Marketing lead assembles claim ledger, asset provenance and open-decision register. Production/QA identifies what can actually be shown; platform lead investigates intended channels. | Owner accepts research-safe facts; no public reveal assumed. |
| Days −75 to −61 | Research lead runs private hook and expectation interviews with the three audience hypotheses. Editorial makes a premise sheet and two or three concept variants, each marked as concept. | Select provisional positioning based on comprehension and objections; log unanswered gameplay/economy questions. |
| Days −60 to −46 | Production and QA validate representative capture if available. Marketing prepares a small content bank, captions, FAQ, press kit and response templates. Community lead sets moderation staffing and escalation rules if a public space is proposed. | Gate 2 reveal review. If capture is unavailable, use approved concept materials with explicit labels or defer reveal. |
| Days −45 to −31 | If Steam is confirmed, platform lead finishes onboarding, store checklist and review submission with enough buffer for Valve's process; marketing prepares channel-tagged links. Otherwise confirm an approved destination appropriate to the actual platform plan. | Gate 3: publish only through explicit owner and platform approval. No wishlist request before a live Steam page. |
| Days −30 to −15 | After reveal approval, publish a small series testing world, operator and verified play hooks. Collect questions and corrections. If a playable preview exists, QA and marketing prepare experience feedback; research relevant creators and outlets. | Keep or revise messaging based on qualified interest and confusion. Any outreach waits for separate authorization. |

If Steam is selected, Valve currently says a Coming Soon page needs a new app and store-presence checklist, Valve review and explicit publication; it recommends submitting at least **seven business days** before the page should go live, and the page must be public at least **two weeks** before release. Steam Direct currently lists a **US$100 per-product fee** and **30-day wait after payment** before an initial title's release. These are platform conditions, **not an approved Boldinbeauty cost or release schedule**. Valve requires at least five actual gameplay screenshots for store slots; its Coming Soon guidance calls a gameplay trailer ideal, while the trailer requirement applies to the game-release process. Recheck requirements before scheduling. ## Use a channel cadence the assigned team can sustain

**Owned home/base:** maintain one approved fact sheet and a link destination; after a storefront exists, keep its copy, tags and FAQ aligned with the build. For Steam only, the Coming Soon page can display “Coming Soon” while an internal intended date exists, though Valve says less specific dates sort behind more specific ones in some upcoming lists. Do not select Steam merely because the source lists desktop platforms. ([Steam release dates](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/release_dates); checked **2026-09-23**.)

**Short video:** after Gate 2, trial **one short video per week for four weeks**, repurposed to one primary platform and optionally a second if editing and replies fit capacity. A video can explain a setting detail using labelled concept art; only actual representative build capture should be called gameplay. Test hooks in sequence, retain the same factual description, and record what viewers understood. YouTube explicitly recommends a schedule the creator can maintain and supports Shorts uploads from phone or computer; current Shorts rules allow square or vertical videos up to three minutes. TikTok's gaming examples include gameplay, tutorials and fandom clips, but its cited gaming guide is from **2022-08-04** and is advice, not a forecast. Creative Center may help inspect formats without ad spend. ([YouTube Shorts tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12921536?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en); [YouTube Shorts length](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en); [TikTok gaming guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/gaming-marketers-ultimate-guide-tiktok-content); [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-center); platform facts checked **2026-09-23**.)

**Community:** begin with replies where approved content already appears and a single weekly question summary. Open a Discord only when a named moderator can cover it, rules and accurate description exist, safety settings are configured, and a response route exists for complaints and spoilers. Discord requires those foundations for Community status and recommends verification, AutoMod and human judgment. A Discord launch is optional; a private feedback form or moderated comment queue may be sufficient during discovery. ([Discord Community Server Guidelines](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035969312-Community-Server-Guidelines); [Discord enablement](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851-Enabling-Your-Community-Server); [Discord moderation](https://discord.com/community/automating-moderation-community-support); checked **2026-09-23**.)

**Press and creators:** maintain a small fit-ranked research sheet now, noting topic, language, contact route, relevant past coverage, asset needs and a personal angle. Draft a factual one-page kit; send only after authorization and a reveal, preview or other concrete news hook. If Steam is confirmed, Curator Connect requires a public Coming Soon or released page, uploaded build and key permission, and curators have no obligation to cover it. Its value is fit, not a follower-count promise. ([Steam Curators](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/curators); [Steam marketing tools](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/tools); checked **2026-09-23**.)

**Paid media:** no spend is proposed. A future test would need a named objective, cap, audience, approved creative, tracking, and owner authorization; do not infer a budget from the software-cost snapshot in the project brief.

## Measure learning before scale

Keep a weekly log with asset/version, channel, CTA, provenance, approval, publish date, feedback themes and what changed. Before launch, the primary signals are **correct understanding of the game**, repeated requests for a specific proof point, willingness to follow a truthful destination, and issues found in playtests. For short video, compare retention and meaningful comments rather than raw starts; YouTube changed Shorts view counting on **2025-03-31** to include starts and replays, while “Engaged views” tracks continued viewing. Treat a small sample as directional. ([YouTube Shorts overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en); checked **2026-09-23**.)

Once a Steam page is live, use channel-specific UTM links and review visits, wishlist additions and qualitative questions together. Valve provides store traffic reports for external links. It says there is **no minimum wishlist count** for visibility, wishlists generally are not an algorithmic visibility factor, and totals do not accurately predict sales; therefore set no invented wishlist target or sales conversion promise. Use a baseline after first real traffic, then compare message variants on qualified response and cost of producing them. ([Steam traffic reporting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/traffic_reporting); [Steam wishlists](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist); [Steam visibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility); checked **2026-09-23**.)

## Treat unresolved risk as a release dependency

| Risk | Practical control and owner |
|---|---|
| Gacha trust and monetization | Game design/economy lead resolves eligible pools, pity interactions, carry-over and purchase terms before any detailed FAQ or promotional claim. Marketing quotes exact approved odds and distinctions only after product, platform and applicable policy review; never describe fairness, affordability or safeguards without evidence. Distinguish fictional sponsorship exploitation from actual spending mechanics. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §2](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L25)) |
| Platform scope and timing | Platform/production lead verifies each supported OS and device, release sequence, intended date and store requirements. Marketing says “planned” only for genuinely intended, approved targets and avoids implying simultaneous release. Recheck external rules before submission. ([BoldInBeauty_MAIN §1](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L12)) |
| Reputation and moderation | Community lead owns published rules, response times, issue routing and a correction log. Escalate credible complaints on economy, representation or misleading footage to project owner and relevant leads; correct materially false copy on every live surface. No defensive testimonial or invented player consensus. |
| Asset provenance and build truth | Art/capture lead keeps source files, authorship and licence/consent records. QA verifies build/version and whether footage is representative. Label concept art, renders, mockups and generated previews plainly; do not place them in Steam gameplay screenshot slots. ([Steam assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard); [BoldInBeauty_MAIN §12.5.32](../BoldInBeauty_MAIN#L597)) |
| Schedule slippage | Production lead updates the internal milestone and marketing revises approved public timing. Pause countdown and outreach tied to a preview if the proof milestone slips. |
